    1. How leaders achieve results1. 領導者如何達成結果

    A leader does not produce results directly. The leader works through work culture, work process and people. The result has two parts: performance and satisfaction.領導者不是直接產出結果，而是透過工作文化、工作流程與人來達成。結果有兩個部分：績效與滿意度。

    3. When communication breaks down3. 溝通失靈的後果

    Slide p.5 lists the consequences in this order:投影片第 5 頁依序列出以下後果：

    1. Lack of information缺乏資訊
    2. Ambiguity, uncertainty模糊、不確定
    3. Need for structure: people create new tasks, and effort is wasted需要結構：自行創造新任務，努力被浪費
    4. Frustration, anger挫折、憤怒
    5. Feelings of isolation孤立感
    6. Inadequacy無能感
    7. Withdrawal退縮
    8. Poor performance, low morale績效差、士氣低

    The list starts with missing information and ends with poor performance. What looks like a people problem at the bottom may be an information problem at the top.清單從缺乏資訊開始，以績效差結束。底部看起來像「人」的問題，源頭可能是頂端的資訊問題。

    The slide prints a list, not arrows between each item. Say “this can develop into,” not “A proves B.”投影片是條列，沒有在每一項之間畫箭頭。可以說「可能發展成」，不要說「A 證明 B」。

    Link to S4: withdrawal resembles Neglect in EVLN. The difference is the starting point: EVLN begins from dissatisfaction, while this list begins from missing information.連結 S4：退縮類似 EVLN 的 Neglect。差別在起點：EVLN 從不滿意開始，這張清單從缺乏資訊開始。

    7Avoid blame避免責怪

    Don't assume poor performance is an individual's fault. It can come from structure and process.不要預設績效差是個人的錯，原因可能來自結構與流程。

    8Effort at the bottom基層的努力

    Effort must occur at the bottom to achieve the goals of the top, so the bottom needs information and support.高層的目標必須靠基層的努力才能達成，所以基層需要資訊與支援。

    Limit: the slides say poor performance can come from structure and process. They do not say individuals are never responsible.界線：投影片說績效差「可能」來自結構與流程，並沒有說個人永遠沒有責任。

    中文邏輯順序不要顛倒：先問結構和流程有沒有給他資訊與發揮空間，再問他個人有沒有問題。

    6. Communication networks simulation6. 溝通網絡模擬

    The second half of the session puts participants in a highly rigid, hierarchical organization: strict work rules, command and control, and top-down management and communication. They observe organizational productivity and their own feeling.本堂後半段讓學員進入一個高度僵化的階層組織：嚴格的工作規則、命令與控制、由上而下的管理與溝通。學員要觀察組織的生產力與自己的感受。

    1Examine how structure influences leadership, communication and decision-making.檢視結構如何影響領導、溝通與決策。
    2Experience its impact on teamwork and productivity; morale, satisfaction and commitment; and personal effectiveness.體驗它對團隊合作與生產力、士氣、滿意度與承諾，以及個人效能的影響。
    3Build a framework for thinking about leadership and communication that helps people learn from experience.建立思考領導與溝通的框架，幫助從經驗中學習。
    4Task: solve the problem as a team, before the other teams, within the time limit.任務：以團隊解題，比其他隊先完成，且在時限內完成。

    8. Cold call practice8. Cold call 練習

    How do leaders achieve results?領導者如何達成結果？

    Through work culture, work process and people. Results are performance and satisfaction.透過工作文化、工作流程與人；結果包括績效與滿意度。

    Describe the linear model of communication.說明線性溝通模型。

    A speaker encodes and speaks a message, and listeners decode it. The arrow runs one way.說話者編碼並說出訊息，聽眾解碼；箭頭只有單一方向。

    What three factors influence communication?影響溝通的三個因素是什麼？

    Context, the setting; frame of reference, what the listener brings; and noise, interference at the moment of delivery.情境（場合）、參照框架（聽者帶來的背景）與干擾（當下的妨礙）。

    What happens when communication breaks down?溝通失靈會發生什麼？

    The slide runs from lack of information and uncertainty, through wasted effort, frustration, isolation and withdrawal, to poor performance and low morale.投影片從缺乏資訊與不確定開始，經過努力浪費、挫折、孤立與退縮，最後是績效差與士氣低。

    Is poor performance the individual's fault?績效差是個人的錯嗎？

    Don't assume so. It can come from structure and process, so check the system before blaming the person.不要預設是。原因可能來自結構與流程，所以先檢查系統再責怪個人。

    What is the best structure?最好的結構是什麼？

    There is no one best structure. Functional suits small firms with few products, matrix medium firms with multiple products, product division really large firms with product groups.沒有最好的結構。功能別適合產品少的小企業，矩陣式適合多產品的中型企業，產品事業部適合有多個產品群的大型組織。

    When can a horizontal structure be used?什麼時候可以用水平式結構？

    Where customer contact is frequent and direct, and the task does not require being correct 100% of the time.與顧客接觸頻繁且直接，且任務不需要百分之百正確時。

    What is a lattice organization?什麼是網狀組織？

    A network where everyone can reach everyone without an intermediary, vision comes from the group, and leaders emerge rather than being appointed.每個人都能不經中間人直接聯繫彼此，願景來自群體，領導者是自然產生而非正式任命。

    In the simulation, why does B matter so much?模擬中為什麼 B 這麼關鍵？

    If messages follow the chart, every route between A and the staff, or between staff, passes through B.如果訊息沿著圖上的線傳遞，A 與員工之間、員工彼此之間的每條路徑都要經過 B。
