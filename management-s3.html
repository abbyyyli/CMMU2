    1. Where motivation comes from1. 動機從哪裡來？

    Motivation 動機 is what drives the direction and persistence of effort at work.動機是驅動工作努力方向與持續性的力量。

    Intrinsic內在動機 內在

    Comes from the work itself: challenge, mastery, meaning, enjoyment. An analyst enjoys cracking a hard problem.來自工作本身：挑戰、精熟、意義與樂趣，例如分析師享受破解難題。

    Extrinsic外在動機 外在

    Comes from outside rewards or consequences: pay, bonus, promotion, punishment. A salesperson works harder for commission.來自外部獎勵或後果：薪資、獎金、晉升與懲罰，例如業務為佣金而更加努力。

    They are not either-or. The useful question is what currently drives behavior, and whether it pushes effort toward the right outcome.兩者不是非此即彼。真正有用的問題是：目前什麼在驅動行為？它是否把努力導向正確成果？

    2. Needs theories2. 需求理論

    Maslow and ERG describe which needs exist. Herzberg explains why meeting some needs does not create motivation.Maslow 與 ERG 描述有哪些需求；Herzberg 解釋為何滿足某些需求，卻不會創造動機。

    Maslow and ERG: same needs, different logicMaslow 與 ERG：需求相似，邏輯不同

    Self-actualization自我實現需求Continuous growth toward new goals持續成長，朝新目標前進
    Self-esteem自尊需求Profession, success, pride專業、成功與自豪
    Social社交需求Community, relationships群體與人際關係
    Safety安全需求Avoiding danger; laws, rules避免危險；法律與規則
    Physiological生理需求Food, shelter, clothing食物、住所與衣物
    Growth成長需求
    Relatedness關係需求
    Existence存在需求

    The slide places self-esteem under both Relatedness and Growth.投影片把自尊需求同時放在「關係」與「成長」之下。

    Maslow馬斯洛需求層級ERGERG 理論
    Categories分類Five levels五個層級Three: Existence, Relatedness, Growth三類：存在、關係與成長
    Order順序Lower needs are met before higher ones are pursued較低層需求滿足後，才追求較高層需求Several needs can be pursued at the same time可同時追求多種需求
    When a higher need is blocked較高層需求受阻時Not addressed未處理此機制Frustration-regression 挫折退化: desire for lower needs increases挫折退化：對較低層需求的渴望增加
    Case question個案問題Does the company give employees chances to fulfill all five levels?公司是否讓員工有機會滿足五個層級？Which needs coexist, and is growth being blocked?哪些需求同時存在？成長是否受阻？

    注意Frustration-regression 是理論機制。個案要有證據顯示員工真的轉向在意薪水或保障，才能說它發生了。

    Herzberg: removing dissatisfaction is not creating motivationHerzberg：消除不滿不等於創造動機

    Hygiene factors 保健因子 and motivators 激勵因子 work on two different scales. Toggle the factors below.保健因素與激勵因素作用於兩條不同的量尺。請切換下方因素。

    The lecture links this to Maslow: hygiene needs form the foundation and must be met first. Motivators without that base may not work, and hygiene alone will not create high motivation.課堂將它與 Maslow 連結：保健需求構成基礎，必須先滿足。缺少基礎時，激勵因素可能無效；但只有保健因素，也不會創造高度動機。

    3. Theory X and Theory Y3. X 理論與 Y 理論

    These describe a manager's assumptions 假設 about workers, and those assumptions shape the management style.兩者描述主管對員工的假設，而這些假設會塑造管理方式。

    Theory XX 理論Theory YY 理論
    Assumes workers對員工的假設Dislike work, avoid responsibility, have little ambition不喜歡工作、逃避責任、缺少企圖心Can enjoy work, accept responsibility, self-direct可以享受工作、接受責任並自我引導
    Style管理方式"Do it!" Direct, control, reward and punish「照做！」指揮、控制、獎勵與懲罰Support, praise, give responsibility支持、讚賞與賦予責任
    Motivates through激勵途徑Pay and performance薪酬與績效Individual goals個人目標
    Focus焦點Organizational goals only只重視組織目標Personal goals connected to work把個人目標連結到工作
    Manager assumes staff cannot be trusted主管認為員工不值得信任→Close control, micromanagement嚴密控制與微觀管理→Staff lose initiative, act passively員工失去主動性，變得被動→Manager feels proven right主管覺得自己的假設得到驗證↺

    Theory Y is not always better. Ask whether the style fits the people and the task. New staff on high-risk work may need structure; experienced experts under micromanagement waste their ability.Y 理論不一定永遠較好。要問管理方式是否適合人員與任務。高風險工作的新手可能需要結構；有經驗的專家若被過度控制，能力反而遭到浪費。

    4. Goal setting and MBO4. 目標設定與 MBO

    Management by Objectives (MBO) 目標管理 works only if people know what is expected, by when, and how they are doing.目標管理（MBO）要有效，員工必須知道期待是什麼、何時完成，以及目前做得如何。

    Goal specificity目標具體性

    What counts as success?怎樣才算成功？

    Explicit time period明確的時間範圍

    By when?何時之前完成？

    Participation參與

    Employees take part in goal decisions.員工參與目標決策。

    Performance feedback績效回饋

    Am I meeting expectations?我達到期待了嗎？

    Slide example of a SMART goal: "Increase customer satisfaction rate by 20% at the end of 2024." It is specific, measurable and time-bound.投影片的 SMART 目標例子：「在 2024 年底，將顧客滿意率提高 20%。」它具體、可衡量且有期限。

    Cascading objectives層層展開的目標

    Organization組織↓Division事業部↓Department部門↓Individual個人

    The point is alignment 對齊: hitting an individual KPI should move the department, division and company goals.重點是對齊：個人達成 KPI，應能推進部門、事業部與公司的目標。

    Four problems with goal setting目標設定的四個問題

    1. Goals become ceilings: people stop at the target.目標變成天花板：員工達標就停止。
    2. Employees set easy goals to protect themselves.員工為自保而設定容易的目標。
    3. What gets measured gets done: people chase the KPI even when it is a weak proxy for the real goal.衡量什麼，就做什麼：即使 KPI 只是實際目標的不佳替代指標，員工仍會追逐它。
    4. Hard-to-measure outcomes get ignored: trust, quality, innovation, long-term customer relationships.難以衡量的成果被忽略：信任、品質、創新與長期顧客關係。

    6. Case analysis method6. 個案分析方法

    An issue exists when there is a gap between the framework and workplace reality.理論架構與職場現實之間存在落差，才形成需要分析的議題。

    Symptom症狀 症狀

    Shows something is wrong. "Morale is low."顯示有問題，例如「士氣低落」。

    Issue議題 問題核心

    Explains it through a framework. "Supervision and relations are poor (hygiene), and growth opportunities are weak (motivators)."用架構解釋症狀，例如「督導與人際關係不佳（保健因素），成長機會也不足（激勵因素）」。

    Worked example: Company X (Maslow)演練：X 公司（Maslow）

    A manufacturer pays well above the industry average and has had zero accidents for years. It focuses on performance, gives employees no chance to build relationships, and pays by the number of products completed each day.一家製造商薪資遠高於業界平均，多年來零事故。它重視績效，不讓員工有機會建立關係，並依每天完成的產品數量付薪。

    Physiological生理需求No gap無落差Pay above industry average薪資高於業界平均
    Safety安全需求No gap無落差Zero accidents for years多年零事故
    Social社交需求Gap有落差No chance to build relationships沒有機會建立關係
    Self-esteem自尊需求No gap無落差Not explained on the slide投影片未解釋
    Self-actualization自我實現需求Gap有落差Not explained on the slide; work is judged only by daily output投影片未解釋；工作只依每日產量評價

    Conclusion: two motivation issues exist.結論：存在兩項動機議題。

    Improved after 18 months18 個月後改善的部分

    Sales, profit, accountability, timely financial reports. LMS reached KP Industries' standards.銷售、利潤、責任歸屬與財務報告時效。LMS 達到 KP Industries 的標準。

    Deteriorated惡化的部分

    Turnover of well-paid top people, morale, relationships, training and career development, feedback, customer satisfaction, new products (none in 12 months).高薪優秀人才流失、士氣、人際關係、訓練與職涯發展、回饋、顧客滿意度，以及新產品（12 個月內為零）。

    The tension: the president, Apichart, fixed financial performance, but the people system and broader strategic results declined.     8. Common mistakes8. 常見錯誤

    Employees lack motivation, so raise pay.員工沒有動機，所以加薪。

    Diagnose first: needs, style or goals?先診斷：需求、管理方式，還是目標？

    They are well paid, so they should be motivated.薪資很高，所以應該有動機。

    Pay is a hygiene factor. It removes dissatisfaction only.薪資是保健因素，只能消除不滿。

    Theory X is bad, Theory Y is good.X 理論不好，Y 理論好。

    The style must fit the people and the task.管理方式必須符合人員與任務。

    Decentralized authority means Theory Y.權限下放代表 Y 理論。

    Authority can be decentralized while the climate stays control-heavy (LMS).即使權限下放，組織氛圍仍可能高度控制，例如 LMS。

    Everyone hit their KPI, so the company is doing well.每個人都達到 KPI，所以公司很好。

    The KPI may be a weak proxy for the real goal.KPI 可能只是實際目標的不佳替代指標。

    Frustration-regression happened.發生了挫折退化。

    Show evidence that people actually shifted to lower needs.必須提出證據，顯示員工確實轉向較低層需求。

    Use all five theories to look thorough.把五種理論都用上，看起來更完整。

    Choose the framework that best fits the evidence.選擇最符合證據的架構。

    Low morale is the issue.士氣低落就是議題。

    It is a symptom. The issue is the framework gap behind it.那是症狀；議題是其背後的理論與現實落差。

    9. Cold call practice9. 課堂即答練習

    Answer aloud first, then open the card. Structure: definition, example, why it matters for a manager.先口頭回答，再打開卡片。架構：定義、例子，以及對管理者的意義。

    What is the difference between intrinsic and extrinsic motivation?內在與外在動機有何不同？

    Intrinsic comes from the work itself, such as challenge or meaning. Extrinsic comes from outside rewards or consequences, such as pay or promotion. Both can operate at once.內在動機來自工作本身，例如挑戰或意義；外在動機來自外部獎勵或後果，例如薪資或晉升。兩者可同時存在。

    What are Maslow's five needs?Maslow 的五種需求是什麼？

    Physiological, safety, social, self-esteem and self-actualization. Lower needs are met before higher ones are pursued.生理、安全、社交、自尊與自我實現。較低層需求滿足後，才追求較高層需求。

    How does ERG differ from Maslow?ERG 與 Maslow 有何不同？

    ERG groups needs into Existence, Relatedness and Growth, allows several to be pursued at once, and adds frustration-regression.ERG 分為存在、關係與成長，允許同時追求多種需求，並加入挫折退化機制。

    What is frustration-regression?什麼是挫折退化？

    When a higher need is blocked, the desire for a lower need increases. An employee with no promotion path may focus more on pay.較高層需求受阻時，對較低層需求的渴望增加。沒有晉升管道的員工，可能轉而更重視薪資。

    Name Herzberg's hygiene factors and motivators.列出 Herzberg 的保健因素與激勵因素。

    Hygiene: supervision, pay, company policies, working conditions, relations with others, job security. Motivators: career advancement, personal growth, recognition, responsibility, achievement.保健因素：督導、薪資、公司政策、工作環境、人際關係與工作保障。激勵因素：職涯晉升、個人成長、肯定、責任與成就。

    Does fixing hygiene factors create motivation?改善保健因素會創造動機嗎？

    No. It only removes dissatisfaction. Motivation requires motivators.不會，只會消除不滿。動機需要激勵因素。

    Are Theory X and Y employee types?X 與 Y 理論是員工類型嗎？

    Mainly they are managers' assumptions about workers, which shape management style.主要是主管對員工的假設，並據以塑造管理方式。

    What are the four key elements of MBO?MBO 的四個關鍵要素是什麼？

    Goal specificity, explicit time period, participation in decision making, and performance feedback.目標具體性、明確時間範圍、參與決策，以及績效回饋。

    What does "what gets measured gets done" warn about?「衡量什麼，就做什麼」提醒什麼風險？

    People work to hit the KPI. If the KPI is a weak proxy, the organization may not actually improve.人會努力達成 KPI；若 KPI 無法良好代表真正目標，組織未必實際改善。

    When does an issue exist?何時存在議題？

    When there is a gap between the framework and workplace reality.理論架構與職場現實存在落差時。

    What are the three ways to motivate people?激勵人的三種方式是什麼？

    Fulfill human needs, adjust management style to employee characteristics, and set goals.滿足人的需求、依員工特質調整管理方式，以及設定目標。

    Quick drill: which framework is sharpest?快問快答：哪個架構最精準？
