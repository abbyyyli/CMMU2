    Markets and Government市場與政府

    Prices coordinate markets well, but markets can fail. Government can help, yet every intervention changes incentives, so compare realistic options and their trade-offs.價格能有效協調市場，但市場也可能失靈。政府可以協助，然而每項干預都會改變誘因，因此必須比較現實可行的方案及其權衡。

    1. The price system1. 價格制度

    Prices solve two problems at once: who knows what, and who has a reason to act.價格同時解決兩個問題：誰掌握哪些資訊，以及誰有誘因採取行動。

    A price is a signal wrapped up in an incentive價格既是訊號，也是誘因

    Signal訊號

    A rising price tells everyone the good has become scarcer. No one needs to know why.價格上漲告訴每個人商品變得更稀缺；不必人人都知道原因。

    Incentive誘因

    The same price change rewards the right response: buyers use less, sellers supply more.同一價格變化會獎勵合適的反應：買方減少使用，賣方增加供給。

    • Dispersed knowledge 分散的資訊: information about costs, resources and wants is spread across millions of people. No planner holds it all.分散知識：成本、資源與需求的資訊分布在數百萬人手中，沒有任何規劃者能完全掌握。
    • Information problem: knowing what to produce and where. Incentive problem: getting people to act on it. Prices address both.資訊問題是「生產什麼、在哪裡生產」；誘因問題是「如何使人據此行動」。價格同時回應兩者。
    • I, Rose: farmers, air freight and florists each pursue their own income, and roses still reach a cold city on Valentine's Day. This is the invisible hand.《我，玫瑰》：農民、航空貨運業者和花店各自追求收入，玫瑰仍會在情人節送達寒冷城市。這就是「看不見的手」。

    Outcome and intent are different questions結果與動機是兩個不同問題

    The class frame separates intention from result. Markets sit in the unintentional-beneficial cell.課堂框架把意圖與結果分開；市場位於「無意但有益」的格子。

    • VHS on eBay: an old player let an elderly buyer recover family videos. Value is subjective 主觀; the seller does not need to know why the buyer values it.eBay 上的 VHS 錄放影機：舊機器讓年長買家找回家庭影像。價值具有主觀性；賣家無須知道買家珍惜它的原因。

    Equilibrium is a welfare benchmark均衡是福利分析的基準

    Equilibrium matters for more than Qd = Qs. Up to the equilibrium quantity, willingness to pay (WTP) ≥ marginal cost (MC). Beyond it, MC > WTP.均衡的意義不只是 Qd＝Qs。在均衡數量之前，支付意願（WTP）大於或等於邊際成本（MC）；超過後，MC 大於 WTP。

    邏輯後面每張政策圖都在比：政策下的數量 vs. efficient quantity。偏離越多，DWL 越大。

    2. Trade and tariffs2. 貿易與關稅

    A tariff 關稅 raises the domestic price. Producers gain, consumers lose more, and society loses the DWL.關稅使國內價格上漲。生產者得益，消費者損失更大，社會則承受無謂損失（DWL）。

    Group群體Effect of the tariff關稅的影響
    Domestic consumers國內消費者Pay more, buy less. CS falls.支付更高價格、購買更少；消費者剩餘下降。
    Domestic producers國內生產者Sell more at a higher price. PS rises.以更高價格賣出更多；生產者剩餘上升。
    Government政府Collects revenue on remaining imports.對仍然進口的商品徵收關稅。
    Society社會整體Deadweight loss from two sources (below).承受來自以下兩種來源的無謂損失。

    Two sources of deadweight loss無謂損失的兩個來源

    Wasted resources資源浪費

    High-cost domestic producers replace imports that cost less. Production inefficiency.成本較高的國內生產者取代成本較低的進口品，造成生產無效率。

    Lost gains from trade錯失的交易利益

    Some buyers value the good above the world price but stop buying at the tariff price.部分買家的估值高於世界價格，卻因關稅後的價格而停止購買。

    Not all consumer loss is DWL. Part is a transfer 移轉 to producers and government. DWL is the part nobody receives.消費者損失並非全是無謂損失。其中一部分轉移給生產者和政府；無謂損失則是任何人都沒有得到的部分。

    Case: the $10,000 electric car個案：一萬美元的電動車

    The US placed a 100% tariff on Chinese EVs in 2024. Analyze it on two levels and keep them separate:美國於 2024 年對中國電動車課徵 100% 關稅。分析時要區分兩個層次：

    Static view靜態視角

    Today's costs: higher prices, lower consumption, wasted resources, DWL.當下的成本：價格上漲、消費減少、資源浪費與無謂損失。

    Dynamic view動態視角

    Protection may give domestic firms time to scale, learn and secure supply chains.保護措施可能給國內企業時間擴大規模、學習並鞏固供應鏈。

    The tariff model shows the short-run cost. Whether long-run benefits outweigh it is an empirical question 要看實證, not something the graph can prove.關稅圖示呈現短期成本；長期利益是否超過成本，必須靠實證判斷，圖本身無法證明。

    3. Price controls3. 價格管制

    A binding control stops the price from clearing the market, so scarcity shows up in other ways.有約束力的價格管制使價格無法使市場出清，稀缺因此以其他形式出現。

    Price ceiling 最高限價最高限價Price floor 最低限價最低限價
    Rule規則Legal maximum法律規定的最高價格Legal minimum法律規定的最低價格
    Binding when何時有約束力Below equilibrium低於均衡價格Above equilibrium高於均衡價格
    Result結果Shortage (Qd > Qs)短缺（需求量大於供給量）Surplus (Qs > Qd)過剩（供給量大於需求量）
    Example例子Rent control租金管制Minimum wage最低工資

    Five effects of a binding ceiling有約束力的最高限價造成五種影響

    1. Shortage: Qd > Qs.短缺：Qd 大於 Qs。
    2. Lower quality: sellers cut costs they cannot pass on.品質下降：賣方削減無法轉嫁的成本。
    3. Queues and search costs: people pay with time instead of money.排隊與搜尋成本：人們以時間代替金錢支付。
    4. Deadweight loss: trades with WTP > cost disappear.無謂損失：支付意願高於成本的交易消失。
    5. Misallocation: goods go to whoever queues or has connections, not to those who value them most.錯誤配置：商品流向會排隊或有人脈的人，而非最重視商品的人。

    A low posted price is not a low true cost. Full cost = money price + time + search + lower quality.標示價格低，不代表真實成本低。完整成本＝金錢價格＋時間＋搜尋成本＋品質下降。

    注意投影片把兩種損失分開：wasted time（排隊搜尋）和 A + B（lost CS + lost PS = DWL）。不要混成同一塊。

    Case: rent control in Mumbai個案：孟買的租金管制

    Rent ceiling below equilibrium租金上限低於均衡水準→Landlord returns fall房東報酬降低→Less maintenance, units withdrawn維修減少、房源撤出→Shortage worsens短缺加劇

    The paradox: very cheap legal rent coexists with decaying buildings, vacant units and a housing shortage.悖論是：法定租金非常便宜，卻同時有老舊失修的建物、空置房屋和住房短缺。

    Blood plasma and outlawed markets血漿與非法市場

    • Blood plasma 血漿: banning donor pay acts like a price fixed at zero. Supply stays low, need stays high, so there is a shortage and DWL. This is an efficiency cost; the moral question needs separate analysis.血漿：禁止給付捐贈者，等同把價格固定在零。供給持續偏低、需求偏高，因此出現短缺與無謂損失。這是效率代價；道德問題須另外分析。
    • Outlawed markets: the slide shows both demand and supply shifting inward. Legal risk lowers participation on both sides. Quantity falls; the price effect is ambiguous 不確定 because it depends on which curve shifts more.非法市場：投影片顯示需求與供給都向內移動。法律風險減少雙方參與，數量下降；價格變化則不確定，取決於哪條曲線移動較多。

    4. Externalities4. 外部性

    An externality 外部性 is a cost or benefit that falls on a third party outside the trade.外部性是交易之外的第三方承受的成本或享有的利益。

    Social cost = Private cost + External cost社會成本＝私人成本＋外部成本
    Social benefit = Private benefit + External benefit

    Negative externality: the market produces too much負外部性：市場生產過多

    Decision-makers see only private cost決策者只看見私人成本→Cost looks too low成本看起來過低→Q market > Q efficient市場數量大於效率數量→DWL from overuse過度使用導致無謂損失
    • Antibiotics (MRU): overuse breeds resistant bacteria, a cost borne by future patients.抗生素（MRU）：過度使用滋生抗藥性細菌，成本由未來的病患承受。
    • Thailand PM2.5: private activity creates air-pollution costs for people outside the trade. The slide does not name one source, so treat it as an illustration.泰國 PM2.5：私人活動使交易外的人承受空污成本。投影片未指明單一來源，因此只能作為示例。
    • Sugar external-cost graph: MSC above MPC, so the market overuses. A classic externality needs a third-party cost; harm to the consumer alone is not one.糖的外部成本圖：社會邊際成本（MSC）高於私人邊際成本（MPC），因此市場使用過多。典型外部性必須有第三方成本；僅傷害消費者本人不算外部性。

    Policy tools for negative externalities處理負外部性的政策工具

    Tool工具How it works運作方式Trade-off權衡
    Pigouvian tax 庇古稅庇古稅Tax equal to marginal external cost, so decision-makers face the full social cost.依邊際外部成本課稅，使決策者面對完整的社會成本。Needs an estimate of the external cost.需要估算外部成本。
    Command and control命令與管制Government sets rules directly, such as an emission limit or required technology.政府直接訂規則，例如排放上限或指定技術。Simple but costly, because firms' reduction costs differ.做法簡單，但各企業的減排成本不同，可能代價高昂。
    Tradable permits可交易許可證Government caps total pollution. Firms that can cut cheaply cut more and sell permits.政府設定總污染上限；能以低成本減排的企業多減排，並出售許可證。Meets the cap at the lowest total cost. Permits do not mean more pollution; the cap is fixed.以最低總成本達到上限。許可證不代表污染量增加；總量上限固定。
    Coase bargaining科斯式協商With clear property rights and cheap bargaining, the parties solve it themselves.若財產權明確且協商成本低，當事人可自行解決。Fails when many people are affected (PM2.5 hits millions).影響人數龐大時難以適用，例如 PM2.5 影響數百萬人。

    Positive externality: the market produces too little正外部性：市場生產過少

    • Vaccination: protects the person and lowers infection risk for others.疫苗接種：保護接種者，也降低他人的感染風險。
    • COVID vaccine development: the social value of fast vaccines far exceeded what firms could earn, so advance purchase commitments and R&D support made sense.COVID 疫苗研發：迅速取得疫苗的社會價值遠高於企業可獲得的收益，因此預先採購承諾與研發支持有其理由。
    • Tools: subsidies, public provision, prizes, purchase commitments.工具：補貼、公共供給、獎金、採購承諾。

    5. Public goods and common resources5. 公共財與共有資源

    Classify a good by two questions: can non-payers be excluded, and does one person's use reduce what others get?用兩個問題分類財貨：能否排除未付款者？一人使用會否減少他人可用的數量？

    Rival 有競爭性競爭性
    Nonrival無競爭性
    Excludable 有排他性排他性
    Private good私有財Food, clothing食物、衣物
    Club good俱樂部財Netflix, SpotifyNetflix、Spotify
    Nonexcludable無排他性
    Common resource共有資源Ocean tuna. Problem: overuse海洋鮪魚；問題是過度使用。
    Public good公共財Asteroid defense, national defense. Problem: free riders小行星防禦、國防；問題是搭便車。
    • Free rider 搭便車: people enjoy a public good without paying, so voluntary funding falls short.搭便車者：人們未付費卻享用公共財，自願出資因此不足。
    • Forced rider: tax funding makes some people pay for things they value below their cost.被迫搭便車者：稅收使部分人為自己估值低於成本的項目付費。
    • A public good is defined by its traits, not by who provides it.公共財由其特性定義，而非由提供者身分決定。

    Tragedy of the commons共有地悲劇

    Each fisher keeps the full gain from one more fish but shares the cost of a smaller stock with everyone. Each choice is rational; the total is overuse.每位漁民多捕一條魚可獨享收益，魚群減少的成本卻由所有人分攤。個別選擇合理，合起來卻造成過度捕撈。

    Elinor Ostrom showed that communities can manage commons with their own rules, monitoring and sanctions. The choice is not only market versus government.Elinor Ostrom 指出，社群能用自己的規則、監督與制裁管理共有資源；選項並非只有市場與政府二選一。

    Other fixes: property rights, quotas, tradable fishing permits.其他方法：財產權、配額、可交易捕魚許可證。

    6. Moral economics6. 道德經濟學

    A voluntary, mutually beneficial trade can still be socially unacceptable. Efficiency is one criterion, not the only one.自願且對雙方有利的交易仍可能不被社會接受。效率是評估標準之一，並非唯一標準。

    Concept概念Meaning意義Example例子
    Repugnance 反感道德反感People reject a trade because paying for it feels wrong, even if both sides agree.即使雙方同意，人們仍因「付錢買賣這件事不對」而拒絕該交易。Kidney sales買賣腎臟
    Framing框架呈現The same exchange is accepted or rejected depending on how it is structured.同一交換行為可能因制度安排或說法不同而被接受或拒絕。Tax credit for donors instead of cash; life insurance framed as protecting family以捐贈者稅額抵免代替現金；將壽險描述為保護家人。
    Domestic ban, foreign supply國內禁止、依賴國外供應Banning a market at home does not end reliance on it.在本國禁止一個市場，不代表不再依賴它。Countries that ban paid plasma import it from paid donors abroad禁止有償血漿的國家，仍可能從國外的有償捐贈者進口血漿。
    Hidden economics隱含的經濟問題Some "moral" objections are also economic problems.部分看似「道德」的反對，其實也涉及經濟問題。Subscription firefighting: fire spreads (externality) and people expect rescue anyway (free rider)訂閱制消防：火會蔓延（外部性），人們也預期無論是否付費都會獲救（搭便車）。
    Viatical contract末期病患壽險保單買賣契約Selling a life insurance policy early for cash. The buyer profits if the seller dies sooner.提前出售壽險保單換取現金；若賣方較早死亡，買方可獲利。Patients during the AIDS crisis愛滋病危機期間的病患。
    Paternalism 父愛主義父愛主義Limiting choices to protect people from harming themselves.限制選擇，避免人們傷害自己。Prescriptions; self-set loss limits on betting apps處方箋制度；賭博應用程式中自設虧損上限。

    Externality: does my choice hurt others? Paternalism: will I hurt myself?外部性問：我的選擇會傷害別人嗎？父愛主義問：我會傷害自己嗎？

    Beyond allow or ban: market design超越准許或禁止：市場設計

    Change the form of payment, who may participate, or set limits. Examples: kidney exchange matching, rules on who may hold a life policy, loss caps on gambling.可以改變付款形式、參與資格或設定限制，例如腎臟交換配對、限制壽險保單持有人資格、設定賭博虧損上限。

    1. Is there a potential gain from trade?是否存在潛在的交易利益？
    2. Why is the market contested?市場為何受到爭議？
    3. What happens if it is banned or restricted? (Demand moves abroad or underground.)禁止或限制後會怎樣？（需求可能轉移至國外或地下市場。）
    4. Can the institution be redesigned?能否重新設計制度？
    5. Evaluate more than efficiency.評估不能只看效率。

    7. Taxes, subsidies and government failure7. 稅、補貼與政府失靈

    Who legally pays a tax does not decide who bears it. Elasticity does.法律規定誰繳稅，不等於誰實際承擔稅負；關鍵在彈性。

    • Tax incidence 租稅歸宿: the less elastic side bears more. Cigarette buyers are inelastic, so they carry most of a cigarette tax.租稅歸宿：彈性較低的一方承擔較多。香菸買方需求較缺乏彈性，因此承擔香菸稅的大部分。
    • Subsidy: a negative tax that encourages an activity, such as EV purchase subsidies.補貼：如同負的稅，用於鼓勵某種活動，例如購買電動車的補貼。
    • Government failure: the policy itself produces an inefficient result. Compare realistic market outcomes with realistic government outcomes, not ideal versions of either.政府失靈：政策本身造成無效率。應比較現實中的市場結果與政府結果，而非拿任何一方的理想版本比較。

    8. Common mistakes8. 常見錯誤

    A high price means market failure.價格高代表市場失靈。

    A high price may simply signal scarcity.高價也可能只是稀缺的訊號。

    A price ceiling makes goods affordable for everyone.最高限價讓所有人都買得起。

    A binding ceiling causes a shortage. Some people get nothing.有約束力的限價造成短缺；有些人根本買不到。

    All consumer loss from a tariff is DWL.消費者因關稅受損的部分全是無謂損失。

    Part is transferred to producers and government.其中一部分轉移給生產者和政府。

    DWL always means too little trade.無謂損失一定表示交易太少。

    A negative externality creates DWL from too much trade.負外部性會因交易太多而造成無謂損失。

    Pollution permits allow more pollution.污染許可證容許更多污染。

    The cap is fixed. Trading only decides who cuts.總量上限固定；交易只決定由誰減排。

    Anything shared is a public good.凡是共享的東西都是公共財。

    A fishery is rival, so it is a common resource.漁業資源具有競爭性，因此是共有資源。

    The tragedy of the commons comes from irrational people.共有地悲劇源於人們不理性。

    Each user is rational. The cost is shared.每位使用者都理性；成本由眾人分攤。

    Banning a market removes demand.禁止市場就能消除需求。

    Demand moves abroad, to imports or to black markets.需求可能轉往國外、進口或黑市。

    Paternalism is the same as an externality.父愛主義等同外部性。

    Externality harms others. Paternalism protects people from themselves.外部性傷害他人；父愛主義旨在防止人們傷害自己。

    The only options are allow or ban.選項只有允許或禁止。

    Market design can change payment form, eligibility or limits.市場設計能改變付款形式、參與資格或限制。

    9. Cold call practice9. 課堂問答練習

    Answer aloud first, then open the card. Structure: definition, example, why it matters.先口頭回答，再打開卡片。架構：定義、例子、重要性。

    What does "a price is a signal wrapped up in an incentive" mean?「價格既是訊號，也是誘因」是什麼意思？

    A price tells people a good has become scarcer and gives them a reason to act: buyers use less, sellers supply more.價格告訴人們財貨變得稀缺，並使人有理由行動：買家少用，賣家多供給。

    What is the invisible hand?什麼是「看不見的手」？

    People pursuing their own interests are coordinated by prices into outcomes that serve others, without a central planner.人們追求私利，在價格協調下也能產生有利他人的結果，無須中央規劃者。

    Why is equilibrium more than Qd = Qs?為什麼均衡不只是 Qd＝Qs？

    Up to the equilibrium quantity WTP ≥ MC, and beyond it MC > WTP. Equilibrium therefore maximizes total surplus in the basic model.均衡數量之前 WTP≥MC，之後 MC>WTP。因此在基本模型中，均衡使總剩餘最大化。

    Who wins and loses from a tariff?關稅讓誰得益、誰受損？

    Domestic producers gain, consumers lose, government collects revenue, and society loses DWL from wasted resources and lost gains from trade.國內生產者得益、消費者受損、政府取得收入；社會因資源浪費與錯失的交易利益而承受無謂損失。

    What is deadweight loss?什麼是無謂損失？

    The value lost because the traded quantity differs from the efficient quantity. Nobody receives it.交易數量偏離效率數量而消失的價值；沒有任何人得到它。

    Why can Mumbai have vacant flats and a housing shortage?為什麼孟買會同時有空屋和住房短缺？

    Rent control cuts landlord returns, so owners reduce maintenance and withhold units. Supply falls even though controlled rent is low.租金管制降低房東報酬，使屋主減少維修並撤出房源。雖然受管制租金低，供給卻下降。

    How does wasted time differ from A + B?排隊浪費的時間和 A＋B 有何不同？

    Wasted time is the real cost of queuing and searching. A + B is lost consumer and producer surplus from trades that no longer happen.浪費的時間是排隊及搜尋的真實成本；A＋B 是不再發生的交易所損失的消費者與生產者剩餘。

    Why does a negative externality cause overproduction?為什麼負外部性導致生產過多？

    Decision-makers ignore the external cost, so they keep trading past the point where social cost exceeds benefit. Q market > Q efficient.決策者忽略外部成本，於是即使社會成本已高於利益仍繼續交易；市場數量大於效率數量。

    How do tradable permits work?可交易許可證如何運作？

    Government caps total pollution. Firms that can cut cheaply do so and sell permits, so the cap is met at the lowest total cost.政府限制污染總量；能低成本減排的企業多減排並出售許可證，以最低總成本達到上限。

    When does the Coase theorem fail?科斯定理何時難以適用？

    When property rights are unclear or bargaining is costly, for example when millions of people are affected by PM2.5.當財產權不明確或協商成本很高時，例如 PM2.5 影響數百萬人。

    Why is a fishery not a public good?為什麼漁業不是公共財？

    It is nonexcludable but rival: one person's catch reduces what others can catch. That makes it a common resource.它雖難以排除他人使用，卻具有競爭性：一人的漁獲減少他人可捕的魚，因此是共有資源。

    What did Elinor Ostrom show?Elinor Ostrom 指出了什麼？

    Communities can govern common resources with their own rules, monitoring and sanctions, without full privatization or central control.社群可透過自訂規則、監督和制裁治理共有資源，不必全面私有化或完全依賴中央控制。

    Why might a voluntary trade be unacceptable?為什麼自願交易仍可能不被接受？

    Repugnance: people object to paying for certain things, such as kidneys, even when both sides gain.道德反感：即使雙方得益，人們仍反對付錢取得某些東西，例如腎臟。

    Besides morality, what problems does subscription firefighting create?除了道德考量，訂閱制消防還有什麼問題？

    A negative externality, because fire spreads to neighbors, and a free-rider problem, because people expect to be saved anyway.負外部性：火災可能蔓延到鄰居；搭便車：未付費者仍期待獲救。

    What happens when a market is outlawed?市場被取締後會怎樣？

    Both demand and supply can shift inward. Quantity falls, while the price effect depends on which shift is larger.需求與供給都可能向內移，交易量下降；價格方向則取決於哪一方移動較多。

    Who bears the burden of a tax?誰承擔稅負？

    The less elastic side of the market, regardless of who legally pays it.市場中彈性較低的一方，與法律規定誰繳稅無關。
