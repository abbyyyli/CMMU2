    1. Case brief and question1. 題幹與問題

    Example例子

    A startup plans lightly flavored water for young gym-goers. It treats 400,000 social-media interests as buyers, says its unique flavor means no competitors, advertises a premium position with heavy discounts, and considers reducing the pack without drawing attention to it.新創計畫賣淡風味水給年輕健身者，把 400,000 社群興趣者當買家、認為口味獨特就無對手，以高級定位搭重折扣，並考慮不明顯告知地減量。

    Question: What is wrong with the plan, and what should the company change before launch?問題：計畫有何問題？上市前該改什麼？

    2. Define a real target2. 定義真正客群

    Key idea重點

    The 400,000 count describes interest, not demand. Define the exercise occasion, buying location, desired benefit and price. Find enough people who will pay and buy again.400,000 是興趣不是需求。界定運動情境、購買地、追求利益與價格，找足夠願付費且再買的人。

    3. Name both kinds of rival3. 列兩種對手

    Key idea重點

    Other flavored hydration drinks are direct rivals. Plain water or another refreshment can be an indirect substitute after exercise. A unique flavor does not remove these alternatives.其他風味補水飲是直接對手，普通水或其他解渴飲可在運動後間接替代。口味獨特不會使選項消失。

    4. Make the promise consistent4. 讓承諾一致

    Key idea重點

    Choose a clear benefit and a price the target accepts. Product, pack, place and promotion should support it. Repeated deep discounts can make a premium promise confusing.選清楚利益與客群接受的價格，產品、包裝、通路與推廣要支持它。重複深折扣會使高級承諾混亂。

    5. Count the whole gain5. 計算整體增加

    Key idea重點

    Compare new-product contribution with launch costs and sales taken from existing products. Use a small launch to observe trial and repeat before expanding.比較新品貢獻、上市成本與取代既有品的業績。先小規模上市，看試買與重購再擴大。

    6. Show the quantity clearly6. 清楚揭露含量

    Key idea重點

    Reject hidden reductions. Compare unit price and label net contents clearly. A useful smaller portion can be sold openly without hiding the change.拒絕隱藏減量。比較單價並清楚標淨含量，有用小份量可公開販售，不必隱藏改變。

    7. Do not mix these up7. 別混淆

    Shortcut捷徑Better question較好問題
    Interest = buyers興趣＝買家Who will pay and repeat?誰會付費再買？
    Unique flavor = no rivals獨特口味＝無對手What else meets the same need?還有什麼滿足同一需要？
    Sales = extra profit銷售＝額外利潤What remains after costs and switching?扣成本與轉購後剩多少？

    8. Cold call practice8. 口答練習

    What should change first?先改什麼？

    Define the target and useful benefit. The position, channels and economics follow from that choice.先定客群與有用利益，再決定定位、通路與經濟性。

    9. Answer template9. 答題模板

    How it works為什麼

    Define the customer and occasion. Name the valued benefit and substitutes. Make the marketing mix consistent. Count contribution after costs and show quantity changes clearly.定顧客情境、利益與替代品，讓行銷組合一致，計扣成本後貢獻並清楚揭露含量改變。
