    4. Q2 · Channel choice plus two further growth ideas4. Q2｜通路選擇，另加兩項成長想法

    A staged omnichannel pilot is one possible choice. First, it may save travel and improve access for customers. Second, existing stores may support pickup and service, but inventory accuracy, acquisition, fulfillment and returns determine feasibility. Growing online orders is insufficient if they transfer purchases from profitable stores or add costly delivery. A store-first answer can also work if its evidence and trade-offs are stronger.分階段全通路試行是一種選擇。第一，可能節省顧客交通、改善取得；第二，既有門市可能支援取貨與服務，但庫存正確、獲客、履約與退貨決定可行性。若只是轉移原本有利潤的店內購買，或新增高成本配送，線上訂單成長並不夠。有較強證據與取捨時，也可支持門市優先。

    10. Cold call practice10. 課堂口答練習

    What makes an indirect rival defensible?如何支持間接對手選擇？

    A different product/format can replace the focal offer for a defined customer job or occasion. Explain overlap in choice, budget, location or time, then test actual substitution.不同產品／業態可在明確任務或情境取代主產品。解釋選擇、預算、地點或時間重疊，再測實際替代。

    What supports the leisure-positioning hypothesis?什麼支持休閒定位假設？

    The reported restaurants and cinema support the possibility of a shopping-plus-leisure offer. They do not establish customer perception, demand or financial return.報導的餐廳與影院支持購物加休閒的可能，但不證明顧客知覺、需求或回報。

    Why might a stronger parent link hurt?母品牌關聯為何可能有害？

    It could blur tiers or make value-sensitive buyers expect higher prices. These are hypotheses to test rather than proof that endorsement fails.可能模糊層級或使重視價格者預期漲價。這些要測試，不能當作背書失敗的證明。

    How should a case recommendation handle missing data?缺資料時如何提出建議？

    Make a provisional decision with explicit assumptions, explain the mechanisms, identify the missing evidence and specify what would change the recommendation.明示假設並暫定決策，解釋機制、指出缺的證據，以及什麼會改變建議。
