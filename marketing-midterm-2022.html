    1. Case brief · What the paper says1. 題幹｜試卷交代了什麼

    Case brief個案背景

    Robinson Department Store Plc belongs to Central Group. It plans to spend THB (Thai Baht) 4.5 billion on expansion in the following year, expecting consumer spending to recover. Management is deciding whether to focus on Thailand or expand further into ASEAN (Association of Southeast Asian Nations).Robinson 百貨隸屬 Central Group，計畫次年投入 45 億泰銖擴張，期待消費支出回升。管理層要決定聚焦泰國，還是進一步向 ASEAN（東南亞國家協會） 擴張。

    The company plans more stores in Thailand and Vietnam over five years. The locations discussed include three in suburban Bangkok, one in Rayong and one in Buriram. It has 52 Thai stores and two Vietnamese stores; the paper also lists three further Thai openings by 2023.公司計畫五年內在泰國與越南增店，提到曼谷郊區三處、羅勇一處及武里南一處。既有泰國 52 店、越南 2 店；試卷另列至 2023 年再開三間泰國店。

    Secondary provinces are important to the plan. An executive says these areas offer strong opportunities and no direct Robinson competitors. After a good response to the first lifestyle center in Trang, the company wants to develop more centers outside Bangkok.次級府是計畫重點。主管認為當地機會大、沒有 Robinson 直接對手。Trang 首間生活中心反應好後，公司希望在曼谷以外再發展更多中心。

    The Prachin Buri lifestyle center costs THB 980 million and covers 20,000 m². It targets younger shoppers. The article describes the province as an industrial, commercial and transport hub that can benefit from the ASEAN Economic Community.Prachin Buri 生活中心投資 9.8 億泰銖、面積 20,000 平方公尺，鎖定年輕消費者。文章把該府描述為工業、商業與運輸樞紐，可受益於東協經濟共同體。

    Its anchors include Power Buy, Super Sport and Tops Supermarket, with about 30 restaurants and SF Cinema. Robinson has also started online and catalog sales. Question 5 adds that Robinson targets lower-income consumers than Central.主力店包含 Power Buy、Super Sport、Tops 超市，另有約 30 間餐廳與 SF Cinema。Robinson 也已開始線上與目錄銷售；第五題補充其目標客群所得低於 Central。

    Five questions, 7 points each, 35 points total. Article credited to Pitsinee Jitpleecheep, Bangkok Post. Open the original paper.五題各 7 分，共 35 分。文章署名 Pitsinee Jitpleecheep，Bangkok Post。開啟原試卷。

    2. Q1 · Choose an expansion market2. Q1｜選擴張市場

    Question · 7 points
    Management is choosing between more Robinson complexes in ASEAN and a stronger focus on Thailand. Recommend one ASEAN country, compare it with Thailand and give at least three reasons.題目｜7 分
    管理層在 ASEAN 擴張與聚焦泰國間選擇。建議一個 ASEAN 國家，與泰國比較，至少三個理由。

    Worked answer完整示範答案

    I would prioritize a small expansion in Vietnam. Choose a location that fits Robinson’s value-shopping and leisure offer.我會優先在越南小規模擴張。選符合 Robinson 價值型購物與休閒提案的地點。

    1. Reach new areas. Vietnam has two stores, compared with 52 in Thailand. Expanding the smaller network can reach new local customers. Another Thai site needs closer attention to overlap with existing stores.一、接觸新地區。越南兩店，泰國 52 店，擴充小型網絡可接觸新的當地顧客；泰國新點則更須注意與既有店重疊。

    2. Use an existing base. Robinson already operates in Vietnam, so it has a starting point for local learning and testing. Thailand has a stronger network and is easier to operate in, but Vietnam allows a controlled next step using an existing overseas base.二、利用既有基礎。Robinson 已在越南營運，可作在地學習和測試起點。泰國網絡更強、經營較容易，但越南可利用海外既有基礎，控制下一步擴張。

    3. Spread the source of growth. More Thai stores keep revenue tied mainly to Thai demand. Successful Vietnamese stores add another national revenue source and reduce dependence on one market.三、分散成長來源。繼續增泰國店，營收仍主要連到泰國需求；成功越南店可增加另一國營收來源，減少單一市場依賴。

    Before a large rollout, compare local spending, competitors, store costs and expected contribution with Thai alternatives. Keep investing only where the return supports it.大量展店前，與泰國選項比較當地支出、對手、成本及預期貢獻，只在回報足以支持時繼續投資。

    Pause & think暫停，想一想

    If the two Vietnamese stores are losing money, which part of this recommendation must be reconsidered first?若兩間越南店都虧損，這份建議哪個部分應先重查？

    3. Q2 · Invest online and add two growth ideas3. Q2｜投資線上，另加兩個成長想法

    Question · 7 points
    E-commerce may threaten Robinson, which has started online and catalog sales. Given conditions in Thailand, should it invest more in these channels or keep its traditional-format focus? Give at least two reasons. Then suggest two other ways to increase customers and sales.題目｜7 分
    電商可能威脅 Robinson，公司已開始線上與目錄銷售。依泰國環境，應多投資這些通路，還是維持傳統業態？至少兩理由，再提兩項其他增加顧客與銷售的方法。

    Worked answer完整示範答案

    I would invest more online in stages, use catalogs selectively and connect these channels with stores.我會分階段增加線上投資，選擇性用目錄，並與門市連接。

    Reason 1: make shopping easier. People can browse and order without a full mall trip. This helps Robinson serve time-sensitive customers and compete with online alternatives.理由一：購物更容易。顧客不必完整逛一趟商場就能瀏覽訂購，可服務重時間者並與線上選項競爭。

    Reason 2: use stores for service. Stores can support collection, returns and help. Restaurants and cinema keep a reason to visit. Connect stock and service across channels so convenience and the physical experience work together.理由二：利用門市服務。門市可支援取貨、退貨與協助，餐廳影院保留造訪理由。把庫存與服務接起，讓便利與實體體驗共同發揮。

    Extra idea 1: win back inactive customers. Use available records to find falling visit frequency. Offer a relevant reason to return, such as an event linked to a category the customer bought before.額外想法一：喚回不活躍顧客。用可得紀錄找造訪下降者，提供與過去購買品類有關活動等回訪理由。

    Extra idea 2: create a family outing. Coordinate shops, restaurants and cinema, then promote the combined occasion locally. This can bring new visitors and spending across tenants.額外想法二：設計家庭出遊。結合商店、餐廳影院並向當地推廣，吸引新訪客與跨租戶消費。

    Measure extra customers, repeat visits and contribution after delivery, returns, rewards and sales moved from stores.衡量新增顧客、回訪，以及扣配送、退貨、獎勵與門市轉移業績後的貢獻。

    中文邏輯理由有兩個：顧客更便利、公司能利用店網；額外成長想法另有兩個：喚回顧客、家庭情境。不要漏後半題。

    Optional: why more online orders may not add profit選讀：為何線上訂單變多，利潤不一定增加

    Example: 1,000 online orders × THB 60 contribution = THB 60,000. If 700 replace store orders earning THB 50 each, subtract THB 35,000. Subtract another THB 10,000 of additional costs: the net gain is THB 15,000. This compares contribution, not the full return on a capital investment.例：1,000 筆線上單×單筆貢獻 60＝60,000 泰銖；若其中 700 筆取代單筆貢獻 50 的門市單，須扣 35,000，再扣新增成本 10,000，淨增加 15,000。這是貢獻比較，不是完整資本投資報酬。

    Incremental contribution calculator增量貢獻試算器

    Enter amounts in THB. Include delivery and expected returns in contribution per order. Add only costs not counted elsewhere.以泰銖輸入，單筆貢獻先含配送與預期退貨，再另加尚未計入成本。

    Pause & think暫停，想一想

    What would change if most online orders came from existing store customers rather than new buyers?若大多數線上單來自原門市顧客，而非新買家，評估會如何改變？

    4. Q3 · Big C, Lazada and Robinson on one map4. Q3｜把 Big C、Lazada 與 Robinson 畫在同一張圖

    Question · 7 points
    Name two indirect competitors to Robinson in Thailand. Plot both and Robinson on a perceptual map. Recommend how Robinson should position itself to compete better as a retailer, with at least two reasons.題目｜7 分
    列泰國兩個 Robinson 間接對手，與 Robinson 畫在同一定位圖，建議零售定位，至少兩理由。

    Worked answer完整示範答案

    I choose Big C and Lazada as indirect rivals for family shopping. Big C offers a hypermarket format; Lazada offers a marketplace without a mall trip. Both can take part of the same household budget.我選 Big C 與 Lazada 為家庭購物的間接對手。Big C 是量販業態，Lazada 提供不用逛商場的平台購物，兩者都可取得同一家庭預算的一部分。

    Position Robinson as affordable one-stop shopping and leisure. The map compares perceived value for money with family-outing appeal.把 Robinson 定位為可負擔的一站式購物休閒。圖比較感知物有所值程度與家庭出遊吸引力。

    Reason 1: a reason to go out. Restaurants and SF Cinema add eating and entertainment to shopping. Lazada cannot deliver that physical outing.理由一：外出理由。餐廳與 SF Cinema 把吃飯娛樂加入購物，是 Lazada 無法交付的實體出遊。

    Reason 2: several needs in one visit. Tops, Power Buy and Super Sport let families complete different tasks. Keep the offer affordable for the target and compete on the whole trip, rather than only price against Big C.理由二：一趟多種需要。Tops、Power Buy 與 Super Sport 讓家庭完成不同任務。保留可負擔品項，以整趟體驗競爭，而非只與 Big C 比價格。

    Ask local target customers to rate all three options on the same axes. Use the results to improve the offer and message.讓當地目標顧客用相同軸評三者，再用結果改善提案與訊息。

    Interactive · Perceptual map互動｜知覺定位圖

    Select a brand. Move its point with the sliders, drag it, or focus the point and use the arrow keys.選品牌後，可用滑桿、拖曳，或聚焦點後用方向鍵移動。

    Key idea重點

    Big C and Lazada meet this question’s two-indirect-rivals requirement. The S5 assignment is different: one direct rival and one indirect rival.Big C 與 Lazada 符合本題兩間接對手；S5 作業不同，是一直接與一間接。

    5. Q4 · Answer a, b and c separately5. Q4｜a、b、c 分別回答

    Question · 7 points
    Choose a research method and explain why for each objective: (a) understand why people visit Robinson; (b) profile age, gender, where they visit shopping malls and how often; (c) explore feelings about online shopping versus mall shopping.題目｜7 分
    各選研究方法並解釋：(a) 為何造訪 Robinson；(b) 年齡、性別、逛哪裡及多常逛商場；(c) 對線上與商場購物的感受。

    Worked answer完整示範答案

    (a) Depth interviews: explore visit motives. Ask recent visitors to describe the trip, other options and why they chose Robinson. Open questions can reveal convenience, family time or a particular shop as the reason.(a) 深訪探索造訪動機。請近期訪客說明行程、備選與選 Robinson 原因，開放問題可找出便利、家庭時間或特定店等動機。

    (b) Structured survey: measure the segments. Ask the same age, gender, mall-choice and visit-frequency questions. Recruit across relevant places, days and times, including infrequent visitors. Compare the size and shopping patterns of the groups.(b) 結構問卷衡量客群。用相同年齡、性別、商場選擇與頻率問題，跨相關地點日時招募，也包含低頻訪客，再比較群體規模與購物型態。

    (c) Focus groups or interviews: explore channel feelings. Discuss convenience, trust, seeing products and social enjoyment with different channel users. Then use a survey if management needs the percentage holding each view.(c) 焦點團體或訪談探索通路感受。與不同通路使用者討論便利、信任、看實物與社交樂趣；若要各想法占比，再用問卷。

    中文邏輯a、b、c 是三個研究目的，不是選項。「我選 b」會漏掉整個 a 與 c。

    6. Q5 · Use Central endorsement, retain value positioning6. Q5｜強化 Central 背書，保留價值定位

    Question · 7 points
    Central owns Robinson, but Robinson targets lower-income customers. Should Robinson strengthen the Central brand link and move its positioning upmarket? Give at least two reasons.題目｜7 分
    Robinson 屬 Central，卻鎖定較低所得客群。是否應強化 Central 品牌連結並高檔化？至少兩理由。

    Worked answer完整示範答案

    I would strengthen Central’s endorsement but keep Robinson’s value positioning. A supporting line such as “a Central Group company” can show the link while Robinson keeps its own name and affordable range.我會強化 Central 背書，但保留 Robinson 價值定位。可用「Central Group 旗下公司」輔助訊息顯示連結，同時保留名稱與可負擔品項。

    Reason 1: keep the offer right for the target. The question describes lower-income customers. A more expensive range could push them away. Parent backing can instead support trust without requiring higher prices.理由一：維持客群契合。題目是較低所得客群，更高價品項可能使人離開；母品牌支持可增加信任，不必提高價格。

    Reason 2: keep two clear brand roles. Robinson serves value-oriented needs; Central serves a more upmarket position. Making them too similar can confuse buyers, move sales within the group and add upgrade costs.理由二：保留清楚品牌角色。Robinson 服務價值型需要，Central 較高檔；變得太像會混淆買家、造成集團內轉購，並增加升級成本。

    Test whether the endorsement raises trust while keeping expected prices affordable. Adjust the message if customers think Robinson has become too expensive.測背書是否提高信任，同時保留可負擔價格印象。若顧客以為變太貴，就調整訊息。

    Pause & think暫停，想一想

    If the parent name raises trust but also raises expected prices, how should the endorsement be adjusted?若母品牌同時提高信任與預期價格，背書訊息應怎樣調整？

    7. Do not mix these up7. 別混淆這些概念

    Mistake錯誤Repair修正
    52 stores prove saturation.52 店就證明飽和。Use counts to describe footprint; use catchment demand and store performance to judge saturation.店數描述據點；飽和須看商圈需求與門市表現。
    “More customers” and “a larger market” are two reasons.「更多顧客」與「更大市場」是兩個理由。Give different mechanisms, such as reach, operating capability and diversification.給不同機制，例如接觸、營運能力與分散。
    Q2 ends after choosing online.Q2 選線上就答完。Also give two reasons and two further growth ideas.還要兩個理由與兩項額外成長想法。
    Q3 can be prose only; Q4 is multiple choice.Q3 可只寫文字；Q4 是選擇題。Q3 needs the map; Q4 needs all three method recommendations.Q3 須有圖；Q4 三個目的都要選方法。
    A stronger Central link means Robinson must become premium.Central 連結變強，Robinson 就必須高級化。Endorsement and target/price repositioning are separate choices.母品牌背書與客群／價格重新定位是分開選擇。

    8. Cold call practice8. 課堂口答練習

    State the five answer directions without details.不用細節，先說五題方向。

    Vietnam in stages; connected online/store investment plus two extra ideas; Big C and Lazada with a map; methods for all a/b/c; Central endorsement without moving Robinson upmarket.分階段越南擴張；線上門市整合加兩額外想法；Big C 與 Lazada 加定位圖；a／b／c 全答方法；Central 背書但不高檔化。

    Why are Big C and Lazada indirect here?Big C 與 Lazada 為何在此屬間接？

    They are different formats that can substitute for some household shopping expenditure. Define the customer occasion, rather than relying on the brand names alone.兩者是不同業態，可替代部分家庭購物支出，須界定顧客情境，不能只列品牌名。

    Which part of the map is case-supported?定位圖哪部分有案例支持？

    Restaurants, cinema and anchors support a shopping-and-leisure proposition. Exact scores and customer perceptions remain assumptions to research.餐廳、影院與主力店支持購物休閒主張；確切分數與顧客知覺仍待研究。

    Can another answer be defensible?可不可以選不同答案？

    Yes. A store-first strategy or a different country can be defensible if it answers every task, uses consistent evidence and explains distinct reasons. The worked answers show one coherent route.可以。門市優先或不同國家也可辯護，但須答完要求、證據一致、理由不同；此處完整答案示範一條連貫路線。
