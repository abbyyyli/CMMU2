    CRM, Customer Data and Marketing Metrics顧客關係管理、顧客資料與行銷指標

    More data is not better insight. Turn customer interactions into useful information, use it to improve relationships and experience, and test whether actions change behavior and create economic value.更多資料不等於更好的洞察。把顧客互動轉成有用資訊，據以改善關係與體驗，再檢驗行動是否改變行為並創造經濟價值。

    1. From transaction to relationship1. 從交易到關係

    Transaction交易 單次交易

    How do I make this sale?如何完成這筆銷售？

    Relationship關係 關係行銷

    How do I create enough value that this customer keeps choosing us? "Buy one time vs a lifetime."如何創造足夠價值，讓這位顧客持續選擇我們？「買一次，還是一輩子持續購買？」

    CRM 顧客關係管理 is managing acquisition, retention and value creation over time. It is not merely software.CRM 是長期管理獲客、留存與價值創造，不只是軟體。

    Customer lifetime value顧客終身價值

    CLV is the economic value of a relationship over time, not today's revenue. It depends on frequency, retention, margin, serving cost and acquisition cost. The professor's warning: 100% loyalty is unrealistic.CLV 是一段顧客關係隨時間帶來的經濟價值，而非今日營收。它取決於頻率、留存、利潤率、服務成本與獲客成本。教授提醒：百分之百忠誠並不切實際。

    An expensive customer to acquire is worth it only if the relationship lasts long enough to repay the cost. Retention is not the goal for every customer at any cost.獲客成本高，只有在關係維持夠久、能回收成本時才值得。留客不代表對每位顧客都要不計代價。

    Pause & think暫停，想一想

    A firm acquired many new customers but most left after one purchase. Why could acquisition growth coexist with weak relationship economics? What would you examine beyond first-purchase revenue?公司取得許多新顧客，但大多只買一次就流失。為什麼獲客成長可能同時伴隨不佳的顧客關係經濟效益？除了首購營收，還要檢查什麼？

    2. Satisfaction, importance and experience2. 滿意度、重要性與體驗

    The lowest satisfaction score is not automatically the top priority. Weight it by how much the attribute matters.滿意度最低的項目不一定最優先。必須依該屬性的重要程度加權判斷。

    Attribute屬性Satisfaction滿意度Importance重要性Priority優先順序
    Store music店內音樂5/102/10Low, despite the lower score即使分數較低，優先程度仍低
    Coffee taste咖啡口味7/1010/10Examine first優先檢查

    Queue time and Wi-Fi are added examples. The quadrant view is a standard importance-performance supplement.排隊時間與 Wi-Fi 是補充例子；四象限圖則是標準的重要性與績效分析補充。

    Hygiene versus motivators保健因素與激勵因素

    Must-have (hygiene)必備項目（保健因素）

    Correct order, cleanliness, working payment, no defects. Failure creates dissatisfaction; success only meets expectations.訂單正確、環境清潔、付款正常、沒有瑕疵。失敗會造成不滿；做好只是達到基本期待。

    Nice-to-have (motivator)加分項目（激勵因素）

    Personal greeting, remembering the usual order, a surprise. Adds delight but cannot replace the basics.親切招呼、記得慣常訂單或帶來驚喜。可以增加愉悅，但不能取代基本要求。

    Fix serious hygiene failures first, then add experience enhancers. Customer experience management means learning fast enough to respond while the problem still matters.先修正嚴重的基本服務缺失，再增加體驗亮點。顧客體驗管理需要及時學習，在問題仍有影響時就能回應。

    Pause & think暫停，想一想

    A café has low satisfaction with its music but only moderate satisfaction with coffee taste. Why might improving taste have greater value? What additional customer evidence would justify the priority?咖啡店的音樂滿意度很低，咖啡口味滿意度則普通。為什麼先改善口味可能更有價值？還要取得哪些顧客證據，才能支持這個優先順序？

    3. Data, information and decisions3. 資料、資訊與決策

    Data資料Observations.觀察紀錄。
    ↓
    Information資訊Organized data with meaning.經整理而具有意義的資料。
    ↓
    Insight洞察Interpretation relevant to a business problem.與企業問題相關的解讀。
    ↓
    Decision決策Action chosen with evidence plus judgment.結合證據與判斷所選擇的行動。
    MIS and garbage in, garbage out管理資訊系統與「垃圾進，垃圾出」

    A management information system turns data into information for decisions. Technology cannot fix wrong data, the wrong question or poor interpretation.管理資訊系統把資料轉為支援決策的資訊。科技無法修正錯誤資料、問錯問題或不佳的解讀。

    Information overload資訊超載

    Ask what decision we are making, which information is relevant, which metric would change the decision, and what can be ignored.先問要做什麼決策、哪些資訊相關、什麼指標會改變決策，以及哪些內容可以忽略。

    中文邏輯科技改變的是 channel、速度與資訊量；顧客需求、價值與比較替代選項的邏輯不變。

    Pause & think暫停，想一想

    A dashboard records thousands of clicks and visits but managers cannot choose a next action. What decision should be defined first, and how would you turn raw observations into usable insight?儀表板記錄數千次點擊與造訪，主管卻無法決定下一步。應先定義什麼決策問題？如何把原始紀錄轉成有用的洞察？

    4. Data mining and evidence discipline4. 資料探勘與證據判讀

    Data mining: computers filter large datasets for patterns; humans interpret. It shows what happened better than why.資料探勘由電腦從大量資料中篩選模式，再由人解讀。它較能顯示發生什麼，卻不一定能解釋為什麼。

    Large dataset大型資料集→Pattern模式→Human interpretation人的解讀→Hypothesis, not conclusion假設，而非結論
    Supported有證據支持

    Morning purchases declined.早晨購買量下降。

    Not yet supported尚無足夠證據支持

    Customers left because prices are too high. Competitors, routines, weather, stock-outs, location or service could explain it.顧客因價格太高而離開。競爭者、日常習慣、天氣、缺貨、地點或服務，也可能解釋這個現象。

    Case案例What data does well資料擅長做什麼Limit限制
    ZaraZaraActual store sales drive production instead of forecasts alone.以門市實際銷售引導生產，而非只靠預測。A pattern in Store A may not hold in Store B, next year or another country.A 店的模式未必適用於 B 店、明年或其他國家。
    7-Eleven7-ElevenMorning patterns differ by school versus factory locations.學校附近與工廠附近的早晨購買模式不同。Local data supports local decisions; do not generalize too quickly.在地資料支持在地決策，不要過快推廣到其他情境。
    Behavioral data行為資料Shows what customers actually did: purchases, searches, clicks, visits.顯示顧客實際做了什麼：購買、搜尋、點擊與到訪。A running-shoe search could be a gift, research or a school project. Behavior is not motive.搜尋跑鞋可能是為了送禮、研究或學校報告。行為不等於動機。

    Investigation versus intervention調查原因與介入測試

    Investigation調查原因 調查原因

    Why is this happening? Compare segments and stores, collect feedback.為什麼會發生？比較客群與門市，蒐集回饋。

    Intervention介入測試 介入測試

    If we do X, will behavior change? Run a targeted test and measure it.如果做 X，行為會改變嗎？進行針對性測試並衡量。

    Pattern模式→Investigate cause調查原因→Hypothesis假設→Intervene介入→Measure behavior衡量行為

    Jumping straight to intervention can fix the wrong problem and makes results hard to interpret. Session 3 covers how to test causality.直接跳到介入，可能修錯問題，也讓結果難以解讀。第 3 堂會說明如何檢驗因果。

    Pause & think暫停，想一想

    Morning sales fell after a price increase. Explain why that sequence is not proof that the price change caused the decline. What investigation or comparison could strengthen the claim?早上銷售在漲價後下滑。為什麼這個前後順序無法證明漲價造成銷售下降？哪種調查或比較能讓判斷更可靠？

    5. Prediction, privacy and trust5. 預測、隱私與信任

    Prediction is not certainty預測不等於確定

    AI and predictive models estimate patterns from historical data. New competitors, technology, shocks or changing preferences can break them. Past behavior is evidence, not destiny.AI 與預測模型從歷史資料估計模式。新競爭者、科技、衝擊或偏好改變，都可能使模式失效。過往行為是證據，不是命運。

    Personalization versus privacy個人化與隱私的取捨

    The more data is linked to a person, the more valuable and the more sensitive it becomes. Too much relevance turns into "how do they know that?"資料越能連結到個人，就越有價值，也越敏感。過度精準的內容，可能讓人疑問：「他們怎麼知道這件事？」

    Do not ask how much data we can collect. Ask what data is necessary to improve the customer experience or the decision.別只問能蒐集多少資料；要問改善顧客體驗或決策，真正需要哪些資料。

    Pause & think暫停，想一想

    Personalized ads accurately predict customer interests, but some customers find them intrusive. What trade-off should the brand evaluate before collecting more personal data?個人化廣告能準確預測顧客興趣，但部分顧客覺得被侵犯隱私。品牌蒐集更多個資前，應評估什麼取捨？

    6. Omnichannel and e-commerce6. 全通路與電子商務

    Multichannel多通路

    Store, website, app and social media, possibly run separately.門市、網站、App 與社群媒體，可能各自獨立運作。

    Omnichannel全通路 全通路

    Channels integrated into one seamless journey: research on mobile, check stock online, buy in store, return elsewhere.把通路整合成無縫旅程：手機研究、線上查庫存、門市購買、另一據點退貨。

    More channels mean more cost, complexity and consistency problems. Ask which channels matter to the target and what role each plays.通路越多，成本、複雜度與一致性問題也越多。要問哪些通路對目標客群重要，各自扮演什麼角色。

    E-commerce is not "just build a website"電子商務不是「架好網站就行」

    Traffic流量

    "If you build it, they will come": probably not. The funnel still applies.「網站建好，人就會來」：通常不會。仍要分析漏斗。

    Trust信任

    Customers cannot touch the product: show company, security, price, delivery and guarantees.顧客無法觸摸產品，因此需要公司資訊、安全性、價格、配送與保證。

    Logistics物流

    Inventory, delivery, fulfillment and returns remain physical.庫存、配送、訂單履行與退貨仍涉及實體作業。

    Revenue model營收模式

    Who pays, for what, how often? Users are not revenue.誰付錢、為了什麼、多久付一次？使用者不等於營收。

    Pure play is fully online; clicks-and-mortar combines both. Do not ask which is modern; ask which creates better value and economics here. E-commerce is a trade-off, not an automatically superior channel.純網路業者完全在線上經營；虛實整合業者同時有線上與實體。別只問哪種比較現代，要問哪種在此情境創造更好的價值與經濟效益。電子商務涉及取捨，不是自動優於其他通路。

    Pause & think暫停，想一想

    A retailer runs an app, website and stores, but a customer cannot return an online purchase in-store. What is missing from this channel strategy, and which customer and operating evidence should guide the fix?零售商有 App、網站和門市，但顧客不能在門市退回網購商品。這項通路策略缺少什麼？應用哪些顧客與營運證據決定改善方式？

    8. Loyalty programs8. 忠誠計畫

    A loyalty program is an intervention, not the objective. Membership is not behavioral loyalty, and behavioral loyalty is not attitudinal loyalty.忠誠計畫是一項介入，不是目標本身。加入會員不等於行為忠誠，行為忠誠也不等於態度忠誠。

    Program value ≈ incremental customer value − reward and program cost計畫價值 ≈ 新增顧客價值 − 獎勵與計畫成本

    Ask the counterfactual 反事實: what would this customer have done without the program? Rewarding behavior that would have happened anyway only reduces margin.追問反事實情境：若沒有計畫，顧客原本會怎麼做？若獎勵的是本來就會發生的行為，只會降低利潤率。

    9. Marketing metrics9. 行銷指標

    The professor: "How should we keep score?" First answer: "What are our objectives?" Measurable does not mean useful.教授問：「我們要如何計分？」首先要答：「我們的目標是什麼？」能衡量不代表有用。

    Right order正確順序

    Objective → desired behavior → metric.目標 → 期望行為 → 衡量指標。

    Wrong order錯誤順序

    Available metric → pretend it is the objective.現成指標 → 假裝它就是目標。

    Impression曝光→Click點擊→Visit到訪→Purchase購買→Repeat重購→Profit利潤

    CTR is an intermediate metric. Likes and views become vanity metrics 虛榮指標 when they are not tied to the objective. Even sales can mislead: the market grew, prices rose, discounts cut margin, or acquisition rose while retention collapsed.CTR 是中間階段指標。按讚與觀看若未連結目標，就可能變成虛榮指標。連銷售也可能誤導：也許市場整體成長、價格上升、折扣侵蝕利潤，或獲客增加卻伴隨留存崩跌。

    Pause & think暫停，想一想

    Loyalty membership and campaign clicks both rise, but profit declines. Which outcomes and counterfactual comparisons would show whether these programs created incremental customer value?忠誠會員數與活動點擊數都上升，利潤卻下滑。哪些結果指標及反事實比較，才能判斷計畫是否真正創造新增的顧客價值？

    11. Do not mix these up11. 別混淆這些概念

    CRM = software.CRM＝軟體。

    CRM is relationship management; tools support it.CRM 是關係管理；工具用來支持它。

    Acquiring customers = growing.獲得顧客＝成長。

    Subtract churn.還要扣除流失。

    Lowest satisfaction = top priority.滿意度最低＝最優先。

    Weight by importance.需要依重要性加權。

    Pattern = cause.模式＝原因。

    Causal claims need stronger evidence.因果主張需要更強的證據。

    Investigation = intervention.調查原因＝介入測試。

    Diagnosing is not testing a solution.診斷問題不等於測試解方。

    AI prediction = certainty.AI 預測＝確定結果。

    Historical patterns can break.歷史模式可能失效。

    Omnichannel = every platform.全通路＝所有平台都經營。

    Integration matters, not channel count.重點是整合，不是通路數量。

    E-commerce = no logistics.電子商務＝沒有物流。

    Fulfillment and returns remain.訂單履行與退貨仍存在。

    Membership = loyalty.會員資格＝忠誠。

    Look for incremental behavior.要看是否增加了原本不會發生的行為。

    High CTR or likes = success.高 CTR 或按讚數＝成功。

    Define the objective first.先定義目標。

    12. Cold call practice12. 課堂即答練習

    We acquired 1,000 customers and lost 900. What does that tell you?獲得 1,000 位顧客，卻流失 900 位，代表什麼？

    Net growth is only 100. Acquisition alone hides a retention problem.淨成長只有 100。只看獲客會掩蓋留存問題。

    Why can small repeated purchases beat one large purchase?為什麼少量但持續的購買，可能勝過一次大額購買？

    Lifetime value depends on frequency, retention, margin and acquisition cost, not today's ticket.終身價值取決於頻率、留存、利潤率與獲客成本，而非今日客單價。

    Why might coffee taste come before store music?為什麼咖啡口味可能比店內音樂優先？

    Taste is far more important to the customer's decision, even with a higher satisfaction score.即使口味的滿意度較高，它對顧客決策的重要性仍遠高於音樂。

    Why can't falling purchases prove prices are too high?為什麼購買量下降不能證明價格太高？

    The pattern shows what changed, not why. Other explanations remain.模式顯示什麼改變，而非原因；仍有其他解釋。

    Investigation versus intervention?調查原因與介入測試有何不同？

    Investigation looks for the cause; intervention tests whether an action changes behavior.調查是找原因；介入則檢驗某個行動是否改變行為。

    When does personalization backfire?個人化何時會適得其反？

    When tracking feels intrusive and reduces trust.當追蹤讓人感到被侵犯，並降低信任時。

    Why is having many channels not omnichannel?為什麼通路很多不等於全通路？

    Channels need consistent information and an integrated experience.通路需要一致的資訊與整合的體驗。

    Why doesn't a large user base mean a viable business?為什麼大量使用者不代表商業模式可行？

    Users are not a revenue model; costs, monetization and retention decide viability.使用者不是營收模式；成本、變現與留存才決定可行性。

    Why can rewards to existing frequent buyers destroy value?為什麼獎勵既有高頻買家可能破壞價值？

    If behavior does not change, the firm pays rewards without incremental purchases.若行為沒改變，企業支付獎勵，卻沒有增加購買。

    Why is high CTR not enough when the goal is repeat purchase?目標是重購時，為什麼高 CTR 不夠？

    CTR measures an earlier step, not repeat behavior or its economic value.CTR 衡量更早的階段，而非重購行為或其經濟價值。
