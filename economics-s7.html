    1. What goes into GDP1. GDP 包含什麼

    Picture the whole economy as one giant supermarket. GDP is what the register rings up in a year.把整個經濟想成一間巨大的超市，GDP 就是收銀機一年記下的金額。

    Gross Domestic Product國內生產毛額 is the market value of all final goods and services produced within a country in a year. The register follows four rules, and almost every GDP question tests one of them.國內生產毛額是一國在一年內生產的所有最終商品與服務的市場價值。收銀機遵守四條規則，幾乎每個 GDP 題目都在考其中一條。

    Final only只算最終財

    Flour a bakery uses is already inside the bread's price. Count the bread, not the flour.麵包店用的麵粉已經包含在麵包售價裡。算麵包，不算麵粉。

    Capital goods count資本財要算

    A tractor helps make other goods, but it is not sold again as part of them, so it counts.曳引機用來生產別的東西，但不會被做成那些東西再賣一次，所以要算。

    New production only只算新生產

    A used car or an old house was counted when it was made. Reselling it adds nothing.二手車或舊房子在生產時已經算過，轉售不增加 GDP。

    Inside the borders只算境內

    A Korean-owned factory in Chonburi adds to Thai GDP. Income Thais earn abroad belongs in GNI instead.韓國人在春武里開的工廠算進泰國 GDP。泰國人在海外賺的所得要算在 GNI。

    Spending is split into four buckets: Y = C + I + G + NX, where net exports are exports minus imports. Imports come off because they were made abroad, not because imports are bad. Pension checks and other transfers移轉支付 buy nothing, so they stay off the register until someone spends them.支出分成四類：Y = C + I + G + NX，淨出口是出口減進口。扣掉進口是因為它們在國外生產，不是因為進口不好。退休金等移轉支付沒有購買任何東西，要等有人花掉才會被記錄。

    Why split GDP at all? Households, firms and governments respond to different forces. When GDP falls, it matters whether consumption, investment or government purchases fell, because the policy response is different.為什麼要拆 GDP？家庭、企業和政府受不同力量影響。GDP 下降時，要知道是消費、投資還是政府購買下降，因為對策不同。

    Amounts are a made-up economy in million baht. The income-side split is illustrative.金額是虛構的經濟體，單位為百萬泰銖。所得面的拆分僅供說明。

    The second bar is the point of the income side. Every baht spent on final output ends up as someone's wages, rent, interest or profit, so adding up incomes (gross domestic income, GDI) should give the same total. In practice the two are measured separately and differ slightly, and in a recession economists watch both.第二條長條就是所得面的重點：花在最終產出上的每一泰銖，最後都成為某人的工資、租金、利息或利潤，所以把所得加總（國內所得毛額，GDI）應該得到同樣的總數。實務上兩者分開計算，會略有差異；經濟衰退時，經濟學家兩個都看。

    2. Three adjustments that make GDP comparable2. 讓 GDP 可以比較的三項調整

    Raw GDP answers "how big is this economy?" It does not answer "how well do people live?" Three adjustments close the gap.原始 GDP 回答的是「這個經濟體有多大」，不是「人們過得多好」。要靠三項調整補上差距。

    A chart of the ASEAN-6 shows why. Indonesia's economy, at about $1.44 trillion, is more than twice the size of Singapore's $565 billion, Thailand's $538 billion or Vietnam's $514 billion. Yet one reader asked the obvious question underneath: then why are Indonesians still poor compared with Singaporeans? A big total spread across a very large population is not the same as a high income per person.一張東協六國的圖說明了原因。印尼經濟約 1.44 兆美元，是新加坡（5,650 億）、泰國（5,380 億）或越南（5,140 億）的兩倍以上。但有讀者在底下問了一個顯而易見的問題：那為什麼印尼人比新加坡人窮？總量大，但分給非常多的人口，不等於每人所得高。

    3. What GDP per person misses3. 每人 GDP 漏掉了什麼

    It is not a measure of happiness, yet it is still the best single summary we have.它不是快樂的指標，但仍是我們最好的單一摘要。

    Why it still works為什麼仍然好用

    The good things in life tend to come together. Countries with higher real GDP per person usually have longer lives, happier people on average, better education, and, over time, higher incomes for the poorest fifth.生活中的好事通常一起出現。每人實質 GDP 較高的國家，通常壽命較長、平均較快樂、教育較好；長期下來，最窮的五分之一所得也會提高。

    What it leaves out它漏掉什麼

    Who gets the income, how much leisure people have, unpaid work at home, and damage to the environment. A rise in GDP can even come with more pollution.所得分給誰、人們有多少休閒、家中的無償勞動，以及環境破壞。GDP 上升甚至可能伴隨更多污染。

    4. Comparing countries fairly4. 公平地比較各國

    In India an iPhone costs about as much as in the United States, roughly $1,000. A haircut with a head massage costs 50 rupees, under a dollar. Same country, same day, two completely different price stories.在印度，iPhone 的價格和美國差不多，大約 1,000 美元；理髮加頭部按摩只要 50 盧比，不到 1 美元。同一個國家、同一天，兩種完全不同的價格故事。

    Why some prices line up and others do not為什麼有些價格會拉齊，有些不會

    Tradable goods可貿易品

    If phones were much cheaper in India, people would buy them there and resell them in the US. That arbitrage keeps going until the gap is gone, so a phone ends up costing about the same everywhere. In Delhi the shop actually asked a little more than the US price.如果手機在印度便宜很多，人們會在印度買、拿到美國賣。這種套利會一直持續到價差消失，所以手機在各地的價格差不多。在德里，店家開價其實還比美國略高。

    Services that cannot move無法移動的服務

    You cannot ship a haircut, and the barber cannot easily move to the US, where he might earn $10 an hour instead of $10 a day. Nothing pulls his price up to the US level. Haircuts, cleaning, taxis and massage stay cheap in poor countries.理髮無法運送，理髮師也很難移民到美國（在那裡他一小時可能賺 10 美元，而不是一天 10 美元）。沒有任何力量把他的價格拉到美國的水準。理髮、清潔、計程車和按摩在窮國一直很便宜。

    The barber in Delhi is just as good as a barber in Ohio. His haircut is cheap because of where he works, not how well he cuts. Laws keep him from moving, so his wage is set by the low-wage economy around him.德里的理髮師和俄亥俄州的理髮師一樣好。他的理髮便宜，是因為他在哪裡工作，而不是他剪得好不好。法律讓他無法移動，所以他的工資由周圍的低工資經濟決定。

    The Balassa-Samuelson effect, step by step巴拉薩－薩繆爾森效應，一步一步看

    The name sounds heavy, but the idea is just a chain of opportunity costs. Start with a rich country.名字聽起來很重，但概念只是一串機會成本。先從富國開始。

    1. Workers in factories and offices are very productive, because they have lots of machines, skills and technology.工廠和辦公室的勞工生產力很高，因為他們有大量機器、技能和技術。
    2. Those tradable jobs pay high wages.這些可貿易部門的工作工資很高。
    3. A barber could quit and take one of those jobs. To keep barbers, a haircut has to pay something close to that wage. This is the barber's opportunity cost.理髮師可以辭職去做那些工作。要留住理髮師，理髮的收入就得接近那個工資。這就是理髮師的機會成本。
    4. Higher cost per haircut means the supply of haircuts sits further left, so the price is high, even though the haircut itself is no better.每次理髮的成本更高，代表理髮的供給曲線更靠左，所以價格高，即使理髮本身並沒有比較好。

    In a poor country the chain runs the other way: low productivity in factories, low wages everywhere, cheap services. Add up all those cheap services and the whole price level物價水準 is lower.在窮國，這串連鎖反過來運作：工廠生產力低，到處工資都低，服務便宜。把這些便宜的服務加總，整體物價水準就比較低。

    What purchasing power parity actually does購買力平價實際上在做什麼

    Purchasing power parity購買力平價 (PPP) asks a simple question: how many baht does it take to buy the same basket of goods and services that one dollar buys in the US? That number is the PPP exchange rate. It is usually very different from the market rate you see at the bank.購買力平價（PPP）問一個簡單的問題：在泰國要多少泰銖，才能買到一美元在美國能買到的同一籃商品與服務？這個數字就是 PPP 匯率。它通常和你在銀行看到的市場匯率差很多。

    Basket in the US: $100
    Same basket in Thailand: ฿1,200
    PPP rate = 1,200 ÷ 100 = ฿12 per $
    Market rate (say) = ฿35 per $
    Income ฿420,000 at market rate = $12,000
    Income ฿420,000 at PPP = $35,000

    Round numbers for illustration, not official data.為了說明而用的整數，不是官方數據。

    Same income, same goods, but the PPP figure is almost three times bigger, because ฿420,000 really does buy almost three times as much in Thailand as $12,000 buys in the US. The class slide shows nearly the same gap with real data: Thailand's 2026 GDP is about $0.6 trillion at market rates and $1.9 trillion at PPP, about 3.2 times bigger.同樣的所得、同樣的商品，PPP 數字卻大了將近三倍，因為 42 萬泰銖在泰國能買到的，真的幾乎是 1.2 萬美元在美國能買到的三倍。課堂投影片用實際數據也顯示幾乎一樣的差距：泰國 2026 年 GDP 以市場匯率計約 0.6 兆美元，以 PPP 計約 1.9 兆美元，約大了 3.2 倍。

    5. How big the gaps are5. 差距有多大

    Measured at common prices, the gaps between countries are not 20% or 50%. They are 10, 20, sometimes nearly 100 times.用共同價格衡量，國家之間的差距不是 20% 或 50%，而是 10 倍、20 倍，有時接近 100 倍。

    IMF data, PPP-adjusted: CAR about $600 a year, Mexico $17,800. The US is given as 90 times the CAR, which works out to roughly 540 baskets.IMF 資料，已做 PPP 調整：中非每年約 600 美元，墨西哥 17,800 美元。美國約是中非的 90 倍，換算約 540 籃。

    Two lessons. First, "poor countries" are not one group: Mexico is far closer to the United States than to the Central African Republic, a small landlocked country torn by civil war. Second, these gaps are recent.     "Rich countries have more capital" is true but too easy. The useful answer comes from asking why, again and again. Each answer raises a deeper question.「富國有更多資本」是對的，但太簡單。有用的答案來自一次又一次地問為什麼。每個答案都會引出更深一層的問題。

    Layer 1: what makes a worker productive第一層：什麼讓勞工有生產力

    Picture one farmer in a rich country and one in a poor country. The rich-country farmer is not working harder. She is working with more.想像一個富國的農民和一個窮國的農民。富國的農民並沒有比較努力，而是她手上有更多東西可用。

    Physical capital實體資本

    Tools in the broadest sense: shovels, tractors, phones, roads, buildings.最廣義的工具：鏟子、曳引機、手機、道路、建築。

    Human capital人力資本

    "Tools in the mind": skills built by education, training and experience.「腦中的工具」：透過教育、訓練和經驗累積的技能。

    Technological knowledge技術知識

    Know-how about the world: seed genetics, soil chemistry, better ways to do the job.對世界運作的理解：種子遺傳學、土壤化學、更好的做事方法。

    Entrepreneurs企業家

    The people who bring ideas, workers and capital together and organize them into something that sells.把點子、勞工和資本組合起來，組織成能賣出去的東西的人。

    This is the immediate cause of wealth, and it is correct. It just leaves the real question open: why did some countries build these factors and others did not?這是財富的直接原因，而且是對的。只是真正的問題還沒回答：為什麼有些國家建立了這些要素，有些沒有？

    Layer 2: incentives, the potato test第二層：誘因，馬鈴薯測試

    In China's Great Leap Forward, from the late 1950s to the early 1960s, private farms were merged into big collectives. Do the math from one farmer's point of view.中國大躍進時期（1950 年代末到 1960 年代初），私人農場被併成大型集體農場。從一個農民的角度算算看。

    Own farm: grow 1 more bag → keep 1 bag
    100-person collective: grow 1 more bag → keep 1/100 bag
    Slack off: group loses 1 bag → you lose 1/100 bag

    Effort and reward came apart, so everyone had a reason to free ride搭便車 on everyone else. Same land, people and tools, yet output collapsed. With other leadership mistakes, some 20 to 40 million people starved. China took off only once farmers could keep what they grew above their quota.努力和報酬脫鉤，所以每個人都有理由搭別人的便車。同樣的土地、人和工具，產出卻崩跌。加上其他領導決策錯誤，約 2,000 萬到 4,000 萬人餓死。直到農民可以保有超過配額的收成，中國才開始起飛。

    Capital, skills and ideas do not pile up by themselves. People build them only when they expect to keep a fair share of what they produce.資本、技能和點子不會自己累積。只有人們預期能保有自己產出的合理份額時，才會去建立它們。

    Layer 3: the five key institutions第三層：五大關鍵制度

    Institutions制度 are the rules of the game. They include formal rules, such as laws, courts and regulations, and informal ones, such as norms of honesty, trust and cooperation. Their job in growth is simple: they decide whether the reward for working, saving, studying and investing stays with the person who earned it. That is where incentives come from.制度是遊戲規則。包括正式規則（法律、法院、法規）和非正式規則（誠實、信任、合作的規範）。它們在成長中的作用很簡單：決定工作、儲蓄、學習和投資的報酬，是否留在賺到它的人手上。誘因就是從這裡來的。

    Rules protect rewards規則保護報酬→Expected payoff from effort up努力的預期報酬上升→People save, study, invest, start firms人們儲蓄、學習、投資、創業→More capital, skills, ideas更多資本、技能、點子→Higher GDP per person每人 GDP 提高

    10. Geography: the natural tariff10. 地理：天然關稅

    Think of the most prosperous cities you know. Most sit on a coast or a major river.想想你知道最繁榮的城市，多半位於海岸或大河旁。

    13. Cold call practice13. 課堂口頭問答練習

    What is GDP?什麼是 GDP？

    The market value of all final goods and services produced within a country in a year, for example cars built in Rayong and massages sold in Bangkok this year.一國在一年內生產的所有最終商品與服務的市場價值，例如今年在羅勇生產的汽車和在曼谷賣出的按摩。

    Why does a tractor count but a bakery's flour does not?為什麼曳引機要算，麵包店的麵粉卻不算？

    The flour is used up inside the bread, whose price already includes it. The tractor is a final capital good that is never resold as part of another product.麵粉在做麵包時被用掉，麵包售價已經包含它。曳引機是最終的資本財，不會變成其他產品再賣一次。

    Why are pensions not part of G?為什麼退休金不算 G？

    They are transfers that buy no output. When retirees spend the money it shows up as consumption, so counting the check as well would double count.它們是移轉支付，沒有購買產出。退休人士花錢時會算進消費，再算支票就重複計算。

    What three adjustments make GDP comparable?哪三項調整讓 GDP 可以比較？

    Real GDP removes inflation, so we can compare over time; GDP per capita removes population size, so we can compare countries of different sizes; PPP removes differences in the cost of living.實質 GDP 去掉通膨，可以跨時間比較；每人 GDP 去掉人口規模，可以比較大小不同的國家；PPP 去掉生活成本的差異。

    What is the difference between nominal and real GDP?名目 GDP 和實質 GDP 有什麼不同？

    Nominal GDP uses current prices, so it rises with inflation. Real GDP holds prices fixed, so it rises only when output rises.名目 GDP 用當期價格，會隨通膨上升。實質 GDP 固定價格，只有產出增加時才會上升。

    Is real GDP per capita a good measure of living standards?每人實質 GDP 是好的生活水準指標嗎？

    It is the best single measure because it moves with life expectancy, happiness and education, but it ignores how income is shared, leisure and the environment.它是最好的單一指標，因為它和壽命、快樂與教育一起變動，但它忽略了所得分配、休閒和環境。

    What is purchasing power parity?什麼是購買力平價？

    Valuing every country's output at the same prices, so comparisons reflect what incomes actually buy. Without it, poor countries with cheap services look poorer than they are.用相同價格計算各國產出，讓比較反映所得實際能買到的東西。不這樣做，服務便宜的窮國會看起來比實際更窮。

    What is the Balassa-Samuelson effect?什麼是巴拉薩－薩繆爾森效應？

    Services that cannot be traded are cheaper in poor countries because wages there are low, which makes the overall price level lower. A haircut in India costs under a dollar.無法交易的服務在窮國比較便宜，因為工資低，整體物價水準也因此較低。在印度理髮不到 1 美元。

    What is the rule of 70?什麼是 70 法則？

    Doubling time is about 70 divided by the growth rate in percent: about 35 years at 2%, about 10 years at 7%.倍增時間約為 70 除以成長率（%）：2% 約 35 年，7% 約 10 年。

    What does a PPP exchange rate measure?PPP 匯率衡量的是什麼？

    How much local currency buys the same basket that one dollar buys in the US. If the basket costs ฿1,200 in Thailand and $100 in the US, the PPP rate is ฿12 per dollar, even if the bank rate is ฿35.多少本國貨幣能買到一美元在美國能買到的同一籃商品。如果這籃商品在泰國是 1,200 泰銖、在美國是 100 美元，PPP 匯率就是 1 美元兌 12 泰銖，即使銀行匯率是 35。

    What does the potato example show?馬鈴薯的例子說明了什麼？

    On a 100-person collective farm, an extra bag earns you 1/100 of a bag, so effort and reward come apart and everyone free rides. Incentives, not land or people, made output collapse.在 100 人的集體農場，多種一袋只能分到 1/100 袋，努力和報酬脫鉤，人人搭便車。讓產出崩潰的是誘因，不是土地或人。

    What are the key institutions for growth?成長的關鍵制度有哪些？

    Property rights, honest government, political stability, a dependable legal system, and competitive, open markets.財產權、誠實的政府、政治穩定、可靠的法律制度，以及競爭開放的市場。

    Pick one institution and explain how it drives growth.選一項制度，解釋它如何推動成長。

    Honest government: if permits need bribes, starting a business costs more and takes longer, so fewer firms open and officials gain from delay. In Mumbai, an honest café stayed shut for three months.誠實的政府：如果許可證要賄賂，創業成本更高、時間更長，開業的企業變少，官員還能從延誤中得利。在孟買，一家誠實的咖啡館停業了三個月。

    Why is North versus South Korea called a natural experiment?為什麼南北韓被稱為自然實驗？

    Same people, language, culture and geography, and the north was if anything richer; only the institutions differed. It is not perfect, because allies, aid and war damage also differed.相同的人民、語言、文化和地理，北方甚至比較富有；只有制度不同。它不完美，因為盟友、援助和戰爭破壞也不同。

    Why is being landlocked called a natural tariff?為什麼內陸被稱為天然關稅？

    High transport costs act like a tax on every import and export: prices inland rise, trade shrinks and specialization suffers.高運輸成本就像對每筆進出口課稅：內陸價格上升、貿易縮小、分工受損。
