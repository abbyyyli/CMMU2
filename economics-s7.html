    GDP and Economic GrowthGDP 與經濟成長

    How rich is a country, and why? First we learn to measure it properly, then we see how small differences in growth turn into enormous differences in how people live, and finally we dig for the causes.一個國家有多富有？為什麼？先學會正確地衡量，再看成長率的小差距如何變成生活水準的巨大差距，最後往下挖出原因。

    1. What goes into GDP1. GDP 包含什麼

    Picture the whole economy as one giant supermarket. GDP is what the register rings up in a year.把整個經濟想成一間巨大的超市，GDP 就是收銀機一年記下的金額。

    Gross Domestic Product國內生產毛額 is the market value of all final goods and services produced within a country in a year. The register follows four rules, and almost every GDP question tests one of them.國內生產毛額是一國在一年內生產的所有最終商品與服務的市場價值。收銀機遵守四條規則，幾乎每個 GDP 題目都在考其中一條。

    Final only只算最終財

    Flour a bakery uses is already inside the bread's price. Count the bread, not the flour.麵包店用的麵粉已經包含在麵包售價裡。算麵包，不算麵粉。

    Capital goods count資本財要算

    A tractor helps make other goods, but it is not sold again as part of them, so it counts.曳引機用來生產別的東西，但不會被做成那些東西再賣一次，所以要算。

    New production only只算新生產

    A used car or an old house was counted when it was made. Reselling it adds nothing.二手車或舊房子在生產時已經算過，轉售不增加 GDP。

    Inside the borders只算境內

    A Korean-owned factory in Chonburi adds to Thai GDP. Income Thais earn abroad belongs in GNI instead.韓國人在春武里開的工廠算進泰國 GDP。泰國人在海外賺的所得要算在 GNI。

    Spending is split into four buckets: Y = C + I + G + NX, where net exports are exports minus imports. Imports come off because they were made abroad, not because imports are bad. Pension checks and other transfers移轉支付 buy nothing, so they stay off the register until someone spends them.支出分成四類：Y = C + I + G + NX，淨出口是出口減進口。扣掉進口是因為它們在國外生產，不是因為進口不好。退休金等移轉支付沒有購買任何東西，要等有人花掉才會被記錄。

    Part項目Definition定義Example例子Do not confuse with不要混淆
    C ConsumptionHousehold spending on final goods and services; the largest part, about 63% of US GDP.家庭購買最終商品與服務的支出；最大的一項，約占美國 GDP 的 63%。Groceries, phone plans, a massage.食物、手機方案、按摩。Buying used goods.購買二手貨。
    I InvestmentNew capital goods, usually bought by firms.新的資本財，通常由企業購買。Machines, tractors, a new warehouse.機器、曳引機、新倉庫。Buying shares or bonds.買股票或債券。
    G Government purchasesGoods and services the government buys directly.政府直接購買的商品與服務。A rail line, teachers' salaries, office supplies.鐵路、教師薪水、辦公用品。Transfers such as pensions, which buy nothing.退休金等移轉支付，沒有購買任何東西。
    NX Net exportsExports minus imports.出口減進口。Tourists paying Phuket hotels is an export; imported fuel is an import.遊客付普吉島旅館費是出口；進口燃料是進口。A sign that imports are bad.「進口是壞事」的訊號。

    Why split GDP at all? Households, firms and governments respond to different forces. When GDP falls, it matters whether consumption, investment or government purchases fell, because the policy response is different.為什麼要拆 GDP？家庭、企業和政府受不同力量影響。GDP 下降時，要知道是消費、投資還是政府購買下降，因為對策不同。

    Amounts are a made-up economy in million baht. The income-side split is illustrative.金額是虛構的經濟體，單位為百萬泰銖。所得面的拆分僅供說明。

    The second bar is the point of the income side. Every baht spent on final output ends up as someone's wages, rent, interest or profit, so adding up incomes (gross domestic income, GDI) should give the same total. In practice the two are measured separately and differ slightly, and in a recession economists watch both.第二條長條就是所得面的重點：花在最終產出上的每一泰銖，最後都成為某人的工資、租金、利息或利潤，所以把所得加總（國內所得毛額，GDI）應該得到同樣的總數。實務上兩者分開計算，會略有差異；經濟衰退時，經濟學家兩個都看。

    A third cut is by sector: who produces the output. Thailand's economy has been shifting toward services.第三種拆法是按產業：產出由誰生產。泰國經濟一直在往服務業移動。

    2. Three adjustments that make GDP comparable2. 讓 GDP 可以比較的三項調整

    Raw GDP answers "how big is this economy?" It does not answer "how well do people live?" Three adjustments close the gap.原始 GDP 回答的是「這個經濟體有多大」，不是「人們過得多好」。要靠三項調整補上差距。

    A chart of the ASEAN-6 shows why. Indonesia's economy, at about $1.44 trillion, is more than twice the size of Singapore's $565 billion, Thailand's $538 billion or Vietnam's $514 billion. Yet one reader asked the obvious question underneath: then why are Indonesians still poor compared with Singaporeans? A big total spread across a very large population is not the same as a high income per person.一張東協六國的圖說明了原因。印尼經濟約 1.44 兆美元，是新加坡（5,650 億）、泰國（5,380 億）或越南（5,140 億）的兩倍以上。但有讀者在底下問了一個顯而易見的問題：那為什麼印尼人比新加坡人窮？總量大，但分給非常多的人口，不等於每人所得高。

    The chart shown in class labels Indonesia "$1.443B"; the comments and the bar heights show it means trillion.課堂圖表把印尼標成「$1.443B」；從留言和長條高度看，應為兆（trillion）。

    Adjustment調整What it removes去掉什麼Use it to compare用來比較
    Real GDPInflation: every year's output is valued at the same base-year prices.通膨：每年的產出都用同一組基期價格計算。One country over time.同一國家的不同時間。
    GDP per capitaDifferences in population size: divide by the number of people.人口規模的差異：除以人口數。Countries of different sizes.規模不同的國家。
    Purchasing power parity (PPP)Differences in the cost of living: value every country's output at the same prices.生活成本的差異：用相同價格計算各國產出。Living standards across countries (section 4).各國的生活水準（第 4 節）。

    Start with the first two. A rising GDP number can be a mirage: prices may have gone up while output stayed put.先看前兩項。GDP 上升可能只是海市蜃樓：物價漲了，產出卻沒變。

    Measure指標What it does做了什麼Answers the question回答的問題
    Nominal GDPValues output at this year's prices.用今年的價格計算產出。How much money changed hands?經手了多少錢？
    Real GDPValues every year's output at the same base-year prices.用同一組基期價格計算每年的產出。Did we produce more?我們有沒有生產更多？
    GDP deflator平減指數Nominal ÷ real × 100.名目 ÷ 實質 × 100。How much did prices rise overall?整體物價漲了多少？
    Real GDP per capitaReal GDP ÷ population.實質 GDP ÷ 人口。Is the average person better off?平均每個人有沒有過得更好？

    US figures come from FRED, in 2009 dollars. The per-person rise is 3.6 times on these rounded numbers, often quoted as "about four times".美國數字來自 FRED，以 2009 年美元計。依這些四捨五入的數字，每人成長是 3.6 倍，常被說成「大約四倍」。

    A fall in real GDP is part of what defines a recession. In 2009 US real GDP shrank 3.6% and unemployment jumped.實質 GDP 下降是「經濟衰退」定義的一部分。2009 年美國實質 GDP 縮減 3.6%，失業率大幅上升。

    3. What GDP per person misses3. 每人 GDP 漏掉了什麼

    It is not a measure of happiness, yet it is still the best single summary we have.它不是快樂的指標，但仍是我們最好的單一摘要。

    Why it still works為什麼仍然好用

    The good things in life tend to come together. Countries with higher real GDP per person usually have longer lives, happier people on average, better education, and, over time, higher incomes for the poorest fifth.生活中的好事通常一起出現。每人實質 GDP 較高的國家，通常壽命較長、平均較快樂、教育較好；長期下來，最窮的五分之一所得也會提高。

    What it leaves out它漏掉什麼

    Who gets the income, how much leisure people have, unpaid work at home, and damage to the environment. A rise in GDP can even come with more pollution.所得分給誰、人們有多少休閒、家中的無償勞動，以及環境破壞。GDP 上升甚至可能伴隨更多污染。

    Country國家Real GDP per person每人實質 GDPLiving on under $2 a day每日生活費不到 2 美元
    Nigeria奈及利亞Similar相近≈ 80%
    Pakistan巴基斯坦Similar相近≈ 60%

    The same average hides very different lives. Nigeria has many very poor people and some very rich ones, so the rich pull the average up while most people stay poor. GDP per person tells you how much there is, not who gets it.同樣的平均數，背後是非常不同的生活。奈及利亞有很多極窮的人，也有一些非常富有的人，富人把平均數拉高，大多數人仍然貧窮。每人 GDP 告訴你有多少，不告訴你誰拿到。

    Be careful with cause and effect. Richer countries are healthier, but health also raises productivity, and good institutions can lift both at once.小心因果關係。富國比較健康，但健康也會提高生產力，好的制度也可能同時提高兩者。

    4. Comparing countries fairly4. 公平地比較各國

    In India an iPhone costs about as much as in the United States, roughly $1,000. A haircut with a head massage costs 50 rupees, under a dollar.在印度，iPhone 的價格和美國差不多，大約 1,000 美元；理髮加頭部按摩只要 50 盧比，不到 1 美元。

    Tradable goods可貿易品

    If phones were much cheaper in India, people would buy them there and resell them in the US. That arbitrage pulls prices together everywhere.如果手機在印度便宜很多，人們會在印度買、拿到美國賣。這種套利讓各地價格趨於一致。

    Services that cannot move無法移動的服務

    You cannot ship a haircut, and the barber cannot easily move to the US, where he might earn $10 an hour instead of $10 a day. Haircuts, cleaning, taxis and massage stay cheap in poor countries.理髮無法運送，理髮師也很難移民到美國（在那裡他一小時可能賺 10 美元，而不是一天 10 美元）。理髮、清潔、計程車和按摩在窮國一直很便宜。

    This is the Balassa-Samuelson effect: where factories and offices pay high wages, every job has to pay well to keep its workers, including the barber's. Cheap services make the whole price level lower in poor countries, so a dollar goes further there. Comparing incomes at market exchange rates therefore makes poor countries look poorer than they are. Purchasing power parity購買力平價 (PPP) fixes this by valuing every country's output at the same prices.這就是巴拉薩－薩繆爾森效應：工廠和辦公室付高薪的地方，每份工作都得付高薪才留得住人，理髮師也一樣。服務便宜，讓窮國整體物價水準較低，一塊錢能買得更多。所以用市場匯率比較所得，會讓窮國看起來比實際更窮。購買力平價（PPP）用相同價格計算各國產出，修正這個問題。

    The 2026 world economy shows how much this matters. At market exchange rates the world produces about $128 trillion; at PPP the same output is worth about $219 trillion, because so much of it happens in countries where prices are low.2026 年的世界經濟顯示了這有多重要。以市場匯率計，全球產出約 128 兆美元；以 PPP 計，同樣的產出約值 219 兆美元，因為大量產出發生在物價低的國家。

    IMF estimates for 2026, compiled by Visual Capitalist, as shown in class.IMF 2026 年估計，由 Visual Capitalist 整理，課堂投影片。

    Why do poorer countries gain the most from the adjustment? The haircut shows the mechanism.為什麼較窮的國家調整後增加最多？理髮的例子說明了機制。

    5. How big the gaps are5. 差距有多大

    Measured at common prices, the gaps between countries are not 20% or 50%. They are 10, 20, sometimes nearly 100 times.用共同價格衡量，國家之間的差距不是 20% 或 50%，而是 10 倍、20 倍，有時接近 100 倍。

    IMF data, PPP-adjusted: CAR about $600 a year, Mexico $17,800. The US is given as 90 times the CAR, which works out to roughly 540 baskets.IMF 資料，已做 PPP 調整：中非每年約 600 美元，墨西哥 17,800 美元。美國約是中非的 90 倍，換算約 540 籃。

    Two lessons. First, "poor countries" are not one group: Mexico is far closer to the United States than to the Central African Republic, a small landlocked country torn by civil war. Second, these gaps are recent. For most of history average income barely moved; sustained growth began with the Industrial Revolution and then shot up like the blade of a hockey stick.兩個重點。第一，「窮國」不是同一群：墨西哥離美國，比離中非共和國（一個飽受內戰之苦的內陸小國）近得多。第二，這些差距是近代才出現的。人類歷史大部分時間平均所得幾乎不動；持續成長從工業革命開始，然後像曲棍球桿的桿頭一樣往上衝。

    6. Why growth rates matter so much6. 為什麼成長率這麼重要

    The United States is rich mainly because it kept growing about 2% a year for two centuries.美國富有，主要是因為它兩個世紀以來每年持續成長約 2%。

    Idea概念Definition定義Example例子
    Compound growth複利Each year's growth builds on last year's higher level.每年的成長都建立在前一年較高的水準上。3% a year for 35 years gives 2.8 times, not 2.05.每年 3%、35 年後是 2.8 倍，不是 2.05 倍。
    Rule of 7070 法則Years to double ≈ 70 ÷ growth rate in percent.倍增年數 ≈ 70 ÷ 成長率（%）。US income went from about $2,000 in 1845 to $4,000 in 1880: doubled in 35 years, so about 2% a year.美國所得從 1845 年約 2,000 美元到 1880 年 4,000 美元：35 年翻倍，約每年 2%。
    Ratio scaleEach step on the axis is a doubling, so steady growth is a straight line.軸上每一格都是翻倍，所以穩定成長是一條直線。On an ordinary scale, steady growth looks like it is speeding up.在一般刻度上，穩定成長看起來像越長越快。

    Run the numbers on the US and the stakes are clear. Had it grown only 1% a year since 1800, it would be about as rich today as it was in 1940. Had it grown 3%, it would have reached today's living standard back in 1917, and real GDP per person today would be about $893,000. At current growth rates, it will not get there until 2159.用美國算一下就很清楚。如果它從 1800 年起每年只成長 1%，今天大約只有 1940 年的水準。如果是 3%，1917 年就達到今天的生活水準，今天的每人實質 GDP 會約 893,000 美元。照目前的成長率，要到 2159 年才會到那裡。

    3. 1917When the US would have reached today's living standard, had it grown 3% a year如果美國每年成長 3%，會在這一年達到今天的生活水準

    6. 1980Italy today今天的義大利

    India and China grow fast, so their places keep moving. The comparison is also imperfect: every country today lives longer than the richest countries did in 1800, thanks to vaccines, antibiotics and sanitation spreading from rich countries.印度和中國成長很快，位置會一直往後移。這個比較也不完美：今天每個國家的壽命，都比 1800 年最富有的國家長，因為疫苗、抗生素和衛生知識從富國擴散出去。

    中文邏輯1% 的差距一年看不出來，一百多年後就是好幾倍。富國的秘密不是哪一年長很快，而是長很久都不停。

    7. Miracles and disasters7. 成長奇蹟與災難

    Since the Industrial Revolution, rich and poor regions have mostly pulled apart. Country by country, though, some have raced ahead and others have stalled.工業革命以來，富裕和貧窮的地區大致越拉越遠。但逐國來看，有些國家衝到前面，有些則停滯不前。

    Japan日本Argentina阿根廷
    1950Poor, about the level of Mexico.窮，大約和墨西哥同一水準。Rich, like much of Western Europe; about twice Japan's living standard.富，像大部分西歐國家；生活水準約是日本的兩倍。
    What happened之後的發展Doubled living standards about every 8 years; by the rule of 70, about 8.5% a year.大約每 8 年翻倍；依 70 法則，約每年 8.5%。Some years of negative growth; doubled only once in 65 years, about 1.1% a year.有幾段負成長；65 年只翻倍一次，約每年 1.1%。
    2015More than 10 times its 1950 level; about twice as rich as Argentina.超過 1950 年的 10 倍；約是阿根廷的兩倍富。Stagnated.停滯。

    The reversal is just the rule of 70 at work. Several Japanese doublings against one Argentine doubling more than wiped out a two-to-one head start.這個逆轉就是 70 法則的結果：日本翻倍好幾次，阿根廷只翻倍一次，就足以抹平兩倍的領先。

    Growth miracle成長奇蹟

    Decades of very fast growth per person: Japan, South Korea, China, and perhaps India now. What took the US 200 years of steady growth can happen in about 40.數十年非常快速的每人成長：日本、南韓、中國，或許還有現在的印度。美國花 200 年穩定成長才做到的，奇蹟國家約 40 年就能做到。

    Growth disaster成長災難

    Long stagnation or decline. Argentina grew for a while, then stalled. Niger and Chad never took off at all. Behind the numbers are shorter, harder lives.長期停滯或倒退。阿根廷成長一陣子後停滯；尼日和查德從未起飛。數字背後是更短、更辛苦的人生。

    Why can poor countries grow faster than rich ones? They can copy technology that already exists and add machines where machines are scarce, so each investment pays off a lot. A country already at the frontier has to invent new ideas, which is slower. That is catch-up growth追趕型成長, and it is possible, not automatic. It needs the right conditions, which is where the next section begins.為什麼窮國能比富國長得快？它們可以複製現成的技術，在缺機器的地方增加機器，每筆投資的回報都很高。已經在技術前緣的國家必須發明新點子，成長較慢。這就是追趕型成長：可能發生，但不會自動發生，需要對的條件，這就是下一節的起點。

    Use doubling times to explain how Japan went from half of Argentina's living standard in 1950 to twice it by 2015. Why is "poor countries naturally catch up" too strong a claim?用倍增時間解釋，日本如何從 1950 年只有阿根廷的一半，變成 2015 年的兩倍。為什麼「窮國自然會追上」說得太強？

    8. Why some countries grow: keep asking why8. 為什麼有些國家會成長：一直問為什麼

    "Rich countries have more capital" is true but too easy. The useful answer comes from asking why, again and again.「富國有更多資本」是對的，但太簡單。有用的答案來自一次又一次地問為什麼。

    The answer comes in layers. The most immediate one is that workers in rich countries have more physical capital, more human capital and better technology, organized by entrepreneurs. But that only moves the question back: why did those countries build so much capital? Because people had incentives to work, save and invest. And why the incentives? Because institutions protected the rewards. Why the institutions? That is the least settled part, a mix of history, ideas, culture, geography and luck.答案分好幾層。最直接的一層是：富國的勞工擁有更多實體資本、人力資本和更好的技術，並由企業家組織起來。但這只是把問題往後推：為什麼這些國家累積了這麼多資本？因為人們有工作、儲蓄和投資的誘因。為什麼有誘因？因為制度保護了成果。為什麼有這些制度？這是最沒有定論的部分，混合了歷史、思想、文化、地理和運氣。

    Five key institutions五大關鍵制度What it protects保護什麼Without it沒有它的話
    Property rights財產權Your investment.你的投資。Someone could build on your farm.別人可能在你的農場上蓋房子。
    Honest governmentPermits without bribes; no seizure.不必賄賂就能拿到許可；不會被沒收。Bribes become a cost of doing business.賄賂變成經商成本。
    Political stabilityLong-term plans.長期計畫。Civil war can wash plans away.內戰可能讓計畫化為烏有。
    Dependable legal systemContracts, debts, borrowing and lending.合約、債務、借貸。Hard to collect a debt or prove who owns what.很難收回債務或證明誰擁有什麼。
    Competitive, open marketsPrice signals and room to innovate.價格訊號和創新的空間。Needless regulations protect monopolies.不必要的法規保護獨占。

    中文邏輯資本、人力、技術是直接原因；要追問為什麼能累積，答案是誘因，誘因來自制度，制度又來自歷史、文化、地理和運氣。

    9. Institutions in action9. 制度的實際作用

    Two cases at very different scales: a divided country and a single coffee shop.兩個尺度差很多的例子：一個分裂的國家，和一家咖啡店。

    One peninsula, two outcomes同一個半島，兩種結局

    Before 1945 the two Koreas had the same people, language, culture and history, and similar economies; the north was, if anything, richer. Then the North got central planning and the South got a market economy. Tyler Cowen calls it an almost perfect natural experiment.1945 年以前，南北韓有相同的人民、語言、文化與歷史，經濟也相似；硬要說的話，北方還比較富有。後來北韓實行中央計畫，南韓走向市場經濟。Tyler Cowen 稱之為幾乎完美的自然實驗。

    The road to opening a café in Mumbai在孟買開一家咖啡館的路

    On paper紙上

    The World Bank once ranked India 130th of 190 for ease of doing business and estimated 14 procedures and 26 days for a simple Mumbai license.世界銀行曾把印度的經商便利度排在 190 國中第 130 名，並估計在孟買申請簡單執照要 14 道程序、26 天。

    In practice實際上

    The owner of Koinonia Coffee Roasters said 26 days "would be a dream".Koinonia Coffee Roasters 的老闆說，如果只要 26 天，「那簡直是夢想」。

    1. Lease a commercial space before the company even exists, just to have an address to register.公司還沒成立，就得先租商業空間，只為了有個地址可以登記。
    2. Write down in advance everything the business might ever do; adding anything later means amending the papers.事先寫下公司將來可能做的所有業務；之後要新增就得修改文件。
    3. Work through 10 to 12 licenses with three consultants, some of which changed along the way.在三位顧問協助下申請 10 到 12 張執照，其中有些中途還改了。
    4. Get a pollution permit for a gas-powered roaster: maps, emissions details and 8 to 12 trips to an office an hour away.為一台瓦斯烘豆機申請污染許可：地圖、排放資料，跑一小時車程外的辦公室 8 到 12 趟。
    5. Refuse to pay bribes, and lose a licensing firm that would not work without them.拒絕行賄，結果一家不付好處就不肯辦事的代辦公司拒絕合作。
    6. Sit closed for three months, paying staff and rent, ready to open but waiting for paper.停業三個月，照付員工薪水和房租，一切準備好了，只等文件。
    More licenses and delays執照更多、拖更久→Costlier, slower entry進入更貴、更慢→Fewer cafés open開業的咖啡館變少→Supply shifts left供給左移→Higher prices, less output, idle capital價格更高、產出更少、資本閒置

    One honest firm shows bribe-free business is possible, not that it is easy or typical. The World Bank has since stopped publishing Doing Business.一家誠實的公司證明不行賄也能經營，但不代表容易或普遍。世界銀行之後已停止發布經商便利度指數。

    10. Geography: the natural tariff10. 地理：天然關稅

    Think of the most prosperous cities you know. Most sit on a coast or a major river.想想你知道最繁榮的城市，多半位於海岸或大河旁。

    Cheap shipping by water水運便宜→Lower trade costs貿易成本降低→Bigger markets市場變大→More specialization and new ideas更多分工與新點子→Growth成長

    That chain goes back to Adam Smith, and a map of output per square kilometer bears it out: GDP piles up along the US coasts and the Great Lakes, in Western Europe, Japan, coastal China and coastal Australia. Landlocked countries are, on average, poorer. Africa is huge, big enough to hold most of the US, China, India and much of Europe, yet Europe has two to three times more coastline. Africa is the most landlocked continent and has the most poor countries.這條推理可以追溯到亞當斯密。每平方公里產出的地圖也證實了它：GDP 集中在美國沿海與五大湖區、西歐、日本、中國沿海和澳洲沿海。內陸國平均比較窮。非洲非常大，裝得下大部分的美國、中國、印度和大半個歐洲，但歐洲的海岸線是非洲的兩到三倍。非洲是最內陸的大陸，也是窮國最多的大陸。

    The lesson結論

    Being landlocked works like a natural tariff天然關稅, a tax on every import and export. If a natural tariff slows growth, a tariff chosen by government is unlikely to help it.內陸就像天然關稅，對每筆進出口課稅。如果天然關稅拖慢成長，政府自己設的關稅也不太可能有幫助。

    The limit限制

    Geography is not destiny. Landlocked Switzerland is rich, and railways and roads can cut the natural tariff. Growth depends on natural advantages and on policy.地理不是命運。內陸的瑞士很富有，鐵路和公路也能降低天然關稅。成長同時取決於天然條件和政策。

    11. When the numbers themselves are weak11. 當數字本身就不可靠

    Every comparison above assumes we can count output and people. In the poorest countries, often we cannot.上面所有比較都假設我們數得出產出和人口。但在最窮的國家，常常數不出來。

    The Central African Republic has no reliable birth and death records and no recent census. In 2022 researchers went house to house by car, boat, motorbike and on foot and found that nearly 6% of the population had died that year, mostly from malaria, diarrhea and violence. The UN's figure was about four times lower, because it projected older data forward as if conditions were stable.中非共和國沒有可靠的出生與死亡紀錄，也沒有近期的人口普查。2022 年研究人員搭車、坐船、騎摩托車、步行逐戶調查，發現當年有近 6% 的人口死亡，主要死於瘧疾、腹瀉和暴力。聯合國的數字約低四倍，因為它把舊資料往後推，假設情況穩定。

    Estimate估計Deaths per 10,000 people a day每天每萬人死亡數Above the emergency line of 1?是否超過緊急門檻 1？
    Household survey家戶調查≈ 1.6Yes是

    The difference is not academic. Aid agencies treat one death per 10,000 people a day as the threshold for an emergency, so which estimate you believe decides whether the CAR counts as a crisis at all.這個差異不只是學術問題。援助機構把每天每萬人死亡 1 人視為緊急門檻，所以相信哪一個估計，決定了中非到底算不算危機。

    Survey rate converted from nearly 6% of the population a year. The CAR government and the UN's chief demographer disputed the survey, so treat it as evidence of large uncertainty rather than a settled figure.調查數字由「每年近 6% 人口死亡」換算而來。中非政府和聯合國首席人口學家對這項調查有異議，所以應視為「不確定性很大」的證據，而非定論。

    The lesson for GDP: the countries at the bottom of every ranking are exactly where both the output figures and the population counts are weakest. "About 90 times richer" is an order of magnitude, not a precise fact.對 GDP 的啟示：排名最底層的國家，正是產出數字和人口數字都最不可靠的地方。「約 90 倍」是數量級，不是精確的事實。

    12. Do not mix these up12. 不要混淆這些概念

    Anything used to make other goods is intermediate.用來生產其他東西的都是中間財。

    Capital goods such as a tractor are final goods and count.曳引機這類資本財是最終財，要算。

    GDP counts what citizens produce anywhere.GDP 算的是本國人民在任何地方的生產。

    That is GNI. GDP counts production inside the borders.那是 GNI。GDP 算的是境內生產。

    Investment means buying shares.投資就是買股票。

    In GDP, investment means new capital goods.在 GDP 裡，投資是新的資本財。

    All government spending is G.政府的所有支出都是 G。

    Transfers such as pensions buy nothing and are left out.退休金等移轉支付沒有購買東西，不計入。

    The US economy grew 55 times since 1950.美國經濟從 1950 年成長了 55 倍。

    That is nominal. Real is about 8 times; per person about 3.6.那是名目。實質約 8 倍；每人約 3.6 倍。

    Indonesia has the biggest economy in ASEAN, so Indonesians are the richest.印尼是東協最大的經濟體，所以印尼人最富有。

    Total GDP measures size. Divide by population to compare living standards.GDP 總量衡量的是規模。要除以人口才能比較生活水準。

    Same GDP per person means the same poverty.每人 GDP 相同，貧窮程度就相同。

    Distribution differs, as Nigeria and Honduras show.分配不同，奈及利亞和宏都拉斯就是例子。

    Haircuts are cheap in India because barbers are worse.印度理髮便宜是因為理髮師比較差。

    Wages are low across the economy and workers cannot move.是整體工資低，而且勞工無法移動。

    Compare incomes at market exchange rates.用市場匯率比較所得。

    Use PPP, or poor countries look poorer than they are.要用 PPP，否則窮國看起來比實際更窮。

    A one-point growth gap is small.成長率差 1 個百分點很小。

    Over 140 years, 3% against 2% is about 3.9 times.140 年下來，3% 對 2% 差約 3.9 倍。

    Poor countries automatically catch up.窮國會自動追上。

    Argentina, Niger and Chad did not.阿根廷、尼日和查德就沒有。

    More capital explains why rich countries are rich.更多資本就解釋了富國為何富有。

    That is the immediate cause. Ask why the capital was built.那只是直接原因。要追問資本為什麼會被建立。

    Geography is destiny.地理決定命運。

    It matters, but institutions and infrastructure can offset it.地理有影響，但制度和基礎建設可以彌補。

    13. Cold call practice13. 課堂口頭問答練習

    What is GDP?什麼是 GDP？

    The market value of all final goods and services produced within a country in a year, for example cars built in Rayong and massages sold in Bangkok this year.一國在一年內生產的所有最終商品與服務的市場價值，例如今年在羅勇生產的汽車和在曼谷賣出的按摩。

    Why does a tractor count but a bakery's flour does not?為什麼曳引機要算，麵包店的麵粉卻不算？

    The flour is used up inside the bread, whose price already includes it. The tractor is a final capital good that is never resold as part of another product.麵粉在做麵包時被用掉，麵包售價已經包含它。曳引機是最終的資本財，不會變成其他產品再賣一次。

    Why are pensions not part of G?為什麼退休金不算 G？

    They are transfers that buy no output. When retirees spend the money it shows up as consumption, so counting the check as well would double count.它們是移轉支付，沒有購買產出。退休人士花錢時會算進消費，再算支票就重複計算。

    What three adjustments make GDP comparable?哪三項調整讓 GDP 可以比較？

    Real GDP removes inflation, so we can compare over time; GDP per capita removes population size, so we can compare countries of different sizes; PPP removes differences in the cost of living.實質 GDP 去掉通膨，可以跨時間比較；每人 GDP 去掉人口規模，可以比較大小不同的國家；PPP 去掉生活成本的差異。

    What is the difference between nominal and real GDP?名目 GDP 和實質 GDP 有什麼不同？

    Nominal GDP uses current prices, so it rises with inflation. Real GDP holds prices fixed, so it rises only when output rises.名目 GDP 用當期價格，會隨通膨上升。實質 GDP 固定價格，只有產出增加時才會上升。

    Is real GDP per capita a good measure of living standards?每人實質 GDP 是好的生活水準指標嗎？

    It is the best single measure because it moves with life expectancy, happiness and education, but it ignores how income is shared, leisure and the environment.它是最好的單一指標，因為它和壽命、快樂與教育一起變動，但它忽略了所得分配、休閒和環境。

    What is purchasing power parity?什麼是購買力平價？

    Valuing every country's output at the same prices, so comparisons reflect what incomes actually buy. Without it, poor countries with cheap services look poorer than they are.用相同價格計算各國產出，讓比較反映所得實際能買到的東西。不這樣做，服務便宜的窮國會看起來比實際更窮。

    What is the Balassa-Samuelson effect?什麼是巴拉薩－薩繆爾森效應？

    Services that cannot be traded are cheaper in poor countries because wages there are low, which makes the overall price level lower. A haircut in India costs under a dollar.無法交易的服務在窮國比較便宜，因為工資低，整體物價水準也因此較低。在印度理髮不到 1 美元。

    What is the rule of 70?什麼是 70 法則？

    Doubling time is about 70 divided by the growth rate in percent: about 35 years at 2%, about 10 years at 7%.倍增時間約為 70 除以成長率（%）：2% 約 35 年，7% 約 10 年。

    What are the key institutions for growth?成長的關鍵制度有哪些？

    Property rights, honest government, political stability, a dependable legal system, and competitive, open markets.財產權、誠實的政府、政治穩定、可靠的法律制度，以及競爭開放的市場。

    Why is being landlocked called a natural tariff?為什麼內陸被稱為天然關稅？

    High transport costs act like a tax on every import and export: prices inland rise, trade shrinks and specialization suffers.高運輸成本就像對每筆進出口課稅：內陸價格上升、貿易縮小、分工受損。

    14. Answer templates14. 答題模板

    Define GDP定義 GDP

    “GDP is the market value of all final goods and services produced within a country in a year. Final means we skip intermediate inputs to avoid double counting; produced means resales and transfers do not count.”「GDP 是一國在一年內生產的所有最終商品與服務的市場價值。『最終』代表略過中間投入以免重複計算；『生產』代表轉售和移轉支付不算。」

    Nominal, real, per person名目、實質、每人

    “Nominal GDP rises with prices. Real GDP holds prices fixed, so it shows whether we produce more. Dividing by population gives real GDP per capita, the closest single measure of how the average person is doing.”「名目 GDP 會隨物價上升。實質 GDP 固定價格，顯示我們是否真的生產更多。再除以人口，就是每人實質 GDP，最接近平均每個人過得如何的單一指標。」

    Comparing countries跨國比較

    “Tradable goods cost about the same everywhere because of arbitrage, but services cannot be traded and workers cannot move freely. Low wages make services and the whole price level cheaper in poor countries, so we compare incomes at PPP.”「可貿易品因為套利，各地價格差不多；但服務無法交易，勞工也不能自由移動。低工資讓窮國的服務和整體物價較便宜，所以要用 PPP 比較所得。」

    Growth rates成長率

    “By the rule of 70, 2% growth doubles income every 35 years and 7% every 10. Small gaps compound: Japan went from half of Argentina's living standard in 1950 to twice it by 2015.”「依 70 法則，2% 成長每 35 年所得翻倍，7% 每 10 年翻倍。小差距會累積：日本從 1950 年只有阿根廷的一半，到 2015 年變成兩倍。」

    Why some countries are rich為什麼有些國家富有

    “The immediate reason is productive workers with more physical capital, human capital and technology, organized by entrepreneurs. The deeper reason is incentives created by institutions: property rights, honest government, stability, dependable courts and open markets.
