    GDP and Economic GrowthGDP 與經濟成長

    GDP tells us how much an economy produces. Once prices and population are taken out, it becomes the best single gauge of average living standards. This session measures GDP, compares it across time and countries, and asks why some countries grew rich while others stayed poor.GDP 告訴我們一個經濟體生產了多少。去掉物價和人口的影響後，它是衡量平均生活水準最好的單一指標。本堂先學怎麼衡量 GDP，再跨時間、跨國家比較，最後問：為什麼有些國家變富，有些國家一直很窮？

    1. What GDP counts1. GDP 算什麼

    MRU pictures the economy as a giant supermarket. Every time a finished good or service is sold, the register rings up its price. Add up a year of sales and you have GDP.MRU 把經濟想成一間巨大的超市：每賣出一個最終商品或服務，收銀機就記下它的價格。把一整年加起來，就是 GDP。

    Gross Domestic Product國內生產毛額 is the market value of all finished goods and services produced within a country in a year. Each phrase is a counting rule.國內生產毛額是一國在一年內生產的所有最終商品與服務的市場價值。定義裡的每個詞都是一條計算規則。

    Phrase用語Rule規則MRU exampleMRU 的例子Do not confuse with不要混淆
    Market value市場價值Prices let us add apples and haircuts.用價格才能把蘋果和理髮加在一起。Polar bears are left out because there is no easy market price, not because they lack value.北極熊沒被算進去，是因為沒有容易取得的市場價格，不是因為牠們沒有價值。Total value to society.對社會的全部價值。
    Finished (final)最終Count goods sold to their end user.只算賣給最終使用者的商品。Eggs for a home omelet count; eggs a baker uses for a cake do not, because the cake's price already includes them.買蛋回家做蛋捲要算；烘焙店用來做蛋糕的蛋不算，因為蛋糕售價已經包含它們。Intermediate goods中間財, used up inside another product.中間財：在生產其他產品時被用掉的東西。
    Capital goods資本財Count them: they are finished goods.要算：它們是最終財。A tractor sold to a farm counts, even though it helps produce other goods.賣給農場的曳引機要算，即使它之後用來生產別的東西。Intermediate inputs, which are not resold as part of another product.中間投入；資本財不會被做成別的產品再賣一次。
    Produced in a year當年生產Count new production only.只算新的產出。A newly built house counts; selling an old house adds nothing.新蓋的房子要算；賣舊房子不增加 GDP。Resales, which were counted when first produced.轉售：最初生產時已經算過。
    Within a country在一國境內Location of production decides.以生產地點為準。French wine bought in the United States adds to France's GDP.在美國買的法國葡萄酒，算進法國的 GDP。GNI, which counts income earned by a country's residents wherever they produce.GNI：計算一國居民在任何地方賺到的所得。

    GDP also leaves out purely financial trades, such as buying shares, and transfer payments, such as pensions. Neither one produces anything new.GDP 也不包含純金融交易（例如買股票）和移轉支付（例如退休金），因為兩者都沒有生產新的東西。

    The transactions are illustrative Thai examples, not national-accounts data.這些交易是用來說明的泰國例子，不是國民所得帳的資料。

    2. Nominal versus real GDP2. 名目 GDP 與實質 GDP

    GDP can rise because the economy produces more, or only because prices went up. MRU calls the second kind a mirage.GDP 上升可能是因為生產更多，也可能只是物價上漲。MRU 把後者稱為「海市蜃樓」。

    Term名詞Definition定義Example例子
    Nominal GDPOutput valued at the prices of the year it is produced.用生產當年的價格計算的產出。A loaf of bread that cost a dime now costs a couple of dollars.以前一角錢的麵包，現在要兩三美元。
    Real GDPOutput valued at the same set of base-year prices, so it changes only when quantities change.用同一組基期價格計算的產出，所以只有數量改變時才會變。Valuing this year's rice and haircuts at last year's prices.用去年的價格計算今年的稻米和理髮。
    GDP deflator平減指數Nominal GDP divided by real GDP, times 100: a price index for all domestic output.名目 GDP 除以實質 GDP 再乘 100，是所有國內產出的物價指數。Nominal 8.66 million ÷ real 7.40 million × 100 ≈ 117.名目 866 萬 ÷ 實質 740 萬 × 100 ≈ 117。
    Real GDP per capitaReal GDP divided by population.實質 GDP 除以人口。Real GDP +5.7% with population +5% leaves about +0.7% per person.實質 GDP 成長 5.7%、人口成長 5%，每人只多約 0.7%。
    RecessionA broad fall in real economic activity; falling real GDP is part of the definition.實質經濟活動全面下降；實質 GDP 下降是定義的一部分。US real GDP fell 3.6% in 2009, and unemployment spiked.美國 2009 年實質 GDP 下降 3.6%，失業率飆升。

    MRU's FRED example for the United States shows each step shrinking the headline number:MRU 用 FRED 的美國資料示範：每做一步，數字就縮小一次。

    3. Is GDP per person a good measure of living standards?3. 每人 GDP 是好的生活水準指標嗎？

    MRU's answer: imperfect, but probably the best single measure, because it moves with many things money cannot buy directly.MRU 的答案：不完美，但可能是最好的單一指標，因為它和許多金錢買不到的東西一起變動。

    What it tracks它反映什麼

    Countries with higher real GDP per capita tend to have longer life expectancy, happier people on average, higher scores on the UN Human Development Index, and, over time, rising incomes for the poorest 20%.每人實質 GDP 較高的國家，通常壽命較長、平均較快樂、聯合國人類發展指數較高；長期來看，最窮 20% 的所得也會上升。

    What it misses它漏掉什麼

    Distribution. Nigeria, Pakistan and Honduras have similar real GDP per capita, yet about 80%, 60% and 33% of their people live on under $2 a day. Nigeria's income is far more unequal.所得分配。奈及利亞、巴基斯坦和宏都拉斯的每人實質 GDP 相近，但每天生活費不到 2 美元的人口比例約為 80%、60% 和 33%。奈及利亞的所得分配不平均得多。

    It also leaves out leisure, unpaid household work and pollution. Because rich countries tend to be healthier, people sometimes read the link as cause and effect. It is a correlation: good institutions could raise both income and health.它也漏掉休閒、無償家務和污染。富國通常比較健康，人們常把這看成因果，但這只是相關：好的制度可能同時提高所得與健康。

    4. Two ways to split GDP4. 拆解 GDP 的兩種方法

    The same total can be split by who buys the output, or by who receives the income from making it.同一個總數，可以按「誰買走產出」來拆，也可以按「誰拿到生產的所得」來拆。

    National spending approach: Y = C + I + G + NX, where NX = exports − imports
    Factor income approach: GDI = employee compensation + rent + interest + profit
    Part項目Definition定義MRU exampleMRU 的例子Do not confuse with不要混淆
    C ConsumptionHousehold spending on final goods and services; about 63% of US GDP.家庭購買最終商品與服務的支出，約占美國 GDP 的 63%。Cakes and massages.蛋糕和按摩。Spending on used goods.購買二手貨的支出。
    I InvestmentNew capital goods, usually bought by businesses.新的資本財，通常由企業購買。Computers and tractors.電腦和曳引機。Buying shares or bonds.買股票或債券。
    G Government purchasesGoods and services bought directly by government, both everyday items and capital.政府直接購買的商品與服務，包括日常用品和資本財。Paper and pens; tanks and computers.紙和筆；坦克和電腦。Transfer payments移轉支付 such as a social security check, which buy nothing.移轉支付，例如社會安全福利支票，它沒有購買任何東西。
    NX Net exportsExports minus imports; small for the United States.出口減進口；對美國來說很小。Foreigners buy our goods; we subtract what C, I and G bought from abroad.外國人買我們的商品；再扣掉 C、I、G 中向國外購買的部分。A judgment that imports are bad.「進口是壞事」的判斷。

    Why exclude the pension check? When the retiree spends it, that spending is already counted as consumption, so counting the check too would double count. Why split GDP at all? Consumption, investment and government purchases respond to different forces, and a recession calls for different tools depending on which one fell.為什麼不算退休金支票？退休人士花掉它時已經算進消費，再算支票就重複計算。為什麼要拆 GDP？消費、投資和政府購買受不同力量影響；衰退時，要看是哪一項下降，才知道用什麼工具。

    The income side works because every baht spent on final output ends up with workers, landlords, lenders or entrepreneurs. In theory GDP equals gross domestic income (GDI). They are measured in different ways, so the two numbers differ slightly, and economists watch both in a recession because one may give an earlier signal.所得面成立，是因為花在最終產出上的每一泰銖，最後都會落到工人、房東、放款人或企業家手上。理論上 GDP 等於國內所得毛額（GDI）。兩者的計算方式不同，所以數字略有差異；衰退時經濟學家兩個都看，因為其中一個可能更早發出訊號。

    5. Comparing countries: the haircut test5. 跨國比較：理髮測試

    To compare GDP across countries, we must account for the fact that the same dollar buys more in a poor country.跨國比較 GDP 時，要考慮同一塊錢在窮國能買到更多東西。

    In India在印度Price價格Why原因
    iPhone 7 PlusAbout 70,000 rupees, roughly $1,000: at least the US price.約 7 萬盧比，大約 1,000 美元，至少和美國一樣貴。Tradable可貿易: if it were much cheaper in India, people would buy there and resell in the US. Arbitrage pulls prices together.可貿易：如果印度便宜很多，人們會在印度買、拿到美國賣。套利讓價格趨於一致。
    Haircut and head massage50 rupees, under $1.50 盧比，不到 1 美元。Service: hard to trade. The barber, Raju, is as good as any in the US but earns $10 a day or less, versus maybe $10 an hour in the US. Laws stop him moving, so labor is immobile.服務：很難交易。理髮師 Raju 不比美國理髮師差，一天卻只賺 10 美元以下，美國大約一小時就有 10 美元。法律限制他移民，勞動無法自由移動。

    Services that cannot move, such as haircuts, housecleaning, taxis and massage, are much cheaper in poorer countries. This is the Balassa-Samuelson effect. Because services are cheap, the overall price level is lower and each dollar goes further. Converting a poor country's GDP at market exchange rates therefore understates its living standard. Purchasing power parity購買力平價 (PPP) values output at a common set of prices, which MRU calls the most accurate way we know to compare countries.無法移動的服務，例如理髮、清潔、計程車和按摩，在較窮的國家便宜得多，這就是巴拉薩－薩繆爾森效應。服務便宜，整體物價水準就較低，每一塊錢能買得更多。所以用市場匯率換算窮國的 GDP，會低估它的生活水準。購買力平價（PPP）用同一組價格計算產出，MRU 說這是我們所知最準確的跨國比較方法。

    High productivity in factories and offices工廠與辦公室生產力高→High wages across the economy整體工資高→A barber's outside option rises理髮師的外部機會變好→Haircut supply shifts left理髮供給左移→Higher service prices服務價格較高

    The supply and demand numbers are a teaching model. MRU's video explains the price gap through low wages and immobile labor; the productivity link is the standard Balassa-Samuelson reasoning.供需數字是教學模型。MRU 的影片用低工資和勞動無法移動來解釋價差；生產力的連結是標準的巴拉薩－薩繆爾森推理。

    How to build this graph如何畫這張圖
    1. Put haircuts per day on the horizontal axis and baht per haircut on the vertical axis. Draw D sloping down and S1 sloping up; mark E1, P1 and Q1.橫軸是每日理髮次數，縱軸是每次理髮的泰銖。畫向下的 D 和向上的 S1，標出 E1、P1、Q1。
    2. Ask what changed. A higher wage elsewhere raises the cost of each haircut, so the supply curve shifts left (up) to S2.問：什麼變了？外部工資提高，每次理髮的成本上升，所以供給線左移（上移）到 S2。
    3. Mark E2 where D meets S2. Read P2 above P1 and Q2 below Q1. If local incomes also rise, shift D right too, and P rises further.在 D 與 S2 交點標 E2：P2 高於 P1，Q2 低於 Q1。如果當地所得也提高，D 也右移，P 再上升。

    中文邏輯理髮便宜不是因為理髮師比較差，而是整個國家的工資低。可貿易品被套利拉平，服務拉不平，所以要用 PPP 才公平。

    6. How big are the gaps?6. 差距有多大？

    Measured in PPP dollars, the differences between countries are not small. They are often 10, 20, 50, sometimes 100 times.用 PPP 美元衡量，國家間的差距一點也不小，常常是 10、20、50 倍，有時到 100 倍。

    IMF data as used by MRU. *MRU states the United States is 90 times the CAR but does not read out the basket count; 90 × 6 baskets gives about 540, roughly $54,000.MRU 使用的 IMF 資料。*MRU 說美國約是中非的 90 倍，但沒有唸出籃數；90 × 6 籃約為 540 籃，大約 54,000 美元。

    The Central African Republic is small, landlocked and has suffered civil war; MRU calls it probably the poorest country in the world. Mexico is about 29 times richer than the CAR, and the United States about 3 times richer than Mexico. Countries we lump together as "poor" are not in the same league: Mexico is much closer to the United States than to the truly destitute.中非共和國面積小、是內陸國，又經歷內戰，MRU 說它可能是全世界最窮的國家。墨西哥約是中非的 29 倍，美國約是墨西哥的 3 倍。我們常歸為「窮國」的國家其實不在同一個等級：墨西哥離美國，比離極度貧窮的國家近得多。

    For most of history, average income barely grew. Sustained growth began with the Industrial Revolution and then bent sharply upward, the "hockey stick" of human prosperity. Today's gaps are therefore recent: they reflect when countries started growing and how steadily they kept going.人類歷史大部分時間，平均所得幾乎沒有成長。持續成長從工業革命開始，然後急速上升，也就是人類繁榮的「曲棍球桿」。所以今天的差距是近代才形成的，反映各國何時開始成長、成長得多穩定。

    7. Growth rates are crucial7. 成長率是關鍵

    The main reason the United States is rich is simply that it grew steadily for a long time.美國富有的主要原因，就是它長期穩定地成長。

    Tool工具Definition定義Example例子
    Compound growth複利Each year's growth builds on last year's higher level.每年的成長都建立在前一年較高的水準上。At 3% a year, income is about 2.8 times higher after 35 years, not 2.05 times.每年 3%，35 年後所得約為 2.8 倍，不是 2.05 倍。
    Rule of 7070 法則Years to double ≈ 70 ÷ growth rate in percent; it also works backward.倍增所需年數 ≈ 70 ÷ 成長率（%）；也可以反推。US income went from about $2,000 in 1845 to $4,000 in 1880: 35 years, so about 2% a year.美國所得從 1845 年約 2,000 美元到 1880 年 4,000 美元，用了 35 年，所以每年約 2%。
    Ratio scaleEach tick is a doubling, so a straight line means constant growth.每一格代表翻倍，所以直線代表固定成長率。On an ordinary scale, steady growth looks like it is speeding up.在一般刻度上，穩定成長看起來像是越長越快。

    MRU's counterfactuals: had the US grown at 1% a year since 1800, it would be about as rich today as in 1940, when few people had cars and the average income would now count as poverty. At 3% it would have reached today's living standard in 1917, and real GDP per capita today would be about $893,000. At current rates, the US will not reach that level until 2159.MRU 的假設情境：如果美國從 1800 年起每年只成長 1%，今天大概只有 1940 年的水準，那時很少人有車，當時的平均所得放到今天算是貧窮。如果是 3%，1917 年就達到今天的生活水準，今天的每人實質 GDP 會約 893,000 美元。照目前的成長率，美國要到 2159 年才會到那個水準。

    Comparing poor countries to past US years is imperfect. Every country today has higher life expectancy than the richest countries had in 1800, thanks to spillovers such as smallpox eradication, antibiotics and better sanitation.把窮國和美國過去某一年相比並不完美。今天每個國家的平均壽命，都比 1800 年最富有的國家還高，這要歸功於天花根除、抗生素和衛生知識等外溢效果。

    中文邏輯1% 的差距一年看不出來，一百多年後就是好幾倍。富國的秘密不是哪一年長很快，而是長很久都不停。

    8. Growth miracles and growth disasters8. 成長奇蹟與成長災難

    Are poor countries catching up? Since the Industrial Revolution, regions have diverged: "Divergence, Big Time". Country by country, the story splits.窮國有在追上嗎？工業革命以後，各地區的差距越拉越大（「大分歧」）。但逐國來看，故事不一樣。

    1950Poor, similar to Mexico.窮，和墨西哥差不多。Similar to many Western European economies; about twice Japan's living standard.和許多西歐國家相近；生活水準約是日本的兩倍。
    Growth成長Doubled living standards about every 8 years; 70 ÷ 8.2 ≈ 8.5% a year.大約每 8 年翻倍；70 ÷ 8.2 ≈ 每年 8.5%。Periods of negative growth; doubled only once in 65 years, about 1.1% a year.有幾段負成長；65 年只翻倍一次，約每年 1.1%。
    2015Over 10 times its 1950 level; about twice as prosperous as Argentina.超過 1950 年的 10 倍；約是阿根廷的兩倍。Stagnated.停滯。
    Growth miracle成長奇蹟

    A long run of very fast per capita growth: Japan, South Korea, China, and perhaps India now. What took the US 200 years of steady growth can happen in about 40.長期非常快速的每人成長：日本、南韓、中國，或許還有現在的印度。美國花 200 年穩定成長才達到的水準，奇蹟國家約 40 年就能做到。

    Growth disaster成長災難

    Long stagnation or decline: Argentina stalled, while Niger and Chad never had significant growth. That means shorter, less happy lives, not just smaller numbers.長期停滯或倒退：阿根廷停滯，尼日和查德從未有明顯成長。這代表更短、更不快樂的人生，不只是數字比較小。

    Catch-up收斂 is possible but not automatic. A poor country can grow fast by adopting technology that already exists and adding capital where it is scarce. A country at the frontier must invent new ideas, which is slower. MRU's condition is "the right factors in place", the subject of the next section.追趕是可能的，但不會自動發生。窮國可以採用現成的技術、在資本稀少的地方增加資本，因而成長很快；位於技術前緣的國家必須發明新點子，成長較慢。MRU 說要「具備正確的條件」，這就是下一節的主題。

    11. Geography and the natural tariff11. 地理與天然關稅

    The most prosperous cities usually sit on a major coast or river. Water makes trade cheap.最繁榮的城市通常位於大海岸或大河旁。水運讓貿易變便宜。

    Cheap water transport水運便宜→Lower trade costs貿易成本降低→Larger markets市場變大→More specialization and innovation更多分工與創新→Growth成長

    This is Adam Smith's argument. MRU's map of GDP per square kilometer shows output piled on US coasts and the Great Lakes rivers, Western Europe, Japan, China's coast and Australia's coast. Even today, landlocked countries are on average poorer. Africa is enormous, big enough to hold most of the US, China, India and much of Europe, yet Europe has two to three times Africa's coastline. Africa is the most landlocked continent and has the most poor countries.這是亞當斯密的論點。MRU 的「每平方公里 GDP」地圖顯示，產出集中在美國海岸與五大湖區河流、西歐、日本、中國沿海和澳洲沿海。即使今天，內陸國平均仍比較窮。非洲非常大，可以裝下大部分的美國、中國、印度和大半個歐洲，但歐洲的海岸線是非洲的兩到三倍。非洲是最「內陸」的大陸，也是窮國最多的大陸。

    MRU's conclusionMRU 的結論Limit限制
    Being landlocked is a natural tariff天然關稅, a tax on trade. If natural tariffs hurt growth, tariffs set by governments probably do not help either.內陸是一種天然關稅，等於對貿易課稅。如果天然關稅不利成長，政府設的關稅大概也沒有幫助。Fits the standard trade model, where a tariff raises prices and creates deadweight loss. Exceptions argued for specific cases need evidence.符合標準貿易模型：關稅推高價格、造成無謂損失。特定情況的例外主張需要證據。
    Growth is not only about policy; some places have natural blessings.成長不只關乎政策；有些地方得天獨厚。Geography is not destiny: landlocked Switzerland is rich, and rail links can cut transport costs.地理不是命運：內陸的瑞士很富有，鐵路建設也能降低運輸成本。

    Event chain: landlocked → higher transport cost per shipment → supply of imports shifts left, so import prices rise and quantities fall; exporters receive less → less trade and specialization.事件鏈：內陸 → 每批貨運成本較高 → 進口供給左移，進口價格上升、數量下降；出口商收入減少 → 貿易和分工減少。

    12. When the numbers themselves are weak12. 當數字本身就不可靠

    GDP per capita needs good counts of output and people. The poorest countries often have neither.每人 GDP 需要可靠的產出和人口數字，最窮的國家往往兩者都沒有。

    The Central African Republic, the poorest country in MRU's comparison, has no reliable birth and death records or recent census. Researchers led by Les Roberts surveyed households by car, boat, motorcycle and on foot and estimated that nearly 6% of the population died in 2022, mostly from malaria, diarrhea and violence. The UN's estimate was about four times lower, because it projected older survey data forward with models that assume stable conditions. Only the survey rate crossed the emergency threshold of one death per 10,000 people a day, which matters for aid. The CAR government and the UN's chief demographer disputed the study.中非共和國是 MRU 比較中最窮的國家，沒有可靠的出生與死亡紀錄，也沒有近期的人口普查。Les Roberts 等研究人員搭車、坐船、騎摩托車、步行逐戶調查，估計 2022 年有近 6% 的人口死亡，主要死因是瘧疾、腹瀉和暴力。聯合國的估計約低四倍，因為它用舊調查資料加上假設情況穩定的模型往後推。只有調查得出的死亡率超過「每天每萬人 1 人死亡」的緊急門檻，這會影響援助。中非政府和聯合國首席人口學家對這項研究提出質疑。

    Official figures in fragile states can badly undercount suffering, and weak data plus low attention keep crises unseen.脆弱國家的官方數字可能嚴重低估苦難；資料薄弱加上關注不足，讓危機被看不見。

    13. Do not mix these up13. 不要混淆這些概念

    Anything used to make other goods is intermediate.用來生產其他東西的都是中間財。

    Capital goods such as a tractor are finished goods and count.曳引機這類資本財是最終財，要算進去。

    GDP counts what citizens produce anywhere.GDP 算的是本國人民在任何地方的生產。

    That is GNI. GDP counts production inside the borders.那是 GNI。GDP 算的是境內的生產。

    Investment means buying stocks.投資就是買股票。

    In GDP, investment is new capital goods.在 GDP 裡，投資是新的資本財。

    All government spending is G.政府的所有支出都是 G。

    Transfers such as pensions buy nothing and are excluded.退休金等移轉支付沒有購買東西，不計入。

    The US economy grew 55 times since 1950.美國經濟從 1950 年成長了 55 倍。

    That is nominal. Real is about 8 times; per person about 3.6 times.那是名目。實質約 8 倍；每人約 3.6 倍。

    14. Cold call practice14. 課堂口頭問答練習

    What is GDP?什麼是 GDP？

    The market value of all final goods and services produced within a country in a year, for example cars built in Rayong and massages sold in Bangkok this year.一國在一年內生產的所有最終商品與服務的市場價值，例如今年在羅勇製造的汽車和在曼谷賣出的按摩服務。

    Why does a tractor count but a baker's eggs do not?為什麼曳引機要算，烘焙店的蛋卻不算？

    The eggs are used up inside the cake, whose price already includes them. The tractor is a finished capital good that is not resold as part of another product.蛋在做蛋糕時被用掉，蛋糕售價已經包含它。曳引機是最終的資本財，不會變成其他產品再賣一次。

    Why are transfer payments not part of G?為什麼移轉支付不算進 G？

    They buy no output. When recipients spend the money, it is counted as consumption, so counting the transfer too would double count.它們沒有購買任何產出。收到的人花錢時已經算進消費，再算移轉就會重複計算。

    What is the difference between nominal and real GDP?名目 GDP 和實質 GDP 有什麼不同？

    Nominal uses current prices, so it rises with inflation; real holds prices at a base year, so it rises only when output rises.名目用當期價格，會隨物價上漲而增加；實質固定在基期價格，只有產出增加時才會上升。

    Is real GDP per capita a good measure of living standards?每人實質 GDP 是好的生活水準指標嗎？

    It is the best single measure because it correlates with life expectancy, happiness and the HDI, but it misses distribution, leisure and the environment.它是最好的單一指標，因為它和壽命、快樂程度和 HDI 相關，但它漏掉了分配、休閒和環境。

    What is purchasing power parity?什麼是購買力平價？

    Valuing output at a common set of prices so that cross-country comparisons reflect real buying power, since a dollar buys more in poor countries.用同一組價格計算產出，讓跨國比較反映真實購買力，因為一塊錢在窮國能買到更多。

    What is the Balassa-Samuelson effect?什麼是巴拉薩－薩繆爾森效應？

    Services that cannot be traded are cheaper in poorer countries because wages there are low, so the price level is lower; a haircut in India costs under $1.無法交易的服務在較窮的國家比較便宜，因為工資低，所以物價水準較低；在印度理髮不到 1 美元。

    What is the rule of 70?什麼是 70 法則？

    Doubling time is about 70 divided by the growth rate in percent: 2% doubles in about 35 years, 7% in about 10.倍增時間約等於 70 除以成長率（%）：2% 約 35 年翻倍，7% 約 10 年。

    What are MRU's five key institutions for growth?MRU 的五大成長關鍵制度是什麼？

    Property rights, honest government, political stability, a dependable legal system, and competitive and open markets.財產權、誠實的政府、政治穩定、可靠的法律制度，以及競爭開放的市場。

    Why is being landlocked called a natural tariff?為什麼內陸被稱為天然關稅？

    High transport costs act like a tax on every import and export, shrinking markets and specialization.高運輸成本就像對每筆進出口課稅，讓市場和分工縮小。

    15. Answer templates15. 答題模板

    Define GDP定義 GDP

    “GDP is the market value of all final goods and services produced within a country in a year. Final means we skip intermediate inputs to avoid double counting, and produced means resales and transfers do not count.”「GDP 是一國在一年內生產的所有最終商品與服務的市場價值。『最終』代表略過中間投入以避免重複計算；『生產』代表轉售和移轉支付不算。」

    Nominal, real, per capita名目、實質、每人

    “Nominal GDP uses current prices, so it rises with inflation. Real GDP holds prices fixed, so it shows whether we produce more. Dividing by population gives real GDP per capita, a rough measure of average living standards.”「名目 GDP 用當期價格，會隨通膨上升。實質 GDP 固定價格，顯示我們是否真的生產更多。再除以人口，就是每人實質 GDP，可大致衡量平均生活水準。」

    Comparing countries跨國比較

    “Tradable goods cost about the same everywhere because of arbitrage, but services cannot be traded and workers cannot move freely. Poor countries have low wages, so services and the price level are lower. PPP adjusts for that.”「可貿易品因為套利，各地價格差不多；但服務無法交易，勞工也不能自由移動。窮國工資低，所以服務和物價水準較低。PPP 會調整這一點。」

    Growth rates成長率

    “By the rule of 70, 2% growth doubles income every 35 years and 7% every 10. Small differences compound: Japan's fast doubling turned half of Argentina's income in 1950 into twice Argentina's by 2015.”「依 70 法則，2% 成長每 35 年所得翻倍，7% 每 10 年翻倍。小差距會累積：日本快速倍增，讓 1950 年只有阿根廷一半的所得，到 2015 年變成阿根廷的兩倍。」

    Why some countries are rich為什麼有些國家富有

    “The immediate reason is productive workers with more physical capital, human capital and technology, organized by entrepreneurs. The deeper reason is incentives created by institutions: property rights, honest government, stability, dependable courts and open markets. North and South Korea show how different institutions can produce a miracle and a disaster from the same start.”「直接原因是勞工有更多實體資本、人力資本和技術，並由企業家組織。更深的原因是制度創造的誘因：財產權、誠實的政府、穩定、可靠的法院和開放的市場。南北韓說明了同樣的起點，不同的制度可以造成奇蹟與災難。」
