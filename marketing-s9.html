    Distribution, Logistics and Retailing通路、物流與零售

    The best product fails if nobody can find it. This session covers why middlemen exist, when to own the chain yourself, how logistics quietly decides profit, and how retailers use format, service, location and atmosphere to fit the shopper.再好的產品，找不到就賣不出去。本章說明中間商為什麼存在、什麼時候該自己掌握整條鏈、物流如何默默決定利潤，以及零售商如何用業態、服務、地點和氛圍去配合顧客。

    Key idea重點

    The work of distribution cannot be eliminated, only reassigned, so the real question is who does it best. Control what is strategic, outsource the rest, and keep building the brand so retailers cannot cut you out.配銷的工作不能被消除，只能換人做，所以真正的問題是誰做得最好。戰略關鍵的部分自己掌握，其餘外包，並持續建立品牌，讓零售商無法把你下架。

    1. Which P matters most, and why middlemen exist1. 哪個 P 最重要，以及中間商為何存在

    Key idea重點

    "You could have the best product in the world, but if nobody can find it, how are they going to buy it?" An ordinary private-label item still sells if 10,000 shoppers walk past it. The lecturer's view: if there is a most important P, it might be place, especially with the online boom.「你可能有全世界最好的產品，但沒人找得到，他們要怎麼買？」一個普通的自有品牌，只要有一萬個購物者走過，總會有人買。老師的看法：如果有最重要的 P，可能是通路，在網購興起後更是如此。

    Channel functions include information, promotion, contact, matching, negotiation, ownership, transport, finance and risk. The work takes time and money and is best done by specialists. You can remove a middleman, but not the function. Somebody still has to do it.通路功能包括資訊、推廣、接觸、配對、談判、所有權、運輸、融資和風險。這些工作花時間也花錢，最好由專業者來做。你可以去掉中間商，但去不掉功能。總要有人做。

    2. Channel levels and channel conflict2. 通路階層與通路衝突

    Key idea重點

    A level 通路階層 is a layer of middlemen doing some of the work; the number of levels is the channel's length. If every level adds profit, a price rise high in the chain multiplies lower down and the whole channel becomes uncompetitive.通路階層是做部分工作的一層中間商；階層數就是通路的長度。如果每層都加利潤，上游的漲價會在下游被放大，整條通路就失去競爭力。

    Direct直接通路

    Dell in the US sold straight to consumers. In Thailand, mom-and-pop shops even buy from Makro or Big C and resell to us.Dell 在美國直接賣給消費者。在泰國，小雜貨店甚至向 Makro 或 Big C 進貨再賣給我們。

    Indirect間接通路

    Flowers pass grower, wholesale florist and retail florist. Buy cheap at the flower market if you know where it is, or pay for convenience at a mall florist.花卉經過種植者、花卉批發商和零售花店。知道花市在哪就去買便宜的，否則在商場花店付錢買便利。

    3. Vertical integration and vertical marketing systems3. 垂直整合與垂直行銷系統

    Supplier供應商→Manufacturer製造商→Wholesaler批發商→Retailer零售商→Customer顧客

    Moving up the chain means supplying yourself; moving down means distributing yourself.往上游走代表自己供應；往下游走代表自己配銷。

    Key idea重點

    In a conventional channel each member maximizes its own profit, the end price gets too high and "we all lose". A vertical marketing system (VMS) 垂直行銷系統 acts as one unit. Asking everyone to "play nice" will not work, so firms either integrate (own the stages) or dominate (become so big that others follow their rules).傳統通路裡每個成員只顧自己的利潤，最終價格太高，「大家都輸」。垂直行銷系統讓整條通路像一個單位運作。叫大家「好好合作」行不通，所以企業要嘛整合（擁有各階段），要嘛主導（大到別人只能照它的規則）。

    4. Hybrid channels4. 混合通路

    Key idea重點

    A hybrid (multichannel) system 混合通路 uses two or more channels for different segments. It can tailor service and reduce conflict, but it can also create conflict. Check two things: does it meet customer needs, and does it match your image and ability?混合（多通路）系統針對不同區隔使用兩個以上的通路。它能量身提供服務、減少衝突，但也可能製造衝突。要檢查兩件事：是否符合顧客需要？是否符合你的形象和能力？

    6. Logistics: small details, big costs6. 物流：小細節，大成本

    Key idea重點

    Marketing logistics 行銷物流 plans and controls the physical flow of goods from supplies to the customer's payment, affecting both satisfaction and cost. Modern thinking starts with the customer and works back: supply chain management. The goal is a targeted service level at the least cost.行銷物流規劃並控制從原料到顧客付款的實體流動，同時影響滿意度與成本。現代思維從顧客出發往回推：供應鏈管理。目標是以最低成本達到設定的服務水準。

    Spatial空間

    Goods where people want them: nearby stores.在顧客想要的地方：附近的店。

    Temporal時間

    When people want them: 7-Eleven went to 24 hours.在顧客想要的時間：7-Eleven 改成 24 小時。

    Quantity and assortment數量與品項

    Bulk breaking 拆大包: the maker sells 100,000 units, you want one, plus a choice of brands.拆大包：製造商一次賣十萬件，你只要一件，還要能挑品牌。

    Specialization專業化

    Daily contact means knowing what customers want.每天接觸，所以了解顧客要什麼。

    The slide lists eight flows: physical possession, ownership, promotion, negotiation, financing, risk taking, ordering and payment. Its reminder: you cannot eliminate functions.投影片列出八種流程：實體持有、所有權、推廣、談判、融資、風險承擔、訂購和付款。它提醒：功能無法消除。

    Class example課堂例子

    Repacking let 16 servers fit where 10 used to go. Pallets make forklift work easy, but they cost money, break, may not fit every truck, and raise questions such as who pays for a broken one. Saving less than one baht per lid across hundreds of millions of units adds up. A full truck that comes home empty is simple waste.重新包裝後，原本放 10 台伺服器的空間能放 16 台。棧板讓堆高機作業方便，但要花錢、會壞、不一定適合每台貨車，還會引發誰賠壞掉的棧板之類的問題。每個瓶蓋省不到一泰銖，乘上數億件就很可觀。滿載出去、空車回來，就是最簡單的浪費。

    7. Do it yourself or outsource? Dell's model7. 自己做還是外包？Dell 的模式

    Key idea重點

    The lecturer's rule: if it is not that important and someone can do it better, cheaper and faster, hire them (payroll, IT). If it is strategically important (Apple, McDonald's) or nobody else can do it (Coke), consider doing it yourself. MBA advice has swung back and forth on this for decades.老師的原則：不那麼重要、別人又能做得更好更便宜更快，就交給他們（薪資、IT）。如果具戰略重要性（Apple、McDonald's）或沒有別人能做（Coke），就考慮自己做。MBA 對這件事的建議幾十年來來回擺盪。

    How it works · Dell為什麼｜Dell

    Dell sold direct to tech-savvy buyers, built a PC within 14 hours of the order, and beat stores that paid rent, staff and inventory. The risks: returns with no store to put them in, and inventory that loses value fast ("gold goes up and down, but thumb drives only go down"). Margins were only 5–7%, so cash flow was everything: get paid fast, pay suppliers slowly.Dell 直接賣給懂科技的買家，接單後 14 小時內組好電腦，打敗了要付租金、人事和庫存的店家。風險在於：退貨沒有門市可放，庫存又貶值很快（「黃金會漲會跌，隨身碟只會跌」）。毛利只有 5% 到 7%，所以現金流就是一切：收款要快、付款要慢。

    9. Retail formats, service levels and atmosphere9. 零售業態、服務層級與氛圍

    Key idea重點

    Retailing is selling to final consumers for personal use. Thailand moved from about 70% traditional trade (fresh markets, mom-and-pop shops) to about 70% modern trade (supermarkets, hypermarkets, convenience stores) in 15–20 years. Atmospherics 賣場氛圍, meaning color, light, music, smell and materials, should be strategic.零售是賣給最終消費者個人使用。泰國在 15 到 20 年內，從約 70% 傳統通路（傳統市場、小雜貨店）轉為約 70% 現代通路（超市、大賣場、便利商店）。賣場氛圍，也就是顏色、燈光、音樂、氣味和材質，應該有策略。

    How it works · why the fresh market survives為什麼｜傳統市場為何還在

    It is hot, wet and smelly, yet people still go: it is close by, the seafood is still alive, the habit of shopping daily in small amounts dates from before refrigerators, and prices can be about 30% lower, though you must know your stuff. A seller will thump a durian to pick one for tonight; in modern trade, "don't touch the durian!"又熱又濕又有味道，大家還是去：離家近、海鮮還活著、每天少量採買的習慣從沒有冰箱的年代就有了，價格可能便宜約 30%，但你要懂得挑。攤販會敲敲榴槤幫你挑一顆今晚吃的；在現代通路，「不要摸榴槤！」

    Entry: low price, low status, minimal service進入：低價、低地位、最少服務→Trading up: nicer stores, more services, higher prices升級：更好的店面、更多服務、更高價格→Vulnerability: top-heavy, conservative脆弱：頭重腳輕、保守→Attacked by the next low-cost entrant被下一個低成本新進者攻擊

    11. Retailer vs manufacturer power11. 零售商與製造商的權力

    Manufacturer製造商

    "I don't care where you buy, as long as you buy Pantene."「我不在乎你在哪裡買，只要你買 Pantene。」

    Retailer零售商

    "I don't care what brand you buy, as long as you shop in my store."「我不在乎你買什麼品牌，只要你在我店裡買。」

    1Traditional傳統期

    Dominant supplier, weak retailer: P&G could even tell small shops how to lay out the store.供應商強、零售商弱：P&G 甚至能告訴小店怎麼擺設。

    2Conflict衝突期

    Stronger retailer: "You only know your sales; I know the sales of every brand." Retailers push for margin and charge for events and in-store ads.零售商變強：「你只知道你的銷量，我知道每個品牌的銷量。」零售商要求更多毛利，並對活動和店內廣告收費。

    3Cooperation合作期

    An alliance is what we hope for, "but in reality you have a lot of conflict".聯盟是我們希望的，「但現實中衝突很多」。

    Class example課堂例子

    Saha Group, tired of retailer power, helped traditional shops modernize for a fee under the 108 Shop name. Meanwhile retailers are carrying fewer brands: there may be about 50 green teas, but a supermarket stocks only some, and one day a lesser-known brand gets cut, "game over". The lecturer's advice: keep building and supporting your brand.盛泰集團受夠了零售商的強勢，以 108 Shop 的名義收費協助傳統商店現代化。同時零售商販售的品牌越來越少：綠茶可能有 50 種，但超市只放其中一些，總有一天知名度較低的品牌會被下架，「遊戲結束」。老師的建議：持續建立並支持你的品牌。

    Keep separate分清楚Reason原因
    Removing a middleman / removing the function去掉中間商／去掉功能The work still has to be done by someone.工作還是要有人做。
    Integrate / dominate整合／主導Owning the stages versus being big enough to set the rules.擁有各階段，對比大到能定規則。
    Selective / exclusive distribution選擇性配銷／獨家配銷Several chosen dealers versus very few.幾個精選經銷商，對比極少數。
    Outsourcing / doing it yourself外包／自己做Depends on strategic importance and whether anyone else can do it.取決於戰略重要性，以及有沒有別人能做。

    13. Cold call practice13. Cold call 練習

    Why might place be the most important P?為什麼通路可能是最重要的 P？

    A great product that nobody can find will not sell, while an ordinary product seen by enough shoppers will.再好的產品找不到就賣不出去，而普通產品只要被夠多人看到就賣得動。

    How many contacts do 3 makers and 3 customers need, with and without a distributor?3 家製造商和 3 位顧客，有無經銷商各需要幾次接觸？

    Nine without (3 × 3), six with (3 + 3).沒有經銷商 9 次（3 × 3），有經銷商 6 次（3 ＋ 3）。

    What does "not a question of if, but who" mean?「不是要不要，而是誰來做」是什麼意思？

    Channel functions cannot be eliminated; the question is which party performs them best.通路功能無法消除；問題在於誰做得最好。

    What is a vertical marketing system?什麼是垂直行銷系統？

    A channel that acts as one unit, either because one firm owns the stages or because one member is strong enough to set the rules.像一個單位運作的通路，可能是一家企業擁有各階段，或某個成員強到能定規則。

    Why did McDonald's invest in its own potato supply?McDonald's 為什麼投資自己的馬鈴薯供應？

    Fries are its most popular item worldwide, so it cannot risk rivals controlling the supply.薯條是它全球最受歡迎的品項，不能冒著被對手控制供應的風險。

    Give one benefit and one risk of a hybrid channel.說出混合通路的一個好處和一個風險。

    It reaches more segments, but it can create conflict, as when Yakult's door-to-door sellers faced 7-Eleven.它能接觸更多區隔，但可能製造衝突，例如養樂多的挨家挨戶銷售員碰上 7-Eleven。

    Piaget, Lexus and Pepsi: which distribution intensity?Piaget、Lexus 和 Pepsi 各是哪種配銷密度？

    Piaget exclusive, Lexus selective, Pepsi intensive.Piaget 獨家、Lexus 選擇性、Pepsi 密集。

    When should a company outsource logistics?企業什麼時候該把物流外包？

    When the work is not strategic and someone else can do it better, cheaper and faster.當工作不具戰略性，而且別人能做得更好、更便宜、更快時。

    What does Dell's negative cash cycle mean?Dell 的現金週期為負代表什麼？

    Dell collects from customers before it pays suppliers, so suppliers' money funds its operations: 6.8 + 22 − 62 = −33.2 days.Dell 先向顧客收款、後付供應商，所以是供應商的錢在支撐營運：6.8 ＋ 22 − 62 ＝ −33.2 天。

    Why did Toyota's JIT not work the same in the US?為什麼 Toyota 的及時生產在美國效果不同？

    It fit a small country with expensive land; the US has long distances and cheap land.它適合面積小、土地貴的國家；美國距離遠、土地便宜。

    Name the three service levels with one example each.說出三種服務層級，各舉一例。

    Self service (Costco), limited service (Makro), full service (a department store shoe section).自助服務（Costco）、有限服務（Makro）、完整服務（百貨公司鞋區）。

    What is the wheel of retailing?什麼是零售之輪？

    Low-cost entrants trade up over time, become top-heavy and vulnerable, and are attacked by the next low-cost entrant.低成本新進者隨時間升級，變得頭重腳輕又脆弱，再被下一個低成本新進者攻擊。

    What are the three stages of the retailer and supplier relationship?零售商和供應商關係的三個階段是什麼？

    Traditional (supplier dominant), conflict (retailer stronger), and cooperation (an alliance).傳統期（供應商主導）、衝突期（零售商變強）和合作期（結盟）。
