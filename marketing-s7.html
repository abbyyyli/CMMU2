    Products, Brands and Brand Strategy產品、品牌與品牌策略

    Customers buy benefits, and most brands deliver about the same core benefit. This session shows where brands can still differ, why people pay more for them, who owns them, and when a brand name can safely move into new products.顧客買的是利益，而多數品牌提供的核心利益差不多。本章說明品牌還能在哪裡做出差別、人們為何願意多付錢、品牌歸誰所有，以及品牌名稱何時能安全延伸到新產品。

    Key idea重點

    The lecturer applies one test to every product, rename and extension: what benefit does it offer, and why would this customer buy it? If that has no clear answer, the launch is unlikely to last.老師對每個產品、改名和延伸都用同一個檢驗：它提供什麼利益？這位顧客為什麼要買？答不出來，這次上市通常撐不久。

    1. People buy benefits: three product levels1. 顧客買的是利益：產品三層次

    Key idea重點

    A product is anything offered to a market that might satisfy a want or need: goods, services, places, people or ideas. Nobody wants a hammer for its own sake. They want the picture on the wall.產品是任何提供給市場、可能滿足欲望或需要的東西：商品、服務、地點、人或想法。沒有人是為了槌子本身買槌子，而是想把畫掛上牆。

    Level層次Definition定義Hammer example槌子的例子Do not confuse with不要混淆
    Core product 核心產品核心產品The basic benefit or the problem being solved. Any brand delivers it.顧客真正買的基本利益或要解決的問題。任何品牌都能提供。Driving a nail to hang a picture釘釘子來掛畫The physical item實體物品本身
    Actual product 實際產品實際產品The brand-specific, tangible parts: brand name, design, quality, features, packaging.屬於這個品牌、看得到摸得到的部分：品牌名、設計、品質、功能、包裝。Brand, grip, weight, look品牌、握把、重量、外觀The benefit those parts deliver這些部分所帶來的利益
    Augmented product 延伸產品延伸產品Optional extras built around the offer: warranty, delivery, installation, after-sale service.圍繞產品的附加服務，可有可無：保固、配送、安裝、售後服務。Lifetime guarantee, free video tutorials終身保固、免費教學影片Something every customer already expects每位顧客本來就預期會有的東西

    How it works為什麼

    Remove the brand name and most competing products give about the same core benefit. The lecturer calls this a “dirty secret” of marketing. Brands therefore compete through the actual and augmented layers and through what the brand means. Grab grew quickly because it solved a real pain point, getting a taxi at busy times, even though riders pay an extra fee.拿掉品牌名，多數競爭產品提供的核心利益都差不多，老師稱之為行銷的「骯髒小秘密」。所以品牌要靠實際產品、延伸產品和品牌意義來競爭。Grab 成長很快，是因為它解決了真正的痛點：尖峰時段叫不到計程車，即使乘客要多付一筆費用。

    2. Emotion is part of the product2. 情感也是產品的一部分

    Key idea重點

    Products are bundles of benefits, and some of those benefits are emotional. People can form relationships with brands, so changing the actual product can damage the core benefit.產品是一組利益，其中有些是情感上的。人會和品牌建立關係，所以改動實際產品，可能傷到核心利益。

    Class example · Perfume課堂例子｜香水

    Asked what a perfume maker works on first, most people guess the fragrance. The lecturer says companies usually start with the brand and fit the scent later. Names such as Obsession, Poison, Envy and Happy carry the emotion. In production, perfume sits in large drums; the fragile bottle is part of what people pay for.問香水公司先做什麼，多數人猜香味。老師說公司通常先做品牌，香味之後再配。Obsession、Poison、Envy、Happy 這些名字承載情緒。生產時香水裝在大桶裡，易碎的瓶子本身就是顧客付錢買的一部分。

    Class example · New Coke課堂例子｜New Coke

    Pepsi ran blind taste tests in unmarked cups, and slightly more people preferred Pepsi. Coke changed its formula until it also won blind tests, then launched New Coke. Loyal drinkers stockpiled the old product and sent angry calls and letters, and the original returned within months. The tests measured taste correctly but missed the relationship.Pepsi 用沒有標示的杯子做盲測，稍多人選 Pepsi。Coke 不斷改配方直到盲測也能贏，推出 New Coke。忠實顧客囤積舊配方、打電話寫信抗議，幾個月內原版就回來了。盲測正確量到口味，卻漏掉了顧客和品牌的關係。

    How it works為什麼

    In the brain-scan studies the lecturer describes, responses to Coke and Pepsi look similar when the brand is hidden and differ once people are told the brand. Memories and advertising shape the experience, not just the label.老師提到的腦部掃描研究：隱藏品牌時，對 Coke 和 Pepsi 的反應差不多；一旦告知品牌，反應就不同。記憶和廣告會改變體驗本身，不只是改變標籤。

    Pause & think暫停，想一想

    What did the New Coke blind tests measure well, and which business question did they fail to answer?New Coke 的盲測量對了什麼？又沒有回答到哪個商業問題？

    Reasoning推理

    The class tells you where and how to sell. A shopping product usually needs a place to see, try and ask questions; a convenience product needs to be everywhere. The lecturer stresses these are general patterns. Some buyers are highly involved even with cheap items: they walk across a mall for a Coke from one shop, or buy plastic-wrapped magazines from Kinokuniya. Tesla sells cars online, and specialty shops can lose buyers who learn in store and then buy online.類別告訴你要在哪裡賣、怎麼賣。選購品通常需要能看、能試、能問的地方；便利品則要到處買得到。老師強調這只是一般規律：有些人連便宜東西都很講究，會走過整個商場去某家店買可樂，或只在 Kinokuniya 買有塑膠封膜的雜誌。Tesla 在網路上賣車；專門店也可能遇到顧客在店裡學完、再上網購買。

    Class example課堂例子

    Specialty does not require a high price. Office workers who insist on som tam and grilled chicken from Soi Polo treat a cheap meal as a destination. For an unsought product, the first job is awareness: nobody plans a shopping trip for life insurance, so it is sold through calls and visits. The discussion video uses Apple AirTags to ask how a new, unsought product gains awareness and interest.特殊品不一定貴。堅持要去 Soi Polo 吃青木瓜沙拉和烤雞的上班族，把便宜的一餐當成專程去的目的地。非尋求品的第一步是讓人知道：沒有人會計畫去「逛」壽險，所以靠電話和拜訪來賣。討論影片用 Apple AirTag 問：一個新的非尋求品要怎麼建立知名度和興趣？

    4. The “why buy?” test4. 「為什麼買？」檢驗

    Key idea重點

    “It’s new” only drives the first purchase. A product needs a reason people will buy it again.「它是新的」只能換到第一次購買。產品需要讓人願意再買的理由。

    Pepsi BluePepsi Blue

    The lecturer calls it a gimmick. Cola is brown for a reason, the roasted kola nut, and blue has no link to the taste. Likely buyers are existing Pepsi drinkers, so most sales are cannibalized 自家互搶.老師說這是噱頭。可樂是褐色有原因：烘焙過的可樂果；藍色跟味道毫無關係。會買的多半是原本就喝 Pepsi 的人，所以大部分銷售是搶自己的。

    Fanta blueberryFanta 藍莓

    The blue color matches a real flavor, so it has a reason to exist and, in the lecturer’s view, a better chance.藍色對應真實的口味，所以有存在的理由；老師認為它的機會比較大。

    Class example課堂例子

    Foaming shampoo from Organics, then Lux foam products, were fun to try at about twice the price but gave adults no real benefit. In the lecturer’s words, someone invested in the technology and then asked what else it could do. These launches failed. The discussion video asks the same question about tom yum tea, a brown-yogurt drink and Coca-Cola Energy.Organics 泡沫洗髮精和之後的 Lux 泡沫產品，試起來好玩、價格約貴一倍，但對大人沒有真正好處。老師的說法是：有人投資了技術，再回頭想還能拿來做什麼。這些產品都失敗了。討論影片也用同一個問題檢視冬蔭功茶、褐色優格飲和 Coca-Cola Energy。

    中文邏輯新奇只換到第一次試買；要有理由讓顧客買第二次。

    5. Brands and distinctive brand assets5. 品牌與獨特品牌資產

    Key idea重點

    A brand is a name, sign, symbol or design that identifies a seller and separates it from others. The word comes from the branding iron used to mark cattle. The lecturer’s complaint: most companies pick a name and a logo and stop there.品牌是用來辨識賣家、和別人區分的名稱、符號、標誌或設計。這個字來自替牛隻烙印的烙鐵。老師的批評是：大多數公司選好名字和 logo 就停了。

    A distinctive brand asset 獨特品牌資產 is a non-name element that is both unique (consumers link it to the right brand, not a competitor) and famous (most consumers know it). Assets need consistent use and regular reinforcement.獨特品牌資產是品牌名以外的元素，必須同時獨特（顧客會連到正確品牌，而不是對手）和有名（大多數顧客都認得）。資產需要一致使用、持續強化。

    Class example課堂例子

    The lecturer twice walked into AEON thinking it was his SCB branch. That shows the risk of a shared color and raises a bigger question: do people choose a bank by color, or by interest rates and their employer’s payroll choice? You cannot own a color, but you can use one consistently, as Tiffany does with its blue. Copycats such as Gookie beside Pocky, and a Melbourne restaurant named Ohina whose font reads like “China” while it sells Japanese food, show how names and fonts can confuse.老師兩次走進 AEON，以為是自己的 SCB 分行。這說明共用顏色的風險，也帶出更大的問題：大家選銀行是看顏色，還是看利率和公司指定的薪轉銀行？顏色無法擁有，但可以一致使用，就像 Tiffany 的藍。Pocky 旁邊的 Gookie 這類模仿品，以及墨爾本一家叫 Ohina、字體看起來像「China」卻賣日本料理的餐廳，都顯示名稱和字體可能讓人混淆。

    Reasoning推理

    If you use a character, make it yours: a standard elephant, tiger or crocodile is shared, so give it features no one else has. The discussion video makes the business point with an iPad beside a Galaxy Tab: when products work in similar ways, the brand, logo and recognizable elements do the differentiating.如果要用角色，就做成自己的：一般的大象、老虎、鱷魚是大家共用的，要加上別人沒有的特徵。討論影片把 iPad 和 Galaxy Tab 放在一起說明：產品功能相近時，靠品牌、logo 和可辨識元素來做出差別。

    6. Why customers pay more for brands6. 顧客為何願意為品牌多付錢

    Key idea重點

    A Muji bag and a Louis Vuitton duffel both carry things. The large price gap comes from what the brand adds.     Brand identity and brand image品牌識別與品牌形象

    Brand identity品牌識別

    What the company sets out: personality, values, how the brand looks and speaks.公司設定的部分：個性、價值、品牌的外觀與說話方式。

    Brand image品牌形象

    What customers actually perceive. Identity drives image over time.顧客實際感受到的。識別會隨時間塑造形象。

    Class example課堂例子

    Staff change, owners retire and companies are sold, so written values keep the brand consistent and guide choices about sponsorships and partners. If Malee is “the fruit expert,” a mushroom product is hard to justify.     7. Positioning, repositioning and rebranding7. 定位、重新定位與品牌更名

    Key idea重點

    Positioning is a planned brand meaning, communicated consistently, that rivals cannot easily copy. The lecturer says positioning, more than segmentation, is really about branding.定位是事先規劃、持續一致溝通、對手不容易複製的品牌意義。老師說，比起市場區隔，定位才真正是品牌的核心。

    Reasoning推理

    Avoid over-positioning 定位過窄: tying the name to one category limits where the brand can go. Starbucks dropped “Coffee” from its logo and Apple dropped “Computer.” A Thai DVD-player ad promising “it will not skip” positions on a basic requirement; any player should do that, though the message may still reassure a first-time buyer.避免定位過窄：名字綁死在一個品類，品牌就走不遠。Starbucks 把「Coffee」從 logo 拿掉，Apple 拿掉「Computer」。泰國一支 DVD 播放器廣告主打「不會跳針」，是在基本要求上做定位；任何播放器都該做到，不過對第一次買的人也許還是有安心作用。

    8. Brand equity: a useful idea, a weak measure8. 品牌權益：有用的概念，難以衡量

    Key idea重點

    Brand equity 品牌權益 is the value a brand adds beyond the product, similar to goodwill in accounting. The slide cites Kapferer: companies can lose money yet still be valued highly for the brands they own.品牌權益是品牌在產品之外多出來的價值，類似會計上的商譽。投影片引用 Kapferer：公司可能虧錢，卻因擁有的品牌而有很高的估值。

    Class example課堂例子

    The lecturer’s test: two identical landlines, 100 baht a month from TOT or 120 baht from True. Paying the extra 20 baht only for the name would be brand equity. He says businesses usually sell for about 5–7 times annual earnings, and recalls Nestlé paying about 26 times for the KitKat business.老師的檢驗：兩條一模一樣的市話，TOT 每月 100 泰銖、True 每月 120 泰銖。只為了名字多付 20 泰銖，就是品牌權益。BMW 和 Toyota 不一樣：兩者承諾的工程與品質不同，所以價差是定位。他說企業通常以年獲利 5–7 倍出售，並記得 Nestlé 買下 KitKat 業務時付了約 26 倍。

    Pitch to the retailer’s buyer向零售商採購提案→Pay a slotting fee 上架費 to each retailer向每家零售商付上架費→Pay more for better shelf space付更多換好位置→Removed if sales are weak賣不好就下架→Possibly copied by a private label可能被自有品牌複製

    A discussion-video slide adds that Thailand was among the five fastest-growing private-label markets in 2000, yet private labels held only about 1.5% share in 2019, compared with 40–50% in leading markets.     14. Cold call practice14. Cold call 練習

    Why buy a hammer?為什麼買槌子？

    To get the benefit, such as a picture on the wall. People buy what a product does for them, and most brands deliver that same core benefit.為了得到利益，例如把畫掛上牆。人買的是產品能為自己做什麼，而大多數品牌提供的核心利益都一樣。

    What are the core, actual and augmented products of a car?汽車的核心、實際和延伸產品是什麼？

    Core: getting from place to place. Actual: brand, design, engine, features and quality. Augmented: warranty, free servicing, financing and roadside assistance.核心：移動。實際：品牌、設計、引擎、功能、品質。延伸：保固、免費保養、分期方案、道路救援。

    Are convenience products risky purchases?便利品是高風險的購買嗎？

    Usually not. They are cheap and bought often, so involvement is low, although some buyers are particular about where they buy even a cheap item.通常不是。便宜又常買，所以涉入度低；不過有些人連便宜東西在哪買都很講究。

    How does product class affect selling in a shop or online?產品類別如何影響在店面或網路銷售？

    Shopping and specialty products often need places to see, try and ask experts; convenience products need wide availability. Exceptions exist, such as Tesla selling online.選購品和特殊品常需要能看、能試、能問專家的地方；便利品需要廣泛鋪貨。也有例外，例如 Tesla 在網路上賣車。

    Why is Pepsi Blue a gimmick?為什麼 Pepsi Blue 是噱頭？

    Its only reason to buy is novelty, which lasts one purchase. The color has no link to taste, and most buyers would have bought Pepsi anyway.唯一的購買理由是新奇，只撐得過一次購買。顏色和口味無關，而大多數買家本來就會買 Pepsi。

    What makes an element a distinctive brand asset?什麼樣的元素才是獨特品牌資產？

    It is unique to the brand and famous among consumers, and it is used consistently. Shared colors and generic animals fail the uniqueness test.對品牌來說獨一無二、消費者普遍認得，而且一致使用。共用的顏色和一般動物通不過獨特性檢驗。

    Why are people willing to pay more for branded products?為什麼人願意為品牌產品多付錢？

    Brands offer consistency, quality assurance and lower risk, plus status, self-expression and emotional value. Familiarity also increases liking.品牌提供一致性、品質保證和較低風險，還有地位、自我表達和情感價值。熟悉感也會增加好感。

    What is the difference between rebranding and repositioning?品牌更名和重新定位有什麼不同？

    Rebranding changes the name; repositioning changes the image. TelecomAsia became True with little change in service; Prickly Heat tried to look younger without renaming.更名是換名字；重新定位是換形象。TelecomAsia 改名 True，服務沒什麼變；Prickly Heat 沒改名，卻想變年輕。

    What is brand equity in the landline example?市話例子裡的品牌權益是什麼？

    If two landlines are identical and customers pay 120 baht to True instead of 100 baht to TOT, the extra 20 baht paid for the name is brand equity.兩條市話完全一樣，顧客卻願意付 True 120 泰銖、而不是付 TOT 100 泰銖，多付給名字的 20 泰銖就是品牌權益。

    Why do retailers create private labels?零售商為什麼要做自有品牌？

    They can earn higher margins and offer something other stores do not have. Their control of the shelf, fees and sales data also gives them bargaining power.能賺更高毛利，也能提供別家店沒有的商品。掌握貨架、上架費和銷售數據，也讓他們更有議價力。

    What is the difference between a line extension and a brand extension?產品線延伸和品牌延伸有什麼不同？

    A line extension stays in the same category with a new flavor or size; a brand extension moves the name into a new category.產品線延伸留在同一品類，推新口味或規格；品牌延伸是把名字帶到新品類。

    Why do most line extensions fail?為什麼大多數產品線延伸會失敗？

    They are barely different, face heavy competition and take sales from the original product, which usually stays the best seller.差異太小、競爭激烈，又搶走原版產品的銷售，而原版通常仍最暢銷。
