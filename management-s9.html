    1. Leadership and management1. 領導與管理

    The lecture calls them “distinct, yet complementary.” They are two different jobs.課程說兩者「不同但互補」，是兩份不同的工作。

    Management

    Controls complexity. Keeps things running with plans, budgets and checks.控制複雜度，用計畫、預算和檢查讓事情順利運作。

    Leadership

    Produces useful change. Gives people a direction and gets them to commit to it.帶來有用的改變，給大家方向，並讓大家願意投入。

    Healthy organizations need both.健康的組織兩者都需要。

    2. Trait theory2. 特質理論

    Traits are personal qualities that set leaders apart from followers. This theory asks what kind of person becomes a leader.特質是讓領導者和追隨者不同的個人特徵。這個理論問的是：什麼樣的人會成為領導者。

    Trait特質In plain words白話說
    Intelligence (EQ and IQ)Thinking ability and emotional skill思考能力與情緒能力
    Dominance or assertivenessWilling to take charge and speak up願意主導、敢說話
    Self-confidenceTrusts their own judgment相信自己的判斷
    Energy and activityHas drive and stamina有衝勁、有耐力
    Task-relevant knowledgeKnows the work they lead懂自己帶領的工作

    Traits describe the leader. They do not say what to do in a given situation. The next theories fill that gap.特質只描述領導者，沒有告訴你在某個情境該怎麼做。後面的理論補上了這一塊。

    連到前面S5 的 EQ 在這裡是領導特質之一。

    3. Behavioral theories3. 行為理論

    The Ohio State studies looked at what leaders do. They found two kinds of behavior.Ohio State 研究看的是領導者做了什麼，找出兩種行為。

    Behavior行為What it is它是什麼Ask yourself問自己Do not confuse with不要混淆成
    Consideration 關懷Building mutual trust, respect and relationships建立互信、尊重和關係Does the leader build trust?領導者有在建立信任嗎？Lowering standards to keep people happy為了讓大家開心而降低標準
    Initiating structure 定結構Setting goals, defining tasks and checking the work設定目標、界定任務、檢查工作Does the leader organize the work?領導者有在安排工作嗎？Watching every step all day整天盯著每一個步驟

    Leaders tend to lean toward one. Effective leadership combines both.領導者通常偏向其中一種，但有效的領導兩者結合。

    中文邏輯關懷看「人」，定結構看「事」。到情境領導時，這兩個就是 support 和 task。

    4. Situational leadership4. 情境領導

    There is no single best style. The right style depends on the situation, and above all on the follower.沒有唯一最好的風格。適合的風格看情境，最重要的是看部屬。

    What shapes the situation情境由什麼構成

    Area面向Factors the lecture lists課程列出的因素
    People人The leader, the followers, the leader’s boss, key colleagues領導者、部屬、上司、重要同事
    Organization組織Power of the role, company policies, norms and traditions (culture), constraints職位權力、公司政策、規範與傳統（文化）、限制
    The work工作Job demands, the nature of the task, time to decide工作要求、任務性質、決策時間

    Readiness準備度

    Readiness = ability + willingness, for one specific task.準備度 = 能力 + 意願，針對某一項特定任務。

    Ability 能力

    Can they do it? Look at skill, knowledge, experience with the task, past results and deadlines met.他做得到嗎？看技能、知識、做過這項任務的經驗、過去成果、有沒有準時。

    Willingness 意願

    Do they want to, and do they feel sure? Look at confidence, commitment, interest, initiative and persistence.他想做、有把握嗎？看自信、投入、興趣、主動和堅持。

    The same person can be R4 on one task and R1 on a new one.同一個人在一項任務是 R4，換了新任務可能是 R1。

    Four levels, four styles四個等級，四種風格

    Follower部屬Style風格What the leader does領導者怎麼做
    R1: unable, and unwilling or insecureR1：不會做，也不想做或沒信心S1 Directing 指導Says what to do, how and when. Checks that they understand. Gives regular feedback. Talk is mostly one-way.說清楚做什麼、怎麼做、何時完成，確認聽懂，定期回饋。以單向溝通為主。
    R2: unable, but willing or confidentR2：不會做，但想做或有信心S2 Coaching 教練Still teaches the task and sets deadlines. Also explains why it matters, shows confidence in them and solves some problems together.仍然教任務、定期限；同時說明為什麼重要、表達信心、一起解決部分問題。
    R3: able, but unwilling or insecureR3：會做，但不想做或沒信心S3 Supporting 支持Outlines the task only. Talks through how and when with them. Solves problems together. Gives credit.只概述任務，和他一起討論怎麼做、何時做，一起解決問題，給予肯定。
    R4: able, and willing or confidentR4：會做，也想做S4 Delegating 授權Agrees on goals and a follow-up plan. Steps in only when they cannot solve a problem. Gives credit.講好目標和追蹤計畫，只在他解決不了時介入，給予肯定。

    Task behavior follows ability: the less able, the more direction. Support is highest in the middle, for R2 and R3.任務指導跟著能力走：越不會做，指導越多。支持在中間最高，也就是 R2 和 R3。

    中文邏輯能力決定要不要教，意願決定要不要鼓勵。

    Pairs people mix up容易混淆的組合

    Pair組合How to tell them apart怎麼分
    R2 / R3Both need high support. R2 cannot do the task yet, so the leader still teaches. R3 already can, so the leader stops teaching and encourages instead.兩者都要高支持。R2 還不會，所以要教；R3 已經會了，所以不用教，改成鼓勵。
    S4 / leaving people aloneS4／放著不管S4 still sets goals, follows up and gives credit. Leaving people alone with no follow-up is laissez-faire.S4 仍然定目標、追蹤、給肯定。完全放著不追蹤，是放任型。

    How to answer a situational leadership question情境領導題怎麼答

    1. Name the task.說出是哪一項任務。
    2. Judge ability with evidence.用證據判斷能力。
    3. Judge willingness with evidence.用證據判斷意願。
    4. Name the R level and the matching style.說出 R 等級和對應風格。
    5. Say what the leader should do.說領導者該怎麼做。
    6. Say what comes next. As readiness rises, move one step toward S4. If it drops, move one step back.說下一步：準備度上升就往 S4 走一步，下降就退一步。

    There is no official answer key. These answers follow the model. Scenarios 3, 4 and 6 have other reasonable answers, shown in the explanations.沒有官方答案，這裡依模型判斷。第 3、4、6 題有其他合理答法，寫在解說裡。

    5. Transactional leadership5. 交易型領導

    Leadership as a trade: “what I give you in return for what you’ll do for me.” The leader directs and controls to reach the organization’s goals.把領導當成交換：「你為我做事，我給你回報。」領導者透過指揮和控制來達成組織目標。

    Form形式What it looks like看起來像什麼Example例子
    Contingent reward 依績效給獎勵Rewards are tied to performance獎勵跟績效掛鉤A bonus for hitting the monthly target達成月目標就發獎金
    Management by exception, activeWatches closely and tracks mistakes密切監看、記錄錯誤Checking every report for errors每份報告都檢查錯誤
    Management by exception, passiveWaits until a problem is reported, then steps in等問題被報上來才介入Acting only after a customer complains客戶抱怨了才處理

    6. Transformational leadership6. 轉換型領導

    Leaders who grow their people, help them see old problems in new ways, and inspire them to give more than expected.培養部屬、幫他們用新方式看舊問題，並激勵他們付出超出預期的領導者。

    Dimension構面What it is它是什麼Example例子Do not confuse with不要混淆成
    Inspirational motivation 激勵鼓舞Gives people an attractive vision of the future給大家一個吸引人的未來願景“We make the parts no one else can make.”「我們做別人做不出來的零件。」A sales target with no larger purpose沒有更大目的的業績目標
    Idealized influence 理想化影響Acts the way they want others to act, and earns respect以身作則，因此贏得尊敬Follows the safety rules even when it costs time就算花時間也照安全規定做Being famous or popular有名或受歡迎
    Intellectual stimulation 智力啟發Gets people to rethink problems and build their own skills讓人重新思考問題、培養自己的能力“How would you fix this old delivery problem?”「這個老問題你會怎麼解決？」Asking for ideas, then ignoring them問了意見卻不採用
    Individualized consideration 個別關懷Coaches each person based on their own needs依每個人的需要個別帶A monthly growth talk with each person每月和每個人談成長Ohio State consideration, which builds trust with the whole groupOhio State 的關懷，那是和整個團隊建立信任

    The lecture groups the first two under “charisma.” Together the four are often called the four I’s.課程把前兩個歸在「魅力」底下。四個合起來常稱為「四個 I」。

    Transactional交易型Transformational轉換型
    The deal關係Work in exchange for rewards用工作換獎勵Growth in exchange for commitment用成長換投入
    Effort付出What was agreed約定好的More than expected, often more than they thought possible超出預期，常常超出自己以為做得到的
    Main tools主要工具Rewards and monitoring獎勵與監控Vision, role modeling, new ideas, coaching願景、以身作則、新想法、教練

    Long-term success may need both.長期成功可能兩者都需要。

    7. The full-range leadership model7. 全範圍領導模型

    All the styles on one chart. The more active the leader, and the more they develop people, the more effective they are.所有風格放在一張圖上。越主動、越培養人，就越有效。

    Laissez-faire means avoiding decisions and not leading at all. The slides show it but do not define it. The chart has one box for management by exception, covering both the active and passive forms.放任型是迴避決策、完全不領導。投影片有畫但沒定義。圖上例外管理只畫一格，主動和被動都包含在內。

    8. What makes a vision work8. 怎樣的願景才有用

    Kantabutra’s research: people commit only when they know where the organization is going. A clear vision also gets different groups pulling in the same direction.Kantabutra 的研究：大家要先知道組織往哪裡走，才會投入。清楚的願景也能讓不同群體往同一個方向努力。

    Attribute屬性In plain words白話說Ask yourself問自己
    ConcisenessShort短Can people remember it?大家記得住嗎？
    ClarityEasy to understand容易懂Does everyone read it the same way?每個人理解都一樣嗎？
    Future orientationAbout the long-term future講長期的未來Does it say where we want to be?有說我們想去哪裡嗎？
    StabilityDoes not change often不常改Will it still hold in five years?五年後還適用嗎？
    ChallengeHard to reach不容易達到Does it make people stretch?會讓人想突破嗎？
    AbstractnessA broad direction, not one target是大方向，不是單一目標Is it bigger than one number?比一個數字更大嗎？
    Ability to inspireMakes people want to work for it讓人想為它努力Does it give people a reason to care?有給大家在乎的理由嗎？

    Source: Kantabutra and Avery (2010).來源：Kantabutra and Avery（2010）。

    Weak弱

    “Increase sales by 8% next year.” A one-year number. It is not about the long-term future, not a broad direction, and not inspiring.「明年營收成長 8%。」這是一年期的數字：沒有長期、不是大方向、也不激勵人。

    Stronger較好

    “Make the parts no one else can make.” Short, clear, long-term, hard to reach and broad. The 8% can sit under it as a goal.「做出別人做不出來的零件。」短、清楚、長期、有挑戰、是大方向。8% 可以當它底下的目標。

    Pause & think暫停，想一想

    A vision says “Be number one in Asia.” Which of the seven attributes does it meet, and which might it miss?某個願景寫「成為亞洲第一」。它符合七個屬性中的哪些？可能缺了哪些？

    Who誰What the case says個案怎麼說
    The company公司Makes small, precise plastic parts for medical and photo equipment. Its molds are hard to design, so it has few competitors. Known for quality.為醫療和攝影器材做精密的小塑膠零件。模具很難設計，競爭者少，以品質聞名。

    Joe CaseyRan tool and die. Focused on quality and let workers decide how to do their work.原本管模具部門。重視品質，讓工人自己決定做法。

    Issue statement問題陳述

    Precision Plastics put a directing, transactional supervisor in charge of R4 experts. His close control works for routine molding, but it tells skilled tool and die workers they are not trusted. Their willingness is falling, work is late and one expert has quit. That puts the company’s core skill at risk. The molding department still has a safety and turnover problem that close control alone has not fixed.Precision Plastics 讓一位指導型、交易型的主管去管 R4 的專家。他的緊盯對例行的成型工作有效，卻讓技術純熟的模具工覺得不被信任。他們的意願下降、進度落後，已有一位專家離職，公司最核心的技術因此受到威脅。成型部門的安全和流動率問題，光靠緊盯也還沒解決。

    Questions the professor may ask老師可能追問

    Why not just train Bill to delegate?為什麼不直接訓練 Bill 授權？

    He enjoys close control and does not know the craft. Training takes time, and skilled workers are already leaving. A new lead is faster. Coaching Bill still helps in molding.他喜歡緊盯，又不懂這門手藝。訓練要時間，而技術工人已經在流失，換人比較快。教練 Bill 對成型部門仍然有幫助。

    Are the molding workers R1 or R3?成型機操作員是 R1 還是 R3？

    Mostly R1 on safety. Their willingness is low, and high turnover means many are new. A few experienced ones may be R3.在安全上大多是 R1：意願低，流動率高代表很多是新手。少數資深的可能是 R3。

    Is standardization wrong?標準化錯了嗎？

    Not in itself. Forcing it without knowing the craft is the problem. The workers should lead it.標準化本身沒錯，錯在不懂手藝就硬推。應該由工人主導。

    The table above shows which framework each action uses. Below are extra, more practical steps for each framework. Numbers in brackets point to the actions above.上面的表格標出每項建議用到的框架。下面是各框架額外、更實際的做法，括號裡的數字對應上面的建議。

    Trait theory特質理論

    • Ask the senior workers to suggest candidates for the new lead. People they already respect are easier to follow. [1]請資深工人推薦新組長人選。大家本來就尊敬的人，比較容易被接受。［1］
    • Make task knowledge a requirement for future supervisor roles, so the next promotion does not repeat this problem.以後選主管，把任務知識列為條件，避免下次升遷又出同樣的問題。

    Limit: It says who to choose, not how that person should lead.限制：它只說選誰，沒說選了之後怎麼帶。

    Behavioral theory行為理論

    • Tool and die: keep only quality standards and delivery dates as structure. Hold a short one-to-one with each worker every two weeks, and ask before changing how work is done. [2]模具部門：結構只保留品質標準和交期。每兩週和每位工人簡短個別談，要改做法前先問他們。［2］
    • Molding: the current structure fits. Turnover is high, so a little more consideration may help, such as learning names and thanking people for safe shifts.成型部門：現有的結構合適。流動率高，多一點關懷可能有幫助，例如記得名字、謝謝安全完成的班次。

    Limit: It cannot say how much of each behavior a group needs. Situational leadership adds that.限制：它說不出每個群體各需要多少，這正是情境領導補上的部分。

    Situational leadership (main lens)情境領導（主框架）

    • Tool and die timeline: S3 in the first month, moving toward S4 in months two and three, then full S4. A short weekly status update serves as the follow-up plan. [2]模具部門時程：第一個月用 S3，第二、三個月走向 S4，之後完全 S4，以每週簡短進度回報作為追蹤方式。［2］
    • Molding S1 tool: a one-page safety checklist, shown step by step to each new worker and checked daily. Move to S2 once violations fall. [6]成型部門的 S1 工具：一頁安全檢查表，每個新人都逐步示範，每天檢查；違規減少後改用 S2。［6］
    • A readiness check sheet for Bill. For each task he rates ability and willingness, then picks the style. Jim reviews it with him every month. [8]給 Bill 的準備度檢查表：每項任務先評能力和意願，再選風格；Jim 每月和他一起檢討。［8］

    Limit: It depends on a fair judgment of readiness, task by task.限制：要能公平地逐項判斷準備度。

    Transactional leadership交易型領導

    • Molding: a monthly reward or public recognition for each shift with zero safety violations, plus an attendance reward. [7]成型部門：每個零安全違規的班次每月給獎勵或公開表揚，另加出勤獎勵。［7］
    • Tool and die: reward quality and on-time delivery, not speed, so the rewards match what the company sells.模具部門：獎勵品質和準時交付，而不是速度，讓獎勵配合公司真正賣的東西。

    Limit: Rewards buy the agreed performance. The experts lost trust, and money does not fix that.限制：獎勵只換得到約定的表現。專家失去的是信任，錢補不回來。

    Transformational leadership and vision轉換型領導與願景

    • Inspirational motivation: one short vision, such as “Make the parts no one else can make,” checked against the seven attributes. Speed targets sit under it. Show workers which medical instruments use their parts.激勵鼓舞：一句簡短的願景，例如「做出別人做不出來的零件」，並用七個屬性檢查；速度目標放在它底下。讓工人看到零件用在哪些醫療器材上。
    • Idealized influence: the new lead still works on molds and meets the same standards he sets for others. [1]理想化影響：新組長自己也做模具，並達到他要求別人的標準。［1］
    • Intellectual stimulation: a monthly methods meeting where workers compare approaches and test one improvement. [3]智力啟發：每月一次做法討論會，工人比較彼此的做法，挑一項改善來試。［3］
    • Individualized consideration: a development talk with each worker, and each senior worker paired with an apprentice. [4, 5]個別關懷：和每位工人談發展，每位資深工人帶一位學徒。［4、5］

    Limit: It takes time and the right person. It is less direct for routine molding work.限制：需要時間和對的人。對例行的成型工作，效果比較間接。

    For the team activity, which asks for situational leadership, use actions 1, 2, 3, 6 and 8. For the transformational activity, use actions 3, 4 and 5 and the vision. In the final presentation you may combine them: situational as the main lens, transactional rewards for molding safety, transformational ideas for the long term.情境領導的小組活動，用第 1、2、3、6、8 項；轉換型的活動，用第 3、4、5 項和願景。期末報告可以結合：情境領導當主框架，交易型獎勵處理成型部門的安全，轉換型處理長期問題。

    11. Do not mix these up11. 常見錯誤

    Leadership is better than management.領導比管理好。

    They are different jobs. Organizations need both.兩者是不同的工作，組織兩個都需要。

    Traits tell you what a leader should do.特質告訴你領導者該怎麼做。

    Traits describe the person. Situational theory says what to do.特質只描述人，情境理論才告訴你怎麼做。

    R1 followers need lots of support.R1 的部屬需要很多支持。

    They first need clear direction. Support rises at R2.他們先需要清楚的指導，到 R2 支持才提高。

    Transactional leadership is bad.交易型領導不好。

    It works for routine tasks. Long-term success may need both types.它適合例行工作。長期成功可能兩種都需要。

    An abstract vision is a vague one.抽象的願景就是模糊的願景。

    Abstract means a broad direction. It should still be clear.抽象是指大方向，仍然要清楚。

    Bill is a bad leader.Bill 是很差的領導者。

    His style fits one group and not the other. The problem is the match.他的風格適合一群人、不適合另一群人。問題在配合。

    The molding workers are lazy.成型機操作員很懶。

    Only Bill says they lack interest. Use the evidence: safety violations and turnover.說他們沒興趣的只有 Bill。要用證據：違反安全規定、流動率高。

    12. Cold call practice12. 課堂問答練習

    Leadership versus management?領導和管理有什麼不同？

    Management controls complexity. Leadership produces useful change. Organizations need both.管理控制複雜度，領導帶來有用的改變。組織兩者都需要。

    What is trait theory?什麼是特質理論？

    Leaders differ from followers in traits such as intelligence, confidence and task knowledge.領導者在特質上和追隨者不同，例如智力、自信和任務知識。

    What are the two Ohio State behaviors?Ohio State 的兩種行為是什麼？

    Consideration builds trust. Initiating structure organizes the work. Effective leaders combine both.關懷建立信任，定結構安排工作。有效的領導兩者結合。

    What is readiness?什麼是準備度？

    A follower’s ability and willingness to do one specific task.部屬對某一項任務的能力和意願。

    Match R1 to R4 with the styles.把 R1 到 R4 配上風格。

    R1 Directing, R2 Coaching, R3 Supporting, R4 Delegating.R1 指導、R2 教練、R3 支持、R4 授權。

    What is transactional leadership?什麼是交易型領導？

    Leading through exchange: rewards for performance, and stepping in when things go wrong.用交換帶人：依績效給獎勵，出問題時介入。

    Name the four I’s.說出四個 I。

    Idealized influence, inspirational motivation, intellectual stimulation, individualized consideration.理想化影響、激勵鼓舞、智力啟發、個別關懷。

    What does the full-range model show?全範圍模型說明什麼？

    Styles from least to most effective: laissez-faire at the bottom, the four I’s at the top.風格從最無效到最有效：放任在最底，四個 I 在最上。

    Name the seven vision attributes.說出願景的七個屬性。

    Conciseness, clarity, future orientation, stability, challenge, abstractness, ability to inspire.簡潔、清楚、未來導向、穩定、挑戰、抽象、激勵人心。

    What went wrong at Precision Plastics?Precision Plastics 出了什麼問題？

    Bill used S1 on R4 experts. They felt distrusted, slowed down, and one quit.Bill 對 R4 的專家用了 S1。他們覺得不被信任、放慢速度，一人離職。
