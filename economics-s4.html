    Competitive Markets and Profit Maximization競爭市場與利潤最大化

    The market sets the price. A competitive firm only decides how much to produce: it adds units while price covers marginal cost, checks profit with average cost, and checks whether to operate with average variable cost.市場決定價格，競爭廠商只決定生產多少：只要價格足以支付邊際成本就增加產量；用平均總成本判斷有無利潤；用平均變動成本判斷短期是否繼續營運。

    Marginal cost邊際成本 Average cost平均成本 Price = MR價格＝邊際收益 Profit利潤 Loss虧損

    1. From perfect competition to monopoly1. 從完全競爭到獨占

    Markets sit on a spectrum. More competition means firms have less control over price and lower profit.市場處於一條競爭程度的光譜上。競爭越激烈，廠商控制價格的能力越弱，利潤也越低。

    Perfect competition完全競爭
    Monopoly獨占
    More competition, small profit競爭較多、利潤較小Less competition, large profit競爭較少、利潤較大

    2. The competitive firm is a price taker2. 競爭廠商是價格接受者

    Many sellers offer an identical product, so a single firm cannot charge more than the market price 價格接受者. Buyers would simply switch.許多賣家提供相同產品，因此單一廠商無法收取高於市場的價格；買家會轉向其他賣家。

    Market整體市場

    Demand slopes down, supply slopes up. Their crossing sets the price ($50 and 82 million barrels on the slide).需求線向下、供給線向上；交點決定價格（投影片為 50 美元、8,200 萬桶）。

    One firm單一廠商

    Faces a horizontal demand line at the market price: demand for its oil is perfectly elastic 完全彈性. Every extra barrel sells for the same P, so MR = P.廠商在市場價格處面對水平需求線：對它的石油需求具有完全彈性。每多賣一桶仍以同一價格 P 成交，所以 MR＝P。

    3. Revenue, cost and profit3. 收入、成本與利潤

    Total revenue TR = P × q總收益 TR＝價格 P × 產量 q
    Total cost TC = fixed cost FC + variable cost VC
    Profit π = TR − TC = (P − AC) × q
    Marginal revenue MR = ΔTR / Δq = P    Marginal cost MC = ΔTC / Δq
    Concept概念Meaning意義Trap易錯點
    Economic profit經濟利潤Revenue minus explicit costs and opportunity costs 機會成本收入減去顯性成本和機會成本。Usually smaller than accounting profit通常低於會計利潤。
    Zero economic profit經濟利潤為零All costs covered, including the owner's next-best alternative所有成本均獲支付，包括業主放棄的次佳選擇。Not the same as earning nothing不代表完全沒有收入或報酬。
    Fixed cost固定成本Does not change with output in the decision period在決策期間內不隨產量變動。Fixed now is not always sunk forever現在是固定成本，不表示永遠都是沉沒成本。
    Sunk cost 沉沒成本沉沒成本Already spent and cannot be recovered已支出且無法收回。Should not affect decisions from now on不應影響之後的決策。
    Marginal cost邊際成本Extra cost of one more unit多生產一單位增加的成本。Not the same as average cost不同於平均成本。

    Shutdown versus exit暫時停產與長期退出

    Short-run shutdown短期停產

    Stop producing for now. Fixed costs like a lease still have to be paid.暫時停止生產；租約等固定成本仍須支付。

    Long-run exit長期退出

    Leave the industry once all costs become avoidable. Persistent losses push firms out; profits pull new firms in.當所有成本都可避免時離開該產業。持續虧損使廠商退出；利潤則吸引新廠商進入。

    With free entry and exit, long-run economic profit in a competitive market tends toward zero. This is a model result; protected or differentiated markets may not behave this way.若可自由進出，競爭市場的長期經濟利潤趨近零。這是模型的結論；受到保護或產品有所差異的市場未必如此。

    Market shock or firm shock?市場衝擊，還是單一廠商的衝擊？

    Market demand rises市場需求上升

    Market P rises → the firm's MR = P line shifts up → the firm produces more along its MC curve.市場價格 P 上升 → 廠商的 MR＝P 水平線上移 → 廠商沿 MC 曲線提高產量。

    Industry-wide input cost rises整個產業的投入成本上升

    Every firm's MC shifts up → market supply shifts left → market P rises, Q falls. Solve the market first, then the firm.每家廠商的 MC 上移 → 市場供給左移 → 市場價格 P 上升、交易量 Q 下降。先解整體市場，再看單一廠商。

    6. Case: the Jones Act6. 個案：瓊斯法案

    US law generally reserves cargo shipping between US ports for US-built, US-owned, coastwise-qualified vessels. It is a domestic eligibility rule, not a tariff on imported goods.美國法律原則上保留美國港口間的貨運給美國建造、美國所有且符合沿海航運資格的船舶。這是國內運輸資格規定，不是對進口商品課關稅。

    Fewer eligible ships符合資格的船舶減少→Higher vessel and operating costs, less competition船舶與營運成本上升，競爭減少→Shipping supply shifts left, freight rates rise海運供給左移，運費上漲→Higher delivered costs for downstream firms下游企業的交貨成本上升→Goods prices up, quantity down商品價格上升、交易量下降

    The law restricts who can carry domestic cargo法律限制哪些船舶能承運國內貨物。Verified legal fact (US Maritime Administration)已核實的法律事實（美國海事管理局）。
    The restriction makes shipping more expensive限制使海運成本提高。The video's argument; a plausible mechanism影片的主張；機制合理，但仍需證據。
    The exact cost to households家庭負擔的確切成本。Needs data and a credible comparison需要數據及可信的比較基準。

    7. Common mistakes7. 常見錯誤

    A competitive firm chooses the price where P = MC.競爭廠商選擇 P＝MC 時的價格。

    It takes P as given and chooses q.它接受既定價格 P，選擇產量 q。

    Price taker means the market price never changes.價格接受者表示市場價格永遠不變。

    One firm cannot move it; market-wide shocks can.單一廠商無法左右價格；整體市場的衝擊仍可使價格變動。

    MR = MC means the firm makes a profit.MR＝MC 代表廠商一定獲利。

    Check P against ATC at q*.還要在 q* 處比較 P 與 ATC。

    If P is below ATC, shut down today.P 低於 ATC，就要立刻停產。

    Check AVC. Operating may lose less.還要比較 AVC；繼續營運可能虧得較少。

    Zero economic profit means the owner earns nothing.經濟利潤為零表示業主一毛錢都沒賺。

    Opportunity costs, including a normal return, are covered.機會成本已獲支付，其中包含正常報酬。

    P − ATC is total profit.P－ATC 是總利潤。

    It is profit per unit. Multiply by q.這是每單位利潤；還須乘以 q。

    Fixed cost is always sunk.固定成本一定是沉沒成本。

    A lease may become avoidable when it ends.租約到期後，租金可能變成可避免的成本。

    The Jones Act is a tariff.瓊斯法案是一種關稅。

    It restricts which ships may carry domestic cargo.它限制可承運國內貨物的船舶資格。

    8. Cold call practice8. 課堂問答練習

    What is a competitive firm?什麼是競爭廠商？

    A firm too small to influence the market price, selling an identical product. It is a price taker that chooses only its output.規模小到無法影響市場價格，且銷售同質產品的廠商。它是價格接受者，只選擇自己的產量。

    Why is the firm's demand curve horizontal?為什麼單一廠商的需求曲線是水平的？

    It can sell any amount it produces at the market price but nothing above it, because buyers switch to identical sellers.它能以市場價格售出所生產的任何數量，但不能賣得更貴，因為買家可轉向相同產品的其他賣家。

    Why is MR equal to P for a competitive firm?為什麼競爭廠商的 MR 等於 P？

    Each extra unit sells at the same given market price, so it adds exactly P to revenue.每多賣一單位都以相同的既定市場價格成交，因此收入恰好增加 P。

    How does a competitive firm maximize profit?競爭廠商如何使利潤最大化？

    It keeps producing while MR (= P) exceeds MC and stops before the unit whose MC exceeds P.只要 MR（＝P）大於 MC，就繼續生產；在下一單位的 MC 超過 P 前停止。

    Accounting versus economic profit?會計利潤與經濟利潤有何不同？

    Economic profit also subtracts implicit opportunity costs, such as the owner's forgone salary.經濟利潤還扣除隱含的機會成本，例如業主放棄的薪水。

    Does MR = MC guarantee a profit?MR＝MC 是否保證獲利？

    No. Profit depends on P versus ATC at that output.不保證。要看該產量下 P 與 ATC 的比較。

    When should a firm shut down in the short run?廠商何時應在短期停產？

    When price is below minimum average variable cost, so producing cannot cover variable cost.價格低於最低平均變動成本時；此時生產所得連變動成本都無法支付。

    Shutdown versus exit?暫時停產與長期退出有何不同？

    Shutdown is temporary and fixed costs remain. Exit means leaving the industry in the long run.停產是暫時性的，固定成本仍在；退出則是在長期離開該產業。

    Using the slide table, what is the best output at $50?依投影片表格，價格為 50 美元時的最適產量是多少？

    8 barrels. Barrel 8 costs $49 and barrel 9 costs $75. Revenue $400 minus cost $207 gives $193 profit.8 桶。第 8 桶成本 49 美元，第 9 桶成本 75 美元；收入 400 美元減成本 207 美元，利潤為 193 美元。

    What happens if the price rises to $100?如果價格升至 100 美元，會發生什麼？

    Output rises to 9 barrels, because barrel 10 costs $102. Profit becomes $618.產量升至 9 桶，因為第 10 桶成本 102 美元；利潤變成 618 美元。

    Why can long-run economic profit approach zero?為什麼長期經濟利潤可能趨近零？

    With free entry and exit, profits attract new firms and losses push firms out, until economic profit is zero.在自由進出市場下，利潤吸引新廠商，虧損使既有廠商退出，直到經濟利潤為零。
