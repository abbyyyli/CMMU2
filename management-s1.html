    Managing People in Organizations · Session 1組織中的人員管理・第 1 堂

    Working with Personality Types與不同人格類型共事

    Performance is not decided by personality alone. Know your own preferences, see how they create strengths and blind spots, and adapt how you lead, communicate and build teams.績效並非只由人格決定。了解自己的偏好，看見偏好帶來的優勢與盲點，再調整領導、溝通及組建團隊的方式。

    Chapter map章節地圖

    Preference偏好→Strength優勢→Blind spot when overused過度使用時的盲點→Work context工作情境→Adaptation調整與適應→Performance and satisfaction績效與滿意度

    Personality type describes preferences, not abilities. Its management value is adaptation, not labeling.人格類型描述偏好，而非能力。對管理的價值在於調整，而非貼標籤。

    1. What people management is1. 什麼是人員管理

    How leaders manage people, teams and the organization to achieve goals and results.領導者如何管理人員、團隊與組織，以達成目標與成果。

    Internal indicators內部指標

    Staff turnover 離職率, satisfaction, morale 士氣, absenteeism 缺勤.員工流動、滿意度、士氣、缺勤。

    Outcome indicators成果指標

    Productivity and performance, profit, survival, customer satisfaction.生產力與績效、利潤、存續、顧客滿意度。

    People Management Performance Model人員管理績效模型

    Leader: personality, beliefs and values, knowledge and experience領導者：人格、信念與價值觀、知識及經驗People人員Work culture工作文化Work process工作流程
    →
    Performance + satisfaction績效＋滿意度

    All inside the work context: national culture, organization, industry以上都置於工作情境之中：國家文化、組織、產業

    中文邏輯Lek 常遲到，不能直接判定是「人格問題」；leader、team、流程、文化都可能是原因。

    Typical problems from the lecture: Lek often comes to work late; part-time workers are frequently absent; morale is low and nobody does more than asked; Jun keeps rating staff high after being told to raise the standard; a manager cannot get the boss to support a project; conflict slows innovation.課堂中的典型問題：Lek 經常遲到；兼職人員經常缺勤；士氣低落，沒有人願意多做；Jun 已被要求提高標準，卻仍持續給員工高評分；主管無法取得上司對專案的支持；衝突使創新變慢。

    Technical skill技術技能

    Doing the specialist work well.把專業工作做好。

    Conceptual skill概念技能

    Seeing the whole and analyzing problems.看見全局並分析問題。

    Human skill人際技能

    Working with and leading people. Personality type connects mainly here.與人合作並帶領他人。人格類型主要與這項技能相關。

    2. Why study personality at work2. 為何在工作中研究人格

    1

    Start with your own self-awareness.先從認識自己開始。

    2

    Adapt your leadership style to the work context.依工作情境調整領導風格。

    3

    Complement your personal capabilities.補足自己的能力。

    4

    Balance the composition of work teams.平衡工作團隊的組成。

    5

    Adjust yourself to the needs of other people.依他人的需要調整自己。

    Background: Carl Jung; Isabel Briggs Myers (Myers-Briggs Type Indicator, MBTI); David Keirsey. Four dimensions combine into 16 types. The slide calls it the most widely used instrument for selection, career counseling, coaching and team development.背景人物：Carl Jung、Isabel Briggs Myers（Myers-Briggs Type Indicator，MBTI）、David Keirsey。四個維度組合成 16 種類型。投影片將其稱為在甄選、職涯諮詢、教練與團隊發展中最廣泛使用的工具。

    3. The four preference pairs3. 四組偏好

    Pair偏好組合Question核心問題First side第一側Second side第二側
    E / IHow do you interact, and where do you get energy?你如何互動，以及從哪裡獲得能量？Extravert: processes verbally, seeks interaction, action-oriented. "Sociable."外向：透過說話處理想法、尋求互動、行動導向。「喜歡社交」。Introvert: processes internally, values privacy, prefers writing. "Territorial."內向：在內心處理想法、重視私人空間、偏好書寫。「重視個人領域」。
    S / NHow do you gather and give information?你如何蒐集與傳達資訊？Sensing: details, data, here and now, sequential. "Perspiration."實感：細節、資料、當下、依序處理。「靠實做與努力」。Intuitive: patterns, possibilities, abstract, non-linear. "Inspiration."直覺：模式、可能性、抽象、非線性。「靈感」。
    T / FHow do you make decisions?你如何做決定？Thinking: logic, facts, pros and cons, impact on measures. "Objective."思考：邏輯、事實、利弊、對衡量指標的影響。「客觀」。Feeling: personal values, impact on people and relationships. "Subjective."情感：個人價值觀、對人與關係的影響。「主觀」。
    J / PHow do you prefer to live and work?你偏好如何生活與工作？Judging: structure, deadlines, plans, closure. "Settled."判斷：結構、期限、計畫、定案。「確定下來」。Perceiving: flexible, spontaneous, options open. "Open-ended."知覺：彈性、隨機應變、保留選項。「保持開放」。
    Introvert ≠ shy內向 ≠ 害羞

    E/I is about processing and energy, not social skill. An introvert can present very well.E／I 談的是處理方式與能量來源，而非社交技巧。內向者也可以很會簡報。

    Thinking ≠ not caring思考型 ≠ 不在乎他人

    T and F are both rational; they differ in what they weigh first: criteria or people impact.T 與 F 都是理性的，差別在於先衡量什麼：判準，或對人的影響。

    Pause & think暫停，想一想

    One colleague wants a detailed step-by-step plan; another prefers to keep options open as conditions change. Which preference dimension is relevant, and how could a manager combine both contributions without judging competence?一位同事要清楚的逐步計畫；另一位希望情況變化時仍保留選項。這涉及哪個偏好維度？主管如何整合兩者，而不是評斷誰能力較好？

    Preference偏好Potential weakness (lecture)潛在弱點（課堂內容）
    EEasily distracted; may not think things through容易分心；可能未充分思考
    IMay not seek out others' opinions可能不主動徵求他人意見
    SMay lose sight of the big picture可能忽略全局
    NMay ignore details; impatient可能忽略細節；缺乏耐心
    TMay be judgmental; may not support people可能急於評斷；對他人支持不足
    FMay avoid conflict; may struggle with criticism可能迴避衝突；難以面對批評

    PMay delay decisions; lacks follow-through可能拖延決策；後續執行不足

    The problem is usually not the preference itself, but overusing it. Strong N helps strategy but can neglect execution; strong P helps adaptability but can prevent closure.問題通常不是偏好本身，而是過度使用。強 N 有助策略思考，卻可能忽略執行；強 P 有助適應，卻可能遲遲無法定案。

    Reading four letters as one pattern: INTP把四個字母讀成一個整體模式：INTP

    The lecture's likes and dislikes slide fits an INTP profile. Read the letters together, not separately.課堂中喜歡與不喜歡的清單符合 INTP 輪廓。要把四個字母一起理解，而非分開看。

    I · think internallyI・在內心思考→N · find patternsN・尋找模式→T · test logicT・檢驗邏輯→P · keep options openP・保留選項
    Combined strength組合後的優勢

    Understanding complex systems and the logic behind them. Likes: research, writing, designing systems, new methods, variety, independence, control over time.理解複雜系統及其背後邏輯。喜歡：研究、寫作、設計系統、新方法、多樣性、獨立、自主掌控時間。

    Combined risk組合後的風險

    Deep analysis → more alternatives → delayed closure → weak follow-through. Dislikes: interruptions, long meetings, routines, implementing policies, telling staff what to do, people problems.深入分析 → 更多替代方案 → 延遲定案 → 後續執行不足。不喜歡：被打斷、冗長會議、例行工作、執行政策、指派員工工作、人際問題。

    中文邏輯不要寫「INTP 討厭開會，所以不適合當主管」；這是把偏好當能力。要寫偏好可能讓哪些情境比較費力，需要怎麼調整。

    5. Adapting and forming better teams5. 調整自己並組成更好的團隊

    The course does not ask which type is best. It asks you to adjust to the needs of other people.課程不是問哪一型最好，而是要求你依他人的需要調整。

    N

    Generates the big-picture concept.提出整體概念。

    S

    Spots missing operational details.找出遺漏的作業細節。

    T

    Tests logic and trade-offs.檢驗邏輯與取捨。

    F

    Identifies people consequences.辨識對人的影響。

    J

    Creates closure and structure.建立結構並促成定案。

    P

    Keeps flexibility when conditions change.情況改變時保持彈性。

    E

    Brings discussion and action.帶來討論與行動。

    I

    Brings reflection and written depth.帶來反思與深入的書面思考。

    Balance team composition 團隊組成. The useful question is "which perspective is missing from this discussion?", not "who is difficult?" More diversity helps only when the perspectives fit the task and the team can integrate them.平衡團隊組成。有用的問題是「這次討論少了哪種觀點？」，而非「誰很難相處？」只有當不同觀點符合任務需要，而且團隊能整合它們時，多樣性才有幫助。

    6. Do not mix these up6. 不要混淆

    Preference = ability.偏好＝能力。

    People can work against their preference when the context requires it.當情境需要時，人們可以採取不符合平常偏好的做法。

    Quiet means Introvert.安靜就代表內向。

    One visible behavior is not enough evidence.單一可見行為不足以判定。

    Performance problem = personality problem.績效問題＝人格問題。

    Check leader, people, culture, process and context.檢查領導者、人員、文化、流程與情境。

    Every team needs one of each type.每個團隊都需要每種類型各一人。

    Cover the blind spots the task actually needs.補足任務真正需要處理的盲點。

    Some types are better managers.某些類型比較適合當主管。

    Effectiveness comes from adaptation.有效性來自調整與適應。

    F and P are more common in Thailand.泰國的 F、P 比較常見。

    A lecturer's suggestion, not proven data.這是講師的看法，沒有已證實的數據。

    7. Cold call practice7. 課堂口答練習

    An employee often arrives late. Why not call it a personality problem?員工常遲到，為何不能直接歸因於人格問題？

    Performance also depends on the leader, other people, work culture, work process and the wider context.績效也取決於領導者、其他人員、工作文化、工作流程及更廣泛的情境。

    Is the main use of personality awareness labeling or adaptation?認識人格的主要用途是貼標籤，還是調整？

    Adaptation: understanding default preferences so you can adjust how you communicate and work with others.調整：了解預設偏好，才能改變自己與他人溝通和合作的方式。

    An introvert gives excellent presentations. Does that contradict the theory?內向者簡報非常出色，這與理論矛盾嗎？

    No. Introversion is a preference for internal processing and energy source, not a limit on ability.不矛盾。內向是內在處理方式與能量來源的偏好，不是能力上限。

    Looking at the same sales report, what do S and N notice first?看同一份銷售報表，S 與 N 會先注意什麼？

    S notices concrete facts and current data; N notices patterns, implications and possibilities.S 注意具體事實與當前資料；N 注意模式、意涵與可能性。

    How would you adapt to an S, F, J colleague?你會如何調整方式，與 S、F、J 的同事合作？

    Use concrete facts and steps, explain the people impact, and give structure, deadlines, ownership and closure.提供具體事實與步驟，說明對人的影響，並給予結構、期限、權責歸屬與明確定案。

    Why is personality awareness useful for managers?人格覺察為何對主管有用？

    It helps managers recognize their own blind spots and adapt their communication and leadership to different people and work contexts.它能幫助主管看見自己的盲點，並因應不同人員與工作情境，調整溝通及領導方式。
