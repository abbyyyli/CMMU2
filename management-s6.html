    1. Culture and communication context1. 文化與溝通情境

    In Hofstede's definition, culture is collective mental programming learned through shared education and life experience. Family, institutions and repeated experience turn expectations into habits. A new “speak up” policy can therefore arrive faster than employees' willingness to challenge a senior person.依 Hofstede 的定義，文化是透過共同教育與生活經驗學得的集體心智模式。家庭、制度與反覆經驗會使期待成為習慣，因此新的「鼓勵發言」政策可能已經推出，員工卻還沒準備好質疑資深者。

    Use cultural patterns to identify expectations; use actual behavior to explain the person.用文化模式辨認期待，再用實際行為理解個人。

    High context高情境 高情境
    Words話語+Relationship關係+Situation and tone情境與語氣

    Meaning depends heavily on shared context.訊息的意思高度依賴雙方共享的情境。

    Lecture examples: Thai smile and Wai.課堂例子：泰式微笑與合十禮（Wai）。
    Low context低情境 低情境
    Explicit words明確的話語+Clear detail清楚的細節→Message訊息

    More of the meaning is stated directly.更多意思直接透過話語說清楚。

    Lecture example: USA.課堂例子：美國。

    How a message gets misread訊息如何被誤解

    Boss asks主管詢問

    “Any objections?”「有任何異議嗎？」

    →
    Employee responds員工回應

    A smile and silence.微笑，然後保持沉默。

    →
    Boss assumes主管認為

    “Everyone agrees.”「大家都同意。」

    The missing step is checking meaning. Ask for one concern or risk, and offer a private or written reply. Agreement, politeness and hesitation can look similar from the outside.缺少的步驟是確認意思。可以請對方提出一項疑慮或風險，並提供私下或書面回覆的管道。同意、禮貌與猶豫，從外表看來可能很相似。

    中文邏輯訊號不等於完整意思；先確認對方怎麼理解，再把決定說清楚。

    Pause & think

    A company introduces an open-door policy, but employees still hesitate to challenge senior staff. What repeated management actions could make the policy credible, and what evidence would show that speaking up has become safer?公司推出開放溝通政策，員工仍不敢質疑資深者。主管可以持續採取哪些行動，讓政策可信？哪些證據能顯示發言變得更安全？

    2. Five dimensions: definition and application2. 五個構面：定義與應用

    The five main dimensions compare different cultural expectations. The lecture also introduces Indulgence/Restraint: how desires and impulses are regulated.五個主要構面比較不同的文化期待。課堂也介紹「享樂／克制」（Indulgence/Restraint），指對慾望與衝動的調節方式。

    Dimension構面Definition and contrast定義與高低差異Workplace example職場例子Do not confuse with不要混淆
    Power Distance (PD) 權力距離權力距離（PD）Acceptance of differences in authority and status.
    Higher: greater deference to rank.
    Lower: smaller status differences.對權威與地位差異的接受程度。較高：更尊重位階。較低：地位差異較小。    		A junior employee prefers to question a senior manager privately rather than in a meeting.資淺員工偏好私下向資深主管提出質疑，而不是在會議上公開提問。Aggression or technical ability攻擊性或專業技能
    Individualism (IDV) 個人／集體個人主義（IDV）Individual versus group-centered identity and obligations.
    Higher: personal independence and goals.
    Lower: belonging, duties and “we.”以個人或群體為中心的認同與責任。較高：重視個人獨立與目標。較低：重視歸屬、群體義務與「我們」。    		Agree whether recognition should emphasize individual ownership, team success or both.先確認表揚制度要強調個人責任、團隊成果，還是兩者兼顧。Introversion or refusing teamwork內向或拒絕團隊合作
    Uncertainty Avoidance (UA) 不確定性規避不確定性規避（UA）Preference for structure over ambiguity.
    Higher: rules, expertise and predictability.
    Lower: more room for experimentation.相較於模糊，更偏好明確結構的程度。較高：重視規則、專業與可預期性。較低：給予更多試驗空間。    		Before a new process launches, staff ask for clear roles, written procedures and a review point.新流程上線前，員工要求清楚的角色分工、書面程序與檢討時點。Hierarchy or J/P preference階層關係或 J／P 人格偏好
    Masculinity (MAS) 成就／關懷成就導向／關懷導向（MAS）Achievement versus care and quality of life.
    Higher: competition, money and assertiveness.
    Lower: harmony, care and life quality.成就相對於關懷與生活品質的取向。較高：重視競爭、金錢與果斷。較低：重視和諧、關懷與生活品質。    		A reward discussion balances performance targets with employee well-being and relationships.討論獎勵時，平衡績效目標、員工福祉與人際關係。Biological sex, intelligence or EQ生理性別、智力或 EQ
    Time Orientation (LTO) 時間取向時間取向（LTO）Longer-run versus shorter-run focus.
    Higher: long-term orientation on the index.
    Lower: shorter-term priorities.長期相對於短期的關注重點。指數較高：較偏長期導向。指數較低：較重視短期優先事項。    		Clarify how an immediate milestone contributes to a longer development plan.說明眼前的里程碑如何促進較長期的發展計畫。Planning ability or an MBO deadline規劃能力或 MBO 的截止期限
    PD: how is rank accepted?PD：如何接受位階差異？

    Who may challenge whom, and how should disagreement be raised?誰可以質疑誰？不同意見應如何提出？

    UA: how is uncertainty handled?UA：如何面對不確定性？

    How much structure is needed before people can act?採取行動之前，需要多少明確結構？

    4. Conflict: different assumptions4. 對衝突的不同假設

    “Western” assumptions on the slide投影片中的「西方」假設

    Productivity is central. Managed conflict, collaboration and confrontation can improve solutions; face-saving may weaken effectiveness.生產力是核心。妥善管理衝突、合作與正面討論可以改善解方；過度顧全面子可能削弱成效。

    “Thai” assumptions on the slide投影片中的「泰國」假設

    Harmony, face and boss loyalty matter. Relationships and hierarchy may take priority; unspoken conflict can remain even when confrontation is avoided.重視和諧、面子與對主管的忠誠。關係與階層可能被優先考量；即使避免正面衝突，未說出口的分歧仍可能存在。

    Source: Roongrengsuke and Chansuthus (1998), cited on lecture pp.20–21.

    Invite concerns邀請提出疑慮→Offer a suitable channel提供合適管道→Discuss evidence討論證據→Confirm the decision確認決定

    中文邏輯沒有公開衝突，不代表沒有問題；直接討論，也不等於可以羞辱對方。

    Pause & think

    Two departments avoid open confrontation but continue blocking each other’s work. How could the manager preserve face while surfacing the disagreement, correcting errors and agreeing accountability?兩個部門避免公開衝突，卻持續阻礙彼此工作。主管如何兼顧面子，同時找出分歧、修正錯誤並確認責任？

    5. Time orientation and personality5. 時間取向與人格

    Lecture illustration課堂例子Emphasis重點
    Japan: longer term日本：較長期Thrift, future savings, persistence and connections across time節儉、為未來儲蓄、堅持，以及跨越時間的連結。
    USA/Britain/Australia: shorter term美國／英國／澳洲：較短期Task schedules, urgency and getting things done工作時程、急迫感與完成任務。
    Thailand: mixed泰國：混合取向Quick results alongside a longer view in Buddhist thinking追求快速成果，也保有佛教思想中的較長期觀點。

    Long- and short-term tendencies can coexist. The lecture links the longer view to patience, cycles and less emphasis on immediate goals. In practice, connect persistence toward a longer purpose with milestones for current work.長期與短期傾向可以並存。課堂將較長遠的觀點連到耐心、循環，以及較不強調立即目標。實務上，可把長期目的與眼前工作的里程碑連起來。

    Culture and personality operate at different levels. The lecturer suggests I could weaken presumed group orientation and J could strengthen performance emphasis in a Thai example. Use this as an interaction idea: ask how the individual prefers to process information, decide and work with others.文化與人格作用於不同層次。老師以泰國為例，提出 I 偏好可能減弱預設的群體取向，J 偏好可能強化績效重點。可用這種交互作用來提問：對方偏好如何處理資訊、做決定，以及與他人共事？

    中文邏輯人格偏好可能影響文化傾向的表現，但不能用四個字母換算國家構面分數。

    7. Four-boss preparation activity7. 與四種主管共事的準備活動

    Choose one boss and present how a hypothetical “typical Thai” employee should prepare: Chinese, Russian, Swiss or INTJ Thai. Use a relevant dimension, explain a possible mismatch, and ask about actual expectations.選擇一位主管，說明假設的「典型泰國」員工應如何準備：中國、俄羅斯、瑞士，或 INTJ 泰國主管。運用相關構面，解釋可能的落差，並詢問實際期待。

    8. Do not mix these up8. 易混淆概念

    PD 80 means 80% of managers are authoritarian.PD 80 代表八成主管都很專制。

    PD is a country-level index of accepted authority differences, not a percentage or a personal score.PD 是國家層次、反映權威差異接受程度的指數，不是百分比，也不是個人分數。

    High context and high PD describe the same thing.高情境與高權力距離描述的是同一件事。

    Context asks where meaning comes from; PD asks how authority differences are accepted. Reading a smile and challenging a boss are different questions.情境問的是意思從何而來；PD 問的是如何接受權威差異。解讀微笑與能否質疑主管，是不同問題。

    High PD means high UA.PD 高，UA 就一定高。

    PD concerns rank; UA concerns ambiguity. China and Russia both have high PD, but their UA scores are 30 and 95.PD 關心位階，UA 關心模糊性。中國與俄羅斯的 PD 都高，但 UA 分別為 30 與 95。

    Individualism means introversion or refusing teamwork.個人主義就是內向，或不願團隊合作。

    IDV contrasts individual and group obligations. E/I describes personal energy and processing preferences; individual ownership can coexist with teamwork.IDV 比較個人與群體的義務；E／I 描述個人的能量與資訊處理偏好。個人責任與團隊合作可以並存。

    Masculinity measures biological sex or emotional skill.Masculinity 衡量生理性別或情緒能力。

    MAS contrasts achievement and assertiveness with care and quality of life. It is a cultural value orientation, not a gender or EQ test.MAS 比較成就與果斷，相對於關懷與生活品質。它是文化價值取向，不是性別或 EQ 測驗。

    Long-term orientation means better planning.長期導向代表比較會規劃。

    LTO compares time horizons. A short-term deadline is a task requirement; it does not measure a worker’s cultural orientation.LTO 比較時間範圍。短期截止日是工作要求，不能用來衡量員工的文化取向。

    A calm employee agrees and is satisfied.員工保持冷靜，就代表同意而且滿意。

    Calm conduct can coexist with unspoken conflict. Check the employee’s meaning, concerns and work attitudes before treating silence as agreement.冷靜的表現也可能伴隨未說出口的衝突。把沉默當成同意之前，應先確認對方的意思、疑慮與工作態度。

    INTJ identifies a manager’s Hofstede scores.知道主管是 INTJ，就能判定他的 Hofstede 分數。

    INTJ describes individual preferences; country indices describe shared patterns. Use both to frame questions, then check the person’s actual expectations.INTJ 描述個人偏好；國家指數描述群體模式。兩者可用來提出問題，再確認對方的實際期待。

    9. Cold call practice9. 課堂問答練習

    What is culture, and why can it resist change?什麼是文化？為什麼文化不容易改變？

    Collective mental programming shaped by shared learning and experience. Early habits and institutions reinforce expectations.由共同學習與經驗形成的集體心智模式。早期習慣與制度會持續強化既有期待。

    High context versus low context?高情境與低情境有何不同？

    High context relies more on shared knowledge and situational cues; low context communicates more explicitly. Neither is inherently better.高情境較依賴共享知識與情境線索，低情境則把意思表達得更明確，兩者沒有天生優劣。

    Name the five core dimensions in lecture order.依課堂順序說出五個核心構面。

    Power Distance, Individualism/Collectivism, Uncertainty Avoidance, Masculinity/Femininity and Time Orientation. Indulgence/Restraint is additional.權力距離、個人／集體主義、不確定性規避、成就／關懷取向、時間取向；另有享樂／克制構面。

    What do Jai Yen, Jai Ron and Sanuk illustrate?Jai Yen、Jai Ron 與 Sanuk 分別呈現什麼？

    Respectively, restraint and harmony; directness, visible emotion and results; enjoyment and relationships in work/life.依序為克制與和諧；直接、情緒外顯與成果；工作和生活中的樂趣與人際關係。

    Why can clear rules coexist with resistance to restrictions?為什麼歡迎清楚規則，也可能抗拒限制？

    Predictability can be helpful while excessive controls still limit freedom. UA does not imply approval of every rule.可預期性有幫助，但過度控制仍會限制自由。UA 不代表認同每一項規則。

    Can INTJ establish cultural scores or EQ?INTJ 能判定文化分數或 EQ 嗎？

    No. Personality preferences, country indices and emotional skills are different constructs.不能。人格偏好、國家文化指數與情緒能力是不同概念。
