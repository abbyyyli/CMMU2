    TAXI-01Class recollection課堂回憶重建

    Taxi drivers: a daily target or responsive hours?計程車司機：每日目標，還是彈性工時？

    Two taxi drivers charge a fixed fare of THB 50 per completed trip. Each trip serves one customer group. Neither driver can raise the fare on a busy day. Busy days have shorter waits between trips; quiet days have longer waits. Both drivers can choose when to stop working.兩位計程車司機每完成一趟、服務一組客人，固定收取 50 泰銖。兩人都不能在忙日提高車資。忙日兩趟之間等待較短，閒日則較長；兩人都能決定何時下班。

    Driver司機Stopping rule下班規則Resulting schedule時間安排
    A: daily targetA：每日目標Stop after exactly ten trips, whether the day is busy or quiet.不論忙閒，每天剛好完成十趟便下班。Usually finish earlier on busy days and spend longer reaching the target on quiet days.忙日通常較早達標；閒日則花更久時間完成目標。
    B: responsive hoursB：彈性工時Work longer on busy days and leave earlier on quiet days.忙日多工作，閒日提早下班。Adjust hours in response to earning opportunities instead of requiring ten trips.依賺錢機會調整工時，不要求每天完成十趟。

    For the analysis: treat the drivers as otherwise comparable. Trip lengths and direct operating costs are comparable across days. The marginal cost of another working hour includes operating expenses, fatigue and the opportunity cost of leisure, and rises with hours worked. Hold that hourly marginal-cost schedule fixed when comparing busy and quiet days.分析條件：先把兩人視為其他方面相近。各日的車程長度與直接營運成本相近；多工作一小時的邊際成本包含營運支出、疲勞與放棄休息的機會成本，並隨工時上升。比較忙閒時，先固定這條每小時邊際成本線。

    Questions題目

    1. Which stopping rule is more consistent with allocating work to its highest-value opportunities? Explain using marginal revenue, marginal cost and opportunity cost.哪種下班規則較符合把工作配置到價值較高的時段？以 MR、MC 與機會成本解釋。
    2. Does a busy day increase the revenue from one completed trip, the revenue from an additional working hour, or both? Explain the difference.忙日提高的是每完成一趟的收益、每多工作一小時的收益，還是兩者？說明差異。
    3. Draw a diagram with working hours on the horizontal axis. Compare the preferred stopping points on busy and quiet days. Then explain how a diagram measured in trips would differ.以工時為橫軸，畫出忙閒日各自適合停止工作的點，再說明以趟數為橫軸時應如何改畫。
    4. Is a ten-trip target by itself a sufficient reason to stop? Does Driver B's stated rule prove that every chosen shift is exactly optimal?只因達到十趟，就足以決定下班嗎？B 的規則是否足以證明每次工時都剛好最適？
    Reveal solution and diagrams展開解法與圖表

    Reasoning解題邏輯

    Driver B's rule is more consistent with marginal incentives under the stated assumptions. Shorter waits allow more trips per hour. A further hour on a busy day therefore brings more revenue, while its marginal-cost schedule is unchanged in the comparison. Work should continue while the extra benefit exceeds the extra economic cost, stopping at the usual interior point where the two are equal.在上述條件下，B 的規則較符合邊際誘因。等待較短使每小時可完成更多趟，因此忙日多工作一小時帶來較高收益，而比較時的每小時邊際成本線不變。額外利益高於額外經濟成本時仍值得工作；一般內點最適解會在兩者相當時停止。

    The fare is unchanged: MR per trip = P = THB 50. What rises on busy days is revenue per working hour. With trips on the horizontal axis, shorter waits can instead be represented by lower time opportunity cost per trip and a lower effective MC curve. Keep all values on the same unit basis.車資不變：每趟 MR=P=50 泰銖。忙日提高的是每工作一小時的收益。若改以趟數為橫軸，等待縮短可表現為每趟時間機會成本降低，使有效 MC 較低。收益與成本的單位必須一致。

    Compare hours and trips比較工時與趟數

    How to build this graph如何開始畫這張圖
    1. Choose H for working hours. Label the vertical axis revenue/cost per hour, not fare per trip.先選 H 作工時；縱軸放每小時收益／成本，不是每趟車資。
    2. Draw the common rising MC per hour. Add the quiet-day and higher busy-day MR per hour. Project each intersection down to H.畫共同且上升的每小時 MC，再加閒日與較高的忙日每小時 MR；各交點向下投影到 H。
    3. If using Q for trips, keep MR=P=50 horizontal and reflect shorter waits in effective MC per trip. This graph alone does not establish total hours.若用 Q 作趟數，維持水平 MR=P=50，把較短等待反映在每趟有效 MC。這張圖單獨不能決定總工時。

    Model answer. B adjusts work toward days when another hour produces more revenue. A fixed ten-trip target may stop work too early on busy days or prolong it on quiet days. The relevant stopping condition compares the next hour's benefit with its full economic cost. B's broad rule is directionally sensible, but exact optimality still requires information about the marginal schedules and any constraints.作答版本。B 把工作移向多一小時報酬較高的日子。固定十趟可能使忙日過早下班、閒日工作過久。停止條件應比較下一小時的利益與完整經濟成本。B 的方向合理，但是否剛好最適，仍要知道邊際條件與限制。

    Do not mix up: a price-taking driver's horizontal demand with the whole taxi market's demand; more trips with more hours; a target for daily earnings with a marginal stopping rule.不要混淆：個別價格接受者的水平需求與整體市場需求；接更多趟與工作更多小時；每日收入目標與邊際下班條件。

    Cooking oil, fuel incentives and unintended consequences食用油、燃料誘因與非預期效果

    A policy encourages fuel producers to use used cooking oil (UCO) as an input into sustainable aviation fuel (SAF). Some producers buy UCO from overseas, including Malaysia. Fresh palm-based cooking oil in Malaysia is subsidized. A historical example described fresh oil at about US$0.60 per kilogram and UCO at about US$1 per kilogram.某項政策鼓勵燃料廠商把廢食用油 UCO作為永續航空燃料 SAF的原料。有些廠商從馬來西亞等地採購；馬來西亞的新棕櫚食用油受到補貼。相近歷史案例描述的新油價格約為每公斤 0.60 美元，UCO 約每公斤 1 美元。

    The price gap may encourage more genuine collection. It may also encourage suppliers to buy fresh oil and present it as waste oil. The quoted prices describe the case background; do not treat them as guaranteed procurement prices or as the equilibrium values in every diagram.價差可能鼓勵真正的回收，也可能誘使供應者購入新油並以廢油名義出售。這兩個價格用來交代背景，不代表保證收購價，也不是所有圖中的均衡價格。

    Analyze each part separately. Unless a part says otherwise, prices can adjust and other curves are held fixed. For the U.S. parts, distinguish imported UCO feedstock from finished SAF. No named U.S. agency or unlimited fixed-price purchase commitment is assumed.各小題分開分析。除非另有說明，價格可調整，其他曲線固定。美國端須區分進口 UCO 原料與 SAF 成品；不假設特定美國機構或無上限固定價格收購承諾。

    Questions題目

    1. Malaysian UCO: holding its supply curve fixed initially, show what stronger fuel-producer demand does to equilibrium price and quantity.馬來西亞 UCO：先固定供給，畫出燃料廠商需求增加後的均衡價格與交易量。
    2. Malaysian fresh cooking oil: suppose suppliers buy more fresh oil for resale as UCO. First allow the fresh-oil price to adjust. Then consider a separate case in which a binding subsidized retail price is held fixed. Compare the outcomes for local households.馬來西亞新食用油：假設供應者增加購入新油以廢油名義轉售。先分析可自由調價的情況；再分開分析受約束、固定補貼零售價的情況，比較本地家庭的處境。
    3. U.S. feedstock: now hold U.S. UCO demand fixed and suppose more usable UCO is available through imports. Show the supply effect. Separately, what happens in the finished-SAF market if this lowers producers' marginal cost and production can expand?美國原料：固定美國 UCO 需求，假設進口使更多可用 UCO 進入市場，畫供給效果。另問：若因此降低廠商 MC，且能擴產，SAF 成品市場如何變化？
    4. Explain why a larger reported UCO volume need not establish an environmental improvement. Is the US$0.40 price gap automatically the supplier's net profit?為什麼公告的 UCO 數量增加，不足以證明環境改善？0.40 美元價差是否自動等於供應者淨利？
    5. Externality: isolate a baseline fresh-palm-oil production market with no taxes, subsidies or price controls. Assume its demand reflects marginal social benefit and production creates uncompensated environmental damage. Draw MPC, MSC, the market quantity, the socially efficient quantity and deadweight loss.外部性：另取一個沒有稅、補貼或價格管制的基礎棕櫚油生產市場。假設 D 反映社會邊際利益，生產造成未補償的環境損害。畫 MPC、MSC、市場量、社會有效率量與 DWL。
    6. Suggest policy responses to the source-verification problem and the environmental cost. Explain what must be measured to judge whether the policy actually improves welfare.針對來源驗證與環境成本提出政策回應。評估福利是否改善，需要衡量什麼？

    Historical price background: Flying on Frying Oil (2025). Palm oil is a vegetable oil; UCO is an input that must be processed into suitable fuel.歷史價格背景：Flying on Frying Oil（2025）。棕櫚油屬植物油；UCO 是原料，仍須加工成合格燃料。

    Reveal solution and diagrams展開解法與圖表

    1. Identify the market before moving a curve1. 先辨認市場，再移動曲線

    Part小題Curve and mechanism曲線與原因Result, other things equal其他條件不變時的結果
    Malaysian UCO馬來西亞 UCOD shifts right as fuel producers seek more input.燃料廠商增加原料需求，D 右移。Price rises; quantity traded rises.價格上升，交易量增加。
    Fresh oil, flexible price新油，可自由調價Extra purchases for resale shift D right.轉售用途帶來新增購買，D 右移。Price and total production rise; household purchases may fall at the higher price.價格與總生產量增加；較高價格可能使家庭購買減少。
    U.S. UCO feedstock美國 UCO 原料Additional usable imports shift S right, with U.S. D fixed.可用進口原料增加，S 右移，固定美國 D。Price falls relative to before the supply change; quantity rises.相對供給變動前，價格下降、交易量增加。
    Finished SAFSAF 成品Lower marginal production cost shifts S right if capacity can respond.若能擴產，MC 降低使 S 右移。Fuel price falls; fuel quantity rises.燃料價格下降，數量增加。

    Follow one market at a time一次追蹤一個市場

    Fixed subsidized retail price: if the price is held below the new market-clearing level and retail supply cannot meet demand, stronger demand creates or worsens a shortage. Households may face queues, rationing or reduced availability rather than a higher posted price. A subsidy by itself does not always fix the price, so the price-control condition matters.固定的補貼零售價：若售價被維持在新市場出清價以下，而且零售供給無法滿足需求，新增需求會造成或加劇短缺。家庭可能遇到排隊、配給或取得量減少，而不是公告價上漲。補貼本身不必然固定價格，因此價格管制條件很重要。

    When both curves move: demand growth and a later supply response are different changes. If D and S both move right in the same market, quantity rises in the standard model, but the final price depends on their relative shifts. Extra imports do not guarantee a lower price than the original pre-policy price.兩條線都改變時：需求增加與後續供給反應是不同變動。同一市場 D、S 都右移時，標準模型下數量增加，但最終價格取決於相對幅度。進口增加不保證價格低於政策前最初的價格。

    2. Distinguish a profitable private response from a social gain2. 區分私人獲利反應與社會利益

    The US$0.40 gap is a gross price difference, before collection, processing, transport and other costs. Greater reported UCO supply could represent useful recycling, diversion from another use or mislabeling of fresh oil. Only the first label on a shipment cannot establish its lifecycle emissions or net environmental benefit.0.40 美元是尚未扣除收集、處理、運輸等成本的價差。公告 UCO 供給增加可能來自有效回收、從其他用途移轉，也可能來自新油冒充。只看貨物的申報名稱，無法確定全生命週期排放或淨環境利益。

    3. Draw the externality in its own baseline market3. 另畫基礎市場的外部性圖

    With uncompensated environmental damage, MSC exceeds MPC. The uncorrected market chooses D=MPC; the socially efficient quantity satisfies D=MSC. Between these quantities, the extra social cost exceeds the extra social benefit. Shade that difference as DWL. A higher payment to sellers is not, by itself, deadweight loss.若生產有未補償的環境損害，MSC 高於 MPC。未修正市場選 D=MPC，社會有效率量則由 D=MSC 決定。兩者之間的額外單位，其社會成本超過利益；將差額塗為 DWL。付給賣方更多錢，本身不等於無謂損失。

    Private choice and the social benchmark私人選擇與社會基準

    How to build this graph如何開始畫這張圖
    1. Put fresh-oil production Q on the horizontal axis and marginal benefit/cost on the vertical axis. Draw D=MSB and MPC.橫軸放新油產量 Q，縱軸放邊際利益／成本；畫 D=MSB 與 MPC。
    2. Add external damage to MPC to obtain MSC above it. Find D=MPC and D=MSC, then project their quantities to the horizontal axis.把外部損害加到 MPC，得到較高的 MSC。找出 D=MPC 與 D=MSC，分別向下投影產量。
    3. Shade only MSC minus MSB over the excess-output range. If a later question adds a subsidy, draw its private incentive separately and still evaluate true social costs and benefits.只在過量區間塗 MSC 超過 MSB 的部分。若另一題加入補貼，另畫私人誘因，福利仍依真正社會成本與利益判斷。

    4. Match the response to the problem4. 政策要對準問題

    Traceability, testing and credible enforcement address false waste claims. Pollution or carbon pricing addresses unpriced damage; verified lifecycle-based incentives can reward actual emission reductions. Check how fresh-oil subsidies interact with waste-oil rewards. Compare genuine recycling benefits, extra land use and emissions, displaced uses, real processing costs and enforcement costs.來源追溯、檢驗與可信的執行可處理冒充廢油；污染或碳定價可處理未計價的損害，依可驗證生命週期減排設計的誘因則可獎勵真正改善。也要檢查新油補貼如何與廢油獎勵互相作用，並比較真回收利益、新增土地與排放、其他用途移轉、實際加工及執行成本。

    Model answer. Policy can raise UCO demand and its price, but the resulting incentive may draw fresh oil into the waste-oil channel. Analyze each input and output market separately. Assess welfare using real benefits and social costs, not export volume, the waste label or seller profit alone.作答版本。政策可能提高 UCO 需求與價格，卻也可能把新油吸入廢油供應鏈。各原料與成品市場須分開分析；福利應依實際利益與社會成本判斷，不能只看出口量、廢油標籤或賣方利潤。

    BUNDLE-01Practice extension依 S5 考點延伸

    Word and Excel: separate sales or a package?Word 與 Excel：單售，還是整組出售？

    A software seller offers Word and Excel. Buyer A values Word more highly than Excel; Buyer B values Excel more highly than Word. For this question, assume values are additive and the buyers' total willingness to pay for both products is more similar than their valuations of either component. The seller has pricing power, each buyer wants at most one copy of each application, and resale is prevented.某軟體廠商出售 Word 與 Excel。A 買方較重視 Word，B 較重視 Excel。本題假設價值可相加，而且兩人對整組產品的總願付價格，比單項估值更接近。廠商有定價力量，每人每項最多需要一份，且無法轉售。

    The seller can sell the applications separately, offer only a two-product package, or offer both the package and individual applications. Marginal delivery cost is low but need not be zero. No numerical willingness-to-pay or cost schedule is supplied.廠商可選擇單售、只提供兩項套裝，或同時提供套裝與單項。邊際交付成本低，但不必為零。本題沒有提供數值願付價格或成本線。

    Questions題目

    1. Identify pure bundling and mixed bundling. How do they differ from separate sales?辨認純組合與混合組合，說明它們與單售的差異。
    2. Explain why the distribution of valuations could make a bundle attractive to the seller. State the purchase rule for each selling method.說明估值分布為何可能使組合銷售對廠商有吸引力，並寫出各方式的購買判準。
    3. Can the exact profit-maximizing prices be found from the information supplied? What additional evidence is needed?依目前資訊能找出精確的最適價格嗎？還需要什麼資料？
    4. Would higher bundle revenue prove higher profit or higher social welfare? Explain what should be compared and whether a demand/MR/MC graph is necessary here.套裝收益較高，就能證明利潤或福利較高嗎？說明應比較什麼，以及本題是否需要 D、MR、MC 圖。
    Reveal solution and diagrams展開解法與圖表

    Start with the buyer-product table先整理買方與產品

    Valuation估值Buyer AA 買方Buyer BB 買方
    WordWordRelatively high相對較高Relatively low相對較低
    ExcelExcelRelatively low相對較低Relatively high相對較高
    Both together整組Add Word and Excel WTP under the stated assumption.依可相加假設，把兩項 WTP 相加。The two totals are assumed to be relatively close.題目假設兩人的合計估值相對接近。

    Pure bundling offers only the package. Mixed bundling also allows component purchases. For separate sales, compare each component's WTP with its price. For a pure bundle, compare total WTP with the bundle price. Under mixed bundling, buyers choose the available option with the highest nonnegative net benefit, so component and bundle choices must be evaluated together.純組合只提供套裝；混合組合也允許單項購買。單售時逐項比較 WTP 與價格，純組合則比較整組 WTP 與套裝價。混合組合時，買方選可得且淨利益最高、不為負的選項，因此單項與套裝選擇須一起評估。

    Opposite component preferences can make the totals more concentrated. A common package price may then capture sales missed by high component prices. This is a possible mechanism, not a guarantee: valuation levels, cost and the alternatives on offer determine the outcome.相反的單項偏好可能使合計估值更集中，因此同一套裝價可能取得單項高價錯失的交易。這是可能機制，不是保證；結果仍取決於估值高低、成本與可選方案。

    Compare component values and package totals比較單項估值與套裝合計

    No exact optimal price follows from “A prefers Word; B prefers Excel.” Numerical WTP, the distribution or number of buyers, marginal costs, fixed costs relevant to the choice and the permitted selling options are needed. With discrete buyers, a WTP table is usually the clearest start. Use D, MR and MC only if a market demand representation is supplied or can be justified.只知道「A 愛 Word、B 愛 Excel」無法推出精確最適價格。還需數值 WTP、買方分布或人數、邊際成本、影響選擇的固定成本及允許的銷售方式。離散買方通常先用 WTP 表格；有整體需求資料或合理依據時，才使用 D、MR、MC。

    Model answer. Bundling can exploit differences in component valuations when package totals are more similar. But revenue must be reduced by relevant costs to obtain profit. Welfare depends on buyer surplus and real resource use as well as seller gains. Neither the optimal price nor a welfare improvement is established by the qualitative preference pattern alone.作答版本。當整組估值較相近時，組合銷售可利用單項偏好的差異；但收益須扣相關成本才能得到利潤。福利還要看買方剩餘與實際資源使用。只靠偏好的定性描述，無法確定最適價格或福利改善。

    TYING-01Practice extension依 S5 考點延伸

    Equipment and ink: adoption, use and tying設備與墨水：採用、使用與搭售

    A firm sells a printer or copier and requires owners to buy compatible ink or consumables from it. Consider a change to a lower equipment price and an ink price above ink's marginal cost. Users differ in how much they print. Assume the equipment requirement is enforceable and there is no practical independent ink substitute in this scenario.某廠商出售印表機或影印機，並要求持有者購買指定相容墨水或耗材。考慮一項調整：設備價格降低，墨水價格則高於墨水 MC。各使用者印量不同。本題假設這項購買要求能執行，且沒有可行的獨立墨水替代品。

    Questions題目

    3. For an existing owner, hold the printer purchase decision fixed. Draw demand for printing uses, constant marginal resource cost and a per-use price above that cost. Compare actual and efficient usage.對既有持有者，固定已購設備的決策。畫每次列印的需求、固定邊際資源成本，以及高於該成本的每次使用價格，比較實際與有效率用量。

    Reveal solution and diagrams展開解法與圖表

    Separate the adoption margin from the usage margin把採用邊際與使用邊際分開

    Tying links acquisition of the equipment to purchases of a related product. A fixed bundle sells specified components together; metering through ink continues to collect revenue as use grows. Heavy users buy more ink and pay more in total. Higher total payment from heavier use alone does not prove a discriminatory per-unit price.Tying把取得設備與後續相關產品的購買連結。固定套裝一次出售指定組件；透過墨水計量，則會隨持續使用取得收入。重度使用者買更多墨水，總支付較多；單憑用得多、總付款高，並不足以證明每單位價格具有差別取價。

    A lower equipment price can attract new owners, but buyers also consider expected ink spending, product quality and alternatives. At the usage margin, an existing owner compares the value of one more use with the per-use ink charge. If that charge exceeds marginal resource cost, some uses with WTP above MC are excluded.較低設備價可能吸引新持有者，但買方也會考慮預期墨水支出、品質及替代品。對既有持有者而言，會比較多用一次的價值與耗材收費；若收費高於邊際資源成本，就會排除某些 WTP 仍高於 MC 的使用。

    Usage by an existing equipment owner既有設備持有者的使用量

    How to build this graph如何開始畫這張圖
    1. Use printing quantity per existing owner on the horizontal axis and value/cost per use on the vertical axis.橫軸放每位既有持有者的列印用量；縱軸放每次使用的價值／成本。
    2. Draw downward-sloping D=WTP and horizontal MC. Add the higher per-use ink charge; find where D meets each line.畫向下的 D=WTP 與水平 MC；再畫較高的耗材收費，分別找需求線的交點。
    3. Mark the lost uses between the paid-price quantity and the MC-price quantity. Their WTP exceeds resource cost. Keep any additional equipment-adoption benefit outside this single-owner diagram.標出實際付費用量與 MC 定價用量之間被排除的使用，其 WTP 高於資源成本。新增設備採用的利益，另在此單一持有者圖外評估。

    Model answer. Tying can combine a lower entry price with continuing usage-based payments. Equipment adoption may expand, while ink markup reduces use by each existing owner. Overall welfare requires combining adoption, usage, equipment and ink costs, and changes in buyer and seller surplus. The diagram establishes a usage distortion under its assumptions, not the total welfare verdict for the entire arrangement.作答版本。搭售可把較低入門價與持續依用量收費結合。設備採用可能擴大，耗材加價卻會減少既有持有者使用。整體福利須合併採用、用量、設備與耗材成本，以及買賣雙方剩餘。這張圖只指出特定條件下的使用扭曲，不能單獨判定整個安排的福利。
