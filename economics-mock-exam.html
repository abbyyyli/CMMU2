    Flying on Frying Oil: A New Demand for Used Cooking Oil用炸油飛行：廢食用油的新需求

    Governments in the United States and the European Union have strongly encouraged the use of cleaner fuel for airplanes. One way to produce this fuel - called sustainable aviation fuel, or SAF - is by using used cooking oil collected from homes and restaurants. This approach has become popular because it turns waste into something useful while also helping reduce pollution from air travel.美國與歐盟政府大力鼓勵使用較乾淨的航空燃料。製造這種稱為永續航空燃料（SAF）的燃料，其中一個方法，是使用從家庭與餐廳收集的廢食用油。這種方法日益普及，因為它把廢棄物變成有用資源，同時有助減少航空旅行的污染。

    Malaysia has become one of the main suppliers of used cooking oil to international markets. The country is also a major producer of palm oil, which is often used as fresh cooking oil. In Malaysia, a kilogram of fresh cooking oil sells for around $0.60. In comparison, used cooking oil for SAF production can sell for as much as $1.00 per kilogram.馬來西亞已成為國際廢食用油市場的主要供應國之一，也是棕櫚油的重要生產國。棕櫚油經常作為新食用油使用。在馬來西亞，一公斤新食用油約售 0.60 美元；相較之下，用於製造 SAF 的廢食用油每公斤最高可售 1.00 美元。

    This price difference has created an unusual situation. A restaurant or street food vendor in Malaysia can buy fresh oil for $0.60, use it once or twice for frying food, and then sell it as used oil for $1.00. Some sellers may go further by skipping the frying step entirely. Since it is difficult to check how many times oil has been used, some producers are simply selling fresh palm oil as used cooking oil.這個價差產生了不尋常的情況。馬來西亞的餐廳或街頭攤販，可用 0.60 美元購入新油，炸過一兩次食物後，再以 1.00 美元作廢油出售。有些賣方甚至完全略過油炸步驟。由於很難查核油到底用過幾次，有些生產者直接把新棕櫚油當成廢食用油出售。

    This practice has raised concerns. Although the goal of SAF is to reduce environmental harm, the growing demand for oil - whether used or not - is encouraging more palm oil production. To make space for palm oil plantations, forests are often cleared, which leads to deforestation and greater carbon emissions.這種做法引發疑慮。雖然 SAF 的目的是降低環境傷害，對食用油不論新舊的需求增加，卻正鼓勵更多棕櫚油生產。為了開闢種植園，森林經常遭砍伐，造成森林破壞與更多碳排放。

Explain your diagram in clear English.由於很難判斷食用油是用過很多次、只用過幾次，還是完全未使用，餐廳有強烈誘因把輕度使用或全新的食用油作為 SAF 出售。請用供需模型，說明（i）新棕櫚油與（ii）永續航空燃料（SAF）的均衡數量與價格如何改變。清楚標示圖中的新舊均衡數量與價格，並以清楚的英文解釋圖形。

    Q2. Palm oil, external costs and SAF popularity第 2 題：棕櫚油外部成本與 SAF 普及

    Use the same reading passage as Q1.使用與第 1 題相同的閱讀材料。

    To make space for palm oil plantations, forests are often cleared, which leads to deforestation and greater carbon emissions. Use a supply & demand model to show the equilibrium quantity and price of palm oil in the free market and the socially-efficient quantity and price. Then show how the situation would change when SAF becomes increasingly popular.為了開闢棕櫚油種植園，森林經常遭到砍伐，導致森林破壞與更多碳排放。請用供需模型，畫出自由市場中的棕櫚油均衡數量與價格，以及社會有效率的數量與價格，再說明當 SAF 越來越受歡迎時，情況會如何改變。

    Reveal worked explanation展開解題說明

    Free-market choice and the social benchmark自由市場選量與社會基準

    Deforestation and carbon emissions impose costs not fully paid by the oil producer. Thus MSC = MPC + marginal external cost, with MSC above MPC. Assuming no separate consumption externality, demand represents marginal social benefit. The free-market intersection D0=MPC gives Qm0 and Pm0. The social intersection D0=MSC gives Qe0 and Pe0. Qe0 is lower than Qm0; Pe0 is the demand/MSC value at the efficient quantity.森林破壞與碳排放有部分成本未由油品生產者承擔，因此 MSC=MPC＋邊際外部成本，MSC 位於 MPC 上方。若沒有其他消費外部性，D 表示社會邊際利益。自由市場交點 D0=MPC 給 Qm0、Pm0；社會交點 D0=MSC 給 Qe0、Pe0。Qe0 低於 Qm0；Pe0 是有效率產量下 D 與 MSC 的高度。

    Q2: before and after greater SAF popularity第 2 題：SAF 更普及前後

    As SAF becomes more popular, derived demand for palm oil shifts from D0 to D1. Hold the cost curves fixed to isolate that change. Both the market quantity and the socially efficient quantity increase; each remains at its own intersection with MPC or MSC. In this diagram their corresponding prices also rise, and the uncorrected market still overproduces.SAF 越普及，對棕櫚油的衍生需求由 D0 右移到 D1。為了單獨分析此變動，先固定成本線。市場量與社會有效率量都增加，但各自仍由 MPC 或 MSC 與需求的交點決定。圖中對應價格也上升，未修正市場仍過量生產。

    How to build this graph如何開始畫這張圖
    1. Draw palm-oil D0, private supply MPC, and social marginal cost MSC above MPC.先畫棕櫚油 D0、私人供給 MPC，以及較高的 MSC。
    2. Mark the market point M0=(Qm0,Pm0) and efficient point E0=(Qe0,Pe0). Project both quantities and prices to the axes.標市場點 M0=(Qm0,Pm0) 與效率點 E0=(Qe0,Pe0)，把各自數量與價格投影到軸上。
    3. Shift demand right to D1 for greater SAF popularity. Find M1 and E1, using the same MPC and MSC curves.SAF 更普及時，把需求右移為 D1，沿原本的 MPC、MSC 找 M1 與 E1。
    4. Shade MSC minus demand over the excessive market output, separately for each demand state.各需求狀態分開比較，在市場過量區間塗出 MSC 超過 D 的部分。

    Read the welfare area carefully謹慎解讀福利面積

    DWL is the social cost above social benefit on units between Qe and Qm. The diagram assumes marginal external damage rises with production, so greater demand enlarges that loss here. A larger DWL is not a universal result of every demand increase: its change depends on the cost curves and external-damage schedule. More output and more total environmental damage do not, by themselves, prove how the DWL area changes.DWL 是 Qe 與 Qm 之間，社會成本超過社會利益的面積。此圖假設邊際外部損害隨產量增加，因此需求增加會擴大圖中的損失。但不能說每次需求增加，DWL 都一定變大：結果取決於成本線與外部損害。產量及總環境損害增加，本身不足以決定 DWL 面積如何改變。

    Suggested answer. The free market ignores external damage and produces where demand equals MPC, beyond the efficient quantity where demand equals MSC. Greater SAF popularity raises palm-oil demand. Holding costs fixed, both market and efficient quantities move right, with new prices read at their respective intersections. The private market continues to produce units whose social marginal cost exceeds marginal benefit.參考作答。自由市場忽略外部損害，在 D=MPC 生產，超過 D=MSC 所決定的有效率量。SAF 更普及會增加棕櫚油需求；成本不變時，市場量與效率量都右移，價格從各自新交點讀取。私人市場仍生產部分社會邊際成本高於邊際利益的單位。

    Price labels: Pe is the demand/MSC height at Qe, not a price the unregulated market automatically adopts. If explaining implementation with a corrective tax, distinguish the buyer price from the producer price net of tax.價格標記：Pe 是 Qe 下 D=MSC 的高度，不是未管制市場會自動採用的價格。若以修正稅說明如何實現，需區分買方價與生產者稅後實收價。

    Q3. Tim and Todd: monthly profit第 3 題：Tim 與 Todd 的月利潤

    Tim and Todd are motorcycle taxi drivers. They both work in the same area, taking passengers in and out of a small alley. The price for one ride is 50 baht per trip. Both drivers have the same fixed costs (such as their motorcycle), while their variable costs include the cost of gasoline and the time they spend waiting for passengers.Tim 與 Todd 是摩托車計程車司機，在同一地區接送乘客進出一條小巷。每趟車資 50 泰銖。兩人有相同固定成本，例如摩托車；變動成本包括汽油與等候乘客所花的時間。

    Sometimes, many people need rides (we call this a busy day), and drivers can quickly find passengers. On other days, there are few passengers, and drivers must wait a long time to get a customer.有些日子很多人需要搭車，稱為忙日，司機很快能找到乘客；其他日子乘客較少，司機得等很久。

    Tim’s plan is to pick up exactly 10 passengers every day, no matter if the day is busy or slow. After he serves 10 passengers, he goes home. Todd’s plan is to work longer hours on busy days to pick up more passengers and go home early on slow days.Tim 的計畫是不論忙閒，每天剛好載十位乘客，完成後便回家。Todd 則在忙日工作較久、載更多乘客，閒日提早回家。

    Over a period of one month, who do you think will earn more profit - Tim or Todd? Explain your answer using what you have learned about costs, revenue, and how businesses make decisions.以一個月為期間，你認為 Tim 與 Todd 誰會賺得較高利潤？請運用成本、收益與企業決策的概念解釋。

    Reveal worked explanation展開解題說明

    Compare profit over the month, not a daily passenger target比較一個月的利潤，不是每日載客目標

    Todd is expected to earn more economic profit under the usual comparable-cost assumptions. Tim earns 50 × 10 = 500 baht in revenue on each working day, but his waiting time varies. Fixed costs are the same for the two drivers, so they do not explain the profit difference. The important differences are revenue opportunities and variable costs, including the waiting time specified in the question.在一般的成本可比較假設下，預期 Todd 的經濟利潤較高。Tim 每個工作日收益是 50×10=500 泰銖，但等待時間不同。兩人的固定成本相同，因此不能用固定成本解釋利潤差異；關鍵是收益機會與變動成本，包括題目明列的等待時間。

    Busy days reduce waiting cost per passenger. Todd takes more trips when earning the next 50 baht requires less waiting, and avoids prolonged waiting on slow days. Tim may reject profitable additional busy-day trips after his tenth passenger, while persisting with expensive-to-obtain slow-day trips just to meet his target.忙日每位乘客的等待成本較低。Todd 在多賺下一筆 50 泰銖所需等待較短時多載客，閒日則避免長時間等候。Tim 可能在第十位乘客後，放棄仍有利潤的忙日新增客人；也可能只為達標，而在閒日繼續等待成本很高的客人。

    Per trip, MR=P=50 baht. Work is worthwhile while incremental revenue exceeds incremental economic cost. If the horizontal axis is working hours, compare revenue per hour with cost per hour instead: busy days offer more trips per hour. The fare has not risen. The diagrams below support the reasoning; Q3 itself asks for an explanation, not a numerical monthly profit calculation.每趟 MR=P=50 泰銖。新增收益高於新增經濟成本時，繼續工作才有利。若橫軸改為工時，就要比較每小時收益與成本；忙日每小時可完成更多趟，但車資沒有上漲。以下圖形輔助解釋；第 3 題本身要求論述，並未要求計算一個月的數值利潤。

    Q3: match revenue and cost to the same unit第 3 題：收益與成本使用相同單位

    How to build this graph如何開始畫這張圖
    1. For trips Q, draw horizontal MR=P=50. Represent shorter waits with lower effective MC per trip on busy days.用趟數 Q 時，畫水平 MR=P=50，並以忙日較低的每趟有效 MC 反映等待較短。

    Suggested answer. Todd is likely to earn more profit over the month because he adjusts work to marginal earning opportunities. Both receive 50 baht per trip and face the same fixed costs, but busy-day trips involve less waiting. Todd takes more of these relatively low-cost trips and avoids spending excessive time obtaining slow-day customers. Tim targets daily revenue rather than comparing the next trip's revenue with its variable and opportunity cost.參考作答。Todd 較可能在一個月賺得較高利潤，因為他依邊際收益機會調整工作。兩人每趟都收 50 泰銖，固定成本相同，但忙日載客等待較少。Todd 多接這些相對低成本的客人，並避免在閒日花過多時間找客人；Tim 則以每日收益目標代替比較下一趟收益、變動成本與機會成本。

    Limit: the paper gives no day-by-day trip totals or full cost schedules, so it does not establish an exact monthly profit gap or prove every Todd shift is optimal. Do not claim he must have more total monthly revenue just because his allocation is better.限制：題目未提供逐日趟數或完整成本線，因此不能算精確的月利潤差，也不能證明 Todd 每班都最適。不能只因配置較佳，就斷言他的月總收益一定較高。

    A defensible policy prediction一組有依據的政策推論

    Tyler is more likely to encourage learning-oriented AI use. If AI helps students investigate ideas, improve analysis or acquire a useful working skill, allowing it can serve a skill-building objective. He could require students to explain their reasoning, verify AI output and demonstrate what they learned, rather than rewarding an unexamined generated answer.Tyler 較可能鼓勵以學習為目的的 AI 使用。若 AI 幫助探索觀念、改善分析或學會有用的工作技能，允許使用可服務技能養成的目標。他仍可要求學生解釋推理、查核 AI 輸出並展現所學，而不是只交一份未經檢驗的生成答案。

    Alex is more likely to restrict AI when assessment must certify the individual. If AI can produce the assessed work without the student demonstrating the relevant ability or persistence, grades become a noisier signal. Independent exams, oral explanations or process checks can help preserve the credibility of that signal.當評量要證明個人能力時，Alex 較可能限制 AI。若學生不需展現相關能力或毅力，AI 就能產出被評量的成果，成績的信號便較難辨認。獨立作答、口頭說明或過程查核，可協助維持信號可信度。

    Suggested answer. I would predict that Tyler is more willing to permit AI as a tool for developing useful skills, provided students can demonstrate understanding. Alex is more likely to limit AI in assessments intended to distinguish individual competence and effort. The difference follows from what each assessment is meant to achieve. This is a reasoned prediction; the debate does not uniquely determine their AI policies.參考作答。我會預測 Tyler 較願意允許 AI 作為培養實用技能的工具，但仍要求學生證明理解。Alex 則較可能在用來區分個人能力與努力的評量中限制 AI。差異來自評量的目的。這是有理由的預測，辯論本身並不能唯一決定兩人的 AI 政策。

    Do not turn a prediction into a fact. A learning-focused instructor could restrict AI if it replaces practice; a signaling-focused instructor could allow it when competent AI use is itself the assessed skill. A strong answer links a proposed rule to the objective and evidence, rather than assuming either theory automatically means “AI allowed” or “AI banned.”不要把預測寫成既定事實。若 AI 取代必要練習，重視學習的教師也可能限制；若熟練使用 AI 本身就是評量目標，重視信號的教師也可能允許。好的答案應把規則連回目的與證據，不是把任一理論直接等同「允許」或「禁止」。

    BUNDLE-01Practice extension依 S5 考點延伸

    Word and Excel: separate sales or a package?Word 與 Excel：單售，還是整組出售？

    A software seller offers Word and Excel. Buyer A values Word more highly than Excel; Buyer B values Excel more highly than Word. For this question, assume values are additive and the buyers' total willingness to pay for both products is more similar than their valuations of either component. The seller has pricing power, each buyer wants at most one copy of each application, and resale is prevented.某軟體廠商出售 Word 與 Excel。A 買方較重視 Word，B 較重視 Excel。本題假設價值可相加，而且兩人對整組產品的總願付價格，比單項估值更接近。廠商有定價力量，每人每項最多需要一份，且無法轉售。

    The seller can sell the applications separately, offer only a two-product package, or offer both the package and individual applications. Marginal delivery cost is low but need not be zero. No numerical willingness-to-pay or cost schedule is supplied.廠商可選擇單售、只提供兩項套裝，或同時提供套裝與單項。邊際交付成本低，但不必為零。本題沒有提供數值願付價格或成本線。

    Questions題目

    1. Identify pure bundling and mixed bundling. How do they differ from separate sales?辨認純組合與混合組合，說明它們與單售的差異。
    2. Explain why the distribution of valuations could make a bundle attractive to the seller. State the purchase rule for each selling method.說明估值分布為何可能使組合銷售對廠商有吸引力，並寫出各方式的購買判準。
    3. Can the exact profit-maximizing prices be found from the information supplied? What additional evidence is needed?依目前資訊能找出精確的最適價格嗎？還需要什麼資料？
    4. Would higher bundle revenue prove higher profit or higher social welfare? Explain what should be compared and whether a demand/MR/MC graph is necessary here.套裝收益較高，就能證明利潤或福利較高嗎？說明應比較什麼，以及本題是否需要 D、MR、MC 圖。
    Reveal worked explanation展開解題說明

    Start with the buyer-product table先整理買方與產品

    Valuation估值Buyer AA 買方Buyer BB 買方
    WordWordRelatively high相對較高Relatively low相對較低
    ExcelExcelRelatively low相對較低Relatively high相對較高
    Both together整組Add Word and Excel WTP under the stated assumption.依可相加假設，把兩項 WTP 相加。The two totals are assumed to be relatively close.題目假設兩人的合計估值相對接近。

    Pure bundling offers only the package. Mixed bundling also allows component purchases. For separate sales, compare each component's WTP with its price. For a pure bundle, compare total WTP with the bundle price. Under mixed bundling, buyers choose the available option with the highest nonnegative net benefit, so component and bundle choices must be evaluated together.純組合只提供套裝；混合組合也允許單項購買。單售時逐項比較 WTP 與價格，純組合則比較整組 WTP 與套裝價。混合組合時，買方選可得且淨利益最高、不為負的選項，因此單項與套裝選擇須一起評估。

    Opposite component preferences can make the totals more concentrated. A common package price may then capture sales missed by high component prices. This is a possible mechanism, not a guarantee: valuation levels, cost and the alternatives on offer determine the outcome.相反的單項偏好可能使合計估值更集中，因此同一套裝價可能取得單項高價錯失的交易。這是可能機制，不是保證；結果仍取決於估值高低、成本與可選方案。

    Compare component values and package totals比較單項估值與套裝合計

    No exact optimal price follows from “A prefers Word; B prefers Excel.” Numerical WTP, the distribution or number of buyers, marginal costs, fixed costs relevant to the choice and the permitted selling options are needed. With discrete buyers, a WTP table is usually the clearest start. Use D, MR and MC only if a market demand representation is supplied or can be justified.只知道「A 愛 Word、B 愛 Excel」無法推出精確最適價格。還需數值 WTP、買方分布或人數、邊際成本、影響選擇的固定成本及允許的銷售方式。離散買方通常先用 WTP 表格；有整體需求資料或合理依據時，才使用 D、MR、MC。

    Model answer. Bundling can exploit differences in component valuations when package totals are more similar. But revenue must be reduced by relevant costs to obtain profit. Welfare depends on buyer surplus and real resource use as well as seller gains. Neither the optimal price nor a welfare improvement is established by the qualitative preference pattern alone.作答版本。當整組估值較相近時，組合銷售可利用單項偏好的差異；但收益須扣相關成本才能得到利潤。福利還要看買方剩餘與實際資源使用。只靠偏好的定性描述，無法確定最適價格或福利改善。

    TYING-01Practice extension依 S5 考點延伸

    Equipment and ink: adoption, use and tying設備與墨水：採用、使用與搭售

    A firm sells a printer or copier and requires owners to buy compatible ink or consumables from it. Consider a change to a lower equipment price and an ink price above ink's marginal cost. Users differ in how much they print. Assume the equipment requirement is enforceable and there is no practical independent ink substitute in this scenario.某廠商出售印表機或影印機，並要求持有者購買指定相容墨水或耗材。考慮一項調整：設備價格降低，墨水價格則高於墨水 MC。各使用者印量不同。本題假設這項購買要求能執行，且沒有可行的獨立墨水替代品。

    Questions題目

    1. Identify tying and explain how it differs from selling Word and Excel in a fixed package. What role can the ink play as a meter of usage?辨認 tying，說明它與 Word、Excel 固定套裝的差異，以及墨水如何計量使用程度。
    2. What could happen to equipment adoption when the equipment price falls? Why is increased adoption not guaranteed once future ink costs are considered?設備降價可能如何影響採用？把未來墨水成本納入後，為什麼不能保證採用一定增加？
    3. For an existing owner, hold the printer purchase decision fixed. Draw demand for printing uses, constant marginal resource cost and a per-use price above that cost. Compare actual and efficient usage.對既有持有者，固定已購設備的決策。畫每次列印的需求、固定邊際資源成本，以及高於該成本的每次使用價格，比較實際與有效率用量。
    4. Can the seller's gain establish an overall welfare improvement? State which effects would need to be combined.賣方得利就能證明整體福利改善嗎？應合併比較哪些效果？
    Reveal worked explanation展開解題說明

    Separate the adoption margin from the usage margin把採用邊際與使用邊際分開

    Tying links acquisition of the equipment to purchases of a related product. A fixed bundle sells specified components together; metering through ink continues to collect revenue as use grows. Heavy users buy more ink and pay more in total. Higher total payment from heavier use alone does not prove a discriminatory per-unit price.Tying把取得設備與後續相關產品的購買連結。固定套裝一次出售指定組件；透過墨水計量，則會隨持續使用取得收入。重度使用者買更多墨水，總支付較多；單憑用得多、總付款高，並不足以證明每單位價格具有差別取價。

    A lower equipment price can attract new owners, but buyers also consider expected ink spending, product quality and alternatives. At the usage margin, an existing owner compares the value of one more use with the per-use ink charge. If that charge exceeds marginal resource cost, some uses with WTP above MC are excluded.較低設備價可能吸引新持有者，但買方也會考慮預期墨水支出、品質及替代品。對既有持有者而言，會比較多用一次的價值與耗材收費；若收費高於邊際資源成本，就會排除某些 WTP 仍高於 MC 的使用。

    Usage by an existing equipment owner既有設備持有者的使用量

    How to build this graph如何開始畫這張圖
    1. Use printing quantity per existing owner on the horizontal axis and value/cost per use on the vertical axis.橫軸放每位既有持有者的列印用量；縱軸放每次使用的價值／成本。
    2. Draw downward-sloping D=WTP and horizontal MC. Add the higher per-use ink charge; find where D meets each line.畫向下的 D=WTP 與水平 MC；再畫較高的耗材收費，分別找需求線的交點。
    3. Mark the lost uses between the paid-price quantity and the MC-price quantity. Their WTP exceeds resource cost. Keep any additional equipment-adoption benefit outside this single-owner diagram.標出實際付費用量與 MC 定價用量之間被排除的使用，其 WTP 高於資源成本。新增設備採用的利益，另在此單一持有者圖外評估。

    Model answer. Tying can combine a lower entry price with continuing usage-based payments. Equipment adoption may expand, while ink markup reduces use by each existing owner. Overall welfare requires combining adoption, usage, equipment and ink costs, and changes in buyer and seller surplus. The diagram establishes a usage distortion under its assumptions, not the total welfare verdict for the entire arrangement.作答版本。搭售可把較低入門價與持續依用量收費結合。設備採用可能擴大，耗材加價卻會減少既有持有者使用。整體福利須合併採用、用量、設備與耗材成本，以及買賣雙方剩餘。這張圖只指出特定條件下的使用扭曲，不能單獨判定整個安排的福利。
