    Market-oriented mission and brand fit以市場為導向的使命與品牌契合度

    Key idea重點

    Define the business by the customer need, not just today’s product. “Help offices work better” gives more room to adapt than “sell photocopiers.”用顧客需要定義企業，而非只看現在產品。「幫辦公室更有效率」比「賣影印機」更能因應變化。

    Example例子

    Malee’s “The Fruit Expert” promise suggests fruit expertise. A mushroom drink must still make sense under that promise. The company can use the main brand, a sub-brand or a different brand, depending on the fit.Malee「水果專家」承諾建立水果專業期待；菇類飲品也須與此承諾相合，再選用主品牌、副品牌或別的品牌。

    2. BCG (Boston Consulting Group): classify the business, then judge the option2. BCG（波士頓顧問公司）：先判斷事業定位，再評估管理選項

    Key idea重點

    BCG compares market growth with relative market share. It helps organize choices about where to put resources.BCG 比較市場成長與相對市占，用來整理資源投入選項。

    How it works為什麼

    Read the two axes separately. A fast-growing market can contain a small brand. A large brand can operate in a slow market. Use profit, cash needs and company ability to decide what to do next.兩軸分開看：高成長市場可有小品牌，大品牌也可在慢成長市場。再用利潤、現金需要與能力選行動。

    Product Life Cycle · different stages, different management questions產品生命週期｜不同階段，管理問題也不同

    Key idea重點

    PLC (Product Life Cycle) follows sales over time: introduction, growth, maturity and decline. Each stage changes the questions about awareness, access, repeat purchase, cost and renewal.PLC（產品生命週期） 看銷售隨時間經過導入、成長、成熟與衰退，各階段對知名度、取得、重購、成本與更新有不同問題。

    PLC asks: what stage?PLC 問的是：處於哪個階段？

    The Product Life Cycle describes how a category's sales may develop over time. It is a useful pattern, not a guaranteed timetable.產品生命週期（Product Life Cycle）描述一項產品類別的銷售量可能如何隨時間發展。它是有用的觀察模式，不是保證會照表發生的時間表。

    BCG asks: what position?BCG 問的是：競爭位置在哪裡？

    The matrix combines market growth with our relative competitive share. A mature category is not automatically a Cash Cow for every brand.這個矩陣結合市場成長與本公司的相對競爭市占率。品類成熟，不代表每個品牌都自動成為金牛。

    中文邏輯PLC 描述市場或產品可能經歷的生命週期；BCG 還要看「本公司的相對市占率」。市場成熟不代表每個品牌都成了金牛事業。

    BCG decision check · A Star is losing money. Must we invest?BCG 決策練習｜明星事業正在虧錢，就一定要投資嗎？

    Not automatically. First check whether losses come from temporary growth investment or an unviable business model. Compare future margins, funding needs, competitive advantage and alternatives. Quadrant → hypotheses → evidence → decision, not quadrant → command.不一定。先檢查虧損來自暫時性的成長投資，還是不可行的商業模式，再比較未來利潤率、資金需要、競爭優勢與替代方案。應是「象限 → 假設 → 證據 → 決策」，不是「象限 → 命令」。

    SWOT and environmental scanningSWOT 與環境掃描

    Internal: Strengths / Weaknesses內部：優勢／劣勢

    Resources, capabilities, execution and constraints inside the firm.企業內部的資源、能力、執行力及限制。

    External: Opportunities / Threats外部：機會／威脅

    Competition, technology, demographics, politics, culture and changing customer needs.競爭、技術、人口結構、政治、文化及不斷變動的顧客需求。

    How it works為什麼

    Turn the four boxes into an action. A retailer can use reliable delivery, an internal strength, to serve a growing area, an external opportunity. Set a clear goal and use the strength to reach it.把四格轉成行動：零售商可用可靠配送這項內部優勢，服務成長地區這項外部機會，設定目標再用優勢達成。

    Key idea重點

    Generation labels depend on local history. The same birth years in Thailand, China and the United States can involve different technology, events and routines.世代標籤與在地歷史有關。泰國、中國與美國相同出生年份，可能經歷不同科技、事件與生活方式。

    Example例子

    Older customers may use online banking when its convenience outweighs their concerns. Kodak shows a related planning lesson: customers still want to keep memories when the technology changes from film.年長顧客可能因便利大於疑慮而用網銀。Kodak 則說明另一規劃重點：底片科技改變時，顧客仍想保存回憶。

    How it works為什麼

    SWOT supports a strategy when each factor leads to a realistic action and a clear goal.當每個因素都連到可執行行動與清楚目標，SWOT 才真正支持策略。

    4. Buyer Decision Process: a guide, not a compulsory ritual4. 購買決策過程：參考框架，不是人人必走的儀式

    Key idea重點

    The consumer’s “black box” includes motives, experience, social context and perception. These shape the response to a marketing offer.顧客黑箱包括動機、經驗、社會情境與知覺，這些共同影響對行銷提案的回應。

    Stage階段What happens發生什麼Marketing implication行銷意涵
    Need recognition需求察覺A gap or stimulus makes a need salient.缺口或刺激使需求變明顯。Connect the offer to a real problem or occasion.把提案接到真實問題或情境。
    Information search資訊搜尋Use memory, reviews, people or trial to learn.透過記憶、評論、他人或試用了解。Make relevant and credible information accessible.提供容易取得且可信的相關資訊。
    Evaluation替代方案評估Compare feasible options on benefits that matter here.以此情境重要的利益比較可行選項。Understand the actual shortlist and trade-offs.了解真正候選名單與取捨。
    Purchase購買Choose and obtain an option.選擇並取得產品／服務。Reduce access, payment and availability friction.降低取得、付款與供貨阻礙。
    Postpurchase購後行為Evaluate, recommend, complain, repeat or dispose.評價、推薦、抱怨、重購或處置。Deliver the promise and support the next experience.交付承諾並支援下一次體驗。

    Who is buying? Demographics, psychographics and self-image誰在購買？人口統計、心理特徵與自我形象

    Demographics人口統計特徵（Demographics）

    Age, family status, income, location: who the buyer is.年齡、家庭狀態、所得與地點：描述買家是誰。

    Psychographics心理特徵（Psychographics）

    Values, interests, lifestyle, identity: why the benefit matters.價值觀、興趣、生活型態與認同：解釋某項利益為何重要。

    Example例子

    Corvette and station-wagon exercises show how a car suggests a lifestyle or desired self-image. Brand image describes an association, rather than every owner’s age or family status.Corvette 與旅行車練習說明車子如何暗示生活方式或理想自我。品牌形象是一種聯想，不是每位車主的年齡與家庭狀況。

    TrapVALS and other segments organize hypotheses; they do not reveal each person's entire identity. 品牌印象 ≠ 真實買家的人口統計。陷阱：VALS（價值與生活方式） 等區隔工具用來整理假設，不能揭露每個人的完整身分。

    中文抓重點人口統計能描述「誰」，但無法單靠年齡預測「為什麼買」。教授用跑車與旅行車讓你看到品牌形象和真實車主可能完全不同。

    A sports-car advertisement suggests a young, affluent lifestyle. Why can that image attract a buyer without establishing the actual age or income of owners? How would psychographics help?跑車廣告呈現年輕富裕的生活形象。為什麼這種形象可能吸引顧客，卻無法證明真實車主的年齡與收入？心理特徵資料能補充什麼？

    Benefit segmentation and self-image利益區隔與自我形象

    Key idea重點

    Benefit segmentation groups people by what they want from the offer. One hotel guest wants luxury and atmosphere; another wants a clean room near transport. This changes the product, price and message.利益區隔按顧客追求利益分組；旅客可能要豪華氣氛，也可能要交通方便的乾淨房間，會導向不同產品、價格與訊息。

    How it works為什麼

    Demographics describe people. Psychographics describe values and lifestyles. Self-image is how people see themselves or wish to be seen. VALS (Values and Lifestyles) is one framework for organizing lifestyle differences.人口統計描述人，心理特徵描述價值與生活方式，自我形象是如何看自己或希望被看待。VALS 是整理生活方式差異的一種框架。

    5. Motivation, involvement, risk and postpurchase behavior5. 動機、涉入程度、風險與購後行為

    Key idea重點

    One purchase can meet several needs: function, safety, belonging, status or personal growth. Involvement depends on how important and risky the decision feels.一次購買可同時滿足功能、安全、歸屬、地位或成長需要。涉入取決於決策感覺多重要、有多大風險。

    Need / context需要／情境→Importance & perceived risk重要程度與知覺風險→Information search資訊搜尋→Choice選擇→Postpurchase feeling購後感受
    Do not mix up不要混淆What it means意義
    Involvement涉入程度（Involvement）How important or risky the decision seems before/during purchase. Price alone does not determine it.購買前或購買時，顧客認為決策有多重要、風險有多高。價格不是唯一決定因素。
    Perceived risk知覺風險（Perceived Risk）Expected consequences: financial, functional, social or safety concerns.預期可能承擔的後果，包括財務、功能、社會評價或安全方面的顧慮。
    Cognitive dissonance認知失調（Cognitive Dissonance）Uncomfortable doubt after choosing between appealing alternatives.在有吸引力的替代方案之間做出選擇後，產生不舒服的懷疑。
    Diffusion創新擴散（Diffusion）How an innovation spreads across adopters with different risk and benefit perceptions.創新如何傳播到不同採用者之間，而每個人對風險與利益的判斷可能不同。

    Example例子

    Water for instant coffee may be a routine choice. Water for preparing infant formula can feel more important to the buyer. The use changes the how important the choice feels.泡即溶咖啡的水可能是日常選擇，用來調嬰兒配方奶時，買家可能覺得更重要；用途改變感知風險。

    Example例子

    A buyer likes a Louis Vuitton bag but keeps thinking about Hermès. This is doubt about a missed alternative: cognitive dissonance after purchase.買家喜歡已買的 Louis Vuitton 包，卻一直想 Hermès，這是對錯過選項的疑慮，也就是購後認知失調。

    How it works為什麼

    Adoption depends on perceived benefit, risk, ease of use and fit with existing habits. Buying early and influencing others are different roles.採用取決於感知利益、風險、易用性與習慣契合；早買與影響別人是不同角色。

    Maslow's needs · several motives can operate at once馬斯洛需求｜多種動機可以同時存在

    Key idea重點

    Several Maslow needs can act at the same time. Use the categories to ask better questions about the buying occasion.Maslow 多種需要可同時作用，用分類幫助更好理解購買情境。

    Example例子

    A restaurant meal can meet hunger, safety, friendship and status needs together. The occasion changes which benefit matters most.一頓餐可同時滿足飢餓、安全、友誼與地位，場合會改變哪種利益最重要。

    中文邏輯同一餐可能同時滿足吃飽、安全、社交與面子需求。不要把 Maslow 當成「第一層完全滿足才能進入第二層」的固定規則。

    Diffusion of Innovations · early buyer vs. influential buyer創新擴散｜早期買家與有影響力的買家不同

    Key idea重點

    Some buyers try new products early. Others wait for better reliability, support or prices. An opinion leader influences choices; a lead user has advanced needs that can guide product ideas.有人早試新品，有人等可靠性、支援或價格改善。意見領袖影響選擇，領先使用者的進階需要則能引導產品點子。

    How it works為什麼

    An early buyer is not always influential. Find both the people willing to try and the people whose advice others trust.早買的人不一定有影響力；要找願意試的人，也要找建議被信任的人。

    A microwave can feel unfamiliar or risky. CD (Compact Disc) recordings offered easier track selection and no rewinding, but replacing a collection cost money. Buyers weigh these gains and costs.微波爐可能讓人覺得陌生或有風險；CD（光碟） 可方便選曲、不用倒帶，但換整套收藏要花錢。買家會權衡這些得失。

    After a hard choice, clear onboarding and helpful support can reassure a buyer. Fixing a faulty product is different from easing doubt about another attractive option.困難選擇後，清楚入門指引與支援可使買家安心。修好故障產品，與減輕對另一好選項的疑慮，是不同工作。

    6. Coffee choice: which benefit matters in this situation?6. 咖啡選擇：在這個情境下哪一項利益最重要？

    Key idea重點

    Utilitarian benefits help a task. Hedonic benefits give enjoyment or experience. Coffee can offer caffeine, taste, convenience, atmosphere and social time at once.功能利益幫助完成任務，享樂利益帶來樂趣與體驗。咖啡可同時提供咖啡因、口味、便利、氣氛與社交。功能性價值, hedonic/social benefits 情感／體驗價值, taste and convenience can all coexist. Their importance changes with the purchase occasion.

    Example例子

    On a workday, speed may matter most. With friends, atmosphere may matter more. At home, taste may lead. The convertible-car ad uses a straw to sell a cool, refreshing feeling instead of listing technical features.上班可能最重速度，與朋友相聚重氣氛，在家重口味。敞篷車廣告用吸管傳達清涼感，而非列技術功能。

    Creative message vs. measurable marketing outcome廣告創意訊息與可衡量的行銷成果

    Key idea重點

    A memorable ad must connect its idea to the brand and a useful benefit. Choose a measure that matches the goal: attention, brand recall, consideration, purchase or profit.讓人記住的廣告，也要把創意連到品牌與有用利益，再依目標衡量注意、品牌回想、考慮、購買或利潤。

    7. Brand awareness is not the same as consideration or purchase7. 品牌知名度不等於納入考慮或購買

    10. Cold call & Class Discussion practice10. 課堂即答與討論練習

    Try saying the answer in English before opening it.打開答案前，先用英文說一次。以下是精簡練習答法，不是課堂錄音逐字引文。

    What makes a mission different from an objective?使命與目標有何不同？

    A mission explains whom the organization serves and the value it aims to create. Objectives turn that direction into measurable results, deadlines and actions.使命說明組織服務誰，以及希望創造什麼價值；目標則把方向轉為可衡量的成果、期限與行動。

    Why doesn't a Star automatically deserve more investment?為什麼明星事業不一定值得更多投資？

    BCG describes growth and relative market position, but investment still depends on profitability, cash needs, capabilities and feasible future returns.BCG 描述成長與相對市場地位；投資仍取決於獲利能力、現金需求、能力與可實現的未來報酬。

    What is Ansoff's diversification strategy?Ansoff 的多角化策略是什麼？

    Entering a new market with a new product. It differs from product development, where the market already exists, and market development, where the product already exists.用新產品進入新市場；不同於產品開發（既有市場），也不同於市場開發（既有產品）。

    Why can a car buyer skip stages in the Buyer Decision Process?為什麼購車者可能跳過購買決策過程中的階段？

    The five stages are a conceptual model. Prior experience, urgency, emotion or an impulse can shorten search or evaluation even in a high-priced category.五階段是概念模型。過往經驗、急迫性、情緒或衝動，即使在高價品類，也可能縮短搜尋或評估。

    What is the difference between demographics and psychographics?人口統計特徵與心理特徵有何不同？

    Demographics describe characteristics such as age or income. Psychographics examine values, lifestyle and motives that may better explain choices.人口統計描述年齡、所得等特徵；心理特徵探討價值觀、生活型態與動機，可能更能解釋選擇。

    Why is postpurchase dissonance different from dissatisfaction?為什麼購後認知失調不同於不滿意？

    A buyer may like a product while wondering whether a forgone alternative would have been better. Dissatisfaction is usually about perceived poor product or service performance.買家可能喜歡產品，卻仍懷疑沒選的替代方案是否更好。不滿意通常來自感知到不佳的產品或服務表現。

    How should a marketer analyze premium bottled water?行銷人員應如何分析高價瓶裝水？

    I would define the segment and buying occasion, identify the important functional, risk-related and symbolic benefits, then test which message improves consideration or trial rather than assuming premium means healthier.我會先界定客群與購買場合，找出重要的功能、風險相關及象徵性利益，再測試什麼訊息提升納入考慮或試買，而不先假設高價就代表較健康。

    What was the marketing weakness of the Xcite creative example?Xcite 廣告創意例子的行銷弱點是什麼？

    A memorable idea may not link to the advertised brand. We should measure brand attribution, recall and relevant consumer response, not just attention.令人難忘的創意未必能連結到廣告品牌。應衡量品牌歸因、回想與相關的消費者反應，而不只是注意力。

    Why can't we copy U.S. generational cohorts directly into Thailand?為什麼不能把美國世代分類直接套用到泰國？

    The experiences defining a cohort depend on local history, culture and technology. Birth-year labels alone do not establish the same motives or behavior in a different market.定義一個世代的經驗取決於當地歷史、文化與科技。只有出生年份標籤，無法證明不同市場有相同動機或行為。
