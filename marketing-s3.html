    1. Why research?1. 為什麼要研究？

    Key idea重點

    Research helps managers understand customers, compare options and reduce the risk of a poor decision.研究幫助管理者理解顧客、比較選項，降低決策失誤風險。不確定性 about wants, needs, motivations, drivers and barriers. Research improves the decision; it cannot guarantee it.

    Type類型Main question主要問題Example例子
    Descriptive描述性What is happening?正在發生什麼？Who buys? How often? How much?誰買？多久買一次？買多少？
    Diagnostic診斷性What may explain the problem?什麼可能解釋這個問題？Why is satisfaction falling?為什麼滿意度下降？
    Predictive預測性What might happen if...?如果……，可能發生什麼？What if we launch, change price or run a promotion?如果上市、調價或促銷，會怎樣？
    Business research商業研究

    A real management problem, practical, fast and usable.針對真實管理問題，重視實用、快速與可採用性。

    Academic research學術研究

    Explanation, theory, prior studies and explicit hypotheses. A different purpose, not a higher quality.重視解釋、理論、既有研究與明確假設。目的不同，不代表品質較高。

    2. Question before method2. 先有問題，再選方法

    Key idea重點

    Start with the business question. Define what must be learned. Choose the method last.先有商業問題，再定義要知道什麼，最後才選方法。

    Wrong order錯誤順序

    "Let's run a survey." Then invent a question for it.「我們來做問卷。」然後才替問卷想一個問題。

    Right order正確順序

    Question → objective → information needed → method.問題 → 目標 → 所需資訊 → 方法。

    Example例子

    Weak drink sales could come from price, unclear benefits, poor access or a bad experience. Each explanation needs different information. Asking only “Do you like it?” will not tell the manager what to change.飲料銷售差可能來自價格、利益不清、買不到或體驗不好，各原因需要不同資訊。只問喜不喜歡，無法告訴管理者該改什麼。

    Business problem versus research question企業問題與研究問題

    Key idea重點

    “Sales are weak” describes a business problem. A research question asks about one possible reason, such as awareness, price or repeat purchase.「銷售差」是商業問題；研究問題則針對一個可能原因，例如知名度、價格或重購。症狀. Research questions turn it into something evidence can answer:

    • Are customers aware of the product?顧客知道這項產品嗎？
    • Do target customers understand the benefit?目標顧客了解它的利益嗎？
    • Is the price seen as too high?顧客認為價格太高嗎？
    • Why did trial customers not buy again?試買顧客為什麼沒有再次購買？

    A useful research objective有用的研究目標

    Weak不夠明確Understand consumers.理解消費者。
    Better較好Understand the main barriers preventing Bangkok office workers from trying Product X.了解阻止曼谷上班族試用 X 產品的主要障礙。
    Best最好Identify and rank the main barriers preventing Bangkok office workers aged 25 to 40 from trying Product X, so management can decide whether to change price, communication or distribution.找出並排序阻止曼谷 25 至 40 歲上班族試用 X 產品的主要障礙，讓管理層決定是否調整價格、溝通或通路。

    Key idea重點

    A useful objective names the people, the information needed and the decision it supports. Specific objectives produce more useful research.好的研究目的要說清楚研究誰、要什麼資訊，以及支持哪個決策。目的越具體，研究越有用。

    When not to do research何時不該做研究？

    Key idea重點

    Skip a new study if the answer already exists, cannot arrive in time, costs more than it is worth, or will not change the decision.若答案已存在、來不及、成本高於價值，或無法改變決策，就不必另做研究。

    Pause & think暫停，想一想

    A manager wants to launch a survey, but the result cannot change a product decision already made. What is wrong with the research plan, and when might skipping research be more rational?主管打算做問卷，但結果無法改變早已決定的產品策略。這項研究計畫有什麼問題？何時不做研究反而更合理？

    3. Research process and evidence sources3. 研究流程與證據來源

    Problem identification辨識問題→Research design研究設計→Method方法→Sampling抽樣→Data collection資料蒐集→Analysis分析→Report報告→Follow-up後續追蹤

    How it works為什麼

    The company writes the brief. The research firm designs and runs the study. Respondents provide information. A clear brief keeps all three groups focused on the same decision.企業寫研究需求，研究公司設計與執行，受訪者提供資訊。清楚需求能讓三方對準同一決策。

    Secondary data次級資料 次級資料

    Already exists: government reports, company records, industry reports, published statistics. Check it first.已存在的資料：政府報告、公司紀錄、產業報告與公開統計。先檢查這些資料。

    Primary data初級資料 初級資料

    Collected for this question: surveys, interviews, focus groups, observation, experiments. Collect it only for the remaining gap; it costs time and money.為目前問題而蒐集：問卷、訪談、焦點團體、觀察與實驗。只為剩餘缺口蒐集，因為它需要時間與金錢。

    Pause & think暫停，想一想

    A research firm delivers a polished survey to answer the vague brief “Understand customers.” Why could technically correct findings still be useless for management? Rewrite the brief as a decision-linked objective.研究公司依據模糊的「了解顧客」簡報，完成一份精美問卷。為什麼技術上正確的結果仍可能對管理者無用？請改寫成能連結決策的研究目標。

    4. Qualitative versus quantitative4. 質性與量化研究

    Key idea重點

    Use qualitative research to explore reasons and meanings. Use quantitative research to measure how many, how often or how much.質性研究探索原因與意義，量化研究衡量多少人、多常或多少量。

    Qualitative 質性質性Quantitative 量化量化
    Questions問題Why? How?為什麼？如何？Who? How many? How often? How much?誰？多少人？多久一次？多少？
    Good for適合用途Motivations, barriers, emotions, meanings, decision processes, concept and ad development動機、障礙、情緒、意義、決策過程，以及概念與廣告開發Awareness, usage, satisfaction, purchase intention, frequency, group differences知名度、使用情形、滿意度、購買意願、頻率與群體差異
    Methods方法Focus groups, in-depth interviews焦點團體、深度訪談Structured surveys結構式問卷
    Evidence standard證據標準Saturation: new interviews add no new themes飽和：新訪談不再帶來新主題The sample can represent the population樣本足以代表母體時
    Not for不適合用途Counting or population percentages計數或推估母體百分比Fully explaining why完整解釋原因

    Example例子

    EV (Electric Vehicle) interviews can reveal worries about charging, price and battery life. Use those themes to design a survey and measure how common each concern is. Interviews find possible answers; a well-designed survey estimates their size.電動車訪談可找出充電、價格與電池壽命疑慮，再把主題轉為問卷量各疑慮多普遍。訪談找答案方向，適當問卷估計規模。

    Pause & think暫停，想一想

    Interviews uncover several reasons customers hesitate to buy an EV. Why does this not tell management how common each barrier is in the target market? What next step could address that gap?訪談發現顧客猶豫購買電動車的幾種原因。為什麼這還不能告訴主管每種障礙在目標市場有多普遍？下一步可用什麼方法補上缺口？

    5. Sampling5. 抽樣

    Key idea重點

    First decide whose experience matters. Then choose how to reach those people and how many to study. A large sample does not fix asking the wrong group.先決定誰的經驗重要，再選如何接觸與研究多少人。問錯群體，樣本再大也無法補救。

    Concept概念What it establishes能說明什麼Limit限制
    Target population and screen目標母體與篩選Define whose experience or decision matters. A recent Siam Paragon visit is relevant to a study of its recent service.界定要研究誰的經驗或決策；研究近期服務，需要近期去過 Siam Paragon 的人。A valid screen does not make recruitment representative.篩選正確，不代表招募具有代表性。
    Probability sampling機率抽樣Known selection probabilities support population estimates and sampling-error calculations under the design.已知入樣機率，在符合設計下支援母體估計與抽樣誤差計算。Coverage, nonresponse and measurement errors can remain.涵蓋、未回應與衡量錯誤仍可能存在。
    Non-probability sampling非機率抽樣Convenient or purposive recruitment can explore relevant experiences.便利或目的性招募可探索相關經驗。Selection probabilities are unknown; a large count alone cannot justify population percentages.入樣機率未知；人數多不能單獨支持母體比例推論。
    Saturation資料飽和Further qualitative discussions add no materially new themes for the question.針對該問題，新增質性討論不再產生重要新主題。Theme coverage differs from representing population proportions.主題覆蓋與母體比例代表性不同。
    Pause & think暫停，想一想

    A mall survey recruits 300 convenient passersby without checking whether they recently shopped there. Why do a large sample and many responses not establish representative satisfaction findings?商場問卷方便抽樣了 300 位路人，也沒有確認他們近期是否來購物。為什麼樣本大、回覆多，仍不能證明滿意度結果有代表性？

    6. From concept to measurement6. 從概念到衡量

    Conceptual framework (class example)概念架構（課堂例子）

    Packaging包裝Functional benefits功能性利益Lifestyle生活型態Health benefits健康利益
    →
    Intention to buy購買意願
    Independent variables 自變數自變數Dependent variable 依變數依變數

    Key idea重點

    A conceptual framework links possible drivers to an outcome. Here, packaging, function, lifestyle and health benefits are tested against purchase intention.概念架構把可能驅動因素連到結果；此例研究包裝、功能、生活型態與健康利益和購買意圖的關係。

    Conceptual versus operational definition概念性定義與操作性定義

    Abstract concept抽象概念Health benefits健康利益
    ↓
    Conceptual definition: what it means概念性定義：它的意義是什麼Low fat, low calories, high fiber, nutritional value, no sugar低脂、低熱量、高纖、營養價值與無糖
    ↓
    Operational definition: how we measure it操作性定義：我們如何衡量它"I prefer my ice cream to be low fat." "I prefer ice cream with low calories." Rated on a scale from 1 (strongly disagree) to 4 (strongly agree).「我偏好低脂冰淇淋。」「我偏好低熱量冰淇淋。」以 1（非常不同意）到 4（非常同意）的量尺評分。

    How it works為什麼

    First define what “health benefits” means. Then write questions about those features. The class scale runs from 1, strongly disagree, to 4, strongly agree. Clear wording helps the answers measure the intended idea.先定義健康利益指什麼，再針對這些特徵寫題目。課堂量尺從 1 非常不同意到 4 非常同意，清楚用字才能對準要量的概念。

    注意問卷有數字不代表量對了。用字、概念定義、受訪者理解和偏誤都會影響品質。

    Validity versus reliability效度與信度

    Key idea重點

    Validity means measuring the right thing. Reliability means getting consistent results. A measure can be consistent but wrong.效度是量對東西，信度是結果穩定。一個量測可以穩定但量錯。效度: are we measuring what we intend to? 信度: is the measurement consistent? A survey can be consistently wrong.

    Reliable and valid有信度，也有效度Consistent and on target結果一致，且對準目標
    Reliable, not valid有信度，沒有效度Consistently measures the wrong thing持續一致地測錯東西
    Valid on average, not reliable平均而言對準目標，但信度不足Scattered around the target結果散布於目標周圍
    Neither兩者皆無Scattered and off target結果分散，且偏離目標
    Pause & think暫停，想一想

    A questionnaire measures “healthy choices” only by asking whether customers prefer low-fat food, with highly consistent answers. Why might the measure still lack validity? What would you check?問卷只靠「是否偏好低脂食物」衡量健康選擇，且回答非常一致。為什麼這項衡量仍可能缺乏效度？應再檢查什麼？

    7. Can we trust what people say?7. 能相信人們說的話嗎？

    Key idea重點

    People’s answers can differ from their behavior. Understand the source of that difference before drawing a conclusion.人說的可能不同於做的，解讀前先分清落差原因。

    Danger風險What happens會發生什麼
    Rationalization事後合理化A logical reason given after the fact, when habit, emotion or impulse drove the purchase購買其實由習慣、情緒或衝動驅動，事後卻提出合理的理由
    Social desirability社會期許偏誤Answers that look responsible or healthy: people overstate exercise and recycling回答看起來負責或健康，例如高報運動與回收行為
    Courtesy bias禮貌性偏誤Overly positive feedback to be polite, common face to face and where criticism is uncomfortable為了禮貌給過度正面的回饋；面訪及不習慣批評的場合較常見
    Attitude-behavior gap態度與行為落差"I care about sustainability," then buys the cheapest option口頭說「我重視永續」，實際卻買最便宜的選項

    How it works為什麼

    Rationalization is a reason formed after the choice. Social desirability makes an answer look responsible. Courtesy avoids offending someone. An attitude–behavior gap occurs when a sincere preference loses to price or convenience at checkout.合理化是事後整理理由；社會期許是讓答案看起來負責；禮貌偏誤是避免得罪人。態度行為落差則可能是真心偏好，最後仍被價格或便利壓過。

    How it works為什麼

    Use neutral questions and compare stated answers with behavior. Good statistics cannot fix a badly worded question.用中性問題，並把回答與行為比較。好的統計無法修補寫壞的問題。

    Pause & think暫停，想一想

    Survey respondents say they care about sustainability, but transaction records show frequent purchases of the cheapest option. What can be concluded, and what competing explanations should be tested before calling the respondents dishonest?受訪者表示重視永續，但交易紀錄顯示他們經常選最便宜的商品。現在能推論什麼？在認定受訪者不誠實之前，還有哪些其他解釋要檢查？

    8. Survey, observation and experiment8. 問卷、觀察與實驗

    Survey問卷調查

    Door-to-door, mall intercept, phone, online, mobile, paper. No single best mode: choose by respondent, complexity, cost, speed and control.到府、商場攔訪、電話、線上、手機與紙本。沒有唯一最佳方式，要依受訪者、複雜度、成本、速度與控制程度選擇。

    Observation觀察

    Records what people actually do and avoids some self-report bias, but cannot directly show motives. Mystery shoppers check real service, prices and displays.紀錄實際行為，避免部分自陳偏誤，但無法直接顯示動機。神秘客會檢查真實服務、價格與陳列。

    Experiment實驗

    Change a treatment (X) and observe the outcome (O). Experiments are designed to test effects; credible control conditions are needed to isolate a cause.改變處理（X）並觀察結果（O）。實驗用來測試效果，需要可信對照條件才能辨識原因。

    Choose the mode for the information needed依資訊需求選擇方式

    Method / setting方法／場域Useful task適合任務Trade-off取捨
    In-depth interview / focus group深度訪談／焦點團體Explore an unfamiliar buying process or an early storyboard.探索陌生購買流程或初期廣告分鏡。Rich meaning; not a population vote.意義豐富；不是母體投票結果。
    Online / phone survey線上／電話問卷Measure defined attributes among reachable qualified respondents.在可觸及且合格受訪者中衡量已定義屬性。Reach, question complexity and participation differ by mode.不同方式的涵蓋、題目複雜度與參與情況不同。
    Mall intercept / central location test商場攔訪／定點測試Evaluate a product or concept in a controlled setting.在可控制場地評估產品或概念。Accessible for testing; recruited visitors may not reflect all buyers.方便測試；現場訪客未必代表所有買家。
    Observation / mystery shopper觀察／神秘客See laundry routines, navigation, displays or actual service execution.觀察洗衣流程、逛店路徑、陳列或實際服務執行。Shows actions; one visit does not represent all experiences or explain motives.看見行動；一次造訪不代表全部體驗，也不解釋動機。
    Controlled taste test / experiment控制條件的口味測試／實驗Compare formulas or test a defined change.比較配方或測試明確變動。Control helps isolate a difference; realism and alternative explanations still matter.控制有助辨識差異；仍須考慮真實性與其他解釋。

    Correlation is not causation: the POP (Point of Purchase) display相關不等於因果：POP（購買現場） 店頭陳列

    Example例子

    Sales rise from THB (Thai Baht) 100,000 to THB 130,000 after a POP display: a 30% increase. To estimate the display’s effect, compare with similar stores over the same period. Payday, stock availability or another promotion may explain some of the rise.POP 陳列後銷售從 100,000 升至 130,000 泰銖，增加 30%。估計陳列效果時，要與同期間相似門市比；發薪、庫存或其他促銷可能解釋部分上升。干擾因素 such as payday, weekend traffic, a competitor stockout, seasonality or another promotion can explain part of a before/after change. Stronger designs use comparison stores or periods and repeated observations.

    How it works為什麼

    A spurious factor is another change that affects the result. Use comparable treatment and control groups and track both. Where practical, assign stores or customers to the groups rather than letting them select themselves.干擾因素是同時影響結果的其他變化。用可比較的實驗與控制群，追蹤兩邊；可行時由研究安排分組，而非自行選組。

    Laboratory實驗室

    High control, but artificial.控制程度高，但情境較人工化。

    Field實地

    Realistic, but less control over other factors.貼近真實，但對其他因素的控制較弱。

    How it works為什麼

    The comparison estimates the counterfactual: what sales would look like without the display. This is the link to Session 2’s loyalty-program question.對照是在估計反事實：沒有陳列時銷售會如何，也呼應 S2 的忠誠計畫問題。

    How it works為什麼

    Customer experience management follows Listen → Learn → Respond. Proactive research looks for new needs early. Reactive research studies a problem after it appears. Both need someone who will act on the findings.顧客體驗管理依聆聽→學習→回應進行。主動研究提前找新需要，被動研究在問題出現後處理，兩者都要有人採取行動。

    Example例子

    “63% satisfied” is a result. Analysis asks which group is unhappy, which feature matters, what changed and what the business should do.「63% 滿意」是結果；分析要問哪群人不滿、哪個項目重要、變了什麼，以及企業該做什麼。

    Pause & think暫停，想一想

    Cluster analysis groups consumers who show similar patterns. Why is a statistical cluster not automatically a useful marketing segment, and what customer and actionability evidence would you need?集群分析把行為模式相似的顧客分成群組。為什麼統計群組不會自動成為有用的行銷區隔？還需要哪些顧客證據及可採取行動的依據？

    From an output to a management choice從分析輸出走到管理選擇

    Example例子

    Cluster analysis groups people with similar habits. Managers then decide whether those groups need different offers. Conjoint analysis compares feature bundles, such as a low price with short battery life versus a high price with long battery life. It shows what people are willing to trade.集群分析把習慣相似的人分組，再由管理者決定是否需要不同提案。聯合分析比較屬性組合，例如低價短續航對高價長續航，找出人們願意交換什麼。

    How it works為什麼

    For a restaurant, measure the problem, explore reasons, observe busy periods, test a process change and measure again. The result should lead to a practical decision.餐廳可先量問題、探索原因、觀察尖峰、測流程改變，再重測，讓研究結果導向可執行決策。

    10. Do not mix these up10. 別混淆這些概念

    A business problem is a research question.企業問題就是研究問題。

    The problem is a symptom; the question targets one uncertainty.企業問題是症狀；研究問題聚焦一個不確定之處。

    We should run a survey.我們應該做問卷。

    The method follows the objective.方法應由目標決定。

    Primary data is always better.初級資料一定較好。

    Existing data may already answer the question at lower cost.既有資料可能已能以較低成本回答問題。

    A big sample is representative.大樣本就有代表性。

    Who you sample matters more than how many.抽到誰比抽多少人更重要。

    Saturation means representation.飽和代表有代表性。

    Saturation is about themes, not population inference.飽和關乎主題，不等於能推論母體。

    An independent variable is a proven cause.自變數就是已證實的原因。

    It is a proposed explanation to test.它是等待檢驗的解釋。

    Reliable means valid.有信度就有效度。

    A measure can be consistently wrong.測量可能很一致地出錯。

    Sales rose after the display, so it worked.陳列後銷售上升，所以陳列有效。

    Rule out outside factors with a comparison group.需要比較組來排除外部因素。

    Observation shows why people act.觀察顯示人們為何行動。

    It shows what they do, not their motives.它顯示人們做什麼，而非動機。

    Reporting numbers is analysis.報告數字就是分析。

    Analysis explains and links to a decision.分析需要解釋結果並連結決策。

    11. Cold call practice11. 課堂即答練習

    Why conduct marketing research?為什麼要進行行銷研究？

    To understand consumers, explore opportunities, diagnose problems and measure outcomes, reducing decision risk without eliminating it.為了理解消費者、探索機會、診斷問題與衡量成果，降低決策風險，但不能消除風險。

    Why must the question come before the method?為什麼必須先有問題，再選方法？

    The objective and the information needed decide the method. Choosing a survey first reverses the logic.研究目標與所需資訊決定方法；先決定做問卷，是把邏輯倒過來。

    When should we not do research?什麼時候不該研究？

    When the decision is already made, the information exists, time is too short, or the cost exceeds the value.決策已定、資訊已存在、時間太短，或成本超過價值時。

    Qualitative or quantitative: which is better?質性與量化，哪個比較好？

    Neither. Qualitative helps us understand; quantitative helps us measure. They often work in sequence.沒有誰必然較好。質性幫助理解；量化幫助衡量，兩者常依序使用。

    What is saturation?什麼是飽和？

    The point where more qualitative interviews add no materially new themes.增加質性訪談，也不再產生實質新主題的時點。

    Why use a screening question?為什麼要用篩選題？

    To make sure respondents belong to the target population, for example recent visitors to Siam Paragon.確認受訪者屬於目標母體，例如近期曾造訪 Siam Paragon 的人。

    What is a conceptual framework?什麼是概念架構？

    A set of proposed relationships between independent variables and an outcome. The arrows are what we test, not proven cause.自變數與結果之間的一組待驗證關係；箭頭代表要檢驗的內容，不是已證實的因果。

    Conceptual versus operational definition?概念性定義與操作性定義有何不同？

    The conceptual definition says what a concept means; the operational definition says how we will measure it.概念性定義說明概念的意義；操作性定義說明如何衡量。

    Why can't we just ask "Why did you buy this?"為什麼不能只問「你為什麼買這個？」

    People may not know or report their real motives: rationalization, memory, social desirability and courtesy bias distort answers.人可能不知道或不願說出真實動機；事後合理化、記憶、社會期許與禮貌性偏誤都可能扭曲答案。

    Why doesn't a before/after sales rise prove the display worked?為什麼銷售前後上升，不能證明陳列有效？

    Payday, weekend traffic, a competitor stockout or another promotion could explain it. A comparison group is needed.發薪日、週末人流、競爭者缺貨或其他促銷，也可能解釋上升；需要比較組。

    Validity versus reliability?效度與信度有何不同？

    Validity is measuring the right thing; reliability is measuring consistently. A measure can be reliable but invalid.效度是測對東西；信度是測得一致。測量可以有信度，卻沒有效度。

    Cluster versus conjoint analysis?集群分析與聯合分析有何不同？

    Cluster groups similar respondents; conjoint reveals trade-offs between bundles of attributes.集群分析把相似受訪者分組；聯合分析揭露屬性組合之間的取捨。
