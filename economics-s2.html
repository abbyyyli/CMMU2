    Elasticity and Its Applications彈性及其應用

    Session 1 showed which direction price and quantity move. Elasticity 彈性 shows how much they move, and decides whether a market change shows up mainly in price, in quantity, or in revenue.第 1 堂說明價格與數量往哪個方向移動；彈性說明變動有多大，並決定市場變化主要反映在價格、數量，還是營收上。

    Demand需求 Supply供給 Revenue gained增加的營收 Revenue lost減少的營收

    Chapter map章節地圖

    Price changes價格變動→How easily can buyers or sellers adjust?買方或賣方有多容易調整？→Elastic or inelastic有彈性或缺乏彈性→Revenue effect營收效果→When a curve shifts: mostly P or mostly Q?曲線移動時：主要改變價格，還是數量？

    一句話Law of demand 告訴你方向；elasticity 告訴你幅度。

    1. Price elasticity of demand1. 需求價格彈性

    Price elasticity of demand (PED) measures how responsive quantity demanded is to a change in price.需求價格彈性（PED）衡量需求量對價格變動的反應程度。

    Elastic有彈性 富彈性

    Quantity changes by a larger percentage than price. Buyers switch, cut back, delay or cancel.數量變動百分比大於價格變動百分比。買方會轉換、減量、延後或取消購買。

    Inelastic缺乏彈性 缺乏彈性

    Quantity changes by a smaller percentage than price. Buyers keep buying: few substitutes, urgent need, small budget share.數量變動百分比小於價格變動百分比。因替代品少、需要迫切或支出占比小，買方仍會購買。

    PED = % change in quantity demanded ÷ % change in price需求價格彈性＝需求量變動百分比 ÷ 價格變動百分比
    |PED| > 1 elastic · |PED| < 1 inelastic · |PED| = 1 unit elastic

    The sign is usually negative because price and quantity move in opposite directions. Classify by the absolute value: PED = −2 is elastic.價格與數量通常反向變動，所以符號通常為負。分類看絕對值：PED＝−2 屬於有彈性。

    Both curves slope down, so the direction is the same. Elasticity is about responsiveness, not direction.兩條曲線都向下，方向相同。彈性關乎反應程度，不是方向。

    Pause & think暫停，想一想

    Two markets face the same percentage price increase. Quantity falls sharply in one market but barely changes in the other. Which market is more price elastic? What additional information would identify whether each is elastic or inelastic?兩個市場的價格上漲百分比相同，但一個市場的需求量大幅下降，另一個只小幅下降。哪個市場的價格彈性較大？還需要什麼資訊，才能判斷各自屬於富彈性或缺乏彈性？

    2. What makes demand more elastic?2. 什麼讓需求更有彈性？

    One question covers all five factors: if the price rises, how easily can consumers change their behavior?五個因素都可用同一問題理解：價格上升時，消費者有多容易改變行為？

    Factor因素More elastic when較有彈性的情況Example例子
    Substitutes 替代品替代品More close substitutes有更多接近的替代品One chocolate brand versus chocolate as a whole單一巧克力品牌，相對於巧克力整個品類
    Market definition市場定義The market is defined more narrowly市場定義得更狹窄Tangerines versus fruit橘子，相對於水果
    Necessity必要性The purchase is discretionary購買屬於可自由取捨的非必需支出Premium snacks versus essential medicine高級零食，相對於必要藥品
    Time horizon時間範圍Consumers have longer to adjust消費者有更長時間調整Fuel: short run you still drive; long run you buy an EV or move closer燃油：短期仍須開車；長期可買電動車或搬近一點
    Budget share 占預算比重預算占比The purchase is a large share of income購買支出占所得比例較高Rent ฿20,000 → ฿24,000 versus snack ฿30 → ฿33房租從 ฿20,000 漲到 ฿24,000，相對於零食從 ฿30 漲到 ฿33

    These factors push elasticity in a direction. In a real case, say which one matters most and why.這些因素影響彈性的方向。分析真實案例時，要說明哪個因素最重要，以及原因。

    Market definition: why narrower means more elastic市場定義：為何越狹窄通常越有彈性？

    A narrower market has more substitutes outside its boundary. If tangerines get expensive, buyers switch to oranges or apples. If all fruit gets expensive, there is little to switch to.較狹窄的市場，其邊界外有較多替代品。橘子變貴，可以改買柳橙或蘋果；所有水果都變貴時，可替換的選擇就少得多。

    Manhattan rentals曼哈頓租屋

    Renters can move to Brooklyn or Queens, so demand is more elastic.租客可搬到 Brooklyn 或 Queens，因此需求較有彈性。

    All NYC rentals整個紐約市租屋

    Leaving means another city or a long commute, so demand is less elastic.離開可能意味搬去另一座城市或長途通勤，因此需求較缺乏彈性。

    注意只看一棟大樓不代表沒有替代品，附近相似的大樓都是替代品。個人「只想住 Manhattan」是個人偏好，不等於市場彈性。

    Elasticity depends on context彈性取決於情境

    Ride-hailing at 1 a.m. in heavy rain凌晨一點大雨中的叫車

    Few alternatives, urgent need: relatively inelastic.替代選項少、需要迫切：相對缺乏彈性。

    Ride-hailing to a café on Sunday週日叫車去咖啡廳

    Walk, take the train, go later or stay home: relatively elastic.可以步行、搭車、晚點去或待在家：相對有彈性。

    Elasticity is not a permanent label on a product. It depends on substitutes, urgency, time and the consumer.彈性不是貼在產品上的永久標籤；它取決於替代品、急迫性、時間與消費者。

    3. Elasticity and total revenue3. 彈性與總營收

    Total revenue = P × Q. A price change creates a price effect and a quantity effect; elasticity tells you which one is stronger.總營收＝P × Q。價格變動產生價格效果與數量效果；彈性告訴你哪個效果較強。

    Demand需求Price rises價格上升Price falls價格下降
    Elastic有彈性Revenue falls營收下降Revenue rises營收上升
    Inelastic缺乏彈性Revenue rises營收上升Revenue falls營收下降
    Unit elastic單一彈性Roughly unchanged大致不變Roughly unchanged大致不變

    Revenue is not profit. Profit also depends on cost, competition, regulation and reputation. "Demand is inelastic, so raise the price" is an incomplete recommendation.營收不等於利潤。利潤也取決於成本、競爭、法規與聲譽。「需求缺乏彈性，所以漲價」是不完整的建議。

    • Essential medicine: likely inelastic (necessity, few substitutes), so a price rise may raise revenue. Ethics, regulation and affordability still limit the decision.必要藥品因必需且替代品少，需求可能缺乏彈性，漲價可能增加營收；但倫理、法規與可負擔性仍會限制決策。
    • One brand of premium snack: likely elastic (many substitutes, easy to skip). A strong category does not give one brand pricing power.單一高級零食品牌因替代品多且容易不買，需求可能有彈性。品類強勢，不代表單一品牌有定價能力。

    If a firm raises price when demand is inelastic, total revenue may rise. Why does this fact alone not establish that the decision increases economic profit?當需求缺乏彈性時，企業漲價可能提高總營收。為什麼這個事實仍不足以證明該決策會提高經濟利潤？

    4. Elasticity of supply4. 供給彈性

    How responsive is quantity supplied to a price change? Ask: how easily can production rise at low cost?供給量對價格變動的反應程度如何？要問：以低成本增加生產，有多容易？

    Elastic supply有彈性的供給

    Spare capacity, available inputs, fast production, easy entry. Toothpicks.有閒置產能、投入要素易取得、生產快且容易進入，例如牙籤。

    Inelastic supply缺乏彈性的供給

    Fixed capacity, scarce resources, legal or natural limits, long production. Original Picasso paintings, durian trees that take years to fruit.產能固定、資源稀缺、有法律或自然限制，或生產時間長，例如畢卡索真跡、需多年結果的榴槤樹。

    Class slide: the same price rise from $40 to $50 moves quantity supplied from 80 to 170 when supply is elastic, but only from 80 to 85 when it is inelastic.課堂投影片：同樣從 $40 漲到 $50，有彈性供給的數量從 80 增至 170；缺乏彈性時則只從 80 增至 85。

    Supply, like demand, becomes more elastic in the long run: firms build capacity, hire, and new firms enter.供給和需求一樣，長期通常較有彈性：企業能擴充產能、招聘人力，新企業也能進入。

    Over time時間拉長後More elastic更有彈性More elastic更有彈性

    5. When a curve shifts, which moves more?5. 曲線移動時，哪個變動較大？

    The elasticity of the curve that does not move decides whether the adjustment shows up in price or in quantity.沒有移動的那條曲線，其彈性決定調整主要反映在價格還是數量。

    6. The cases6. 案例

    Ending food waste (class slides)終結食物浪費（課堂投影片）

    Less waste浪費減少→Market demand for food falls市場對食物的需求下降→P and Q fall價格與數量下降→Size depends on supply elasticity幅度取決於供給彈性

    With inelastic supply, price falls a lot, which helps poor food buyers most. But many poor people are food farmers, whose income falls. A full answer covers both groups.供給缺乏彈性時，價格會大幅下降，最有利於貧困的食物買家。但許多窮人本身是食物生產者，收入反而下降。完整答案要涵蓋兩群人。

    War on Drugs: methamphetamine (class slides)毒品戰爭：甲基安非他命（課堂投影片）

    Enforcement shifts supply left. Because demand is inelastic, price rises sharply while quantity falls only a little. Total spending, and seller revenue, can rise. "Quantity fell, so the policy worked" is incomplete.執法使供給左移。因需求缺乏彈性，價格大幅上升，數量卻只減少一點，總支出與賣方營收可能上升。「數量減少，所以政策成功」並不完整。

    Onionomics (assigned reading, BBC)洋蔥經濟學（BBC 指定閱讀）

    Onions are a staple in India with few substitutes, so demand is very inelastic. Bad weather, storage losses or transport limits cut supply, and prices spike while quantity barely falls. In a bumper harvest the reverse happens: prices collapse and farmers lose. The price rise came from supply falling, not demand rising.洋蔥是印度的主食原料之一，替代品少，因此需求非常缺乏彈性。惡劣天氣、儲存損耗或運輸限制使供給減少，價格暴漲，數量卻僅小幅下降。豐收時反向發生：價格崩跌，農民受損。漲價來自供給下降，不是需求增加。

    Slave redemption (MRU)贖回受奴役者（MRU）

    Humanitarian groups buying enslaved people's freedom add demand. If supply is very inelastic, price rises but few additional people are supplied, so the program frees people who would otherwise stay enslaved. If supply is elastic, the higher price draws in more supply and can encourage more captures. Good intentions do not remove supply incentives.人道團體出資贖回自由會增加需求。供給非常缺乏彈性時，價格上升，但新增供給很少，計畫釋放原本會繼續受奴役的人。供給有彈性時，較高價格會吸引更多供給，甚至鼓勵更多擄掠。善意不能消除供給誘因。

    Housing (MRU)住宅（MRU）

    More jobs raise housing demand. Where land and building rules make supply inelastic, as in San Francisco, the demand increase shows up mostly as higher prices.工作增加會提高住宅需求。在土地與建築規範使供給缺乏彈性的地方，例如舊金山，需求增加主要反映為更高價格。

    Gun buyback (MRU)槍枝回購（MRU）

    A local area is linked to a much larger market, so supply is close to perfectly elastic. Guns bought back are replaced from outside at the same price, so the number of guns in use barely changes.地方市場連結到更大的市場，供給因此接近完全有彈性。被回購的槍枝由外地以相同價格補入，使用中的槍枝數量幾乎不變。

    7. Common mistakes7. 常見錯誤

    The law of demand and elasticity are the same thing.需求法則與彈性是同一件事。

    One gives direction, the other gives magnitude.前者說明方向，後者說明變動幅度。

    Necessities are always inelastic.必需品一定缺乏彈性。

    Substitutes, time and budget share also matter.替代品、時間與預算占比也重要。

    A more specific product is more inelastic.產品定義越具體，需求越缺乏彈性。

    A narrower market usually has more outside substitutes.市場越狹窄，通常有越多市場外替代品。

    Inelastic demand plus a price rise means higher profit.需求缺乏彈性又漲價，代表利潤增加。

    It means higher revenue. Profit depends on cost.代表營收增加；利潤還取決於成本。

    Inelastic demand means quantity never changes.需求缺乏彈性，代表數量完全不變。

    Quantity changes, just relatively little.數量仍會改變，只是相對變動較小。

    Onion prices rose, so demand increased.洋蔥漲價，所以需求增加。

    Supply fell; demand did not need to move.供給下降就能造成漲價，不需要需求移動。

    Inelastic supply means supply shifted left.供給缺乏彈性，代表供給左移。

    Elasticity is the curve's shape. A shift is a separate event.彈性關乎曲線特性；曲線移動是另一件事。

    Ending food waste helps all poor people.減少食物浪費對所有窮人都有利。

    Poor buyers gain; poor farmers who sell food lose.貧困買家受益；販售食物的貧困農民受損。

    8. Cold call practice8. 課堂即答練習

    What is elasticity?什麼是彈性？

    How responsive quantity is to a change in price.數量對價格變動的反應程度。

    Law of demand versus elasticity?需求法則與彈性有何不同？

    The law of demand gives the direction; elasticity gives the size of the change.需求法則說明方向；彈性說明變動大小。

    What makes demand more elastic?什麼讓需求更有彈性？

    More close substitutes, a narrower market, a discretionary good, more time to adjust, and a larger budget share.更多接近的替代品、較狹窄的市場、非必需品、較長調整時間，以及較高預算占比。

    Why is demand for tangerines more elastic than for fruit?為什麼橘子的需求比水果更有彈性？

    Tangerine buyers can switch to many other fruits; there is little to switch to from fruit as a whole.橘子買家可以改買許多其他水果；水果整個品類則較少可替代。

    How does elasticity affect total revenue?彈性如何影響總營收？

    With inelastic demand a price rise raises revenue; with elastic demand it lowers revenue.需求缺乏彈性時，漲價提高營收；有彈性時，漲價降低營收。

    Does higher revenue mean higher profit?營收增加代表利潤增加嗎？

    No. Profit also depends on costs.不一定。利潤也取決於成本。

    What makes supply inelastic?什麼使供給缺乏彈性？

    Fixed capacity, scarce resources, legal or natural limits, or a long production process.固定產能、稀缺資源、法律或自然限制，或很長的生產過程。

    When demand shifts, what decides whether P or Q changes more?需求移動時，什麼決定價格或數量哪個變動較大？

    The elasticity of supply. Inelastic supply means mostly price; elastic supply means mostly quantity.供給彈性。供給缺乏彈性時，主要改變價格；有彈性時，主要改變數量。

    Why do farmers sometimes earn less in a good harvest?為什麼農民在豐收時有時反而賺得更少？

    Food demand is inelastic, so a large supply increase causes a big price fall and revenue drops.食物需求缺乏彈性，供給大幅增加使價格大跌，營收下降。

    Why are housing prices so high in some cities?為什麼某些城市房價這麼高？

    Land limits and building rules make supply inelastic, so rising demand mostly raises price.土地限制與建築規範使供給缺乏彈性，需求增加主要推高價格。
