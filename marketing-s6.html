    2. Changing needs, meaningful value and the funnel2. 需求變化、有意義的價值與漏斗

    A restaurant customer needs food, but “food” does not specify the desired meal. Hunger, companions, time, mood and location change the benefit sought. A favorite dish may offer less additional satisfaction when eaten repeatedly: diminishing marginal utility 邊際效用遞減. Switching for novelty is variety-seeking; it can occur without dissatisfaction. The same person can therefore choose a different brand next week while still liking the first.餐廳顧客需要食物，但「食物」沒有說明想要的餐點。飢餓、同伴、時間、心情與地點改變追求利益。最愛菜色反覆吃時，額外滿足可能下降，即邊際效用遞減；為新鮮感換選擇是追求多樣性，不一定源於不滿。因此同一人下週可選別牌，同時仍喜歡原品牌。

    Different occasion情境改變→Different valued benefit重視利益改變→Different choice選擇改變

    A USP is a distinct selling claim; an RTB 可信理由 supports belief in a promised benefit. Shampoo features may use similar ingredients or be copied. A useful difference must be noticed, valued, believed and delivered at a viable cost. The lecturer’s skepticism does not establish that all shampoos perform identically.USP 是獨特銷售訴求，RTB 支持顧客相信所承諾的利益。洗髮精功能可能使用相似成分或被模仿。有用差異須被注意、重視、相信，並以可行成本交付。教授的質疑不能證明所有洗髮精表現相同。

    4. Buyer, user and the limits of narrow targeting4. 買家、使用者與狹窄目標的限制

    Medical supplies for Buddhist monks reveal a buyer–user distinction 買家與使用者: a monk may use the product while a lay donor chooses and pays. Researching only the end user can miss the buying decision. A niche can improve relevance and willingness to pay, yet fewer reachable buyers and fewer purchase occasions can cap growth. Broad positioning can expand occasions but still needs a coherent benefit.僧侶醫療用品說明買家與使用者不同：僧侶可能使用，俗家捐贈者卻選擇並付款。只研究使用者會漏掉購買決策。利基可提高相關性與付費意願，但可觸及買家與情境較少，可能限制成長；廣泛定位可增加情境，仍需要一致利益。

The recap supplies anecdotes and claims, not independent scientific verification.冰山水啤酒說明特殊原料與付費意願間的缺環。特殊來源可能增加成本或供應風險，卻未改善買家注意的利益。富氧、O3 標示或「活化」水也須同樣檢查：技術描述不能證明健康效果。複習提供軼例與宣稱，並非獨立科學驗證。

    7. Match research to the uncertainty; compare markets fairly7. 研究對準不確定性，市場比較採一致標準

    Functional and experiential motives can coexist: detergent may remove stains and provide a valued fragrance. Start with what must be learned. Interviews explore reasons; measurement estimates their prevalence; observation records actions; a credible intervention tests whether an action changes behavior. One method does not answer every question.功能與體驗動機可同時存在，例如清潔劑去污，也提供被重視的香味。先確認要知道什麼：訪談探索理由，衡量估計普遍性，觀察記錄行動，可信介入測試行動是否改變行為。單一方法不能回答所有問題。

    9. Real substitutes, channel costs and ethical growth9. 真實替代品、通路成本與倫理成長

    A shopper may replace a fruit drink with water, a sports drink or another fruit drink on the same occasion. Direct/indirect labels depend on the category boundary; managerial competition concerns actual alternatives. Public or gift-giving choices may also value presentation and status more than private routine consumption. Neither a category label nor a public image predicts every person’s choice.同一情境下，顧客可能用水、運動飲料或另一果汁取代原飲品。直接／間接取決於品類界線，管理上的競爭關乎實際備選。公開或送禮選擇也可能比私人日常消費更重外觀與地位。品類標籤或公開形象都不能預測每個人的選擇。

    11. Cold call practice11. 課堂口答練習

    Why can a satisfied customer switch?滿意顧客為何會換品牌？

    The occasion, available alternatives or desire for variety may change. Satisfaction is an evaluation; repeat choice is behavior under a particular context.情境、可用選項或多樣性偏好可能改變。滿意是評價，重複選擇是特定情境下的行為。

    Why is buyer different from user?買家與使用者為何不同？

    The person choosing or paying may seek benefits different from those of the end user, as with a donor buying supplies for monks. Both roles can matter to research and communication.選擇或付款者與最終使用者可能追求不同利益，例如捐贈者購買僧侶用品。研究與溝通可能需涵蓋兩種角色。

    What makes two reasons distinct?什麼使兩個理由不同？

    Each explains a different mechanism, such as customer access versus operating cost. Restating “large market” as “many people” does not create a second reason.每個理由解釋不同機制，例如顧客接觸與營運成本。「大市場」改說「很多人」不是第二理由。

    When can attitudes be measured quantitatively?態度何時可量化？

    After the attitude or construct has been defined, a suitable survey can measure its prevalence or intensity. Exploratory interviews can first uncover meanings that fixed options might miss.態度或構念定義後，適當問卷可量普遍性或強度。探索訪談可先找出固定選項可能漏掉的意義。

    Why is a BCG progression not compulsory?BCG 為何不必依序演進？

    Its categories depend on market growth and relative share, which can change differently. PLC describes sales over time and is a different tool.分類取決於市場成長與相對市占，兩者可不同變化；PLC 描述時間中的銷售，是不同工具。

    What is the final business test?最後商業檢查是什麼？

    Whether the action produces valued customer behavior and incremental contribution after relevant costs, while preserving truthful communication and trust.行動是否產生有價值的顧客行為，扣除相關成本後增加貢獻，同時維持誠實溝通與信任。
