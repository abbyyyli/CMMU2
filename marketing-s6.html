    2. Perceptual map: a complete worked example2. 知覺定位圖：看一個完整範例

    Key idea重點

    Choose a category and buying occasion. Plot one focal brand, one direct rival and one indirect rival. Explain the position with two reasons.選品類與購買情境，畫主品牌、一直接與一間接對手，再以兩理由解釋定位。

    Role角色Choice選擇Why it belongs分類理由
    Focal brand主品牌GatoradeGatoradeCategory: sports drinks; occasion: hydration after exercise.品類是運動飲料，情境是運動後補水。
    Direct competitor 直接競爭直接對手Pocari SweatPocari SweatA similar-category drink considered for the same occasion.同品類飲料，可在相同情境被考慮。
    Indirect competitor 間接競爭間接對手Plain bottled water普通瓶裝水A different category that can satisfy the hydration need.不同品類，但可滿足補水需要。

    How it works為什麼

    Use sports-performance image and everyday-hydration suitability as the axes. These compare what the brands mean to buyers.用運動表現形象與日常補水適合度作軸，比較品牌對買家的意義。

    Interactive · Perceptual map互動｜知覺定位圖

    Select a brand. Move its point with the sliders, drag it, or focus the point and use the arrow keys.選品牌後，可用滑桿、拖曳，或聚焦點後用方向鍵移動。

    Worked answer完整示範答案

    I would position Gatorade around hydration after exercise. Pocari Sweat is a direct sports-drink rival. Plain water is an indirect way to meet the hydration need.我會把 Gatorade 連到運動後補水。Pocari Sweat 是直接運動飲料對手，普通水則是滿足補水需要的間接方式。

    Reason 1: clear recall. A link with exercise gives buyers a reason to remember the brand when that occasion comes.理由一：清楚回想。與運動連結，讓買家在該情境出現時有理由想起品牌。

    Reason 2: a useful choice. A clear exercise-related benefit gives people a reason to choose it over basic water. Keep that promise clear against Pocari Sweat and support it with the product and message.理由二：有用選擇。清楚運動利益使人有理由選它而非普通水；相對 Pocari Sweat 保持承諾清楚，再用產品與訊息支持。

    How it works為什麼

    Drawing order: category and occasion → required rivals → two axes with low/high labels → three plotted names → position and two reasons. S5 uses one direct + one indirect. The old Robinson Q3 uses two indirect rivals.作圖順序：品類情境→指定對手→兩軸高低標示→三個名稱位置→定位與兩理由。S5 是一直接＋一間接。Robinson 舊卷 Q3 是兩間接。

    4. Compare countries with three different reasons4. 國家比較：寫出三個不同理由

    Key idea重點

    Choose a country. Compare it directly with Thailand on three different points. Each point must explain a business effect, such as demand, competition or cost.先選國家，與泰國直接比較三個不同面向，每點說明需求、競爭或成本等商業影響。

    Comparison比較面向What to compare on both sides兩邊用相同標準Business implication商業意義
    Customer demand顧客需求Purchasing power and shopping occasions in reachable target cities.可觸及目標城市的購買力與購物情境。A retailer needs buyers who can and want to buy its actual basket, not population alone.需要買得起、也想買實際商品組合的人，不只人口。
    Competitive access競爭與接觸Existing stores, underserved catchments and customer substitutes.既有門市、未充分服務商圈與顧客替代品。Expansion should reach additional demand rather than mainly transfer sales between nearby stores.擴張應接觸新增需求，而非主要轉移鄰近店業績。
    Company feasibility公司可行性Local capability, sites, logistics, investment and expected contribution.在地能力、店址、物流、投資與預期貢獻。An attractive market still fails if the format cannot be delivered profitably.市場再吸引人，若無法有利潤地交付業態，仍會失敗。

    How it works為什麼

    SWOT separates company strengths and weaknesses from outside opportunities and threats. Use the factors to choose an action. Q1 shows a complete Vietnam answer.SWOT 分內部優劣與外部機會威脅，再用因素選行動。Q1 有完整越南答案。

    5. Growth: identify what is actually additional5. 成長：分清楚真正增加了什麼

    Key idea重點

    Look for what the whole business gains. Online orders can replace store orders. A bundle can bring next month’s purchase forward. Count added purchases and contribution after these effects and costs.看整家公司真正增加什麼：線上單可能取代門市單，組合包可能提前下月購買。扣這些效果與成本後，再看新增購買與貢獻。

    Action行動How it could grow sales成長機制Check檢查
    New users新使用者Reach an underserved segment or catchment.接觸未充分服務客群或商圈。Additional customers and repeat purchases, not reach alone.新增顧客與重購，不只觸及。
    New uses新用途Create another relevant occasion for the same product.為同一產品創造相關新情境。Genuine additional occasions, not switching from another company product.確有新增情境，而非換掉公司另一產品。
    Higher usage / multipacks增加用量／多入組More availability can increase consumption.更容易取得可能增加消費。Separate consumption from stockpiling, earlier buying and waste.分開消費、囤貨、提前買與浪費。
    Direct selling / disintermediation 去中介化直接銷售／去中介化Bypass a reseller and control the customer relationship.跳過經銷商、直接掌握顧客關係。Fulfillment, returns and partner conflict can offset saved intermediary margin.履約、退貨與通路衝突可能抵銷省下的中介利潤。
    Downsizing減量Keep the shelf price while reducing quantity.標價不變但含量減少。330 → 320 mL: quantity −3.03%; price/mL +3.125%, if price is unchanged. Check disclosure and trust.330→320 mL：價格不變時，含量減 3.03%，每 mL 價格增 3.125%；注意揭露與信任。

    How it works為什麼

    For a channel question, state the choice, two reasons, two extra ideas and the result to measure. Q2 includes a complete answer and calculator. Use S5 for package-size calculations.通路題依選擇、兩理由、兩額外想法與衡量結果作答。Q2 附完整答案與試算器。容量計算看 S5。

    A promotion increases sales this week. Which later result would distinguish extra demand from purchases brought forward?促銷使本週銷售增加。之後看到什麼結果，才能分辨新增需求與提前購買？

    6. Customer value, targeting and relationships6. 顧客價值、客群與關係

    Key idea重點

    STP (Segmentation, Targeting, and Positioning) means grouping customers by relevant needs, choosing whom to serve and building a clear meaning for the brand.STP（市場區隔、目標市場選擇、定位） 是按相關需要分群、選要服務誰，再建立品牌清楚意義。

    Concept概念Meaning and example定義與例子Do not confuse with不要混淆
    Needs, wants and occasions需要、想要與情境A need for food can lead to different meals depending on time, companions and mood.同樣需要食物，會因時間、同伴與心情而想吃不同餐點。The same person does not want the same offer in every situation.同一人不是每種情境都想要同一產品。
    Satisfaction versus repeat choice滿意與重複選擇Diminishing marginal utility and variety-seeking can lead a satisfied customer to choose something else.邊際效用遞減與追求多樣性，可能使滿意顧客仍換選擇。Switching is not always dissatisfaction.換品牌不一定是不滿。
    Benefit, USP (Unique Selling Proposition) and RTB (Reason to Believe) 可信理由利益、USP（獨特銷售主張）與 RTB（相信的理由）Benefit: why it helps. USP: distinct selling claim. RTB: support for believing the claim.利益回答有何幫助；USP 是獨特銷售訴求；RTB 是相信訴求的支持理由。A copied feature or scientific-sounding ingredient does not establish customer value.可模仿功能或科學感成分不會自動證明顧客價值。
    Buyer versus user購買者與使用者A donor buys medical supplies for monks; the chooser and end user have different roles.捐贈者買僧侶醫療用品，選擇者與最終使用者角色不同。Researching users alone can miss the purchase decision.只研究使用者可能漏掉購買決策。
    Demographic versus psychographic人口統計與心理特徵Age/income describe people; motives, values and lifestyles help explain choices.年齡／所得描述人；動機、價值與生活型態有助解釋選擇。Age does not determine classical-music preference or a universal generation mindset.年齡不能決定古典樂偏好，也不能直接套用普遍世代心態。
    CRM (Customer Relationship Management) and customer experienceCRM（顧客關係管理）與顧客體驗Manage the relationship through actual touchpoints, service and relevant communication.透過真實接觸點、服務與相關溝通管理關係。A loyalty reward is one tactic; enrollment alone is not retention.獎勵只是手段之一，入會不等於留存。

    Example例子

    Greyhound’s birthday benefit was blocked because the professor’s membership had expired. The earlier VIP (Very Important Person) relationship did not carry through to that visit. Improve reminders and service recovery, then track return visits and contribution.教授在 Greyhound 因會員到期領不到生日福利，先前 VIP（重要貴賓） 關係未延續到這次體驗。可改善提醒與服務補救，再看回訪與貢獻。

    中文邏輯寫案例時，先說顧客在意什麼，再說公司行動怎樣改變選擇；不要只貼 CRM、STP 等名詞。

    10. Cold call practice10. 課堂口答練習

    What is positioning?定位是什麼？

    The meaning a brand is intended to hold in a chosen customer’s mind relative to alternatives. It needs a clear target, a valued benefit and consistent delivery.品牌希望相對替代品，在選定顧客心中占有的意義，需要明確客群、被重視利益與一致交付。

    Why can a satisfied customer switch?滿意顧客為何換品牌？

    The occasion or desired benefit can change, and variety-seeking can occur without dissatisfaction. Investigate the reason before offering a discount.情境或追求利益可能改變，也可能沒有不滿卻想換口味；折扣前先查原因。

    How do three country reasons differ?三個國家理由如何區分？

    They explain different mechanisms, such as reachable demand, competitive access and operating feasibility. Rephrasing population size is still one reason.分別解釋可觸及需求、競爭接觸與營運可行性等不同機制；人口多換個說法仍是同一理由。

    When can a survey measure attitudes?問卷何時能量態度？

    When the attitude and response scale are clearly defined, a suitable survey can estimate prevalence or strength. Interviews can first discover the themes.態度與量尺定義清楚後，適當問卷可估普遍性或強度；訪談可先探索主題。

    What distinguishes extra online revenue from extra company profit?線上營收增加與公司利潤增加差在哪？

    Online purchases may replace store purchases and bring fulfillment, return or acquisition costs. Assess incremental contribution after those effects.線上購買可能取代門市購買，並增加履約、退貨與獲客成本，須扣除後看增量貢獻。

    11. Answer templates: short, complete reasoning11. 答題模板：短，但推理完整

    Key idea重點

    State the decision. Use a relevant case fact. Explain why it matters. Add the next distinct reason and the main trade-off. A metric supports this answer; it does not replace it.先決策，再用相關案例事實解釋原因，接不同理由與主要取捨。指標用來支持答案，不能取代答案。

    Worked answer完整示範答案

    Value claim: I would not rely on a special water ingredient alone. The feature needs a credible benefit that customers value enough to change purchase or repeat behavior.
