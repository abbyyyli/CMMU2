    1. Customers change even when they are satisfied1. 顧客滿意，也可能改變選擇

    Key idea重點

    A need is the underlying requirement; a want is how someone chooses to meet it. Time, companions, location and mood can change that choice. Repeating the same purchase can also reduce the extra enjoyment from it.需要是底層需求；想要是選擇如何滿足它。時間、同伴、地點與心情會改變選擇；重複消費同一樣東西，額外帶來的享受也可能下降。

    Class example課堂例子

    The lecturer asks whether a favorite dish would still be attractive every day. A diner can like the food and still want variety. A switch therefore calls for understanding the occasion, rather than immediately offering a discount.老師問：最愛的菜若天天吃，還會一樣想吃嗎？顧客喜歡食物，仍可能想換口味。因此，遇到轉換選擇，要先了解情境，而非立刻降價。

    Concept概念Meaning意思Do not confuse with別混淆
    Diminishing marginal utility邊際效用遞減Each additional unit can add less satisfaction.每多消費一單位，增加的滿足可能變少。The first purchase being a bad choice.第一次購買就是錯的。
    Variety-seeking追求多樣性Switching to experience something different.為了體驗不同選項而轉換。Dissatisfaction with the previous brand.對原品牌不滿。
    Marketing funnel行銷漏斗Awareness → interest → trial/purchase → repeat. Measure each step.知曉→興趣→試用／購買→重購；各階段分別衡量。Views automatically becoming purchases.觀看次數自然變成購買。

    中文邏輯顧客沒再買，先分清楚是不滿意、情境改變，還是想換口味。

    Pause & think暫停想一想

    A satisfied diner chooses another restaurant. What would distinguish variety-seeking from a service problem?滿意的顧客改吃另一家，要如何分辨追求多樣性與服務問題？

    2. A different feature must create a useful benefit2. 屬性不同，還要帶來有用利益

    Key idea重點

    A USP (Unique Selling Proposition) explains a distinctive reason to choose. An RTB (Reason to Believe) supports the promised benefit. 可信理由 Ask what improves for the customer and why the claim is believable. Marketing myopia starts when the feature becomes more important than the customer’s need.USP（獨特銷售主張）說明選擇品牌的獨特理由；RTB（相信承諾的理由）支持承諾的利益。要問顧客得到什麼改善、為何可信。只重視產品屬性而忽略需求，就容易形成行銷近視。

    Lecturer example老師例子Decision lesson決策重點
    Shampoo洗髮精Similar functional claims can be copied. Explain a difference buyers value.相似功能訴求容易被複製，要說清楚顧客重視的差異。
    Iceberg-water beer冰山水啤酒An unusual ingredient needs a benefit worth choosing and paying for.特殊原料仍須帶來值得選擇與付費的利益。
    Mont Fleur, Minéré, Heartsease and “Another bloody water”Mont Fleur、Minéré、Heartsease 與「Another bloody water」Origin, natural-source imagery, playful naming and humor give water different meanings.產地聯想、天然水源形象、趣味命名與幽默，讓水有不同意義。
    True Coffee-branded waterTrue Coffee 品牌水A name and package should help customers recognize the product category.名稱與包裝應協助顧客理解產品品類。
    Oxygen-enhanced / activated water富氧／活化水A technical claim alone does not explain a useful benefit or a reason to pay more.技術宣稱本身，還沒解釋有用利益與溢價理由。

    Reasoning推理

    Use a short chain: feature → credible benefit → customer choice → repeatable delivery. A missing link weakens the position, even when the feature sounds impressive.用簡短因果鏈：屬性→可信利益→顧客選擇→持續交付。其中一環不成立，即使屬性聽起來很厲害，定位仍會變弱。

    3. Relationships and brand meaning must carry into the experience3. 關係與品牌意義，要落實到體驗

    Key idea重點

    CRM (Customer Relationship Management) uses knowledge of customers to improve relationships. CEM (Customer Experience Management) focuses on the experience across interactions. Membership rules and data are tools; the visit is where the promise is delivered.CRM（顧客關係管理）運用顧客知識改善關係；CEM（顧客體驗管理）重視各次互動的體驗。會員規則與資料是工具，真正的承諾要在造訪時交付。

    Class example課堂例子

    At Greyhound, the lecturer had previously received VIP (Very Important Person) attention. Later, an expired membership blocked his birthday ice-cream benefit. His question was why a once-heavy customer’s declining engagement had not led to useful follow-up.在 Greyhound，老師過去曾得到 VIP（重要貴賓）待遇，後來卻因會員到期領不到生日冰淇淋。他關心的是：曾經常消費的顧客逐漸不再來，為何沒有適當追蹤？

    Example例子What to learn學習重點
    McDonald’s “gone in 60 seconds”McDonald’s「60 秒」訴求A speed benefit must fit the local dining occasion. Research the local motive before copying a campaign.速度利益須符合在地用餐情境；複製活動前，先理解當地動機。
    Ralph Lauren and Beverly Hills Polo ClubRalph Lauren 與 Beverly Hills Polo ClubSimilar rider, number and bear imagery can borrow familiar brand codes. Ask how the brand keeps a distinct identity.相似騎士、數字與熊圖像可借用熟悉品牌符號，仍須思考品牌如何維持獨特識別。

    Application應用延伸

    A practical response to the Greyhound problem is to review reminders, falling visit frequency and service recovery. These are possible follow-up actions; the lesson is to connect relationship information with a useful next experience.對 Greyhound 問題的可行延伸，是檢視到期提醒、造訪頻率下降與服務補救。重點是把關係資訊，轉成下一次有用的體驗。

    Pause & think暫停想一想

    A company has many members but fewer return visits. What relationship problem could the member count hide?會員很多，回訪卻下降，會員數可能掩蓋什麼關係問題？

    4. Define the buyer, the need and the occasion4. 界定買家、需要與情境

    Key idea重點

    STP (Segmentation, Targeting, and Positioning) groups customers by relevant differences, chooses whom to serve and builds a clear brand meaning. Demographics describe traits such as age; psychographics describe interests, motives, values and lifestyles. 心理生活型態 Neither age nor a generation label fully explains a purchase.STP（市場區隔、目標市場選擇與定位）依相關差異分群、選擇服務對象，再建立清楚品牌意義。人口統計描述年齡等特徵；心理生活型態描述興趣、動機、價值與生活方式。年齡或世代標籤，都不能完整解釋購買。

    Class example課堂例子Key distinction關鍵區別
    Medical supplies for monks僧侶醫療用品The monk uses the product; a lay donor may buy it. Buyer and user can differ.僧侶使用產品，在家捐贈者可能才是買家；買家與使用者可能不同。
    Brand’s Essence of Chicken白蘭氏雞精Messages aimed at students, patients and older adults illustrate broad positioning across occasions.對學生、病人與長者的訊息，呈現跨情境的廣泛定位。
    Phillip Wain and snake foodPhillip Wain 與蛇飼料A narrow target can make an offer relevant, while limiting the reachable audience.窄客群可提高關聯性，也限制可觸及人數。
    Dom PérignonDom Pérignon 香檳Special-occasion prestige can support a high price but limit purchase frequency.特殊場合的地位意義可支持高價，也限制購買頻率。
    Classical music and generation labels古典樂與世代標籤Study local experiences and motives instead of treating birth year as behavior.研究在地經驗與動機，不把出生年份直接當成行為。

    Reasoning推理

    Public consumption and gift-giving can add a status-signaling motive. A private everyday purchase may use different criteria. The same person can therefore want different brands in different situations.公開消費與送禮可能增加地位表達動機；日常私人購買可能使用不同標準。同一個人，在不同情境下也可能想買不同品牌。

    5. Keep the strategy, tools and delivery consistent5. 策略、工具與交付保持一致

    Key idea重點

    Start with the company’s purpose, turn it into specific objectives and align the customer promise with actions. The 4Ps (Product, Price, Place, and Promotion), plus the lecturer’s emphasis on People, should support the same position. Seasonality changes decisions about stock, price and staffing.先從企業存在目的出發，轉成具體目標，再讓承諾與行動一致。4Ps（產品、價格、通路與推廣），加上老師強調的人員，應支持同一定位；季節性則影響庫存、定價與人力安排。

    Tool工具Question it answers回答什麼Do not confuse with別混淆
    SWOT (Strengths, Weaknesses, Opportunities, and Threats)SWOT（優勢、劣勢、機會與威脅）Which internal and external factors shape the decision?哪些內、外部因素影響決策？Calling an external trend a company strength.把外部趨勢當公司優勢。
    BCG (Boston Consulting Group) growth–share matrixBCG（波士頓顧問公司）成長市占矩陣How do market growth and relative market share differ across business units?各事業的市場成長與相對市占如何？A fixed sequence that every business must follow.每個事業必經的固定順序。
    PLC (Product Life Cycle)PLC（產品生命週期）How do sales change through introduction, growth, maturity and decline?銷售如何經歷導入、成長、成熟與衰退？The BCG matrix’s two axes.BCG 的兩個分類軸。

    Class example課堂例子

    The lecturer links environmental scanning to changing vehicle technology and fuel rules. Scanning means noticing changes early enough to adjust plans. CSR (Corporate Social Responsibility) and sustainability also ask whether growth supports long-term customer and community welfare.老師以車輛科技與燃料規則變化談環境掃描：及早察覺變化，才能調整計畫。CSR（企業社會責任）與永續，也要求思考成長是否支持顧客與社區的長期福祉。

    6. Choose research for the information needed6. 依資訊需要選研究方法

    Information needed需要的資訊Method方法Why / main limit原因／主要限制
    Why people choose or avoid an offer為何選擇或避開產品Qualitative interviews or focus groups質性訪談或焦點團體Explore motives and the choice process; do not estimate population percentages from a few interviews.探索動機與選擇過程；少數訪談不能估整體比例。
    How many, how often or how much多少人、多常、多少量Quantitative surveys or numerical records量化問卷或數值紀錄Compare counts, amounts and patterns with a suitable sample and clear measures.以適當樣本與清楚衡量，比較人數、數量與模式。
    What people actually do人們實際怎麼做Observation or behavioral records觀察或行為紀錄Shows actions; the private reason still needs exploration.看得到行動，內在原因仍需探索。

    Class example課堂例子

    Fabric cleaner can offer utilitarian value through stain removal and hedonic value through fragrance or feel. A car or phone can also provide both. Research should find which benefits matter for the target occasion.衣物清潔劑可透過去污提供功能價值，透過香味或觸感提供享樂價值；汽車或手機也可同時提供兩者。研究要找出目標情境中，哪些利益真正重要。

    中文邏輯先寫需要知道什麼，再選方法；「為什麼」與「有多少」需要不同證據。

    Pause & think暫停想一想

    Interviews reveal a possible motive. What additional method would show how common it is?訪談找到一種可能動機後，用什麼方法了解它有多普遍？

    7. Compare markets through business effects7. 用商業影響比較市場

    Key idea重點

    At 22:02, the lecturer says a comparison of two markets or an application of SWOT may be asked. His examples include Thailand, Laos, Vietnam and Singapore, with differences in population, age, income, growth and religion.22:02，老師提到可能要求比較兩個市場或運用 SWOT。他舉泰國、寮國、越南與新加坡，討論人口、年齡、所得、成長與宗教差異。

    Compare比較面向Explain the effect解釋影響
    Customers and cultural fit顧客與文化適配Can the reachable target afford, use and accept the offer? Religion can affect food choices such as pork.可觸及客群是否買得起、用得到、願接受？宗教會影響豬肉等食物選擇。
    Competition and access競爭與接觸What alternatives and channels already serve the need?哪些替代品與通路已滿足這個需要？
    Company feasibility公司可行性Can the company deliver the offer at a workable cost and scale?公司能否以可行成本與規模交付產品？

    Reasoning推理

    A useful comparison uses the same criteria on both sides and explains how each difference changes the decision. A larger national population is only a starting fact; it does not identify reachable buyers or expected profit.有用的比較要對兩邊使用相同標準，並解釋差異如何改變決策。全國人口多只是起點，還沒有指出可觸及買家與預期利潤。

    8. Follow the customer’s alternatives and the channel effects8. 看顧客替代選項與通路影響

    Concept概念Definition定義Class example課堂例子
    Direct competitor 直接競爭直接競爭者A similar-category offer considered for the same occasion.同一情境下考慮的相似品類產品。Similar fruit drinks from Sappe and Unif.Sappe 與 Unif 的相似果汁飲品。
    Indirect competitor 間接競爭間接競爭者A different category that can meet the same need. Define the buying occasion.不同品類卻可滿足同一需要；須先定購買情境。Plain water can replace a sports or fruit drink when the buyer wants refreshment.想解渴時，普通水可能取代運動或果汁飲品。
    Disintermediation 去中介化去中介化Selling without a previous distribution intermediary.跳過原本的通路中介銷售。The ice-cream vendor example introduces direct selling and retailer conflict.冰淇淋販售例子帶出去中介與零售商衝突。

    Reasoning推理

    If a customer can choose another offer instead of this one, it matters competitively. Direct selling can improve control and access to customers, but fulfillment work and conflict with existing retailers also change.顧客若能改買另一個選項，它就有競爭意義。直接銷售可提高控制與接觸顧客的能力，但履約工作與既有零售商衝突也會改變。

    10. Separate sales growth, consumption and customer trust10. 分清銷售成長、消耗量與顧客信任

    12. Cold call practice12. 口答練習

    Why can a satisfied customer switch?滿意顧客為何換品牌？

    The occasion can change, or the customer may seek variety without being dissatisfied.情境可能改變，顧客也可能在沒有不滿下追求多樣性。

    What makes an RTB useful?RTB 何時才有用？

    It credibly supports a benefit the target customer values. A technical feature alone is incomplete.它可信地支持目標顧客重視的利益，單有技術屬性並不完整。

    Why did Greyhound matter in the review?複習中 Greyhound 的重點？

    A membership rule overrode the relationship experience of a once-heavy customer.會員規則蓋過了曾經重度消費顧客的關係體驗。

    Why define the purchase occasion before naming rivals?列對手前為何先定情境？

    Different products can replace one another when they meet the same need on that occasion.不同產品可能在該情境中，因滿足同一需要而互相替代。

    What does observation miss?觀察缺少什麼？

    It records behavior, but the private motive needs further exploration.它記錄行為，但內在動機仍須進一步探索。

    Why are the two downsizing percentages different?減量的兩個百分比為何不同？

    The volume reduction uses 330 as its base; the increase in price per mL uses the new volume, 320.含量減幅以 330 為分母；每 mL 價格增幅涉及新含量 320。

    13. Answer templates13. 答題模板

    Study answer structure複習用答題結構

    Decision → relevant fact → reason → trade-off → useful next action. For a comparison, apply the same criteria to both options. For research, state the information needed before naming the method. For positioning, connect the chosen benefit to the customer’s alternatives.決策→相關事實→理由→取捨→有用下一步。比較題對兩個選項使用相同標準；研究題先說資訊需要，再選方法；定位題將選定利益連到顧客替代選項。

    Try the separate S6 mock paper and expand its suggested answers.到獨立的 S6 模擬頁作答，再展開參考答案。
