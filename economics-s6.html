    Asymmetric Information資訊不對稱

    When one side of a deal knows more than the other, two things can go wrong. Hidden quality or risk changes who shows up to trade, and hidden behavior changes what people do after a contract is signed. This session traces both problems, the fixes that reduce them, and what they mean for the value of a degree.交易一方比另一方知道得多時，會出兩種問題：隱藏的品質或風險，改變「誰來交易」；隱藏的行為，改變「簽約後怎麼做」。本堂追蹤這兩種問題、能減輕它們的方法，以及它們對學歷價值的意義。

    1. Who knows what, and what is hidden?1. 誰知道什麼？隱藏的是什麼？

    Start every case by naming the informed side. Then ask whether the hidden thing already exists or is a later choice.每個案例先指出掌握資訊的一方，再問被隱藏的東西是原本就存在，還是之後才做的選擇。

    Concept概念Definition定義Example例子Do not confuse with不要混淆
    Imperfect information不完全資訊A decision maker lacks some relevant knowledge.決策者缺少部分相關知識。Two traders are both unsure about next year's flood.兩位交易者都不確定明年會不會淹水。Asymmetric information, which needs one side to know more.資訊不對稱，那需要其中一方知道得更多。
    Asymmetric information資訊不對稱資訊不對稱One party has better relevant information than the other.一方掌握的相關資訊比另一方好。A seller knows the car was flood-damaged; the buyer cannot tell.賣家知道車子泡過水，買家看不出來。Uncertainty that both sides share.雙方都一樣不確定的情況。
    Hidden type隱藏類型An existing characteristic the other side cannot observe, such as quality, health risk or ability.對方觀察不到的既有特徵，例如品質、健康風險或能力。Engine damage that existed before the sale.出售前就已存在的引擎損傷。A choice made after the contract.簽約後才做的選擇。
    Hidden action隱藏行動Behavior the other side cannot fully observe, such as effort or precaution.對方無法完全觀察的行為，例如努力程度或預防措施。An owner skips agreed maintenance after buying coverage.車主投保後，略過原本約定的保養。A trait the person already had.當事人原本就有的特質。

    “Before or after signing” is a handy shortcut, not the definition. Ask whether the problem is selection among existing types or behavior that responds to incentives. Plans can be made before signing, and private information can last after it.「簽約前或簽約後」只是方便的捷徑，不是定義。要問的是：問題出在既有類型的篩選，還是行為對誘因的反應？行為可能在簽約前就計畫好，私有資訊也可能在簽約後繼續存在。

    2. Adverse selection in used cars2. 二手車市場的逆向選擇

    Buyers who cannot tell quality apart pay for the average car, and paying the average drives good cars away.分不出品質的買家只肯付「平均車」的價錢，而這個平均價正是把好車趕走的原因。

    In Akerlof's lemons model, sellers know whether a car is a reliable plum or an unreliable lemon劣質車, but buyers cannot tell. Buyers therefore pay for the expected quality on offer, and that price is too low for some owners of good cars.在 Akerlof 的檸檬車模型中，賣家知道車子是可靠的好車（plum）還是不可靠的劣質車（lemon），買家卻分不出來。所以買家只肯按市場上的預期品質出價，而這個價格對部分好車車主來說太低。

    Quality hidden品質被隱藏→Buyers pay for the average買家按平均品質出價→Good cars withdrawn好車退出→Average quality falls平均品質下降→Offers fall again出價再下降

    In this example the market shrinks to lemons only, but it does not disappear: lemons still trade between ฿80,000 and ฿100,000. Adverse selection逆向選擇 destroys the gains from good-car trades, not necessarily the whole market. Real used-car markets survive partly because inspections, vehicle histories, warranties and dealer reputation narrow the information gap.這個例子裡，市場縮小到只剩劣質車，但並沒有消失：劣質車仍在 8 萬到 10 萬泰銖之間成交。逆向選擇毀掉的是好車交易的利益，不一定是整個市場。現實的二手車市場能存在，部分原因是檢查、車輛紀錄、保固與車商聲譽縮小了資訊差距。

    中文邏輯不是「便宜的車一定差」；是買家分不出品質，只能出平均價，好車車主因此退出，市場平均品質真的被拉低。

    3. Insurance pools and the death spiral3. 保險池與惡性循環

    In insurance the buyer often holds the private information, so the premium decides which risks stay in the pool.保險市場裡，掌握私有資訊的常常是買方，所以保費高低決定了哪些風險留在保險池裡。

    Term名詞Meaning意義Example例子
    Expected loss預期損失The probability-weighted average loss.以機率加權的平均損失。A 10% chance of a ฿100,000 claim gives ฿10,000.有 10% 機率發生 10 萬泰銖理賠，預期損失是 1 萬泰銖。
    Actuarially fair premium精算公平保費A premium equal to expected covered claims, before admin costs and profit.等於預期理賠金額的保費，尚未加上行政成本與利潤。Expected claims of ฿10,000 imply a fair premium of ฿10,000.預期理賠 1 萬泰銖，公平保費就是 1 萬泰銖。
    Risk pooling風險分攤風險分攤Combining many uncertain losses so premiums can pay the claims.把許多不確定的損失集合起來，讓保費足以支付理賠。Many households pay into one accident fund.許多家庭繳錢進同一個意外基金。
    Risk aversion風險趨避風險趨避Preferring a certain payment to a risky one with the same expected value.在預期值相同時，偏好確定的支出，而不是有風險的支出。Paying ฿15,000 to avoid a risk that averages ฿10,000.願意付 1.5 萬泰銖，避開平均只有 1 萬泰銖的風險。
    Death spiral惡性循環（死亡螺旋）Higher premiums push out lower risks, which raises premiums again.保費上升把低風險者擠出去，保費因此再上升。Each repricing leaves a riskier pool.每次重新定價，留下的保險池都更危險。

    A common premium follows the pool's average claims. If lower-risk buyers find it poor value and leave, the average and the premium rise. Risk aversion slows this: a careful buyer may pay above expected claims for peace of mind.統一保費跟著保險池的平均理賠走。如果低風險買家覺得不划算而退出，平均理賠和保費就會上升。風險趨避會減緩這個過程：謹慎的買家為了安心，可能願意付超過預期理賠的保費。

    Adverse selection逆向選擇

    Higher-risk people are keener to buy, so the insured pool is riskier than the population.高風險的人更想買保險，所以投保者的風險高於整體人口。

    Advantageous selection有利選擇

    Cautious people may both prevent losses and buy coverage, so the insured pool can be safer than the uninsured. Which pattern holds is an empirical question for each product and price.謹慎的人可能既會預防損失、也會投保，所以投保者反而可能比未投保者安全。哪一種情況成立，要看各產品與價格的實證資料。

    Optional: MRU's stylized death spiral可展開補充：MRU 的簡化惡性循環例子

    In MRU's teaching example, covered costs spread evenly from $0 to $30,000, so the first premium averages $15,000. If everyone expecting less than that drops out, the remaining pool averages $22,500, and the cycle can repeat. A continuous range of risks is what turns a smaller pool into a full spiral.MRU 的教學例子中，承保成本平均分布在 0 到 3 萬美元，所以第一次保費平均為 1.5 萬美元。若預期成本低於這個數字的人全部退出，剩下的保險池平均為 2.25 萬美元，循環可能一再重複。正是風險連續分布，才讓「保險池縮小」變成完整的惡性循環。

    Suppose cautious drivers are both safer and keener on insurance. How could an insurer's pool end up less risky than the uninsured? What evidence would you need before assuming either adverse or advantageous selection?假設謹慎的駕駛人既比較安全，也比較想投保。保險公司的投保者為什麼可能比未投保者風險更低？在假定是逆向選擇或有利選擇之前，你需要什麼證據？

    4. Fixing adverse selection4. 處理逆向選擇

    Either reveal the hidden type or change who joins the pool. Each fix has a cost.要嘛揭露隱藏類型，要嘛改變誰加入保險池。每種方法都有代價。

    Screening篩選 is started by the less-informed side, such as a buyer ordering an inspection or an insurer asking for verifiable records. Certification passes on the result of a credible test; a badge with no real test behind it adds little.篩選由資訊較少的一方發動，例如買家請人驗車，或保險公司要求可驗證的紀錄。認證則是傳達一項可信檢驗的結果；背後沒有真正檢驗的標章，作用很小。

    Response方法How it helps如何發揮作用Limit限制
    Independent inspection or vehicle history獨立驗車或車輛紀錄Verified quality lets prices match quality, so good sellers can return.品質經驗證後，價格能反映品質，好車賣家可能回到市場。Costs money, can miss damage, and the inspector may have conflicts.需要花錢、可能漏看損傷，檢查者也可能有利益衝突。
    Seller warranty賣方保固Lemons generate more claims, so the promise carries information (section 8).劣質車的理賠較多，所以這個承諾本身帶有資訊（見第 8 節）。Works only if enforceable and costly for low types to copy.必須可執行，而且低品質者模仿的成本夠高，才有效。
    Risk classification風險分類Premiums follow observed risk predictors, so low risks face fairer prices.保費依可觀察的風險指標訂定，低風險者面對較公平的價格。High risks may face unaffordable terms, and less risk is shared.高風險者可能負擔不起，風險分攤的程度也下降。
    Group insurance團體保險Membership is not chosen on individual health risk, which broadens the pool.成員資格不是依個人健康風險選擇，保險池因此較廣。Plan design can still attract some risks, and coverage may be tied to a job.方案設計仍可能吸引特定風險，保障也可能綁在工作上。
    Broad enrollment incentives or requirements擴大投保的誘因或規定Bringing in lower risks lowers average claims and steadies premiums.讓低風險者加入，可降低平均理賠並穩定保費。Results depend on take-up, subsidies, enforcement and benefit design.效果取決於參加率、補貼、執行力度與給付設計。

    中文邏輯分得越清楚，越不會逆向選擇；但高風險的人也可能越難負擔。效率、風險分攤和公平是不同的問題。

    5. Moral hazard: coverage changes behavior5. 道德風險：保障改變行為

    Moral hazard is about incentives under imperfect monitoring, not about bad people.道德風險談的是監督不完全時的誘因，不是在說誰品德不好。

    Moral hazard道德風險 arises when someone does not bear the full consequences of an action that others cannot fully observe. Insurance, guarantees, delegation and incomplete contracts can all shift consequences. No lie or fraud is needed.當某人不必承擔自己行為的全部後果，而別人又無法完全觀察這個行為時，就會出現道德風險。保險、保證、委託與不完整的契約都可能轉移後果。過程中不需要有人說謊或詐欺。

    Shop buys fire insurance商店投保火險→Insurer bears much of a fire loss保險公司承擔大部分火災損失→Private payoff from prevention falls自己做預防的好處變少→Precautions slip預防措施鬆懈→Expected claims rise預期理賠上升

    This is not adverse selection: the same owner changes behavior after the contract, rather than a riskier type choosing to buy.這不是逆向選擇：是同一位店主在簽約後改變行為，而不是風險較高的類型選擇投保。

    Insurance and the use of care保險與醫療使用

    If a visit uses ฿1,000 of real resources but the patient pays a ฿200 copayment, the patient faces a lower price. That is a movement along the demand curve for visits, not a shift of it. The ฿1,000 cost does not disappear; the pool pays the difference.如果一次看診使用 1,000 泰銖的實際資源，病人卻只付 200 泰銖的定額負擔，病人面對的價格就變低了。這是沿著看診需求線移動，不是需求線移動。1,000 泰銖的成本並沒有消失，差額由保險池支付。

    More insured care is not automatically waste. Some extra visits are worth less than their cost; others were blocked by cash or uncertainty and are worth more. Compare marginal benefit with resource cost before calling the change a loss.保險帶來的額外醫療不一定是浪費。有些新增看診的價值低於成本；有些原本因為缺錢或不確定而被擋下，價值反而高於成本。判斷是不是損失之前，要先比較邊際利益與資源成本。

    中文邏輯「道德」二字容易誤導。重點是行為的成本與利益分配變了，未必有人說謊。

    An insurer's pooled premium attracts higher-risk applicants, and some customers then cut back on preventive effort. Separate the two problems and suggest one targeted response for each.保險公司的統一保費吸引了風險較高的投保人，之後部分客戶又減少預防措施。請把這兩個問題分開，並各提出一個對症的處理方法。

    6. Principal-agent problems6. 委託代理問題

    Hiring an expert helps because they know more, and that same gap creates room for conflict.請專家幫忙有用，是因為他們懂得比較多；同樣的落差，也留下利益衝突的空間。

    A principal委託人 hires an agent to do a task. Problems arise when the agent's better information combines with incentives that differ from the principal's. MRU's examples are a mechanic who may recommend unneeded repairs, a taxi driver who knows the routes better than a tourist, and an employee whose effort is hard to see.委託人雇用代理人完成某項工作。當代理人掌握較多資訊，誘因又和委託人不同時，問題就出現了。MRU 的例子包括：可能建議不必要維修的技師、比觀光客更熟路線的計程車司機，以及努力程度難以觀察的員工。

    Element要素Repair case修車例子
    Principal委託人Car owner who wants a reliable repair at a fair cost.希望以合理價格得到可靠維修的車主。
    Agent代理人Mechanic who diagnoses the problem and also sells the repair.負責診斷、同時也賣維修服務的技師。
    Information gap資訊落差Only the mechanic can judge whether a part really needs replacing.只有技師能判斷零件是否真的需要更換。
    Conflict利益衝突Extra billed work pays the mechanic even when the owner does not need it.即使車主不需要，多做的工程也能讓技師多賺。
    Wider loss更大的損失Fearing overcharges, owners refuse even useful preventive work.車主怕被多收錢，連有用的預防保養也拒絕。

    The deepest cost is not only overcharging. Distrust makes people turn down trades that would have helped them.最大的代價不只是被多收錢，而是不信任讓人拒絕了原本對自己有利的交易。

    Shirking is the effort version: when effort is costly and pay barely depends on it, an agent may do less than agreed. A bad outcome alone does not prove shirking, because luck, demand and tools also matter.怠工是努力程度上的版本：努力有成本、薪酬又幾乎不受努力影響時，代理人可能做得比約定的少。單看壞結果不能證明有人怠工，因為運氣、需求與工具也會影響結果。

    7. Reducing moral hazard7. 減輕道德風險

    Narrow the information gap, or change who bears the consequences.縮小資訊落差，或改變由誰承擔後果。

    Tool工具How it works運作方式Limit限制
    Monitoring and audits監督與稽核Better observation raises the chance of detection, so misconduct pays less.觀察得更清楚，被發現的機率提高，不當行為就不划算。Costly, and people may game the measured number.成本高，而且人們可能只針對被衡量的指標做表面功夫。
    Reputation and reviews聲譽與評價Bad conduct today costs future customers, and reviews also inform buyers.今天的不當行為會讓未來流失客人，評價也同時提供買家資訊。Fake reviews and one-off sellers weaken it.假評價和只做一次生意的賣家會削弱效果。
    Independent diagnosis獨立診斷獨立診斷The assessor cannot profit from selling the repair, so there is less reason to exaggerate.診斷者無法從賣維修中獲利，誇大問題的理由就變少。Independence does not guarantee competence.獨立不代表一定有能力。
    Warranty on the work施工保固Poor work leads to seller-paid repeat repairs.做得不好，就要由賣方出錢重修。Must be clear and enforceable.條款必須清楚且可執行。
    Pay linked to results績效連動薪酬The agent shares the gains from good outcomes.代理人分享好結果帶來的利益。Outcomes are noisy, and metrics can be gamed.結果受雜訊影響，指標也可能被操弄。
    Cost sharing部分負擔The insured bears part of each loss or bill, so private incentives move closer to real cost.被保險人自付部分損失或帳單，讓私人誘因更接近真實成本。Weakens protection and may deter valuable care.保障變弱，也可能讓人放棄有價值的治療。

    These are separate examples, not one combined policy.這些是各自獨立的例子，不是同一張保單的合併條款。

    Information as a public good資訊是一種公共財

    A trustworthy product test is costly to produce, but once shared it is nonrival and hard to keep from non-payers. Users free ride, so reliable testing may be underprovided. That raises the question of how independent information is financed; it does not mean every review platform fails.可信的產品測試製作成本高，但一旦公開，就不具競爭性，也很難排除沒付錢的人。使用者會搭便車，所以可靠的測試可能供給不足。這提出的問題是獨立資訊的經費從哪裡來，而不是說每個評價平台都會失敗。

    8. Signaling: making a claim believable8. 訊號傳遞：讓宣稱變得可信

    A signal works when the low type would not want to copy it.訊號有效的條件，是低品質的一方不想模仿。

    Signaling訊號傳遞 is started by the informed side. In MRU's historical example, Hyundai improved its cars and backed them with an unusually long warranty. For reliable cars, expected warranty claims are low; for unreliable cars, the same promise would be expensive. That cost difference is what makes the warranty believable.訊號傳遞由掌握資訊的一方發動。在 MRU 的歷史例子中，Hyundai 提升了車子的品質，並提供特別長的保固。可靠的車預期保固理賠少；不可靠的車若做同樣承諾，代價會很高。正是這個成本差異，讓保固變得可信。

    Question問題Signaling訊號傳遞Screening篩選
    Who acts?由誰行動？The better-informed side.資訊較多的一方。The less-informed side.資訊較少的一方。
    Car example汽車例子Seller offers a warranty.賣家提供保固。Buyer orders an inspection.買家請人驗車。
    Key test判斷重點Would a low type want to copy it?低品質者會想模仿嗎？Does the test actually separate the types?這項檢驗真的能區分類型嗎？

    Separating outcome分離結果Different types make different choices, so the choice reveals the type.不同類型做出不同選擇，選擇本身就揭露了類型。

    A credible signal need not be costly for the good type. What matters is that copying is costly for the bad type and that the promise can be honored.     9. Education: skills, signals or prior ability?9. 教育：技能、訊號，還是原本的能力？

    Graduates earn more. Three mechanisms can explain it, and they can work at the same time.大學畢業生賺得比較多。有三種機制可以解釋，而且可能同時發生。

    Explanation解釋What changes改變的是什麼Chain因果鏈Example例子
    Human capital人力資本人力資本Productive skills.生產技能。Study builds skills, output rises, and employers value the worker more.讀書培養技能，產出提高，雇主更看重這位員工。An analyst learns spreadsheet modeling and forecasts better.分析師學會試算表建模，預測得更準。
    Signaling訊號傳遞What employers know about existing traits.雇主對既有特質的了解。Finishing a hard degree shows persistence, employers update their view, and offers improve even with unchanged skills.完成困難的學位顯示了毅力，雇主改變看法，即使技能沒變，條件也變好。A demanding degree helps an employer infer persistence.要求嚴格的學位，幫助雇主推斷應徵者有毅力。
    Ability bias能力偏誤Who gets educated.誰接受了教育。Prior ability or resources raise both schooling and earnings, so the raw gap overstates the effect of schooling.原本的能力或資源同時提高受教程度與收入，所以表面上的差距會高估教育的效果。Highly motivated students might earn more even without the degree.動機很強的學生，即使沒有學位也可能賺得比較多。

    中文邏輯薪水較高，可能是學到東西，可能是雇主終於看出能力，也可能本來就有優勢；同一筆相關資料分不出三者的比例。

    The sheepskin effect文憑完成溢價 is a jump in earnings at the point a credential is completed, beyond what extra years of study would predict. It fits signaling, but completion can also open licensed jobs, finish a key skill, or mark people who differ in unobserved ways. It does not prove that education is pure signaling.文憑效應（sheepskin effect）指的是：拿到學位那一刻的收入跳升，超過多讀幾年所能解釋的幅度。這和訊號理論相符，但完成學位也可能打開需要執照的工作、補齊關鍵技能，或標示出在看不見的方面本來就不同的人。它不能證明教育純粹只是訊號。

    Optional: from skills to wages可展開補充：從技能到工資
    MPL = Δoutput / Δlabor (physical units)
    MRPL = MR × MPL (revenue); for a price taker, MRPL = P × MPL
    A wage-taking firm hires while MRPL ≥ wage勞動邊際產量 MPL＝產出變動／勞動變動（實物單位）。勞動邊際收益產值 MRPL＝MR×MPL（收入）；價格接受者的 MRPL＝P×MPL。接受既定工資的廠商，只要 MRPL≥工資就繼續僱人。

    MRU's lesson calls a dollar-valued gain the “marginal product of labor”. Standard texts call that the marginal revenue product. Keep the units straight: output versus money.MRU 的課程把以金額計算的增加量稱為「勞動邊際產量」；一般教科書稱之為邊際收益產值。單位要分清楚：產出量還是金額。

    Training raises skills訓練提高技能→MPL and MRPL riseMPL 與 MRPL 上升→Labor demand shifts right勞動需求右移→With supply unchanged, wage and employment rise供給不變時，工資與就業上升

    That market result needs a broad rise in demand for the skill, not one person's degree. If many people gain the skill, labor supply also shifts, and the wage effect can be smaller.這個市場層面的結論，需要的是對該技能的需求普遍上升，而不是某一個人拿到學位。如果很多人都學會這項技能，勞動供給也會移動，工資效果可能較小。

    10. The Econ Duel: learning or signaling?10. Econ Duel：學習還是訊號？

    Tyler Cowen and Alex Tabarrok agree that graduates earn more. They disagree about why, and about what that means for society.Tyler Cowen 和 Alex Tabarrok 都同意畢業生賺得比較多，分歧在於原因，以及這對社會代表什麼。

    Speaker講者Position立場Examples used使用的例子
    CowenLasting learning explains much of the gap; education builds critical thinking and social skills.持久的學習解釋了大部分差距；教育培養批判思考與社交能力。Educated self-employed workers, Scandinavian schooling changes and Asian development; education works alongside infrastructure and governance.受過教育的自雇者、北歐的學制改革與亞洲的發展；教育要和基礎建設及治理一起發揮作用。
    TabarrokCredentials reveal ability and persistence and open doors to careers.文憑揭露能力與毅力，並打開職涯大門。Students cheering canceled classes yet valuing the degree; a credential race that disadvantages people without degrees.學生為停課歡呼卻仍看重學位；文憑競賽讓沒有學位的人處於劣勢。

    These are debate positions. The video does not give enough study detail to split the wage gap into learning and signaling shares.以上是辯論中的立場。影片提供的研究細節不足以把薪資差距拆成學習與訊號各占多少。

    Private return versus social return個人報酬與社會報酬

    Private return個人報酬

    The student's extra earnings and other benefits, minus tuition, effort, time and forgone pay.學生增加的收入與其他好處，扣掉學費、努力、時間與放棄的薪水。

    Social return社會報酬

    Extra real output, better job matching and spillovers, minus the real resources used. Tuition can be a transfer; teachers' time and buildings are real costs.增加的實際產出、更好的工作配對與外溢效果，扣掉實際使用的資源。學費可能只是移轉；老師的時間和校舍才是實際成本。

    In a pure credential race, one person's degree can lift their rank without raising total skill. If everyone responds by studying longer for unchanged jobs, credential inflation學歷門檻膨脹 uses resources and shuts out non-graduates. Better matching can still create some social value, and some jobs really have become more complex.在純粹的文憑競賽中，一個人的學位能提高他的排名，卻沒有增加整體技能。如果大家都為了工作內容沒變的職位多讀幾年，學歷門檻膨脹就會耗用資源，並把沒有學位的人排除在外。不過，較好的配對仍可能創造一些社會價值，有些工作也確實變得更複雜。

    What evidence could separate the explanations?什麼證據能區分這幾種解釋？

    Comparison比較方式What it can test可檢驗什麼Why caution remains為何仍需謹慎

    A wage gap compares different people, so it lacks the counterfactual反事實: what the same people would earn without the degree.     11. Do not mix these up11. 不要混淆這些概念

    Uncertainty means asymmetric information.有不確定就是資訊不對稱。

    Both sides can lack the same facts. Look for a relative gap.雙方可能缺少同樣的資訊；要找的是相對落差。

    Adverse selection means any bad outcome.逆向選擇泛指任何壞結果。

    Hidden types must change who participates.必須是隱藏類型改變了誰參與。

    Moral hazard means dishonest people.道德風險代表有人不誠實。

    It is an incentive and monitoring problem; no fraud is needed.這是誘因與監督的問題，不需要有詐欺。

    The signing date is the whole distinction.簽約日期就是唯一的分界。

    Ask: selection among types, or behavior under incentives?要問：是類型的篩選，還是誘因下的行為？

    Adverse selection always destroys the market.逆向選擇一定會毀掉市場。

    It can leave a smaller, stable pool.它可能只留下一個較小但穩定的市場。

    Screening and signaling are the same.篩選和訊號傳遞是同一件事。

    Check who starts the revealing action.要看是誰發動揭露資訊的行動。

    An expensive signal is automatically credible.昂貴的訊號自動可信。

    Ask whether a low type would copy it.要問低品質者會不會模仿。

    More insured care is all waste.保險帶來的額外醫療全是浪費。

    Compare each visit's benefit with its resource cost.要比較每次看診的利益與資源成本。

    Cost sharing has no downside.部分負擔沒有缺點。

    It weakens protection and can deter valuable care.它削弱保障，也可能讓人放棄有價值的治療。

    A graduate wage gap proves learning.畢業生的薪資差距證明了學習的效果。

    Signaling and ability bias can contribute too.訊號與能力偏誤也可能有貢獻。

    12. Cold call practice12. 課堂口頭問答練習

    What is adverse selection?什麼是逆向選擇？

    Hidden characteristics change who participates, so the uninformed side faces a worse average pool, as when a pooled used-car offer drives good cars away.隱藏特徵改變了參與者的組成，使資訊較少的一方面對較差的平均群體，例如二手車的混同出價把好車趕走。

    Why can risk aversion keep low-risk buyers insured?為什麼風險趨避能讓低風險者繼續投保？

    They value avoiding a large uncertain loss, so they may pay a premium above their expected claims.他們重視避開大額且不確定的損失，所以可能願意付高於預期理賠的保費。

    What is moral hazard?什麼是道德風險？

    Behavior that others cannot fully observe changes because someone else bears part of the consequences, as when an insured shop cuts fire precautions.別人無法完全觀察的行為，因為部分後果由他人承擔而改變，例如投保後的商店減少防火措施。

    How do reviews reduce moral hazard?評價如何減輕道德風險？

    They inform buyers and make bad conduct cost future business. Fake reviews weaken both effects.評價提供買家資訊，也讓不當行為付出流失未來生意的代價。假評價會同時削弱這兩個作用。

    Why can reliable test information be underprovided?為什麼可靠的測試資訊可能供給不足？

    It is costly to produce, but once shared it is nonrival and hard to exclude, so users free ride.製作成本高，但公開後不具競爭性又難以排除他人，使用者就會搭便車。

    How does signaling differ from screening?訊號傳遞和篩選有什麼不同？

    The informed side signals, for example with a warranty. The uninformed side screens, for example with an inspection.掌握資訊的一方傳遞訊號，例如提供保固；資訊較少的一方進行篩選，例如請人驗車。

    What is cheap talk?什麼是空口宣稱？

    A costless claim any type can make, so it does not separate types.任何類型都能說、沒有成本的宣稱，因此無法區分類型。

    What is the sheepskin effect?什麼是文憑效應？

    An earnings jump at credential completion beyond smooth years-of-schooling effects. It fits signaling but does not prove pure signaling.拿到文憑時的收入跳升，超過就學年數所能解釋的部分。它和訊號理論相符，但不能證明教育純粹是訊號。

    When can a contract menu screen types?合約選單何時能篩選類型？

    When different risks reliably choose different options. The resulting pool still has to be checked, not assumed.當不同風險的人會穩定地選擇不同方案時。選擇後形成的保險池仍要實際檢查，不能直接假設。

    13. Answer templates13. 答題模板

    Adverse selection逆向選擇

    “One party privately knows its type. Common terms attract a different mix of participants, which changes the quality or risk of the pool. That feedback can worsen the terms and eliminate useful trades, although the market need not collapse completely.”「一方私下知道自己的類型。統一條件吸引到不同組成的參與者，改變了群體的品質或風險。這種回饋可能讓條件更差、消滅有益的交易，但市場不一定完全崩潰。」

    Moral hazard道德風險

    “The contract changes the private consequences of an action that is hard to observe. Behavior can then shift costs to someone else. I identify the action that changes and compare monitoring or incentive fixes with their costs.”「契約改變了一項難以觀察的行為的私人後果，行為可能因此把成本轉嫁給別人。我會先指出改變的是哪個行為，再比較監督或誘因調整方法的成本。」

    Principal-agent委託代理

    “The principal delegates to an agent with better information. Their incentives differ, and the principal cannot fully verify the agent's action or advice. A good remedy narrows the information gap or the conflict, rather than simply assuming the expert is dishonest.”「委託人把工作交給資訊較多的代理人。兩者誘因不同，委託人又無法完全驗證代理人的行動或建議。好的解方是縮小資訊落差或利益衝突，而不是直接假設專家不誠實。」

    Credible signaling可信的訊號

    “The informed party takes an action that an inferior type would not want to copy. Buyers then update their beliefs. The key is differential cost and enforceability, not expense alone.”「掌握資訊的一方採取低品質者不想模仿的行動，買家因此更新看法。關鍵是成本差異與可執行性，而不只是花了多少錢。」

    Education debate教育辯論

    “Education can build skills and signal existing traits at the same time, and prior ability can raise both schooling and earnings. A wage premium alone cannot identify the causal shares, and private returns need not equal the gain in social output.”「教育可以同時培養技能並傳遞既有特質的訊號，原本的能力也可能同時提高受教程度與收入。單看薪資溢價無法辨認各因素的因果比例，個人報酬也不一定等於社會產出的增加。」
