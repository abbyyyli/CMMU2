    The Financial System金融體系

    Some people have money they do not need yet; others have plans they cannot pay for yet. This chapter follows the bridges between them: the interest rate that prices the crossing, the banks and markets that carry the money, why the bridges collapsed in 2008, and what all of it means for your own savings.有些人手上有暫時用不到的錢，有些人有計畫卻暫時付不起。本章追蹤連接兩邊的橋：決定過橋價格的利率、運送資金的銀行與市場、2008 年橋為什麼斷掉，以及這一切對你自己的儲蓄有什麼意義。

    2. Why people save and borrow2. 人為什麼儲蓄和借錢

    Saving and borrowing move spending across time. Income is hump-shaped over a life; spending does not have to be.儲蓄和借款是把花費在時間上搬動。一生的所得像一座山丘，花費不必跟著起伏。

    Stage階段Income vs consumption所得與消費Action行動Example例子
    Young年輕Income below consumption所得低於消費Borrow借款A loan for CMMU tuition.為 CMMU 學費貸款。
    Prime working years工作高峰期Income above consumption所得高於消費Repay debt, save還債、儲蓄Contributions to a provident fund.提撥公積金。
    Retired退休Income below consumption所得低於消費Dissave: spend past savings動用儲蓄Living on the fund after 60.60 歲後靠公積金生活。

    This is the life-cycle theory of saving. Smoothing consumption this way raises well-being even though lifetime income is unchanged. Three more forces shape how much people save. Time preference: some people strongly prefer spending now, others are patient. Nudges: when an employer enrolls workers in a retirement plan by default, they save more, although nothing about the incentives changed. Big investments: students borrow for education, firms borrow for projects, and entrepreneurs such as Howard Schultz, building Starbucks, raise money they do not have.這就是生命週期儲蓄理論。這樣平滑消費，即使一生總所得不變，生活也會更好。另外三股力量影響人們存多少：時間偏好：有人很想現在花，有人很有耐心。推力：雇主預設幫員工加入退休計畫，員工就存得比較多，雖然誘因完全沒變。大筆投資：學生借錢念書、企業借錢做專案，像 Howard Schultz 這樣的創業者要籌自己沒有的錢來建立 Starbucks。

    3. The market for loanable funds3. 可貸資金市場

    Treat saving and borrowing like any other market. The good being traded is the use of money for a while, and the interest rate is its price.把儲蓄和借款當成一般市場來看。交易的是「一段時間的資金使用權」，利率就是它的價格。

    Part組成Definition定義Example例子Do not confuse with不要混淆
    Supply of savingsSlopes up: a higher interest rate brings more savings into the market.向上斜：利率越高，進入市場的儲蓄越多。Higher deposit rates pull cash out from under the mattress.存款利率提高，把藏在床墊下的現金吸出來。Money supply, which the central bank controls.貨幣供給，那是央行控制的。
    Demand to borrowSlopes down: a lower interest rate makes more projects worth borrowing for.向下斜：利率越低，值得借錢去做的專案越多。A Thai SME buys a machine when loan rates fall.貸款利率下降時，泰國中小企業買新機器。Demand for money to hold as cash.持有現金的貨幣需求。
    Interest rateThe price of borrowing and the reward for saving.借款的價格，也是儲蓄的報酬。2% on a deposit, 7% on a business loan.存款 2%，企業貸款 7%。The real rate: nominal rate minus expected inflation.實質利率：名目利率減預期通膨。

    There is no single building called the market for loanable funds. There are many markets for different borrowers and lenders, linked by banks, bond markets and stock markets. The model shows the raw forces behind interest rates.     4. What banks do4. 銀行在做什麼

    The slides draw three bridges from lenders and savers to borrowers: banks, bond markets and stock markets. These are financial intermediaries金融中介.投影片畫了三座從放款人與儲蓄者通往借款人的橋：銀行、債券市場和股票市場。它們就是金融中介。

    Pay interest支付利息

    Deposits earn interest, which attracts savings.存款有利息，吸引儲蓄。

    Screen borrowers篩選借款人

    Loan officers do the due diligence that no single saver could afford.放款人員做盡職調查，單一存款人負擔不起這種工作。

    Pool and spread risk匯集資金、分散風險

    Many small deposits fund many loans, so a few bad loans hurt no one badly.許多小額存款支撐許多筆貸款，幾筆壞帳不會重傷任何人。

    Keep reserves保留準備金

    Only a fraction of deposits is kept as cash for withdrawals; the rest is lent.只留一部分存款當現金應付提領，其餘都借出去。

    The bank earns the spread: it might pay depositors 2% and lend at 7%. MRU builds the case with Starbucks.銀行賺的是利差：例如付存款人 2%，放款收 7%。MRU 用 Starbucks 一步步說明。

    Arrangement安排Problem問題
    You alone lend Howard Schultz $1 million.你一個人借 Howard Schultz 100 萬美元。If the venture fails, the loss to you is huge.事業失敗的話，你的損失很大。
    You and 99 friends lend $10,000 each.你和 99 個朋友每人借 1 萬美元。Risk is shared, but 100 people each studying the business plan is slow and costly.風險分攤了，但 100 個人各自研究營運計畫又慢又貴。
    Appoint one coffee-market expert to evaluate for everyone.指派一位咖啡市場專家替大家評估。This is what a bank does: specialists do the screening.這就是銀行在做的事：由專業人員篩選。
    A bank pools deposits.銀行匯集存款。Now it is like 100,000 people lending Schultz $10 each, and lending to thousands of other entrepreneurs too. A few bad loans will not bankrupt the bank.變成像 10 萬人每人借 Schultz 10 美元，同時也借給其他數千位創業者。幾筆壞帳不會讓銀行倒閉。

    The graph assumes borrowers default independently. In 2008 many mortgages failed together, which pooling cannot fix (section 9).圖表假設借款人彼此獨立違約。2008 年許多房貸同時違約，這是匯集資金解決不了的（第 9 節）。

    Liquidity transformation流動性轉換

    Your deposit can be withdrawn any day, while the loan it funds is locked up for years.你的存款隨時可以領，但它支撐的貸款要鎖好幾年。

    Maturity transformation期限轉換

    Short-term deposits fund long-term loans such as a 20-year mortgage.短期存款支撐長期貸款，例如 20 年房貸。

    The same feature makes banks both useful and fragile. A bank counts on depositors not all asking for their money on the same day. If they do, even a sound bank runs out of cash, because the money is tied up in loans. The 2022 Nobel Prize in economics, shown in the slides, went to Ben Bernanke, Douglas Diamond and Philip Dybvig "for research on banks and financial crises". Diamond and Dybvig modeled exactly this: why banks that turn short deposits into long loans are valuable, and why that leaves them open to runs.同一個特性讓銀行既有用又脆弱。銀行賭的是存款人不會同一天都來領錢。如果真的同時來領，即使是健全的銀行也會缺現金，因為錢都綁在貸款裡。投影片上的 2022 年諾貝爾經濟學獎頒給 Ben Bernanke、Douglas Diamond 和 Philip Dybvig，理由是「對銀行與金融危機的研究」。Diamond 和 Dybvig 建立的模型正是在講這件事：把短期存款變成長期貸款的銀行為什麼有價值，又為什麼因此容易被擠兌。

    中文邏輯銀行賺的是「資訊和分散風險」的錢；它把隨時要還的存款變成很久才收回的貸款，這是它最大的貢獻，也是最容易出事的地方。

    5. Stock markets5. 股票市場

    A stock is a share of ownership with a claim on the firm's profits. Most stock trading does not give the firm a single baht.股票是公司所有權的一部分，可以分享公司利潤。但大部分的股票交易，公司一毛錢都拿不到。

    Market市場Definition定義Example例子Does the firm get money?公司拿到錢嗎？
    Primary marketThe firm sells new shares, first at its IPO首次公開發行, later in new issues.公司出售新股，第一次是 IPO，之後是增資發行。Starbucks went public in 1992; a Thai startup listing on the SET.Starbucks 在 1992 年上市；泰國新創在 SET 掛牌。Yes是：儲蓄變成投資
    Secondary marketInvestors trade existing shares with each other.投資人之間買賣既有股票。Everyday trading on the Stock Exchange of Thailand.泰國證券交易所的日常交易。No否：錢付給上一個股東

    The secondary market still matters. Because shares are easy to resell, investors are willing to buy new issues in the first place. An IPO also rewards founders and venture capitalists who backed the firm when it was risky, and lets them diversify. Buying a stock is a bet on the company, so some shareholders end up happy and others "a little bit sad"; bank depositors usually do not see the value of their deposits swing.次級市場仍然重要：股票容易轉賣，投資人才願意一開始就買新股。IPO 也讓在公司還很冒險時投入的創辦人和創投拿到回報，並能分散自己的持股。買股票是押注這家公司，有些股東很開心，有些「有點難過」；銀行存款人通常不會看到存款價值上下起伏。

    Same distinction as Session 7: buying existing shares is not investment in GDP, which counts only new capital goods.和第 7 堂一樣的區分：買既有股票不是 GDP 裡的投資，GDP 的投資只算新的資本財。

    6. Bonds: IOUs, risk and yield6. 債券：借據、風險與殖利率

    A bond is an IOU: it says who owes how much, and when. A well-known firm like Starbucks, which has issued over $1 billion of bonds, can borrow directly from investors and skip the bank.債券是借據：寫明誰欠多少、何時還。像 Starbucks 這種知名企業（發行過超過 10 億美元的債券）可以直接向投資人借錢，不必透過銀行。

    Bond債券Stock股票

    You receive你得到A fixed sum on a fixed date, sometimes with regular coupon payments固定日期的固定金額，有時還有定期票息A share of profits, as dividends or growth分享利潤：股利或增值

    Riskier borrowers pay more. Rating agencies such as Standard & Poor's grade bonds from AAA down to D; anything below BBB- is called a junk bond. In the video Starbucks is rated A-, and Illinois (A-) pays more to borrow than Virginia (AAA). Collateral lowers the rate too: if you default on a mortgage the bank takes the house, but "once you've been to Maui, the bank can't repossess your vacation."風險越高的借款人付越多利息。標準普爾等評等機構從 AAA 評到 D；低於 BBB- 的叫垃圾債。影片中 Starbucks 是 A-，伊利諾州（A-）借錢的成本比維吉尼亞州（AAA）高。擔保品也會降低利率：房貸違約，銀行可以收走房子；但「去過茂宜島之後，銀行收不回你的假期」。

    A bond's yield殖利率 is the gain from holding it divided by the price paid. The face value is fixed, so the only way a bond can offer a higher yield is to sell for less.債券的殖利率是持有的收益除以買價。面額固定，所以債券要提供更高的殖利率，唯一的方法就是賣得更便宜。

    MRU's lesson from the three bonds: the 14.8% bond is not a free lunch. Equally risky assets must pay the same return, or everyone would buy the higher-yield one until its price rose. A high yield is a signal of high risk. Which bond to buy depends on how much risk you can live with.MRU 從三張債券得出的結論：14.8% 那張不是免費午餐。風險相同的資產報酬必須相同，否則大家都會去買高殖利率的那張，直到它的價格上升。高殖利率代表高風險。要買哪一張，取決於你能承受多少風險。

    7. Crowding out: when the government borrows7. 排擠效果：政府來借錢

    In 2016 the US government owed the public almost $14 trillion. A borrower that large moves the whole market.2016 年美國政府欠大眾將近 14 兆美元。這麼大的借款人會移動整個市場。

    $ billion十億美元Before之前After the government borrows 100政府借 100 之後
    Interest rate利率7%9%
    Savings supplied儲蓄供給量200250
    Private borrowing民間借款200150

    Two things give way. Saving rises by 50, and to save more people consume less, so private consumption falls by 50. At 9% private borrowers want only 150, so private investment falls by 50. Together that is crowding out排擠效果: 100 of government borrowing displaces 50 of consumption and 50 of investment.有兩樣東西讓路。儲蓄增加 50，多存就得少花，所以民間消費減少 50。利率 9% 時民間只想借 150，所以民間投資減少 50。合起來就是排擠效果：政府借 100，擠掉 50 的消費和 50 的投資。

    The 50/50 split五五分

    It comes from the slopes MRU drew. A steeper savings supply curve puts more of the burden on investment.這來自 MRU 畫的斜率。儲蓄供給線越陡，負擔越落在投資上。

    Limit限制

    This is a full-employment, closed-economy result. Crowding out is smaller in a recession or when foreign savers fund the borrowing, and borrowing for productive infrastructure can raise future output.這是充分就業、封閉經濟下的結果。在經濟衰退時，或由外國儲蓄者出錢時，排擠會比較小；借錢蓋有生產力的基礎建設也可能提高未來產出。

    8. Four reasons the bridges fail8. 橋斷掉的四個原因

    Tyler Cowen compares intermediaries to bridges: we notice how much we rely on them only when they crumble. In many poor countries a working bridge never existed.Tyler Cowen 把金融中介比喻成橋：橋塌了，我們才發現自己多依賴它。在許多貧窮國家，能用的橋從來沒有存在過。

    9. The Great Recession9. 大衰退

    Most of the slides follow this story. The economy stands on a cliff edge with a load hanging below it, and MRU adds four weights one at a time. Then house prices fall, and the load drops.大部分投影片都在講這個故事。經濟站在懸崖邊，下面吊著一個貨板，MRU 一次放上一個重物，共四個。接著房價下跌，貨板就掉下去了。

    Weight 1: homeowner leverage重物 1：屋主槓桿

    A $100,000 house bought with a typical 20% down payment carries an $80,000 mortgage and $20,000 of owner's equity: what the owner would keep after selling and repaying the loan. Equity is the cushion. The leverage ratio槓桿比率 divides the mortgage by the owner's equity.一棟 10 萬美元的房子，頭期款照一般的 20%，就有 8 萬美元房貸和 2 萬美元屋主權益：賣掉房子、還完貸款後屋主留下的錢。權益就是緩衝墊。槓桿比率是房貸除以屋主權益。

    Leverage ratio = mortgage ÷ owner's equity
    20% down: 80,000 ÷ 20,000 = 4
    5% down: 95,000 ÷ 5,000 = 19槓桿比率 = 房貸 ÷ 屋主權益；頭期 20%：4；頭期 5%：19

    Before the crisis many homes were bought with 10%, 5% or even zero down. With 5% down, a fall to $90,000 leaves the house worth less than the $95,000 still owed: the owner is underwater, and equity is gone. That hurts the bank too, because in a foreclosure it gets a house worth less than the loan.危機前很多房子是用 10%、5%，甚至零頭期款買的。頭期 5% 時，房價跌到 9 萬美元，房子的價值就低於還欠的 9.5 萬：屋主溺水（資不抵債），權益歸零。銀行也受傷，因為法拍時拿到的房子價值低於貸款。

    Weight 2: bank leverage重物 2：銀行槓桿

    Banks did the same thing with their own balance sheets. Lehman Brothers' leverage ratio rose from about 20 in 2004 to 44 by 2007. At 44, a fall of about 2.2% in the value of its assets wipes out its equity; when the fall came, Lehman was underwater and went bankrupt on September 15, 2008. Why would experienced managers do this? Cowen gives two reasons: overconfidence that house prices could not fall much, and incentives, since bonuses rose with profits while the managers did not personally go bankrupt when the bank did.銀行在自己的資產負債表上也這樣做。雷曼兄弟的槓桿比率從 2004 年約 20 升到 2007 年的 44。44 倍時，資產價值只要跌約 2.2%，權益就歸零；跌勢一來，雷曼就資不抵債，在 2008 年 9 月 15 日破產。有經驗的經理人為什麼這樣做？Cowen 給了兩個理由：過度自信，認為房價不會大跌；以及誘因，獎金隨利潤上升，銀行倒了經理人自己卻不會破產。

    Standard & Poor's, Moody's and Fitch rated such packages, often AA- or even AAA. Many turned out to be riskier than advertised, through outright fraud, rating failures, and above all complacency about house prices. Securitization itself is a useful tool: it frees cash for banks and spreads risk to investors who want it. The failure was in quality, transparency and ratings.標準普爾、穆迪和惠譽替這些包裹評等，常常是 AA-，甚至 AAA。結果很多比宣傳的更危險，原因包括詐欺、評等失靈，最主要是對房價過度安心。證券化本身是有用的工具：讓銀行拿回現金，把風險分給願意承擔的投資人。失敗出在品質、透明度和評等。

    Weight 4: the shadow banking system重物 4：影子銀行體系

    A commercial bank is funded by insured deposits. An investment bank such as Lehman is funded by investors, mostly through short-term loans, with no comparable guarantee: "use at your own risk." These lenders are watchful and quick to flee. Shadow banks borrow short to lend long like a bank, but without deposit insurance, and by 2008 they lent more than traditional commercial banks.商業銀行的資金來自有保險的存款。雷曼這類投資銀行的資金來自投資人，大多是短期借款，沒有類似的保障：「風險自負」。這些放款人非常警覺，一有風吹草動就跑。影子銀行像銀行一樣借短放長，卻沒有存款保險，到 2008 年它們的放款已超過傳統商業銀行。

    Names on the slide: investment banks (Lehman Brothers, Bear Stearns), uninsured parts of commercial banks (Citibank, Bank of America), money market funds (Vanguard, PIMCO), issuers of asset-backed securities (BNP Paribas) and hedge funds (Bridgewater, Man, Brevan Howard).投影片上的名字：投資銀行（雷曼兄弟、貝爾斯登）、商業銀行沒有存款保險的部分（花旗、美國銀行）、貨幣市場基金（Vanguard、PIMCO）、資產擔保證券發行機構（法國巴黎銀行）、避險基金（Bridgewater、Man、Brevan Howard）。

    Subtract inflation: the real rate is what counts.要扣掉通膨，看的是實質利率。

    The bank keeps my deposit in the vault.銀行把我的存款放在金庫裡。

    Most is lent out; only a fraction is held as reserves.大部分都借出去了，只留一部分當準備金。

    Buying shares on the SET gives the company money.在 SET 買股票，公司就拿到錢。

    Only new issues do; trading existing shares pays the previous owner.只有發行新股才有；買舊股的錢付給上一個股東。

    Bondholders own part of the firm.債券持有人擁有公司的一部分。

    They are lenders; shareholders are the owners.他們是放款人，股東才是所有人。

    A high yield is a bargain.高殖利率很划算。

    Equally risky assets pay the same; a high yield signals high risk.風險相同的資產報酬相同，高殖利率代表高風險。

    When interest rates rise, bond prices rise.利率上升，債券價格也上升。

    They move in opposite directions.兩者反向變動。

    An interest rate cap makes loans cheaper for everyone.利率上限讓所有人借錢都更便宜。

    It creates a shortage; total lending falls and risky borrowers are shut out.它造成短缺，總放款減少，高風險借款人被排除。

    Bank runs only hit bad banks.擠兌只會打垮爛銀行。

    With fractional reserves, even a sound bank cannot pay everyone at once.部分準備制下，健全的銀行也無法同時付給所有人。

    Leverage ratio = debt ÷ assets.槓桿比率 = 負債 ÷ 資產。

    Here it is debt ÷ equity: 95,000 ÷ 5,000 = 19.這裡是負債 ÷ 權益：95,000 ÷ 5,000 = 19。

    2008 was a run on ordinary bank deposits.2008 年是一般銀行存款被擠兌。

    It was a run on the uninsured shadow banking system.被擠兌的是沒有保險的影子銀行體系。

    Securitization is fraud.證券化就是詐欺。

    It is a legitimate tool; the problem was quality, opacity and ratings.它是正當工具，問題在品質、不透明和評等。

    A five-year winning record proves skill.連贏五年證明有實力。

    About 31 of 1,000 coin flippers would have one.1,000 個擲銅板的人裡約有 31 個會做到。

    Diversification removes all risk.分散投資能消除所有風險。

    It removes firm-specific risk, not market-wide risk.它消除單一公司風險，不能消除整體市場風險。

    The Thai bond market is mostly corporate bonds.泰國債市主要是公司債。

    Corporate bonds are 27%; government-related bonds are about 72% (Sep 2021).公司債占 27%；政府相關債券約 72%（2021 年 9 月）。

    Deontology and consequentialism always disagree.義務論和結果論一定意見相反。

    They can condemn the same act, such as the Airbnb discrimination, for different reasons.它們可以用不同理由譴責同一行為，例如 Airbnb 的歧視。

    14. Cold call practice14. 課堂口頭問答練習

    What is the difference between deontology and consequentialism?義務論和結果論有什麼不同？

    Deontology judges an act by the duty or rule it follows; consequentialism judges it by its outcomes. On kneeling during the anthem, one calls it a breach of patriotic duty, the other a harmless way to raise awareness.義務論用遵守的義務或規則判斷行為；結果論用結果判斷。對奏國歌時下跪，前者認為違背愛國義務，後者認為無害又能喚起關注。

    What is the market for loanable funds?什麼是可貸資金市場？

    The model where the supply of savings meets the demand to borrow, with the interest rate as the price, for example Thai household deposits meeting Thai firms' demand for loans.儲蓄供給與借款需求相遇、以利率為價格的模型，例如泰國家庭的存款遇上泰國企業的貸款需求。

    What happens when households save more?家庭多存錢會怎樣？

    The supply of savings shifts right, so the interest rate falls and the quantity of lending rises.儲蓄供給右移，利率下降，放款量增加。

    What does a bank do beyond holding your money?除了保管你的錢，銀行還做什麼？

    It pays interest, screens borrowers, pools many deposits into many loans so risk is spread, and keeps reserves for withdrawals. It earns the spread between loan and deposit rates.它支付利息、篩選借款人、把許多存款匯集成許多貸款以分散風險，並保留準備金應付提領。它賺的是放款和存款利率的差。

    When does the stock market turn saving into investment?股票市場什麼時候把儲蓄變成投資？

    Only when new shares are issued and sold, as in an IPO. Trading existing shares only changes who owns them.只有發行並出售新股時，例如 IPO。買賣既有股票只是換人持有。

    A $1,000 one-year bond sells for $871. What is its yield, and what does it tell you?一年期、面額 1,000 美元的債券賣 871 美元。殖利率多少？代表什麼？

    129 ÷ 871 = 14.8%. Equally risky assets pay the same return, so a yield this high signals high default risk.129 ÷ 871 = 14.8%。風險相同的資產報酬相同，這麼高的殖利率代表違約風險高。

    What is crowding out?什麼是排擠效果？

    Government borrowing raises the interest rate and displaces private spending. In MRU's example, 100 billion of borrowing raises the rate from 7% to 9% and cuts investment and consumption by 50 billion each.政府借款推高利率，排擠民間支出。MRU 的例子中，政府借 1,000 億使利率從 7% 升到 9%，投資和消費各減少 500 億。

    Name the four reasons financial intermediaries fail.說出金融中介失靈的四個原因。

    Insecure property rights, interest rate controls, politicized lending, and runs, panics and scandals.財產權不安全、利率管制、政治化放款，以及擠兌、恐慌和醜聞。

    Why can a bank run destroy a healthy bank?擠兌為什麼能弄垮健全的銀行？

    Banks hold only fractional reserves and lend the rest long term, so no bank can repay all depositors at once.銀行只保留部分準備金，其餘長期貸出，所以沒有銀行能同時還給所有存款人。

    What is the leverage ratio with 5% down on a $100,000 house?10 萬美元的房子頭期 5%，槓桿比率是多少？

    95,000 ÷ 5,000 = 19. A fall of just 5% in the house price wipes out the owner's equity.95,000 ÷ 5,000 = 19。房價只要跌 5%，屋主權益就歸零。

    What is the shadow banking system, and why was it vulnerable?什麼是影子銀行體系？為什麼脆弱？

    Investment banks, hedge funds, money market funds and other institutions that borrow short and lend long like banks, but without deposit insurance, so lenders run at the first sign of trouble.投資銀行、避險基金、貨幣市場基金等機構，像銀行一樣借短放長，卻沒有存款保險，所以一有風吹草動放款人就跑。

    What is the efficient market hypothesis?什麼是效率市場假說？

    Prices already reflect all public information, so you cannot systematically beat the market by trading on it. Morton Thiokol's shares fell within hours of the Challenger explosion.價格已經反映所有公開資訊，所以靠它交易無法持續打敗市場。挑戰者號爆炸後幾小時內，Morton Thiokol 的股價就下跌了。

    Why is your employer's stock a bad retirement investment?為什麼自己公司的股票不適合當退休投資？

    It concentrates wealth in one asset, and if the company fails you lose your job and your savings together, as Enron's employees did.它把財富集中在單一資產上，而且公司倒了，你會同時失去工作和存款，就像 Enron 員工那樣。

    How did Thailand's financial system change after 1997?1997 年後泰國金融體系怎麼改變？

    The bond market grew from 12% to 94% of GDP while bank loans fell from 131% to 112%, giving firms a second way to borrow. About 72% of bonds are government-related.債市從 GDP 的 12% 成長到 94%，銀行放款從 131% 降到 112%，企業多了第二條借錢管道。約 72% 的債券與政府相關。

    15. Answer templates15. 答題模板

    Loanable funds可貸資金

    “In the market for loanable funds, savers supply funds and borrowers demand them, and the interest rate is the price. If households save more, supply shifts right, the interest rate falls and lending rises. If firms become more optimistic or get an investment tax credit, demand shifts right and the interest rate rises.”「在可貸資金市場，儲蓄者供給資金、借款人需求資金，利率是價格。家庭多存錢，供給右移，利率下降、放款增加。企業更樂觀或獲得投資抵減，需求右移，利率上升。」

    What banks do銀行做什麼

    “A bank pools many small deposits, screens borrowers on savers' behalf, and spreads risk across many loans, earning the spread between loan and deposit rates. Because it turns deposits that can be withdrawn any day into long-term loans, it is useful but open to runs.”「銀行匯集許多小額存款，替儲蓄者篩選借款人，並把風險分散到許多貸款上，賺取放款與存款利率的差。因為它把隨時可提領的存款變成長期貸款，所以既有用又容易被擠兌。」

    Bonds and risk債券與風險

    “A bond is an IOU with a fixed face value. Riskier issuers must offer higher yields, and since the face value is fixed, a higher yield means a lower price. Ratings and collateral lower the rate a borrower pays.”「債券是面額固定的借據。風險較高的發行人必須提供較高的殖利率；面額固定，殖利率高就代表價格低。評等和擔保品可以降低借款人要付的利率。」

    Crowding out排擠效果

    “Government borrowing shifts the demand for loanable funds right and raises the interest rate. In MRU's example, 100 billion of borrowing raises the rate from 7 to 9 percent, cutting private investment by 50 billion and consumption by 50 billion.”「政府借款使可貸資金需求右移、利率上升。MRU 的例子中，借 1,000 億使利率從 7% 升到 9%，民間投資減少 500 億、消費減少 500 億。」

    The Great Recession大衰退

    “Four weights hung on the economy: homeowners with little equity, banks such as Lehman with leverage of 44, mortgage securities riskier than their ratings, and shadow banks funded by uninsured short-term loans. When house prices fell in 2007, lenders ran from the shadow banks, fire sales pushed prices lower, and the result was a credit crunch and high unemployment.”「經濟上吊著四個重物：權益很少的屋主、槓桿 44 倍的雷曼等銀行、比評等更危險的房貸證券，以及靠沒有保險的短期借款支撐的影子銀行。2007 年房價下跌，放款人從影子銀行撤資，賤價拋售讓價格更低，結果是信用緊縮和高失業。」

    Your money個人理財

    “Prices already reflect public information and past winners rarely repeat, so do not pick stocks. Buy a diversified, low-fee index fund, including international stocks, and avoid holding your employer's stock.”「價格已經反映公開資訊，過去的贏家很少再贏，所以不要挑股。買分散、低費用的指數基金，包括海外股票，並避免持有自己公司的股票。」
