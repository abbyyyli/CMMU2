    Values and Attitudes in the Workplace職場中的價值觀與態度

    Why do different employees react differently to the same workplace? Personal values meet corporate values and real management practice. The fit shapes attitudes, which may then shape behavior.為何不同員工面對同一職場，反應卻不同？個人價值觀會與企業價值觀及實際管理作法相遇。兩者的契合程度形塑態度，態度又可能影響行為。

    Chapter map章節地圖

    Values價值觀→Fit and workplace experience契合程度與職場經驗→Attitudes態度→Behavior行為→Management response管理回應

    中文邏輯每一個箭頭都需要 case 的證據；values 「可能」影響 attitude 和 behavior，不是直接決定。答題用 may / could，不用 will。

    1. Values1. 價值觀

    Personal beliefs or convictions about what is right or wrong, good or bad, proper or improper.對何者正確或錯誤、好或壞、適當或不適當的個人信念或確信。

    Content內容

    What we consider important.我們認為什麼重要。

    Intensity強度 強度

    How important it is. Two people both value autonomy; the one who values it more reacts more strongly to micromanagement.它有多重要。兩人都重視自主，但更重視自主的人，面對微觀管理時反應更強烈。

    Values are fairly stable, shaped by family, school, friends and experience, vary across cultures, and are difficult to change. How people allocate their time reveals what they value; the professor's example was his years of long-distance running.價值觀相當穩定，受到家庭、學校、朋友及經驗形塑，跨文化有所差異，也不容易改變。人們如何分配時間，會透露他們重視什麼；教授舉的是自己多年長跑的例子。

    When a value conflict appears, first examine job design, management style and the reward system, not how to change the employee.出現價值衝突時，先檢查工作設計、管理風格與獎酬制度，而非先想如何改變員工。

    Terminal versus instrumental終極價值與工具性價值

    Terminal終極價值 終極價值

    The destination. Freedom, happiness, harmony, self-respect, wealth, recognition, wisdom, social contribution, world peace.目的地：自由、快樂、和諧、自尊、財富、認可、智慧、社會貢獻、世界和平。

    Instrumental工具性價值 工具性價值

    How you get there. Ambitious, self-disciplined, honest, sincere, logical, obedient, risk-taking, clean, imaginative.達成目的的方法：有抱負、自律、誠實、真誠、合乎邏輯、服從、勇於冒險、整潔、有想像力。

    Pause & think暫停，想一想

    Two employees value autonomy, but only one reacts strongly to frequent approvals. How could value intensity explain the difference, and what other workplace evidence would be needed?兩名員工都重視自主性，卻只有一人對頻繁核准反應強烈。價值強度如何解釋差異？還需哪些職場證據？

    2. Fit and corporate values2. 契合與企業價值觀

    Corporate values企業價值觀+Personal values個人價值觀→Employee attitudes and behavior員工態度與行為
    • Person-organization fit 人與組織契合 concerns values as well as personality.人與組織契合不只涉及人格，也涉及價值觀。
    • Value alignment affects job satisfaction.價值觀一致程度會影響工作滿意度。
    • People tend to select organizations whose values align, or leave.人們傾向選擇價值觀契合的組織，或選擇離開。

    Kantabutra (2022): sustainable corporations see themselves as a "special" place to work where individual and corporate values are aligned. Fit means meaningful alignment, not identical people.Kantabutra（2022）：永續企業視自己為「特別」的工作場所，讓個人與企業價值觀契合。契合是有意義的一致，不是人人相同。

    A strong culture aligns members' values and attitudes with the organization's values. It does not mean everyone has the same personality.強文化會使成員的價值觀與態度和組織價值觀一致，不代表每個人都有相同人格。

    Values common in sustainable enterprises: virtuous values (perseverance, moderation, generosity, honesty, integrity, ethics), social and environmental responsibility, and innovation. A manager's own values also shape what they see, what they think is important, how they spend time and what they decide.永續企業常見的價值觀：美德價值（堅毅、適度、慷慨、誠實、正直、倫理）、社會與環境責任，以及創新。主管本人的價值觀，也會影響他注意什麼、認為什麼重要、如何分配時間，以及做出何種決定。

    3. Value mismatch versus stated-practice discrepancy3. 價值觀不契合與宣稱／實務落差

    A key distinction from the Ken case discussion.這是 Ken 個案討論中的關鍵區分。

    Value mismatch價值觀不契合

    The employee genuinely values something different. The employee values autonomy; the organization emphasizes control.員工確實重視不同的事物。例如員工重視自主，組織卻強調控制。

    Stated-practice discrepancy宣稱與實務落差 說做不一

    The employee may agree with the stated value, but practice rewards something else. "We value work-life balance," yet promotions go to people who work late.員工可能同意組織宣稱的價值，但實際作法獎勵另一件事。例如宣稱「我們重視工作與生活平衡」，升遷卻給經常加班的人。

    For a value to feel real, it must appear in four layers: the stated value, leader behavior, rewards and policy, and repeated communication.價值觀要讓人感到真實，必須出現在四個層面：宣稱的價值、領導者行為、獎酬與政策，以及反覆溝通。

    Pause & think暫停，想一想

    An organization promotes empowerment but requires approval for every minor choice. How would you test whether the issue is a genuine value mismatch or a gap between stated values and actual practice? What should management examine first?組織宣稱重視賦權，卻要求每個小決定都先核准。如何判斷是真正的價值不合，還是宣稱與實務落差？管理者應先檢查什麼？

    4. Attitudes4. 態度

    Judgments we make about objects, people or events. "I like my job."我們對事物、人物或事件所做的評價。例如：「我喜歡我的工作。」

    Value價值觀

    What matters to me? Broad and stable. "Honesty is important to me."什麼對我重要？廣泛且穩定。例如：「誠實對我很重要。」

    Attitude態度

    How do I evaluate this? Specific. "I don't trust my manager."我如何評價這個對象？針對特定對象。例如：「我不信任我的主管。」

    CCognitive認知成分

    Opinion or belief: "My boss is a mean person."意見或信念：「我的主管是個刻薄的人。」

    AAffective情感成分

    Emotion: "I am angry because my boss is mean."情緒：「因為主管刻薄，我感到生氣。」

    BBehavioral行為成分

    Intention to act: "I am going to report my boss to HR."行動意圖：「我要向人資檢舉主管。」

    Workplace attitudes職場態度

    Concept概念Definition定義Diagnostic question診斷問題
    Job satisfaction工作滿意度Attitude of fulfillment toward the job對工作感到滿足的態度Am I satisfied with this job?我對這份工作滿意嗎？
    Job involvement 工作投入工作投入Identification with the job對工作的認同Is this job part of who I am?這份工作是我自我認同的一部分嗎？
    Organizational commitment 組織承諾組織承諾Identification with organizational goals; desire to remain a member認同組織目標，並希望繼續成為其成員Do I believe in this organization and want to stay?我認同這個組織，並希望留下嗎？
    Organizational citizenship (OCB)組織公民行為（OCB）Discretionary behavior that helps the organization出於自願、對組織有幫助的行為Do I voluntarily do extra-role work?我是否自願做角色要求以外的工作？
    Satisfaction ≠ commitment滿意度 ≠ 組織承諾

    "I love being a designer, but I no longer believe in this company." Satisfaction may stay high while commitment falls.「我喜歡當設計師，但已不再認同這家公司。」滿意度可能仍高，組織承諾卻下降。

    OCB is a behaviorOCB 是行為

    An employee who stops helping new colleagues shows lower OCB, not automatically lower task performance.員工不再協助新同事，表示 OCB 降低，不自動代表本職任務績效下降。

    Pause & think暫停，想一想

    An employee still enjoys professional work but no longer identifies with the company and stops voluntarily helping newcomers. Distinguish the evidence about job satisfaction, organizational commitment and OCB. What cannot be concluded about task performance?員工仍喜歡專業工作，卻不再認同公司，也停止自願協助新人。請區分工作滿意、組織承諾與 OCB 的證據；對工作績效還不能推論什麼？

    5. Type A and Type B5. A 型與 B 型

    Type AA 型

    Aggressive, impatient, goal and outcome driven, time conscious, multitasks.積極進取、缺乏耐心、目標與結果導向、重視時間、同時處理多項工作。

    Type BB 型

    Little urgency, no need to display achievement, takes time, plays for fun, relaxes easily.急迫感較低、不需要展示成就、從容行事、為樂趣而玩、容易放鬆。

    Type A and B relate to national culture: competitive versus cooperative. These are tendencies; a time-conscious employee will not definitely burn out without evidence.A、B 型也與國家文化的競爭或合作傾向有關。這些只是傾向；沒有證據時，不能斷言重視時間的員工一定會倦怠。

    6. Changing attitudes and building culture6. 改變態度與建立文化

    Can you change other people's attitudes? It depends on three things.能否改變別人的態度？取決於三項條件。

    1Their attitude對方的態度

    How committed they are to it, how aware they are of their position, how big the change is.對原有立場有多堅定、對立場有多清楚，以及需要改變的幅度。

    2You你本身

    Trust and credibility, expertise and influence, perceived honesty and fairness.信任與可信度、專業與影響力，以及對方感受到的誠實與公平。

    3Your strategy你的策略

    Understand their values and personality, use social influence, give training and moral support, provide incentives.理解對方的價值觀與人格、運用社會影響、提供訓練與精神支持、給予誘因。

    Workforce diversity in age, experience, culture, gender, race and religion is a strength through variation in ideas, attitudes and knowledge.員工在年齡、經驗、文化、性別、種族與宗教上的多樣性，能透過不同想法、態度與知識形成優勢。

    7. Six-step case analysis7. 六步個案分析

    1 · Value1・價值觀

    What matters to this employee?這名員工重視什麼？

    2 · Fit / experience2・契合／經驗

    Does the organization support or contradict it?組織支持還是違背這項價值？

    3 · Attitude3・態度

    Satisfaction? Commitment?工作滿意度？組織承諾？

    4 · Evidence4・證據

    What behavior is observed: OCB, turnover intention, performance?觀察到什麼行為：OCB、離職意圖、績效？

    5 · Boundary5・推論界線

    What can we not conclude yet?目前還不能下什麼結論？

    6 · Response6・回應

    Which practice, leader behavior or reward should management examine?管理者應檢查哪種作法、領導者行為或獎酬？

    Worked example: an employee values autonomy; the company claims empowerment, but every small decision needs approval. "I still like my profession, but I don't trust this company," and she has stopped helping coworkers. This is a stated-practice discrepancy that may reduce commitment and OCB, while job satisfaction may hold. The case gives no evidence she will resign. Management should first examine its own control practices and rewards.示範個案：員工重視自主；公司宣稱賦權，但每個小決定都要批准。她說「我仍喜歡我的專業，但不信任這家公司」，也停止協助同事。這是宣稱與實務落差，可能降低組織承諾與 OCB，而工作滿意度可能維持。個案沒有證據顯示她會辭職。管理者應先檢查自身控制作法與獎酬制度。
    Pause & think暫停，想一想

    A firm announces collaboration as a core value, but promotions reward individual output only and optional helping declines. Separate the observable behavior, possible stated-practice discrepancy and unproven assumptions. What would an aligned response change?公司宣稱合作是核心價值，升遷卻只獎勵個人產出，自願協助同事的行為也減少。請分出可觀察行為、可能的說做不一，以及尚未證實的假設。真正一致的做法應改變什麼？

    8. Do not mix these up8. 不要混淆

    Values determine behavior.價值觀決定行為。

    Values may influence attitudes, which may influence behavior.價值觀可能影響態度，態度又可能影響行為。

    Poor fit means he will leave.不契合就代表他會離職。

    Need evidence of turnover intention or leaving.需要離職意圖或實際離職的證據。

    Fix value conflicts by changing the employee.透過改變員工來解決價值衝突。

    Check job design, management and rewards first.先檢查工作設計、管理與獎酬。

    Strong culture = same personality.強文化＝相同人格。

    It means aligned values and attitudes.意思是價值觀與態度一致。

    "I don't trust my manager" is a value.「我不信任主管」是一項價值觀。

    It is an attitude toward a specific person.這是對特定人物的態度。

    Satisfied = committed.滿意＝有組織承諾。

    Job and organization are different targets.工作與組織是不同評價對象。

    OCB is an attitude.OCB 是一種態度。

    It is an observable, optional behavior.它是可觀察、出於自願的行為。

    Incentives change attitudes.誘因會改變態度。

    Also depends on the person and your credibility.還取決於對方本身與你的可信度。

    9. Cold call practice9. 課堂口答練習

    Why should managers care about value intensity, not just content?主管為何不只要了解價值內容，也要了解強度？

    Stronger values produce stronger reactions when the workplace supports or violates them.價值越強烈，職場支持或違背它時，反應也越強烈。

    Freedom or honest: which is terminal?自由與誠實，哪個是終極價值？

    Freedom is a desired end state (terminal); honest is a way of behaving (instrumental).自由是希望達到的最終狀態（終極價值）；誠實是行為方式（工具性價值）。

    A firm says sustainability is core, but KPIs reward only short-term sales. Mismatch or discrepancy?公司宣稱永續是核心，但 KPI 只獎勵短期銷售。這是不契合還是落差？

    Stated-practice discrepancy: the reward system contradicts the stated value.宣稱與實務落差：獎酬制度違背宣稱的價值。

    "My boss is unfair" → "I feel angry" → "I will report this." Classify.「主管不公平」→「我生氣」→「我要檢舉」。如何分類？

    Cognitive, affective, behavioral intention.認知成分、情感成分、行為意圖。

    Is Type A always better than Type B?A 型一定比 B 型好嗎？

    No. Type A suits sales and goal focus but risks burnout and quantity over quality; Type B may innovate and is more common in senior management but has less urgency.不一定。A 型適合銷售與目標導向，但有倦怠及重量不重質的風險；B 型可能較具創新，且在高階管理層較常見，但急迫感較低。

    Why might the same message work from a trusted manager but not a consultant?為何同樣的訊息，由受信任的主管傳達可能有效，由顧問傳達卻未必？

    Trust and credibility increase influence over attitudes.信任與可信度會提高對態度的影響力。

    What do the five culture-development techniques have in common?五種文化發展技巧有何共同點？

    They make core values repeated, observable and consistent signals.它們都讓核心價值成為反覆出現、可觀察且一致的訊號。
