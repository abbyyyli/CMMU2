    1. EQ: recognize and manage1. EQ：辨識與管理

    Emotional intelligence (EQ) 情緒智商 is the ability to assess emotions in oneself and others, and to manage oneself and relationships.情緒智商（EQ）是評估自己與他人情緒，並管理自己及人際關係的能力。

    Recognition辨識情緒

    Notice the emotion, its trigger and the other person's view.察覺情緒、引發情緒的事件，以及對方的觀點。

    Management管理自己與關係

    Choose a response that fixes the issue, keeps the standard and protects the relationship.選擇能解決問題、維持標準並保護關係的回應。

    EQ comes from nature and nurture: genetics, life experience and training. It can be developed.EQ 受先天與後天共同影響，包括遺傳、生活經驗與訓練，因此可以培養。

    EQ origins: Goleman, Working with Emotional Intelligence (1999), cited on lecture p.4.EQ 的發展來源：Goleman，《Working with Emotional Intelligence》（1999），引自課堂投影片第 4 頁。

    Self-awareness自我覺察 自我覺察

    Accurate knowledge of your emotions, preferences, typical reactions and core values, including what happens when you compromise them.準確了解自己的情緒、偏好、慣常反應及核心價值，也了解妥協這些價值可能帶來的後果。

    Self-control自我調節 自我調節

    Feeling positive and negative emotions without letting them drive unproductive conduct.可以感受正面與負面情緒，但不讓情緒支配無助於工作的行為。

    Notice the trigger察覺觸發點→Name the feeling辨認感受→Choose a response選擇回應→Review the impact檢視影響

    A person can recognize anger accurately and still respond badly. Awareness without control is not enough.一個人即使知道自己正在生氣，仍可能做出不當回應。只有覺察而沒有調節並不足夠。

    S1 personality preferences show default habits and blind spots. They do not measure EQ. A Thinking preference does not explain cruelty, and a Judging preference does not mean being judgmental.S1 的人格偏好呈現慣常習慣與盲點，不能衡量 EQ。Thinking 偏好不代表殘酷，Judging 偏好也不等於愛批判別人。

    4. Empathy with honesty4. 同理與誠實並重

    The lecturer balances empathy and compassion with honesty. Understanding a concern does not mean dropping the standard. A good response covers both the person and the work.老師強調同理與關懷必須和誠實取得平衡。理解對方的困難不等於降低標準；好的回應同時照顧人與工作。

    中文邏輯同理是理解處境，誠實是說清楚標準。兩者可以同時做到，不需要二選一。

    9. Do not mix these up9. 易混概念

    Awareness and control are two of the five factors.自我覺察與自我調節分別是五因素中的兩項。

    They are taught separately but counted together as Factor 1. Awareness identifies the emotion; control governs the response.兩者分開教學，但合計為第一個因素。覺察是辨認情緒，調節則是管理回應。

    EQ means hiding anger.EQ 就是把憤怒藏起來。

    EQ recognizes positive and negative emotions and manages the response. Feeling frustrated and giving respectful correction can coexist.EQ 包括辨認正面與負面情緒，並管理回應。感到挫折與尊重地指出錯誤，可以同時做到。

    Empathy means agreeing or dropping the standard.同理就是同意對方，或降低工作標準。

    Empathy understands another perspective. Acknowledge the difficulty, ask about support and still require accurate work.同理是理解另一個人的觀點。可以承認困難、詢問支援需求，同時要求準確的工作成果。

    Empathy and social expertise are interchangeable.同理與關係處理能力是同一回事。

    Empathy understands how another person sees the situation. Social expertise handles the interaction, including conflict, support and relationships.同理是理解對方如何看待情境；關係處理能力則是處理互動，包括衝突、支持與人際關係。

    Compliance proves personal influence.員工服從，就證明主管有個人影響力。

    Authority can secure obedience. Personal influence inspires willing support, especially when work needs initiative or cross-team cooperation.職權可以取得服從；個人影響力則帶來自願支持，對需要主動性或跨團隊合作的工作尤其重要。

    A profit target is a shared vision.利潤目標就是共同願景。

    A target specifies a result. Vision connects that result to purpose and values; employees need to understand the broader direction.目標指定要達成的結果；願景把結果連到目的與價值觀，員工也需要理解更完整的方向。

    T or J explains poor EQ.T 或 J 偏好可以解釋 EQ 差。

    T emphasizes decision criteria; J favors structure and closure. Neither measures EQ or explains public humiliation.T 重視決策準則；J 偏好結構與定案。兩者都不是 EQ 指標，也不能用來解釋公開羞辱他人的行為。

    Completing coaching proves EQ improved.完成教練課程，就證明 EQ 改善。

    Attendance is an activity measure. Check repeated behavior, employee experience and business results separately.出席是活動層次的指標。持續行為、員工感受與企業成果，需要分開檢查。

    10. Cold call practice10. 課堂問答練習

    Define EQ and name its five factors in order.定義 EQ，並依序說出五個因素。

    EQ assesses emotions in self and others and manages self and relationships. The factors are self-awareness and self-control, empathy, social expertise, personal influence, and mastery of vision.EQ 評估自己與他人的情緒，並管理自己及關係。五因素依序為自我覺察與調節、同理、關係處理、個人影響力、願景掌握。

    What distinguishes awareness from control?覺察與調節有何不同？

    Awareness recognizes feelings, triggers, values and patterns. Control governs the behavioral response to those feelings.覺察是辨認感受、觸發點、價值及模式；調節是管理這些感受所引發的行為回應。

    How does empathy differ from social expertise?同理與關係處理有何不同？

    Empathy understands another perspective. Social expertise uses that understanding to handle interactions, conflict, support and relationships.同理是理解對方的觀點；關係處理則運用理解來處理互動、衝突、支持與人際關係。

    What does nature plus nurture mean here?先天加後天在這裡是什麼意思？

    Genetics, experience and training all shape EQ, so it can be developed.遺傳、經驗及訓練共同形塑 EQ，因此它可以培養。

    What makes vision more than a target?為什麼願景不只是目標？

    It connects priorities and actions to purpose and values, giving direction through obstacles. A numeric target can sit inside that direction.願景把優先事項與行動連到目的和價值觀，協助克服障礙。數字目標可以是其中一部分。

    What does the lecturer recommend for development?老師建議如何培養 EQ？

    Coaching, feedback and support; honest assessment from trusted people; a personal plan with work as the learning lab; meditation for self-awareness.教練、回饋及支持；信任者的誠實評估；把工作當實驗室的個人計畫；用冥想培養自我覺察。
