    Monopoly and Price Discrimination獨占與差別取價

    A monopolist can influence price, but demand still limits sales. Choose output using marginal revenue and marginal cost, read price from demand, and use average total cost to check profit. Then ask who gains when prices differ across customers.獨占廠商能影響價格，但銷量仍受需求限制。先比較邊際收益與邊際成本來選產量，再從需求線讀價格，並用平均總成本判斷利潤。接著分析對不同顧客收取不同價格時，誰得到好處。

    1. Market power still faces demand1. 市場力量仍受需求約束

    Control over entry creates pricing discretion, not unlimited willingness to pay.控制進入條件帶來定價空間，但不代表顧客願意支付任何價格。

    Medicine and legal exclusion藥物與法律排他權

    The historical Combivir example links patent protection to restricted entry and pricing power. Scale economies, exclusive licenses, and temporary technological advantages are other possible barriers.Combivir 的歷史案例把專利保護、限制進入與定價力量連在一起。規模經濟、獨家許可與暫時的技術優勢，也可能形成進入障礙。

    The medicine example uses historical prices and patent conditions.藥物例子使用的是歷史價格與專利條件。

    A price maker selects a feasible point on demand. Raising price generally reduces quantity demanded; a higher price can therefore reduce total revenue or profit. Define the market and available substitutes before judging how much pricing power a firm has.價格制定者仍須在需求線上選擇可行組合。提高價格通常會減少需求量，因此可能降低總收益或利潤。判斷市場力量前，先界定市場與可替代選擇。

    2. Why marginal revenue is below price2. 為什麼邊際收益低於價格

    The key is how an extra sale changes revenue across the whole sales plan.關鍵是新增銷量如何改變整個銷售方案的收益。

    Demand D shows the highest common price buyers will pay for each quantity. Marginal revenue MR shows how much total revenue increases when the seller expands output. They answer different questions.需求 D 表示每個產量下，買方願意支付的最高統一價格。邊際收益 MR 表示增加產量時，總收益增加多少。兩條線回答不同問題。

    Under uniform pricing, a larger quantity requires a lower price on all units in that sales plan. The extra unit brings revenue in, while the lower price reduces receipts on the other units. As a result, MR lies below D. The relevant comparison is the extra revenue after this price effect, not simply the extra unit's selling price.在統一定價下，要賣更多數量，就必須降低該銷售方案中所有單位的價格。多賣一單位帶來收益，但其他單位降價會減少收入。因此 MR 位於 D 下方。應比較扣除這個降價效果後的新增收益，而不是只看新增單位的售價。

    Competitive firm競爭廠商

    Takes market price as given. An extra unit does not require a price cut on the other units, so MR=P.接受既定市場價格。多賣一單位不需要降低其他單位的價格，因此 MR＝P。

    Single-price monopolist單一定價的獨占廠商

    Faces downward-sloping demand. Expanding sales lowers the common price, so MR<P.面對向下傾斜的需求。擴大銷量需要降低統一價格，因此 MR＜P。

    If the next unit adds more revenue than cost, expansion improves profit. If it adds less, reducing output improves profit. The usual MR–MC crossing identifies where that incentive changes.如果下一單位增加的收益高於成本，擴產會提高利潤；反之，減產會提高利潤。一般的 MR、MC 交點用來辨認這個誘因改變的位置。

    Optional: linear demand and the numerical example可展開補充：線性需求與數字例子

    Linear demand is a straight-line relationship: each additional unit requires the same price reduction. For linear inverse demand, MR has the same vertical intercept and twice the negative slope. This shortcut is specific to linear demand.線性需求是直線關係：每增加一單位，都需要相同幅度的降價。在線性反需求下，MR 與 D 有相同的縱軸截距，但 MR 的負斜率是兩倍。這個捷徑只適用於線性需求。

    TR = P × Q; MR = ΔTR / ΔQ
    P = a − bQ; continuous MR = a − 2bQ
    D reaches zero at Q = a/b; MR reaches zero at Q = a/(2b)總收益 TR＝P×Q；邊際收益 MR＝ΔTR／ΔQ。線性反需求 P＝a−bQ 的連續型 MR＝a−2bQ。D 在 Q＝a/b 時降至零；MR 在 Q＝a/(2b) 時降至零。

    In MRU's example, two units at $16 earn $32. Three units at $14 earn $42. The extra unit adds $10: its $14 receipt minus the $4 lost on the other two units. For whole-unit tables, use the actual change in revenue rather than a continuous derivative.MRU 的例子中，兩單位各賣 16 元，收入 32 元；三單位各賣 14 元，收入 42 元。新增收益是 10 元：新增單位收入 14 元，扣掉另外兩單位降價少收的 4 元。整數表格應使用實際收益差額，不直接套連續型微分值。

    How to build this graph如何開始畫這張圖
    1. Identify the question. A single-price monopolist chooses along downward-sloping demand. Use Q on the horizontal axis and price/cost per unit on the vertical axis.先辨認題目。單一定價的獨占廠商沿向下傾斜的需求選擇。橫軸放 Q，縱軸放每單位價格／成本。
    2. Start with D, then add MR. D gives buyers' price constraint. MR lies below it because expanding sales lowers the common price.先畫 D，再加 MR。D 是買方願付價格的約束；因為擴大銷量需要降低統一價格，MR 在 D 下方。
    3. Add MC and find output. Locate the relevant MR–MC crossing and project down to Qm. For whole-unit tables, find where an additional unit stops improving profit; exact equality is not required.加上 MC 找產量。找出適當的 MR、MC 交點，再向下投影到 Qm。整數表格則找下一單位從提高利潤轉為降低利潤的地方，不必剛好相等。
    4. Move up to D for price. At Qm, mark the point on D and read Pm. The height of the MR–MC crossing is not the selling price.從 Qm 往上回到 D 找價格。標出需求線上的點，再讀 Pm。MR、MC 交點的高度不是售價。
    5. Add only what the question needs. For profit or loss, add ATC and compare it with Pm at Qm. For efficiency, find D–MC and mark Qe; excluded beneficial trades lie between D and MC.依題目補上需要的線與點。問盈虧時加 ATC，並在 Qm 比較 ATC 與 Pm；問效率時找 D、MC 交點及 Qe。被排除的有益交易位於 D 與 MC 之間。

    Class drug graph: mark MR–MC at (80, 0.50), D at (80, 12.50), and ATC at (80, 2.50). These points share the same output but play different roles.課堂藥物圖：MR、MC 交點是 (80, 0.50)，D 上的點是 (80, 12.50)，ATC 上的點是 (80, 2.50)。三點的產量相同，但用途不同。

    3. The professor's whole-gallon decision3. 教授的整加侖產量決策

    Compare the benefit and cost of the next unit, rather than chasing the largest revenue.比較下一單位的收益與成本，不只追求最大的營收。

    The class example makes the marginal decision concrete. The fourth gallon still increases profit; the fifth reduces it. The best listed choice is therefore four gallons per day at $7, with $11/day profit.課堂例子把邊際決策具體化：第四加侖仍提高利潤，第五加侖則降低利潤。因此表格中的最佳選擇是每天四加侖、每加侖 7 元，每日利潤 11 元。

    Revenue keeps rising beyond the best output, but additional production also costs resources. More sales need not mean more profit. When quantities come in whole units, the decision can switch from a profitable extra unit to an unprofitable one without any row having exactly equal MR and MC.超過最適產量後，收益仍可能增加，但生產也要付出更多資源成本。賣更多不一定賺更多。數量只能整單位變動時，可能直接從有利的新增單位跳到不利的新增單位，而沒有任何一列剛好 MR＝MC。

    At the chosen output, price lies above ATC, so the firm earns a profit. The vertical gap between P and ATC is profit per gallon; the rectangle's width is the number of gallons. Fixed cost is paid even with zero production in this short-run example, so shutdown can be worse than operating.在所選產量下，價格高於 ATC，因此廠商獲利。P 與 ATC 的垂直距離是每加侖利潤；矩形的寬度是產量。在這個短期例子中，不生產也要付固定成本，因此停產可能比營運更差。

    5. Elasticity, markup, and cost changes5. 彈性、加價與成本變動

    Less price sensitivity can support a larger markup; a steeper line alone is not enough.價格敏感度較低時可能支持較高加價；單看線較陡還不夠。

    Elasticity需求彈性 measures percentage quantity response to percentage price change. Fewer close substitutes can make buyers less responsive. MRU's medicine discussion adds two mechanisms: serious illness can increase the priority of treatment, and third-party payment can reduce the user's sensitivity to the bill.彈性衡量價格百分比變動引起的需求量百分比變動。相近替代品較少時，買方可能較不敏感。MRU 藥物討論另有兩個機制：嚴重疾病可能提高治療的優先性；第三方支付則可能降低使用者對帳單的敏感度。

    The intuition: when buyers can easily switch, a price increase loses many sales and the seller's markup is constrained. With weaker substitutes, the seller can sustain a larger gap between price and MC. In the class comparison, “relatively inelastic” means less responsive than the other demand case.直覺是：如果買方很容易轉換，漲價會流失很多銷量，廠商加價就受限制。替代選擇較弱時，價格與 MC 的差距可能較大。課堂所說「相對缺乏彈性」，是相對另一種需求更不敏感。

    The standard positive-MC interior optimum remains on the elastic part of demand. On an inelastic part, reducing output and raising price would increase revenue while reducing variable cost, so the seller would want to change its choice.在 MC 為正、一般內點最適條件下，廠商仍選在需求有彈性的區段。如果位於缺乏彈性的區段，減產漲價會增加收益並降低變動成本，廠商就有誘因改變選擇。

    Absolute markup = P − MC
    Lerner index = (P − MC) / P = 1 / |ε|絕對加價＝P−MC。Lerner 指數＝(P−MC)／P＝1／|ε|。

    The Lerner formula applies at a differentiable, unconstrained interior single-price optimum; with positive MC, |ε|>1. The comparison uses P=60−2Q and P=140−4Q, both with MC=20. Their optimal markups are 20 and 60, and elasticity magnitudes are 2 and 4/3. Both the intercept and slope change; steepness alone does not identify elasticity.Lerner 公式適用於可微、沒有額外約束的單一定價內點最適解；MC 為正時，|ε|＞1。比較的需求是 P＝60−2Q 與 P＝140−4Q，兩者 MC 都為 20。最適加價分別為 20、60，彈性絕對值為 2、4/3。截距與斜率都改變，因此不能只看陡峭程度判斷彈性。

    Deadweight loss無謂損失無謂損失Combined gains from trade that are lost.未能實現的總交易利益。Area between D and MC over excluded beneficial units.被排除的有益單位中，D 與 MC 之間的面積。

    Uniform monopoly pricing excludes some units whose WTP exceeds MC but falls below the monopoly price. Efficient output satisfies D=MC; monopoly output satisfies MR=MC. Lost consumer surplus includes both transfers and DWL.統一獨占價格排除了一些願付價格高於 MC、卻低於售價的交易。有效率產量滿足 D＝MC；獨占產量滿足 MR＝MC。失去的消費者剩餘同時包含轉移與無謂損失。

    The monopoly price transfers some surplus from buyers to the seller. It also prevents trades that would create value because the buyer's willingness to pay exceeds the resource cost. The transfer changes who receives the gains; the missing trades reduce total gains. That is why all lost consumer surplus cannot be labeled DWL.獨占價格把部分剩餘從買方移給賣方，也阻止了願付價格高於資源成本的有益交易。轉移改變誰取得利益；缺少的交易則降低總利益。因此不能把全部消費者損失都叫作無謂損失。

    Higher fixed cost can turn profit into a loss without changing the marginal output choice. If that fixed cost is unavoidable now, operating may still lose less than shutting down. Conversely, pricing at MC can serve efficient output while leaving fixed cost uncovered; efficient allocation and financing are separate questions.固定成本上升可能把獲利變成虧損，卻不改變邊際產量選擇。如果固定成本目前無法避免，營運仍可能比停產少虧。反過來，以 MC 定價可服務有效率產量，但未必支付固定成本；配置效率與資金來源是兩個問題。

    7. Different prices, different customers7. 不同顧客、不同價格

    Identify the information used, the resale constraint, and the relevant counterfactual.辨認使用了哪些資訊、轉售受到何種限制，以及比較基準是什麼。

    Price discrimination差別取價 charges different prices for comparable units for reasons beyond different supply costs. Sustaining it requires pricing power, a way to segment customers or induce sorting, and limits on arbitrage套利轉售.差別取價是對可比較的單位收取不同價格，而且價差不只是供應成本差異。要維持差別取價，須有定價力量、可行的分群或自我選擇機制，以及對套利轉售的限制。

    Resale can defeat segmentation: full-price buyers obtain discounted units through others. ID checks or nontransferable admission can help sustain separate prices. If resale forces a uniform price, the new optimum need not equal the former low price. Product differences also matter: a business-class fare gap alone does not isolate discrimination.轉售可能破壞分群：原本付全價的人，可透過別人取得折扣商品。身分驗證或不可轉讓的入場權有助維持不同價格。如果轉售迫使統一定價，新最適價格不一定等於原本的低價。產品差異也重要：商務艙價差本身不能單獨識別差別取價。

    Separate pricing lets the seller respond to each group's demand instead of compromising on one common price. The optimal group choices equate each group's MR with common MC. The group with less elastic demand at its chosen point supports a larger markup.分別定價讓賣方依各群需求反應，而不必妥協於同一價格。最適客群產量會使各群 MR 等於共同 MC。在其選定點，需求較缺乏彈性的客群支持較高加價。

    Higher seller profit does not settle welfare. In this example, total output stays unchanged, but sales shift away from higher-value marginal buyers in A toward lower-value buyers in B. The seller gains while combined gains fall. Who receives the units matters alongside how many are sold.賣方多賺，不代表福利提高。這個例子的總產量不變，但部分單位由 A 群邊際價值較高的買方轉到 B 群價值較低的買方。賣方得利，總利益卻下降。分給誰與賣多少同樣重要。

    Under perfect discrimination, the seller can charge each unit's WTP without lowering prices on the other units. It expands until WTP meets MC, serving efficient output and capturing the gains. Consumer surplus can fall to zero even as DWL disappears; efficiency does not imply that every buyer benefits.完全差別取價下，賣方可對每單位收取其 WTP，不必降低其他單位的價格。會擴產到 WTP＝MC，服務有效率產量並取得交易利益。CS 可降至零，同時 DWL 消失；有效率不代表每位買方都受益。

    8. Tying and bundling solve different problems8. 搭售與組合銷售處理不同問題

    Usage metering differs from a fixed package; revenue differs from profit.依用量計費與固定套裝不同；收益也不同於利潤。

    Tying and metering搭售與計量搭售與計量

    A base good is linked to repeated purchases of a tied good, such as a printer and required proprietary ink. A low access price can attract light users; recurring marked-up purchases collect more from heavy users.基本商品與重複購買的綁定商品連結，例如印表機與必須使用的專用墨水。較低的入門價格可吸引輕度使用者；持續購買加價耗材，則讓重度使用者支付更多。

    Bundling組合銷售組合銷售

    Two or more goods are sold as a package. Pure bundling offers only the package; mixed bundling also offers components separately. The fixed package does not charge each time an app is used.把兩種以上商品作為套裝出售。純組合只提供套裝；混合組合也單售零件。固定套裝不會在每次使用應用程式時再收費。

    Tying can increase system adoption while an above-MC consumable price reduces use per adopter. Both margins matter for welfare. Complementarity alone does not prove a tie; some restriction or practical dependency must connect purchases.搭售可能增加系統採用者，但耗材價格高於 MC，會壓低每位採用者的使用量。福利須同時看兩者。商品互補本身不代表搭售；須有某種限制或實際依賴來連結購買。

    A package can solve a pricing problem: one buyer values the first component highly, while another values the second highly. Separate high prices lose some useful sales. If their combined values are more similar, one package price can appeal to both buyers and increase consumption.套裝可解決定價問題：一位買方最重視第一項，另一位最重視第二項。分開高價銷售會失去部分有益交易；若合計價值更接近，同一套裝價可吸引兩人並增加消費。

    10. Cold call practice10. 課堂口頭問答練習

    What makes a market a monopoly?什麼條件讓一個市場成為獨占？

    One seller faces no close substitutes in a defined product and geographic market; entry barriers sustain its position.在明確的產品與地理市場中，一個賣方面對沒有相近替代品的需求；進入障礙維持其地位。

    What is marginal revenue?什麼是邊際收益？

    The change in total revenue from an additional unit. Under uniform monopoly pricing, it includes revenue lost by repricing earlier units.多賣一單位帶來的總收益變化。在統一獨占定價下，還包含其他單位降價造成的收入減少。

    What is economic profit?什麼是經濟利潤？

    Total revenue minus all explicit and implicit economic costs, equal to (P−ATC)Q under a common price.總收益減所有顯性與隱性經濟成本；統一定價時等於 (P−ATC)Q。

    Why can a price maker not choose any price and quantity?價格制定者為何不能任選價格與數量？

    Demand links the two. A higher common price generally means fewer sales, so the firm must choose a feasible point on demand.需求連結兩者。較高統一價格通常意味較少銷量，因此須選需求線上的可行點。

    What is deadweight loss?什麼是無謂損失？

    Lost total gains from trade. In the monopoly diagram, it lies between demand and MC over beneficial units not sold.未實現的總交易利益。在獨占圖中，是 D 與 MC 之間那些有益卻未出售單位的面積。

    What sustains group price discrimination?什麼條件能維持分群差別取價？

    Pricing power, workable segmentation or sorting, and limits on resale. Separate group demand and costs determine the optimal prices.定價力量、可行的分群或自我選擇，以及轉售限制。各群需求與成本決定最適價格。

    How does perfect discrimination change revenue?完全差別取價如何改變收益？

    An extra unit can be sold at its own willingness to pay without reducing prior unit prices, so the seller expands while WTP covers MC.新增單位可按其 WTP 出售，不必降低先前單位的價格；因此只要 WTP 足以支付 MC，賣方就擴產。

    How do static and dynamic efficiency differ?靜態效率與動態效率有何不同？

    Static efficiency concerns current allocation; dynamic efficiency concerns future innovation and improvements. A current price diagram does not measure the latter.靜態效率關注當下配置；動態效率關注未來創新與改善。當期價格圖不能衡量後者。
