    Strategic Marketing Management · Session 8策略行銷管理｜第 8 堂

    New Products, Pricing and Grey Markets新產品、定價與灰色市場

    Most new products fail because buyers see no real benefit, and most price cuts backfire because they teach buyers to watch price instead of the brand. This session covers where ideas come from, why tests mislead, how to set and signal a price, and why price gaps between markets invite grey imports.大多數新產品失敗，是因為買家看不到真正的好處；大多數降價會反噬，是因為它教會買家看價格而不是看品牌。本章說明點子從哪裡來、測試為何會誤導、怎麼訂價和用價格傳遞訊號，以及市場間的價差為何會引來灰色進口。

    Chapter map本章地圖

    Real benefit真正的利益→Test it honestly誠實測試→Price that fits the position符合定位的價格→Price psychology價格心理→Avoid discount traps避開折扣陷阱→Grey markets灰色市場

    Key idea重點

    A new product needs a benefit people will pay for, not just a new form. Price is the only part of the marketing mix that brings in money, so it must match the brand's position and stay consistent.新產品需要一個人們願意付錢的利益，而不只是新的形式。價格是行銷組合裡唯一帶進收入的部分，所以必須符合品牌定位並保持一致。

    1. Why most new products fail1. 為什麼大多數新產品會失敗

    Key idea重點

    Huge amounts are spent on new products, yet most fail. The lecturer's main reason is simple: a lack of real benefits. The product is not very new, and buyers do not see a need for it.企業在新產品上花了大筆資金，但大多數仍失敗。老師給的主因很簡單：缺乏真正的利益。產品不夠新，買家也看不出需要它。

    Part of a concept概念的組成Meaning意思Example例子
    Form形式What the product actually is產品實際是什麼樣子Toothpaste as a paste, a powder or a liquid牙膏做成膏狀、粉狀或液狀
    Technology技術How it is made; can we do it?怎麼做出來；我們做得到嗎？A foldable screen可摺疊螢幕
    Benefit 利益利益Something buyers want and will pay for買家想要而且願意付錢的東西Shaving cream as a paper sheet you can carry on a flight or a camping trip做成紙片的刮鬍膏，可以帶上飛機或去露營
    Pair組合How to tell them apart怎麼分辨
    New form / new benefit新形式／新利益Liquid toothpaste changes the form. It does not change what the toothpaste does for you.液態牙膏改變的是形式，牙膏能為你做的事並沒有改變。
    Technology / benefit技術／利益Being able to build something does not mean buyers want it.做得出來，不代表顧客想要。
    Benefit / novelty利益／新奇感A real benefit keeps people buying. Novelty wears off after the first try.真正的利益會讓人持續購買；新奇感試過一次就沒了。

    How it works為什麼

    Changing the form is the easiest way to make something look new, so ask "why?" each time. A new form can also change the benefit, for better or worse. Whatever you make will probably be copied quickly. Being too different is a risk too, because people resist change at first.改變形式是讓東西看起來新最容易的方法，所以每次都要問「為什麼？」。新形式也可能改變利益，變好或變差都有可能。你做出來的東西很可能很快被模仿。太不一樣也有風險，因為人一開始會抗拒改變。

    中文邏輯形式、技術、利益三者裡，最常缺的是利益。換個形式不等於多了好處，要一直問「為什麼要買」。

    2. Where new product ideas come from2. 新產品點子從哪裡來

    Key idea重點

    Start close to the customer. People may not know what engineers can build, but pain points show what is worth building. Grab spread fast because it solved a real problem with taxis.從靠近顧客的地方開始。顧客也許不知道工程師能做出什麼，但痛點會告訴你什麼值得做。Grab 擴張得很快，因為它解決了叫計程車的真實問題。

    Class example · the problem list課堂例子｜問題清單

    List the problems people have with a product, how often and how serious they are, then look for clusters. The telephone story shows both the method and its trap:列出人們使用產品時遇到的問題、多常發生、有多嚴重，再找出相似的群組。電話的故事同時示範了方法和陷阱：

    Rotary phone轉盤電話→Push-button按鍵電話→Hand tied up, wired to the wall手被綁住、線接在牆上→Cordless phone無線電話→"I lost my phone"「我的電話不見了」→Idea: connect it to the wall點子：把它接回牆上

    Ideas can drift into "stupid territory", like a paid service to remember where you parked. Test every idea with one question: would people pay for this?點子可能越走越離譜，例如付費幫你記住車停在哪。每個點子都用一個問題檢驗：人們會為此付錢嗎？

    Gap map 缺口圖缺口圖

    A perceptual map can show empty positions. On a "crunch" axis, apples sit next to potato chips; on "healthy crunch", apple chips win. But a gap is not automatically an opportunity: think of cheap luxury cars.知覺圖可以顯示空白位置。以「酥脆」為軸，蘋果和洋芋片放在一起；換成「健康的酥脆」，蘋果脆片勝出。但空白不等於機會：想想便宜的豪華車。

    Product Innovation Charter (PIC)產品創新章程

    Before investing, check the fit: do we have the skills and technology, do we know these customers, what are cost and competition, and how long before others copy us? Then size the market, profit and rivals.投資前先檢查是否合適：我們有沒有技術與能力、了不了解這群顧客、成本與競爭如何、多久會被模仿？接著估算市場規模、利潤和對手。

    3. New benefit, or just a new form?3. 是新利益，還是只是新形式？

    Practice · Real benefit or just a new form?練習｜真正的利益，還是只是新形式？

    Class example · detergent課堂例子｜洗衣劑

    Powder could turn hard in a wet or humid yard. Liquid is easier but heavy, and "you're paying me for the water". Tablets are tidy, yet people stop over-pouring because nobody breaks a tablet in half, so the seller loses volume. Pods combine detergent, softener and color care: convenient, expensive, and quickly copied by every brand.粉狀洗衣粉放在潮濕的戶外會結塊變硬。液態比較好用但很重，「你是在付錢買水」。錠狀很整齊，但大家不再多倒，因為沒人會把一錠剝成一半，賣方反而少賣。洗衣球把洗劑、柔軟精和護色合在一起：方便、昂貴，而且很快被每個品牌模仿。

    4. Testing new products, and why tests mislead4. 測試新產品，以及測試為何會誤導

    Idea generation產生點子→Screening篩選→Concept development and testing概念發展與測試→Business analysis商業分析→Prototype原型→Test marketing試銷→Commercialization商品化

    Key idea重點

    Concept testing checks the biggest cause of failure early: the buyer did not see a need. It saves money and sets the positioning, but no test is 100% sure.概念測試提早檢查失敗的最大原因：買家看不出需要。它能省錢、也能定下定位，但沒有任何測試是百分之百準確的。

    Too new: people say no太新：大家說不要

    People resist change and cannot imagine a microwave, fax or video call before using one. Computers, fax machines and VCRs were rejected at first. Do not kill a radical idea too quickly.人會抗拒改變，在用過之前想像不到微波爐、傳真機或視訊通話的好處。電腦、傳真機和錄影機一開始都被拒絕。不要太快扼殺激進的點子。

    Not new: people say yes不新：大家說好

    Politeness and over-claiming push stated intent up: "Would you buy it? Yes", then they don't. The honeymoon effect gives high early scores that fade. New Coke and Cherry Coke scored well and failed.禮貌和過度宣稱會拉高表面意願：「你會買嗎？會」，結果沒買。蜜月效應讓早期分數很高，之後下滑。New Coke 和 Cherry Coke 測試分數很好，上市卻失敗。

    How it works為什麼

    The discussion video asks why the most successful new products were often kept away from the formal NPD system. Its own answer: formal systems are routine and slow, big committees lose a shared team identity, and they judge new ideas by old criteria, which favors more of the same.討論影片問：為什麼最成功的新產品常常不是走正式的新產品開發流程？影片自己的答案：正式流程按部就班又慢，大型委員會失去團隊的共同認同，而且用舊標準評判新點子，結果偏向做更多相同的東西。

    中文邏輯測試兩頭都會錯：太新的東西大家先說不要，不新的東西大家客氣說要。所以要看實際的重複購買，而不是嘴上說的意願。

    Pause & think暫停，想一想

    Test-market results are usually better than results after launch. Give three reasons, and say what you would measure instead of first-time sales.試銷結果通常比正式上市後好。請給出三個理由，並說明你會用什麼指標取代首購銷量。

    5. Price: the only P that brings in money5. 價格：唯一帶進收入的 P

    Key idea重點

    Product, place and promotion are costs; price is the only P that generates revenue. It must link to positioning and stay consistent, because switching between premium and value makes a brand look like a yo-yo. "Me too but cheaper" means everyone makes less money.產品、通路和推廣都是成本；價格是唯一帶來收入的 P。它必須和定位連結並保持一致，因為在高價和平價之間跳來跳去，品牌看起來就像溜溜球。「我也有，但更便宜」只會讓大家都少賺。

    Pricing objective定價目標Meaning意思Lecture point課堂重點
    Survival求生存Cope with fierce competition or excess capacity應付激烈競爭或過剩產能"Not good at all"「一點都不好」

    How it works · buying share is not buying loyalty為什麼｜買到市占不等於買到忠誠

    Many firms enter cheap, planning to raise prices once customers are "loyal". When the price rises, people move to the next cheap brand. Blank CDs at 4–5 baht kept creeping up, and buyers left. Oppo entered around 4,000 baht against a 25,000-baht iPhone and has had to launch higher models to build reputation.很多公司低價進場，打算等顧客「忠誠」後再漲價。價格一漲，大家就換到下一個便宜品牌。4 到 5 泰銖的空白光碟價格一路往上加，買家就走了。Oppo 以約 4,000 泰銖對抗 25,000 泰銖的 iPhone，之後只能推出更高階的機種來建立聲譽。

    6. Price signals and reference prices6. 價格訊號與參考價格

    Key idea重點

    Price signaling 價格訊號: a high price suggests high quality, a low price suggests "must be pretty bad". A reference price 參考價格 is the rough price in a buyer's head used to judge every brand in a category.價格訊號：高價暗示高品質，低價暗示「大概不怎麼樣」。參考價格是買家心中用來評判同品類所有品牌的大概價格。

    Class example · Thai green tea課堂例子｜泰國綠茶

    For years most people said a bottle of green tea costs 20 baht, because Oishi and Unif priced there. Unif's Haru-cha at 35 baht, sold as first-harvest leaves, failed. The lecturer heard that the tea costs about 2 baht to produce, before transport, distribution and marketing.多年來大多數人都說一瓶綠茶 20 泰銖，因為 Oishi 和 Unif 都訂這個價。Unif 的 Haru-cha 標榜第一次採收的茶葉、賣 35 泰銖，結果失敗。老師聽說茶的生產成本大約 2 泰銖，還沒算運輸、配銷和行銷。

    Decision lab · Price a new green tea決策實驗｜替新綠茶訂價

    The shelf already has famous brands at a 20-baht reference price. Choose a launch price and see what the shopper is likely to think.貨架上已有知名品牌，參考價格是 20 泰銖。選一個上市價格，看購物者可能怎麼想。

    How it works為什麼

    In surveys people say price is "extremely important", yet asked right after an item goes into the cart, most cannot say what they paid or whether it was on sale. "Customers don't care about price" sounds good but can be bad news: it may mean they see all brands as the same and will drift to private label.問卷裡大家都說價格「極度重要」，但在商品剛放進購物車時問，多數人說不出付了多少錢、有沒有特價。「顧客不在乎價格」聽起來很好，其實可能是壞消息：代表他們覺得所有品牌都一樣，最後會轉向自有品牌。

    7. Setting the price: floor, ceiling and strategy7. 訂價：下限、上限與策略

    Floor = production cost下限＝生產成本→Price somewhere between價格落在中間→Ceiling = consumer perception上限＝消費者認知→Capped in practice by competition實際上受競爭限制

    How it works為什麼

    Textbook models say nobody would pay 500 baht for water. The lecturer disagrees: some people always buy the most expensive brand, applied unevenly across categories. What really caps the price is that "you're not usually alone in the market": Intel faces AMD, and Oculus leans on its game library, because hardware without games is "a TV with no channels".教科書模型說沒人會花 500 泰銖買水。老師不同意：有些人總是買最貴的品牌，只是在不同品類套用得不一致。真正壓住價格的是「市場上通常不只你一家」：Intel 要面對 AMD；Oculus 靠的是遊戲庫，因為沒有遊戲的硬體就像「沒有頻道的電視」。

    9. Price psychology9. 價格心理

    10. Price promotions and price cuts10. 價格促銷與降價

    Key idea重點

    The more often a brand runs price promotions, the more shoppers focus on price, and everyone ends up trapped in promotions that cost money. The lecturer's advice: "stand tall", support the brand with marketing, and stay away from discounting unless you are clearing old stock.品牌越常做價格促銷，購物者就越注意價格，最後大家都被困在花錢的促銷裡。老師的建議：「站穩腳步」，用行銷支持品牌，除非是清舊庫存，否則遠離打折。

    Buyers question the product's quality.買家開始懷疑產品品質。

    Bigger rivals can last longer; a family firm with one brand against Unilever's hundreds. Fabric softener fell from 67 to 13 baht.更大的對手撐得更久；只有一個品牌的家族企業對上擁有上百個品牌的 Unilever。衣物柔軟精從 67 跌到 13 泰銖。

    11. Grey markets11. 灰色市場

    Key idea重點

    A grey market 灰色市場 (parallel import 平行輸入) is genuine branded goods bought in one market and resold in another outside authorized channels. It is unauthorized, but not illegal by definition, though tax or other laws may be broken in practice. The elderly market, once also called "grey", is now the silver market.灰色市場（平行輸入）是在一個市場買進真品，再到另一個市場、在授權通路之外轉賣。它未經授權，但本質上不違法，不過實務上可能違反稅法或其他法規。過去也被叫做「灰色」的高齡市場，現在改稱銀髮市場。

    1A big price gap價差大

    The legitimate channel offers significant value to undercut.正規通路的價格有很大空間可以壓低。

    2A well-known product知名產品

    It sells itself, so no marketing is needed.產品自己會賣，不需要行銷。

    3Price or currency differences價格或匯率差異

    Taxes, refunds or exchange rates create the margin.稅、退稅或匯率創造利潤空間。

    13. Cold call practice13. Cold call 練習

    Why do most new products fail?為什麼大多數新產品會失敗？

    Mostly because buyers do not see a need: the product is not very new and offers no real benefit beyond a new form.主要是買家看不出需要：產品不夠新，除了新形式之外沒有真正的利益。

    Should new products be technology-driven or market-driven?新產品應該由技術驅動還是市場驅動？

    Start from the customer's pain point. Technology answers "can we?", while the market answers "should we?". Grab and online banking spread quickly because they solved real problems.從顧客的痛點出發。技術回答「做不做得到」，市場回答「該不該做」。Grab 和網路銀行擴散得快，是因為解決了真實問題。

    Why are test-market results usually better than results after launch?為什麼試銷結果通常比上市後好？

    Novelty and the honeymoon effect, extra support in test areas, competitors not yet reacting, and politeness inflating stated intent.新奇感與蜜月效應、試銷區有額外支援、對手還沒反應，以及禮貌讓表面意願偏高。

    Why is price the most important P for revenue?為什麼價格是帶來收入最重要的 P？

    It is the only part of the mix that generates revenue; product, place and promotion are costs.它是行銷組合中唯一產生收入的部分；產品、通路和推廣都是成本。

    What is a reference price?什麼是參考價格？

    The rough price in a buyer's head for a category, used to judge every brand. For bottled green tea in Thailand, it was about 20 baht.買家心中某個品類的大概價格，用來評判每個品牌。在泰國，瓶裝綠茶大約是 20 泰銖。

    What is price signaling?什麼是價格訊號？

    Using price as a quality cue: a high price suggests high quality and a low price suggests low quality, which private labels suffer from.把價格當作品質線索：高價暗示高品質，低價暗示低品質，自有品牌常吃這個虧。

    Skimming or penetration: what is the difference?吸脂定價和滲透定價有什麼不同？

    Skimming launches an innovation at a high price that falls later; penetration sets a low price for high volume and share.吸脂定價讓創新產品高價上市、之後再降；滲透定價用低價換取高銷量和市占。

    Why did the Tata Nano fail?Tata Nano 為什麼失敗？

    Its very low price signaled low status; people saw it as "a motorcycle with four wheels" and feared being judged as poor.超低價傳遞了低地位的訊號；大家把它看成「四個輪子的機車」，怕被人當成窮人。

    What is captive product pricing?什麼是專屬耗材定價？

    Selling the main product cheaply and earning the margin on items that must be used with it, such as razor blades or printer ink.主產品賣得便宜，利潤賺在必須搭配使用的品項上，例如刮鬍刀片或印表機墨水。

    What is the contrast principle?什麼是對比原則？

    When the second item compared is quite different from the first, the gap seems bigger than it is, so starting high makes later items feel cheaper.比較時如果第二樣和第一樣差很多，差距會顯得比實際大，所以先貴後便宜，後面的就顯得划算。

    Why are price promotions dangerous?為什麼價格促銷很危險？

    They train shoppers to focus on price and wait for sales, lower margins, and can trigger the low-quality, fragile-share and shallow-pockets traps.它們訓練購物者只看價格、等特價，壓低毛利，還可能觸發低品質、脆弱市占和口袋不夠深三種陷阱。

    What is a grey market, and when is it most likely?什麼是灰色市場？什麼時候最可能出現？

    Genuine goods resold across markets outside authorized channels. It is likely when the price gap is large, the product is well known and sells itself, and prices or currencies differ.在授權通路之外跨市場轉賣的真品。價差大、產品知名到自己會賣、價格或匯率有差異時最可能出現。
