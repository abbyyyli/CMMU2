    1. Job Characteristics Model1. 工作特徵模型

    Five job dimensions create three psychological states, which lead to work outcomes. Source: Robbins and Judge.五項工作特徵創造三種心理狀態，進而影響工作成果。資料來源：Robbins 與 Judge。

    Dimension構面Meaning意義Do not confuse with不要與下列概念混淆
    Skill variety 技能多樣性技能多樣性The job uses different skills and activities工作運用不同技能與活動。Simply adding more tasks, which can mean overload只是一味增加工作項目，可能反而造成負荷過重。
    Task identity 任務完整性任務完整性Completing a whole, identifiable piece of work完成一項完整且可辨識的工作。Task significance (impact on others)任務重要性（對他人的影響）。
    Task significance任務重要性The work affects other people工作會影響他人。An important-sounding job title聽起來很重要的職稱。
    Autonomy 自主權自主性Freedom in how the work is done決定如何完成工作的自由。No accountability or no standards不等於沒有問責或標準。
    Feedback回饋The work itself shows how well it was done工作本身讓人知道做得好不好。Having a KPI, which does not guarantee useful feedback有 KPI 不代表能得到有用的回饋。

    Growth-need strength 成長需求強度 moderates the model: people who want challenge respond more strongly to enriched jobs. More autonomy is not a universal fix.成長需求強度會調節模型的效果：渴望挑戰的人對內容更豐富的工作反應更強。提高自主性不是對所有人都有效的萬用解方。

    2. Satisfaction, commitment and OCB2. 滿意度、承諾與組織公民行為

    Related, but not the same. The lecture links job satisfaction to citizenship, commitment, productivity, absenteeism and turnover. The arrows show relationships to examine, not proven causes.這些概念有關，卻不相同。課堂將工作滿意度連結到組織公民行為、承諾、生產力、缺勤與流失；箭頭代表值得檢查的關係，並非已證明的因果。

    Job satisfaction工作滿意度

    "I like my job."「我喜歡我的工作。」

    Does not prove they will stay or perform well不代表此人一定留下或表現出色。
    Organizational commitment組織承諾 組織承諾

    "I believe in this organization and want to stay."「我認同這個組織，並希望留下。」

    Does not mean they like every task不代表喜歡工作中的每項任務。
    OCB組織公民行為（OCB） 組織公民行為

    "I help a colleague even though no one pays me to."「即使沒人付錢，我也會幫助同事。」

    Does not follow from hitting sales targets達成銷售目標不代表一定會如此。

    Three components of commitment承諾的三個組成部分

    1. Strong acceptance of the organization's goals and values強烈認同組織的目標與價值。
    2. Willingness to put in considerable effort for the organization願意為組織投入相當程度的努力。
    3. Desire to remain a member希望繼續成為組織成員。

    Organizational citizenship behavior (OCB) is discretionary, outside the formal reward system, and done without expecting a direct reward. A top performer can still show weak OCB.組織公民行為（OCB）是自主選擇、超出正式獎酬制度之外，而且不期待直接回報的行為。高績效員工也可能缺乏 OCB。

    3. Responses to dissatisfaction: EVLN3. 對不滿意的反應：EVLN

    Dissatisfied employees do not all quit. Two axes, active or passive and constructive or destructive, give four responses.不滿意的員工不一定離職。以主動／被動、建設性／破壞性兩條軸線，可區分四種反應。

    注意Low morale 本身不能判斷員工會選哪一種反應，要看實際行為證據。投影片把 lateness / absence 放在 Exit；Robbins 課本放在 Neglect，考試照投影片寫，必要時向老師確認。

    The slide notes that exit, voice and loyalty apply to all stakeholder groups, not only employees.投影片指出，離職、建言與忠誠也適用於其他利害關係人，不限於員工。

    4. Reinforcement theory4. 增強理論

    Consequences shape future behavior. A reward strengthens whatever behavior it actually pays for.行為的後果會影響未來行為。獎勵實際與哪種行為連動，就會強化哪種行為。

    Behavior行為→Consequence (reward or punishment)後果（獎勵或懲罰）→Likelihood of repeating changes重複該行為的可能性改變
    Positive reinforcement正向增強 正向增強

    Slide example: a bonus for reaching a sales quota, to encourage more sales effort.投影片例子：達成銷售配額後給獎金，以鼓勵更努力銷售。

    Punishment懲罰

    Slide example: a salary deduction for lateness, to encourage punctuality.投影片例子：遲到扣薪，以鼓勵準時。

    Term術語After the behavior行為發生後的措施Aim目的
    Positive reinforcement正向增強Add something desired加入員工想要的事物。Increase the behavior增加該行為。
    Negative reinforcement負向增強Remove something unpleasant移除令人不快的事物。Increase the behavior增加該行為。
    Punishment懲罰Apply something unpleasant施加令人不快的後果。Decrease the behavior減少該行為。

    Negative reinforcement is a standard textbook term added for clarity; it is not on the slide. "Positive" and "negative" mean adding or removing, not good or bad.「負向增強」是為釐清概念而補充的標準教科書術語，投影片並未列出。「正」與「負」指加入或移除，不是好與壞。

    Managers should understand employees' personality and values to know what each person actually finds rewarding.主管應了解員工的人格與價值觀，才知道每個人真正重視什麼獎勵。

    5. Why reward systems fail, and how to fix them5. 獎酬制度為何失效，以及如何修正

    Signs of a poor reward system, quoted on the slide:投影片引用以下話語，作為獎酬制度不良的徵兆：

    It doesn't matter how hard I work around here.「我在這裡再努力也沒差。」 Who cares.「誰在乎？」 Hey, we can get away with anything around here!「嘿，在這裡做什麼都不會有事！」 If management doesn't care, why should I?「管理層都不在乎了，我為什麼要在乎？」

    Six elements of a recognition system表揚制度的六個要素

    1

    Commitment and support from top and middle management高階與中階管理層的承諾與支持。

    2

    Formal standards and expectations set in policy以政策訂定正式標準與期待。

    3

    A system of performance measurement設立績效衡量制度。

    4

    Consequences that participants see as meaningful參與者認為有意義的後果。

    5

    Fair and regular application公平且定期地執行。

    6

    Periodic evaluation of the system定期評估制度。

    A KPI is only one of six. If leaders do not back the system, rewards mean little to staff, or rules are applied unevenly, behavior will not change the way management wants.KPI 只是六要素之一。若領導者不支持、獎勵對員工沒有意義，或規則執行不一致，行為就不會照管理層期望的方向改變。

    6. Schedules of reinforcement6. 增強時制

    First ask: is the reward tied to time (interval) or to output (ratio)? Then: is the rule fixed or variable?先問：獎勵依時間（時距）還是產出（比率）發放？再問：規則是固定還是變動？

    Schedule時制Rule規則Effect on behavior (slide)對行為的影響（投影片）Slide example投影片例子
    Continuous連續增強Every desired behavior每次出現期望行為都給獎勵。Fast learning, fast extinction學習快，停止獎勵後也消退得快。Compliments稱讚。
    Fixed interval固定時距Fixed times固定的時間點。Average performance, rapid extinction表現普通，消退快。Weekly paychecks每週發薪。
    Variable interval變動時距Variable times不固定的時間點。Moderate to high performance, slow extinction表現中等至高，消退慢。Pop quiz, unannounced inspection突擊小考、無預警檢查。
    Fixed ratio固定比率Fixed amount of output固定的產出數量。High, stable performance, rapid extinction表現高且穩定，消退快。Piece-rate pay按件計酬。
    Variable ratio變動比率Variable amount of output不固定的產出數量。Very high performance, slow extinction表現很高，消退慢。Commissioned sales佣金銷售（投影片的例子）。

    Extinction 消退 means a learned behavior fades when reinforcement stops. In a real case, classify by the actual reward rule: a fixed commission on every sale behaves more like fixed ratio.「消退」指停止增強後，已學會的行為逐漸減少。分析真實個案時，應依實際獎酬規則分類：每筆銷售都支付固定佣金，更接近固定比率。

    Pause & think暫停，想一想

    Continuous reinforcement can speed early learning but may fade quickly once rewards stop. Why might a less predictable schedule sustain responding longer? What unintended behavior would still need monitoring?連續增強可讓行為較快學會，但停止獎勵後也可能很快消退。為什麼較不可預測的時制可能讓行為維持較久？仍需監測哪些非預期行為？

    9. Common mistakes9. 常見錯誤

    Task identity means the job is important.任務完整性表示工作很重要。

    Identity is completing a whole job. Significance is impact on others.完整性指完成整項工作；重要性指對他人的影響。

    Autonomy tells employees how well they did.自主性告訴員工自己做得好不好。

    That is feedback. Autonomy creates responsibility.那是回饋；自主性帶來責任感。

    Happy workers always perform better.快樂的員工一定表現較好。

    They may be related; the slide does not prove cause.兩者可能相關；投影片沒有證明因果。

    Commitment and OCB are the same.承諾與 OCB 一樣。

    Commitment is an attitude. OCB is voluntary behavior.承諾是態度；OCB 是自願行為。

    Low morale means people will resign.士氣低落表示員工一定會辭職。

    They may also voice, wait or neglect.也可能提出意見、等待或消極怠忽。

    Negative reinforcement is punishment.負向增強就是懲罰。

    It removes something unpleasant to increase a behavior.它移除不愉快的事物，目的在增加某行為。

    Any sales commission is variable ratio.任何銷售佣金都是變動比率。

    Check the actual rule linking reward to output.須檢查獎勵與產出連動的實際規則。

    High KPI means organizational success.KPI 高就代表組織成功。

    The system may reward the wrong output.制度可能獎勵了錯誤的產出。

    10. Cold call practice10. 課堂問答練習

    What is the Job Characteristics Model?什麼是工作特徵模型？

    It links five job dimensions to three psychological states and to outcomes such as motivation, quality, satisfaction, and lower absenteeism and turnover.它連結五項工作特徵、三種心理狀態，以及動機、品質、滿意度和較低缺勤率與流失率等成果。

    Which dimensions create experienced meaningfulness?哪些構面產生工作的意義感？

    Skill variety, task identity and task significance together.技能多樣性、任務完整性與任務重要性三者共同作用。

    Autonomy versus feedback?自主性與回饋有何不同？

    Autonomy creates experienced responsibility. Feedback gives knowledge of actual results.自主性帶來責任感；回饋讓人知道實際成果。

    What is growth-need strength?什麼是成長需求強度？

    How strongly a person wants challenge and growth. It explains why people respond differently to enriched jobs.一個人渴望挑戰與成長的程度；它解釋為何人們對豐富化工作的反應不同。

    What is organizational commitment?什麼是組織承諾？

    Accepting the organization's goals and values, willingness to work hard for it, and wanting to stay.認同組織目標與價值、願意投入努力，並希望留下。

    What is OCB?什麼是 OCB？

    Discretionary helpful behavior outside the formal reward system, done without expecting a direct reward.正式獎酬制度以外、不期待直接回報的自發助人行為。

    What is EVLN?什麼是 EVLN？

    Four responses to dissatisfaction: Exit (active, destructive), Voice (active, constructive), Loyalty (passive, constructive), Neglect (passive, destructive).對不滿意的四種反應：離職（主動、破壞性）、建言（主動、建設性）、忠誠（被動、建設性）、怠忽（被動、破壞性）。

    What is reinforcement theory?什麼是增強理論？

    Consequences shape future behavior. Rewarded behavior tends to be repeated; punished behavior tends to decrease.後果會塑造未來行為。獲獎勵的行為傾向重複；受懲罰的行為傾向減少。

    Why can a reward system fail?獎酬制度為什麼會失效？

    Employees see no reliable link between effort, performance and meaningful, fairly applied consequences.員工看不到努力、績效與有意義且公平執行的後果之間有可靠連結。

    Name the six recognition elements.說出表揚制度的六要素。

    Management support, formal standards, performance measurement, meaningful consequences, fair and regular application, periodic evaluation.管理層支持、正式標準、績效衡量、有意義的後果、公平且定期的執行、定期評估。

    How do the schedules differ?各種增強時制有何差別？

    Interval schedules depend on time, ratio schedules on output. Fixed is predictable, variable is not. Continuous rewards every occurrence.時距時制依時間、比率時制依產出；固定時制可預測，變動時制不可預測；連續增強則在每次行為發生時獎勵。
