    1. Market segmentation1. 市場區隔

    Segmentation divides a diverse market into groups that share needs, characteristics or usage conditions. The challenge is choosing a difference that actually affects value or behavior.市場區隔將多樣的市場分成需求、特徵或使用條件相近的群體。難點在於選出真正影響價值或行為的差異。

    Geographic地理區隔

    Country, region, access, climate; where the offer is feasible.國家、地區、可取得性與氣候；判斷在哪裡能實際提供產品或服務。

    Demographic人口統計區隔

    Age, income, family stage; describes who, not necessarily why.年齡、所得與家庭階段；描述「是誰」，不一定能解釋「為什麼」。

    Psychographic心理特徵區隔 心理特徵

    Motives, interests, values and lifestyle; the reason benefits may matter.動機、興趣、價值觀與生活型態；解釋某項利益為何可能重要。

    Behavioral行為區隔

    Occasion, frequency, brand repertoire, benefit sought and switching.使用情境、頻率、慣常購買的品牌組合、追求的利益與品牌轉換。

    Four tests for an effective segment有效區隔的四項檢驗

    Measurable可衡量Can you size or describe the opportunity?能否估計機會的規模或描述其特徵？
    Accessible可接觸Can your channels and message reach it?通路與訊息能觸及這個群體嗎？
    Substantial具足夠規模與價值Is demand large and valuable enough?需求的規模與價值是否足夠？
    Actionable可執行Can you serve it with a viable offer?能否以商業上可行的提案服務這個群體？

    A platform reporting 500,000 people with an interest does not show that 500,000 will buy. A countable left-handed-consumer group shows that a countable group is not automatically a relevant market for every product.平台顯示有 500,000 人對某事感興趣，不代表 500,000 人都會購買。左撇子消費者這類可以計算人數的群體，說明可計數的群體不一定對每種產品都是有意義的市場。

    Decision lab · Segment before you target決策實驗｜先分群，再選定目標

    Change the proposed segment. Identify which unanswered question could invalidate the business case.切換提議的區隔，找出哪個尚未回答的問題，可能推翻整個商業論證。

    2. Targeting2. 目標市場選擇

    Targeting chooses segments the company can reach, satisfy and serve sustainably. No strategy is automatically best: balance coverage, specialization, costs and competitive response.目標市場選擇是挑出公司能觸及、滿足並持續服務的區隔。沒有任何策略必然最好：須平衡涵蓋範圍、專業聚焦、成本與競爭者反應。

    Undifferentiated無差異行銷

    One broad offer for many buyers; simpler to produce and communicate, but may be too generic.以一種廣泛適用的提案服務多種買家；生產與溝通較簡單，但也可能過於籠統。

    Candy / basic gum糖果／基本款口香糖
    Differentiated差異化行銷

    Multiple products or messages for different benefits; potentially higher sales, complexity and cannibalization 自家互搶.針對不同利益提供多種產品或訊息；可能提高銷售額，也增加複雜度與自家產品間的銷售侵蝕。

    Breeze / Omo / shampoo linesBreeze／Omo／洗髮精產品線
    Concentrated / niche集中／利基行銷 利基

    Deep focus on a small group; strong fit, but limited customer pool and purchase frequency.深入服務小群體；契合度高，但顧客總量與購買頻率有限。

    Product line proliferation · Sales are not incremental profit產品線擴張｜銷售額不等於增量利潤

    Variant launches can produce trial and fill shelf space, yet some purchases simply move from an existing company brand. Breeze versions, shampoo variants, unusual Pepsi flavors and Lay's/Pringles illustrate why added SKUs do not necessarily create new demand.新款式可以帶來試用並占據貨架空間，但部分購買只是從公司既有品牌轉移過來。Breeze 各版本、洗髮精款式、特殊 Pepsi 口味及 Lay’s／Pringles，說明新增 SKU 不一定創造新需求。

    Single-month observation單月觀察

    A customer visits one Indian restaurant exactly once. That outlet has 100% of their observed Indian-restaurant visits that month.一位顧客剛好只去了一家印度餐廳一次。在該月觀察到的印度餐廳造訪中，這家店占了 100%。

    What the observation cannot prove這項觀察無法證明什麼

    Permanent loyalty, no future switching, or an exclusive consumer segment. Test a longer period and other occasions.無法證明永久忠誠、未來不轉換，或顧客只屬於某個排他的區隔。應觀察更長期間與其他情境。

    The same individual can use an Apple and a Samsung device, or own a Volvo and a sports car. One person can belong to several apparent psychographic profiles. A target does not exclude other buyers.同一個人可以同時使用 Apple 與 Samsung 裝置，或同時擁有 Volvo 和跑車。一個人可能符合數種表面上的心理特徵輪廓。選定目標客群不代表排除其他買家。

    3. Positioning3. 定位

    Positioning is the place a brand occupies in consumers' minds relative to alternatives. A firm's intended slogan is not the same as the associations that shoppers actually form.定位是品牌相對於替代選項，在消費者心中占據的位置。公司想傳達的標語，不等於購物者實際形成的品牌聯想。

    Overpositioning 定位過窄定位過窄The promise is so narrow that other relevant buyers or uses feel excluded.承諾太狹窄，讓其他相關買家或用途感到被排除。Cooler Club's gendered message.Cooler Club 帶有性別指向的訊息。

    Meaningful differentiation can come from the product, service, employees or recognizable brand assets.     5. Disintermediation5. 去中間化

    Disintermediation 去中間化 bypasses intermediaries. Vertical integration 垂直整合 brings an activity under ownership or control; the two can overlap, but are not synonyms.去中間化是繞過中間商；垂直整合是將某項活動納入自身的所有權或控制。兩者可能重疊，但不是同義詞。

    Manufacturer製造商→Wholesaler批發商→Retailer零售商→Customer顧客
    Manufacturer製造商→Direct web / delivery網路直銷／直接配送→Customer顧客

    Direct sales may improve customer relationships and pricing control, but the producer must now handle acquisition, fulfillment, service, returns and possible retailer conflict. The middleman's markup is not pure free profit.直銷可能改善顧客關係與定價控制，但生產者必須自行處理獲客、履約配送、服務、退貨，以及可能的零售商衝突。中間商的加價並非可直接拿走的純利潤。

    6. Direct and indirect competition6. 直接與間接競爭

    Direct competitors sell the same product or service type. Indirect competitors come from another category but can satisfy the same need, occasion, budget or time constraint.直接競爭者銷售相同類型的產品或服務；間接競爭者來自另一品類，卻能滿足相同需求、情境，或預算與時間限制。

    Direct competition直接競爭

    Coke versus Pepsi; one coffee brand versus another; one pretzel seller versus another.Coke 與 Pepsi；一個咖啡品牌與另一個；一家椒鹽捲餅店與另一家。

    Indirect competition間接競爭

    Auntie Anne's versus a Dairy Queen snack option; sports drinks versus water; mouth strips versus chewing gum.Auntie Anne’s 與 Dairy Queen 的點心選項；運動飲料與水；口氣清新薄片與口香糖。

    Case · Auntie Anne's vs. Dairy Queen案例｜Auntie Anne’s 與 Dairy Queen

    In a Thai shopping-mall example, a pretzel stand may compete with a different-category snack at a comparable price.     10. Cold call practice10. 課堂口答練習

    Click a question to reveal its answer.點擊問題，查看答案。

    What is STP?什麼是 STP？

    Segmentation divides the market by relevant differences, targeting selects attractive and feasible groups, and positioning builds the intended brand meaning relative to alternatives.市場區隔依相關差異劃分市場；目標市場選擇挑出有吸引力且可行的群體；定位則建立品牌相對於替代選項的預期意義。

    Why is strict STP sometimes misleading?為什麼過度嚴格的 STP 有時會造成誤導？

    One person can buy across brands, categories and apparent segments. Segments guide decisions but do not dictate behavior.一個人可以跨品牌、品類與表面上的區隔購買。區隔用來引導決策，不能決定消費者的行為。

    What makes segmentation effective?什麼讓市場區隔有效？

    Measurability, accessibility, substantiality and actionability.可衡量、可接觸、具足夠規模與價值，以及可執行。

    What is a niche's biggest risk?利基市場最大的風險是什麼？

    A specialized group may be too small or buy too infrequently; rivals can divide the limited demand further.專門群體可能太小，或購買次數太少；競爭者還可能進一步瓜分有限需求。

    What is cannibalization?什麼是自家銷售侵蝕？

    A firm's new product takes sales from its existing products instead of attracting genuinely additional purchases.公司的新產品搶走既有產品的銷售，而非吸引真正額外的購買。

    What is positioning?什麼是定位？

    The meaning or place a brand occupies in consumers' minds relative to competitors. Managers can intend a position, but consumers form the actual perception.品牌相對於競爭者，在消費者心中的意義或位置。管理者可以設定想要的定位，但實際知覺由消費者形成。

    What is differentiation?什麼是差異化？

    A meaningful, credible distinction that customers value and that the company can deliver. Difference alone is not enough.顧客重視、具有意義且可信，而且公司有能力交付的區別。只有不同還不夠。

    How do you create one?如何建立一張定位圖？

    Define the consumer occasion, select meaningful axes, label the directions, plot relevant brands using evidence or explicitly marked hypotheses, and interpret the alternatives.界定消費情境、選擇有意義的座標軸、標示方向，依證據或明確註明的假設放入相關品牌，再解讀替代選項。

    What is underpositioning?什麼是定位不足？

    Consumers do not have a sufficiently clear association with the brand.消費者對品牌沒有足夠清楚的聯想。

    What is overpositioning?什麼是定位過窄？

    A brand is perceived too narrowly, potentially excluding other relevant users or occasions.品牌被理解得太狹窄，可能排除其他相關使用者或情境。

    What is confused positioning?什麼是定位混亂？

    Customers receive inconsistent messages from communication, pricing, distribution or actual experience.消費者從溝通、定價、通路或實際體驗中，收到不一致的訊息。

    Can an empty map area be an opportunity?定位圖的空白區可能是機會嗎？

    Possibly, but it may also represent a combination customers do not want. Validate demand and economics first.有可能，但也可能代表顧客不想要的組合。應先驗證需求與商業經濟性。

    Direct vs. indirect competition?直接與間接競爭有何不同？

    Direct competitors belong to the same product/service category; indirect competitors come from different categories but can substitute for a similar need or occasion.直接競爭者屬於相同產品／服務品類；間接競爭者來自不同品類，卻能替代性地滿足相似需求或情境。

    Why is indirect competition important?為什麼間接競爭重要？

    Customers choose based on needs, money, time and availability, not always the category definitions managers use.顧客依需求、金錢、時間與可取得性做選擇，不一定遵循管理者使用的品類定義。

    What is disintermediation?什麼是去中間化？

    Removing intermediaries between producers and users, often by selling or delivering directly.移除生產者與使用者之間的中間商，常透過直接銷售或配送達成。

    Disintermediation vs. vertical integration?去中間化與垂直整合有何不同？

    Disintermediation removes a stage; integration takes control of a stage. They may overlap but are not identical.去中間化移除某個中介環節；垂直整合取得某個環節的控制權。兩者可能重疊，但不相同。

    Why can D2C fail?為什麼 D2C 可能失敗？

    A producer may underestimate fulfillment, acquisition and service costs or provoke conflict with existing retailers.生產者可能低估履約配送、獲客與服務成本，或引發與既有零售商的衝突。

    What are entry and exit barriers?什麼是進入與退出障礙？

    Entry barriers make it hard to start competing; exit barriers make it costly or difficult to leave, such as long-term service obligations.進入障礙使開始競爭更困難；退出障礙使離開市場成本高或不易，例如長期服務義務。

    What are three consumption-growth strategies?三種消費成長策略是什麼？

    Attract new category users, find new uses or occasions, and increase the volume or frequency of use.吸引新的品類使用者、尋找新用途或情境，以及提高使用量或使用頻率。

    What is planned obsolescence?什麼是計畫性汰舊？

    Deliberately designing products to wear out or become replaceable sooner; this differs from useful innovation and raises ethical concerns.刻意將產品設計成更早耗損或需要更換；這與有用的創新不同，且會引發倫理問題。

    What is downsizing?什麼是縮水包裝？

    Reducing net quantity without a proportionate reduction in selling price, increasing the effective unit price.減少淨含量，售價卻未同比例下降，使實際單位價格提高。

    Why is hidden downsizing problematic?為什麼隱瞞縮水包裝有問題？

    Consumers may misjudge value if package size changes are concealed; trust and future repeat purchases may suffer.若隱瞞包裝含量變動，消費者可能誤判價值，進而損害信任與未來重複購買。

    Why is revenue not enough?為什麼只看營收不夠？

    Sales can shift across the firm's products or be generated through high costs. We must evaluate truly incremental contribution and profit.銷售可能只是公司內部產品間轉移，也可能靠高成本取得。我們必須評估真正新增的貢獻利益與利潤。

    What is wrong with using 400,000 interests as demand?把 400,000 筆興趣受眾當成需求，有什麼問題？

    Interests are an audience estimate, not proof of willingness to pay, category frequency, retail availability or repeat behavior. Qualify a real segment and test trial and retention with representative research.興趣是受眾估計，不是願付價格、品類購買頻率、零售可取得性或重複購買行為的證據。應確認真實區隔，並透過具代表性的研究測試試用與留存。

    Who are the direct and indirect competitors?誰是直接與間接競爭者？

    Direct competitors may include other flavored hydration or sports-water products in the defined category. Indirect competitors could include bottled water, fruit juice or other refreshments that serve the same post-exercise occasion. Confirm substitution with actual buyer evidence.直接競爭者可能包括界定品類中其他調味補水飲品或運動水；間接競爭者可能是瓶裝水、果汁或其他滿足相同運動後情境的飲品。應以實際買家證據確認替代關係。

    Does premium positioning fit heavy discounting?高級定位與大幅折扣相容嗎？

    It may produce confused signals if price, product, packaging, retail environment and advertising conflict. Research perceived value and use a coherent pricing strategy, while recognizing that not all temporary promotions necessarily damage a premium brand.若價格、產品、包裝、零售環境與廣告互相矛盾，可能傳送混亂訊號。應研究感知價值並採一致的定價策略；同時也要承認，並非所有短期促銷都必然傷害高級品牌。

    What should the manager decide about undisclosed downsizing?管理者應如何決定未揭露的縮水包裝？

    Reject concealed reductions. Price and quantity changes should be transparent, with clear net-content disclosure and realistic assessment of long-term customer trust, repeat purchase and profitability.拒絕隱瞞減量。價格與數量變動應透明，清楚揭露淨含量，並務實評估長期顧客信任、重複購買與獲利能力。
