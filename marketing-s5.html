    1. Market segmentation1. 市場區隔

    Key idea重點

    Segmentation groups customers by differences that affect their needs, choices or the way the company can serve them.市場區隔依會影響需要、選擇或服務方式的差異分組。

    Geographic地理區隔

    Country, region, access, climate; where the offer is feasible.國家、地區、可取得性與氣候；判斷在哪裡能實際提供產品或服務。

    Demographic人口統計區隔

    Age, income, family stage; describes who, not necessarily why.年齡、所得與家庭階段；描述「是誰」，不一定能解釋「為什麼」。

    Psychographic心理特徵區隔 心理特徵

    Motives, interests, values and lifestyle; the reason benefits may matter.動機、興趣、價值觀與生活型態；解釋某項利益為何可能重要。

    Behavioral行為區隔

    Occasion, frequency, brand repertoire, benefit sought and switching.使用情境、頻率、慣常購買的品牌組合、追求的利益與品牌轉換。

    How it works為什麼

    Age can describe a buyer without explaining the need. The same person may want speed on one occasion and comfort on another. Children, parenting and an empty nest can also change time, roles and spending.年齡能描述人，卻未必解釋需要；同一人可能這次要快、下次要舒適。育兒或空巢等階段也會改變時間、角色與支出。

    Four tests for an effective segment有效區隔的四項檢驗

    Measurable可衡量Can you size or describe the opportunity?能否估計機會的規模或描述其特徵？
    Accessible可接觸Can the company’s channels and message reach it?通路與訊息能觸及這個群體嗎？
    Substantial具足夠規模與價值Is demand large and valuable enough?需求的規模與價值是否足夠？
    Actionable可執行Can you serve it with a viable offer?能否以商業上可行的提案服務這個群體？

    Example例子

    A platform shows 500,000 interested users. That is possible reach, not 500,000 buyers. Ask whether they need the offer, can be reached, will pay and buy often enough.平台列出 500,000 名有興趣者，是可能觸及，不是 500,000 個買家。要問有無需要、能否觸及、願不願付費與買多常。

    Example例子

    The class example counts 24 million left-handed people in the United States. The group is measurable, but a product still needs a useful left-handed benefit.課堂以美國 2,400 萬左撇子為例，群體可衡量，但產品仍須有對左撇子有用的利益。

    Decision lab · Segment before you target決策實驗｜先分群，再選定目標

    Would you launch a special product for this group?你會為這群人推出專用產品嗎？

    Change the proposed segment. Identify which unanswered question could invalidate the business case.切換提議的區隔，找出哪個尚未回答的問題，可能推翻整個商業論證。

    Age is a description, not a need.年齡是描述，不是需求。

    Research the benefit, purchasing occasion and substitutes; age alone does not establish a separate product requirement.研究所需利益、購買情境與替代品；年齡本身不能證明需要另一項專用產品。

    Why can a large measurable segment still be a poor target market? Give an example of a better behavioral variable.為什麼人數多、可衡量的區隔，仍可能不是好的目標市場？請舉出更合適的行為變數。

    Ethical targeting: effectiveness and responsibility倫理投放：效果與責任

    Key idea重點

    Targeting should help people choose, not exploit children or vulnerable buyers. Keep the claim clear and truthful, and ask whether the offer improves customer welfare. A gender label alone does not establish a different functional need.鎖定客群應幫助選擇，而非利用兒童或脆弱買家。訴求須清楚真實，也要看是否改善福祉；性別標籤本身不能證明功能需要不同。

    2. Targeting2. 目標市場選擇

    Key idea重點

    Choose groups the company can reach, satisfy and serve profitably. Compare the sales opportunity with costs, competition and the size of the customer pool.選企業能觸及、滿足且有利潤服務的群體；把銷售機會與成本、競爭、客群規模一起比較。

    Undifferentiated無差異行銷

    One broad offer for many buyers; simpler to produce and communicate, but may be too generic.以一種廣泛適用的提案服務多種買家；生產與溝通較簡單，但也可能過於籠統。

    Candy / basic gum糖果／基本款口香糖
    Differentiated差異化行銷

    Multiple products or messages for different benefits; potentially higher sales, complexity and cannibalization 自家互搶.針對不同利益提供多種產品或訊息；可能提高銷售額，也增加複雜度與自家產品間的銷售侵蝕。

    Breeze / Omo / shampoo linesBreeze／Omo／洗髮精產品線
    Concentrated / niche集中／利基行銷 利基

    Deep focus on a small group; strong fit, but limited customer pool and purchase frequency.深入服務小群體；契合度高，但顧客總量與購買頻率有限。

    Ryka pool shoes / Jolt ColaRyka 水中運動鞋／Jolt Cola

    Product line proliferation · Sales are not incremental profit產品線擴張｜銷售額不等於增量利潤

    Key idea重點

    A new variant can win sales from the company’s own products. Count what the whole business gains, not just what the new product sells.新品可能搶自家舊品業績，要看整家公司增加多少，不只新品賣多少。

    Example例子

    Of 1,000 new-product sales, 700 replace an existing product. Only 300 units are additional. Start with the new product’s contribution, subtract the old contribution lost, then subtract launch and operating costs.新品賣 1,000 件，其中 700 件取代舊品，新增僅 300 件。先算新品貢獻，扣失去的舊品貢獻，再扣上市與營運成本。

    Interactive · Cannibalization economics互動｜自家銷售侵蝕的經濟分析

    What did the company actually gain?公司實際增加了什麼？

    Count new-product contribution on all new sales. Subtract the old contribution lost when buyers switch between the company’s products.先計全部新品貢獻，再扣買家從自家舊品轉購所失去的貢獻。

    Gross new SKU (Stock Keeping Unit) units新 SKU（存貨單位） 的總銷售數量1,000
    Truly additional units (assumed)真正新增的數量（假設）300
    Incremental operating contribution增量營運貢獻利益+THB 10,000

    How it works為什麼

    A niche can be too small even with a useful benefit. Potential buyers × purchase frequency sets the sales ceiling.利基即使有用，也可能太小；潛在買家×購買頻率決定銷售天花板。

    Example例子

    Ryka water-exercise shoes and Jolt Cola serve narrow needs. Tennis shoes show how specialist design can help performance, but the market must still support repeated demand. Few rivals can mean a small market rather than easy profit.Ryka 水中運動鞋與 Jolt Cola 服務較窄需要；網球鞋說明專門設計可幫助表現，但仍需足夠重複需求。對手少也可能是市場太小，而非容易賺。

    A Taste of Marketing · Repertoire is not the absence of preferenceA Taste of Marketing｜購買品牌組合不代表沒有偏好

    Single-month observation單月觀察

    A customer visits one Indian restaurant exactly once. That outlet has 100% of their observed Indian-restaurant visits that month.一位顧客剛好只去了一家印度餐廳一次。在該月觀察到的印度餐廳造訪中，這家店占了 100%。

    What the observation cannot prove這項觀察無法證明什麼

    Permanent loyalty, no future switching, or an exclusive consumer segment. Test a longer period and other occasions.無法證明永久忠誠、未來不轉換，或顧客只屬於某個排他的區隔。應觀察更長期間與其他情境。

    Example例子

    One person can use Apple and Samsung, or own a Volvo and a sports car. A target guides the offer; it does not place each buyer in one permanent box.同一人可用 Apple 與 Samsung，或同時有 Volvo 與跑車。客群引導提案，不是把每人永久放入單一盒子。

    Pause & think暫停，想一想

    Why might differentiated marketing increase total sales but reduce net profit? How does observed share over one month differ from loyalty?為什麼差異化行銷可能提高總銷售額，卻降低淨利？單月觀察到的購買占比與忠誠有什麼不同？

    3. Positioning3. 定位

    Key idea重點

    Positioning is what customers think a brand means compared with alternatives. The company’s slogan is only one way to shape that meaning.定位是顧客相對其他選項，認為品牌代表什麼；公司口號只是影響這個意義的方法之一。理由支撐 or a "No. 1" claim is not proof that customers value the advantage. Always ask

    Find a meaningful advantage找出有意義的優勢→Select a credible promise選擇可信的承諾→Deliver with the 4Ps透過 4Ps 交付承諾→Research the perceived result研究實際感知結果
    Failure定位問題What it looks like表現方式Class illustration課堂例子
    Underpositioning 定位不足定位不足Consumers recognize the name but cannot say what the brand stands for.消費者認得品牌名稱，卻說不出它代表什麼。Weak or fuzzy associations.品牌聯想薄弱或模糊。
    Overpositioning 定位過窄定位過窄The promise is so narrow that other relevant buyers or uses feel excluded.承諾太狹窄，讓其他相關買家或用途感到被排除。Cooler Club's gendered message.Cooler Club 帶有性別指向的訊息。
    Confused positioning 定位混亂定位混亂Product, price, placement and communication send different signals.產品、價格、通路陳列與溝通傳送不同訊號。Lux: celebrity luxury image versus low shelf placement and discounting in the class anecdote.課堂軼例中的 Lux：名人塑造的奢華形象，與低貨架陳列及折扣形成矛盾。

    How it works為什麼

    A useful RTB (Reason to Believe) explains why people should believe the promised benefit. A “No. 1” claim needs a clear category and measure to mean anything.有用 RTB（相信承諾的理由） 解釋為何能相信承諾利益。「第一名」要有清楚品類與衡量方式才有意義。

    Differentiation type差異化類型Definition / example定義／例子What makes it useful何時有用
    Product產品Features, performance, style or attributes.功能、性能、外觀或屬性。Buyers notice a benefit and value it enough to choose.買家注意利益，並重視到足以選擇。
    Service服務Speed, installation, care or training.速度、安裝、照護或訓練。Actual delivery repeatedly supports the promise.實際交付持續支持承諾。
    Personnel人員People selected and trained to provide a better experience.經挑選與訓練的人員提供較佳體驗。Training changes behavior at real touchpoints.訓練改變真實接觸點行為。
    Image / distinctive assets形象／識別資產Recognizable symbols, atmosphere or spokespeople, such as golden arches or the Mercedes logo.可辨識符號、氣氛或代言人，例如金色拱門與 Mercedes 標誌。People recognize the brand and connect it with valued associations.人們辨識品牌，連到重視的聯想。

    How it works為什麼

    Product features can be copied. Recognizable names, symbols and experiences can keep a brand clear in memory. Choose one useful promise and support it through the 4Ps. Frequent conflicting changes make the brand harder to understand.功能可被模仿，可辨認名稱、符號與體驗能讓品牌留在記憶。選有用承諾，用 4Ps 支持；頻繁互相矛盾的改變會讓人不懂品牌。

    Perceptual map · The axes determine the comparison知覺定位圖｜座標軸決定比較方式

    Key idea重點

    A perceptual map compares how customers see brands on two relevant dimensions. Start with the buying occasion and choose axes that reveal a useful difference.知覺圖用兩個相關面向比較顧客怎樣看品牌。先定購買情境，再選能看出有用差異的軸。

    How it works為什麼

    Label both ends of both axes, plot the focal brand and rivals, then explain the pattern. “Old-fashioned–Modern” and “Older–Younger” describe image. A gap is a place to investigate, not an automatic business opportunity.標示兩軸兩端，畫主品牌與對手，再解釋型態。「老派; 現代」「較年長; 較年輕」是形象。空白可作研究方向，不是自動商機。

    5. Disintermediation5. 去中間化

    Key idea重點

    Disintermediation removes an intermediary. Vertical integration brings an activity under company control. One action can do both, but the terms describe different changes.去中介化是移除中間商，垂直整合是把活動納入公司控制；一個行動可同時做到，但兩詞描述不同改變。去中間化 bypasses intermediaries. Vertical integration 垂直整合 brings an activity under ownership or control; the two can overlap, but are not synonyms.進入障礙 (capital, technology, distribution) differ from exit barriers 退出障礙 (warranties, contracts, obligations to existing customers). Four common market structures differ in seller concentration and pricing power:

    Manufacturer製造商→Wholesaler批發商→Retailer零售商→Customer顧客
    Manufacturer製造商→Direct web / delivery網路直銷／直接配送→Customer顧客

    How it works為什麼

    Selling direct gives control over price and the relationship. The company must also handle customer acquisition, delivery, service and returns. Savings must cover these tasks and any lost retailer business.直售可掌握價格與關係，但也須處理獲客、配送、服務與退貨，省下的費用須涵蓋這些工作，以及可能失去的零售商生意。

    What is STP?什麼是 STP？

    Segmentation divides the market by relevant differences, targeting selects attractive and feasible groups, and positioning builds the intended brand meaning relative to alternatives.市場區隔依相關差異劃分市場；目標市場選擇挑出有吸引力且可行的群體；定位則建立品牌相對於替代選項的預期意義。

    Why is strict STP sometimes misleading?為什麼過度嚴格的 STP 有時會造成誤導？

    One person can buy across brands, categories and apparent segments. Segments guide decisions but do not dictate behavior.一個人可以跨品牌、品類與表面上的區隔購買。區隔用來引導決策，不能決定消費者的行為。

    What makes segmentation effective?什麼讓市場區隔有效？

    Measurability, accessibility, substantiality and actionability.可衡量、可接觸、具足夠規模與價值，以及可執行。

    What is a niche's biggest risk?利基市場最大的風險是什麼？

    A specialized group may be too small or buy too infrequently; rivals can divide the limited demand further.專門群體可能太小，或購買次數太少；競爭者還可能進一步瓜分有限需求。

    What is cannibalization?什麼是自家銷售侵蝕？

    A firm's new product takes sales from its existing products instead of attracting genuinely additional purchases.公司的新產品搶走既有產品的銷售，而非吸引真正額外的購買。

    What is positioning?什麼是定位？

    The meaning or place a brand occupies in consumers' minds relative to competitors. Managers can intend a position, but consumers form the actual perception.品牌相對於競爭者，在消費者心中的意義或位置。管理者可以設定想要的定位，但實際知覺由消費者形成。

    What is differentiation?什麼是差異化？

    A meaningful, credible distinction that customers value and that the company can deliver. Difference alone is not enough.顧客重視、具有意義且可信，而且公司有能力交付的區別。只有不同還不夠。

    How do you create one?如何建立一張定位圖？

    Define the consumer occasion, select meaningful axes, label the directions, plot relevant brands using evidence or explicitly marked hypotheses, and interpret the alternatives.界定消費情境、選擇有意義的座標軸、標示方向，依證據或明確註明的假設放入相關品牌，再解讀替代選項。

    What is underpositioning?什麼是定位不足？

    Consumers do not have a enoughly clear association with the brand.消費者對品牌沒有足夠清楚的聯想。

    What is overpositioning?什麼是定位過窄？

    A brand is perceived too narrowly, potentially excluding other relevant users or occasions.品牌被理解得太狹窄，可能排除其他相關使用者或情境。

    What is confused positioning?什麼是定位混亂？

    Customers receive inconsistent messages from communication, pricing, distribution or actual experience.消費者從溝通、定價、通路或實際體驗中，收到不一致的訊息。

    Can an empty map area be an opportunity?定位圖的空白區可能是機會嗎？

    Possibly, but it may also represent a combination customers do not want. Validate demand and economics first.有可能，但也可能代表顧客不想要的組合。應先驗證需求與商業經濟性。

    Direct vs. indirect competition?直接與間接競爭有何不同？

    Direct competitors belong to the same product/service category; indirect competitors come from different categories but can substitute for a similar need or occasion.直接競爭者屬於相同產品／服務品類；間接競爭者來自不同品類，卻能替代性地滿足相似需求或情境。

    Why is indirect competition important?為什麼間接競爭重要？

    Customers choose based on needs, money, time and availability, not always the category definitions managers use.顧客依需求、金錢、時間與可取得性做選擇，不一定遵循管理者使用的品類定義。

    What is disintermediation?什麼是去中間化？

    Removing intermediaries between producers and users, often by selling or delivering directly.移除生產者與使用者之間的中間商，常透過直接銷售或配送達成。

    Disintermediation vs. vertical integration?去中間化與垂直整合有何不同？

    Disintermediation removes a stage; integration takes control of a stage. They may overlap but are not identical.去中間化移除某個中介環節；垂直整合取得某個環節的控制權。兩者可能重疊，但不相同。

    Why can D2C fail?為什麼 D2C 可能失敗？

    A producer may underestimate fulfillment, acquisition and service costs or provoke conflict with existing retailers.生產者可能低估履約配送、獲客與服務成本，或引發與既有零售商的衝突。

    What are entry and exit barriers?什麼是進入與退出障礙？

    Entry barriers make it hard to start competing; exit barriers make it costly or difficult to leave, such as long-term service obligations.進入障礙使開始競爭更困難；退出障礙使離開市場成本高或不易，例如長期服務義務。

    What are three consumption-growth strategies?三種消費成長策略是什麼？

    Attract new category users, find new uses or occasions, and increase the volume or frequency of use.吸引新的品類使用者、尋找新用途或情境，以及提高使用量或使用頻率。

    What is planned obsolescence?什麼是計畫性汰舊？

    Deliberately designing products to wear out or become replaceable sooner; this differs from useful innovation and raises ethical concerns.刻意將產品設計成更早耗損或需要更換；這與有用的創新不同，且會引發倫理問題。

    What is downsizing?什麼是縮水包裝？

    Reducing net quantity without a proportionate reduction in selling price, increasing the effective unit price.減少淨含量，售價卻未同比例下降，使實際單位價格提高。

    Why is hidden downsizing problematic?為什麼隱瞞縮水包裝有問題？

    Consumers may misjudge value if package size changes are concealed; trust and future repeat purchases may suffer.若隱瞞包裝含量變動，消費者可能誤判價值，進而損害信任與未來重複購買。

    Why is revenue not enough?為什麼只看營收不夠？

    Sales can shift across the firm's products or be generated through high costs. We must evaluate truly incremental contribution and profit.銷售可能只是公司內部產品間轉移，也可能靠高成本取得。我們必須評估真正新增的貢獻利益與利潤。
