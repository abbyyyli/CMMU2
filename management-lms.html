    Lee Medical Supply李氏醫療用品公司

    A financial turnaround that worked, and a people system that broke. The case runs across sessions: Session 3 diagnoses the issues, Session 4 recommends fixes.財務轉型奏效，人員管理體系卻出了問題。這個個案橫跨兩堂課：第 3 堂診斷問題，第 4 堂提出改善建議。

    1. The case at a glance1. 個案概覽

    KP Industries buys distressed companies and installs new leaders to turn them around. Lee Medical Supply (LMS) distributes medical items, with sales of about $300 million and net earnings of about $12 million.KP Industries 收購經營困難的公司，派任新主管推動轉型。李氏醫療用品公司（LMS）經銷醫療用品，營收約 3 億美元，淨利約 1,200 萬美元。

    Jan 4, 20252025 年 1 月 4 日Apichart appointed PresidentApichart 獲任總裁

    Task: bring sales and profit up to KP standards. Review planned after 18 months.任務：將營收與獲利提升至 KP 的標準；原訂 18 個月後檢討。

    Jul 20, 20252025 年 7 月 20 日CEO Krisada reviews results執行長 Krisada 檢視成果

    Finances meet KP standards, but a consultant report shows serious people and strategic problems.財務已達 KP 標準，但顧問報告指出嚴重的人員與策略問題。

    Aug 6, 20252025 年 8 月 6 日New expectations新的要求

    Apichart must deliver a plan within two weeks to cure the "corporate cancer". First review after six months.Apichart 必須在兩週內提出計畫，醫治所謂的「企業癌症」；六個月後進行第一次檢討。

    The handout's dates are about 6.5 months apart but the text says 18 months. Refer to "the stated 18-month turnaround" and do not guess which is wrong.講義所列日期相距約 6.5 個月，文字卻寫 18 個月。可稱為「講義所述的 18 個月轉型」，不要自行猜測哪個有誤。

    KPI in this case can mean KP Industries, the parent company, not key performance indicator.本案的 KPI 也可能指母公司 KP Industries，不一定是「關鍵績效指標」。

    2. Two sides of the story2. 故事的兩個視角

    Consultant and employees顧問與員工

    Written orders, intolerance of mistakes, unwillingness to discuss problems. Managers "only interested in numbers". Tense competition replaced a collegial, family-oriented atmosphere.書面命令、不容許犯錯、不願討論問題；主管「只在乎數字」。緊張的競爭取代了原先友善、如家庭般的氛圍。

    ApichartApichart 的觀點

    He inherited "a happy but unproductive workplace": 22-year average tenure, low technical skills, seniority-based promotion, straight salaries, tolerance of poor performance. Progress has costs; his job is results.他接手的是「快樂卻缺乏生產力的職場」：平均年資 22 年、技術能力不足、依年資升遷、固定薪資制，並容忍低績效。進步有代價；他的工作是交出成果。

    What Apichart changedApichart 做了哪些改變

    People人員Replaced managers he judged incompetent; encouraged mediocre staff to leave撤換他認為能力不足的主管，鼓勵表現平庸的員工離開。
    Structure組織結構Divisions, regions and districts; each unit a profit center; staffing and pay authority decentralized設立事業部、區域與地區單位；各單位成為利潤中心，並下放人事及薪酬權限。
    Control控制機制Performance review department, better accounting, resources allocated by performance設置績效考核部門、改善會計制度，並依績效分配資源。
    Goals目標Management by objectives (MBO), performance standards, required appraisals導入目標管理（MBO）、績效標準與強制考核。
    Rewards獎酬Profit-based bonuses, sales commissions, incentives tied to measurable results以利潤為基礎的獎金、銷售佣金，以及與可衡量成果連動的誘因。
    Culture文化Internal competition made formal policy將內部競爭制度化。

    The CEO's new definition of success執行長對成功的新定義

    Financial performance財務績效+Lower turnover among high performers降低高績效員工的流失率+More customer influence on operations提高顧客對營運決策的影響+Better new-product development改善新產品開發

    3. Evidence discipline3. 證據使用原則

    Strong answers separate what the case states from what we infer.好的答案會區分個案明確記載的事實與我們的推論。

    Stated in the case個案明確記載

    Turnover; leavers well paid; training stopped; weak feedback; no new products; rising complaints; bonuses, commissions and mandated competition exist.員工流失；離職者薪資高；訓練停止；回饋薄弱；沒有新產品；客訴增加；獎金、佣金與強制競爭確實存在。

    Reasonable inference合理推論

    Relationships and growth opportunities weakened; financial KPIs may be too narrow; the climate may discourage ideas.人際關係和成長機會可能變弱；財務指標可能過於狹窄；職場氛圍可能抑制創意。

    Cannot conclude無法下結論

    That low pay caused turnover; that bonuses alone caused competition; that staff fear speaking up (this is only the CEO's suspicion); the exact reward schedule.不能斷定低薪造成流失、單靠獎金造成競爭、員工害怕發言（這只是執行長的懷疑），也不知道獎酬的確切發放規則。

    7. An integrated plan: keep the gains, repair the gaps7. 整合計畫：保留成果、修補缺口

    A
    Baseline and root causes.建立基準與找出根本原因。

    Separate resignations from terminations; confidential interviews; audit rewards, communication and supervision.區分自願離職與解聘；進行保密訪談；檢查獎酬、溝通與督導。

    B
    Low-regret repairs.先做低後悔風險的修補。

    Communicate CEO priorities; give feedback; reopen development; fix disrespectful supervision; pilot cross-functional teams.溝通 CEO 的優先目標、提供回饋、重啟發展機會、改善不尊重員工的督導，並試行跨職能團隊。

    C
    Align incentives.調整誘因，使其與目標一致。

    Keep a few financial KPIs; also recognize customer problem-solving, teamwork, learning and innovation.保留少數財務 KPI，也肯定解決顧客問題、團隊合作、學習與創新。

    D
    Evaluate, then scale.評估效果後再擴大。

    Compare units and periods. A better score after a change does not prove the change caused it.比較不同單位與期間。改變後分數變好，不代表改變就是改善的原因。

    CEO outcome執行長期望的成果Measure衡量方式Watch out注意事項
    Lower high-performer turnover降低高績效員工流失Voluntary high-performer resignation rate; exit reasons高績效員工的自願離職率；離職原因。Separate resignations from terminations區分自願辭職與遭解聘。
    More customer influence提高顧客影響力Complaint resolution quality; customer feedback used in decisions客訴處理品質；決策中採納顧客回饋的情形。Complaints may rise because reporting improves客訴數可能因通報改善而上升。
    Better new-product development改善新產品開發Ideas tested, launches, project cycle time已測試的構想、上市產品、專案週期。More ideas is not the same as profitable innovation構想變多不等於創新能創造利潤。
    Keep financial discipline維持財務紀律Margin, cost control, quality利潤率、成本控制、品質。Margins boosted by cutting needed investment利潤率也可能因刪減必要投資而提高。

    Integrated answer (60 to 90 seconds)整合回答（60 至 90 秒）

    Lee Medical Supplies achieved an important financial turnaround, so I would not simply remove performance controls. However, the case also documents turnover among qualified employees, declining collaboration, stopped training, limited feedback and deteriorating customer and innovation outcomes. Under Maslow, I would strengthen safety, belonging and growth opportunities. Under goal-setting theory, I would communicate shared objectives and create regular feedback while balancing financial with strategic measures. Under Theory X and Y, I would give suitable employees more responsibility and participation without sacrificing accountability. Under Herzberg, I would improve supervision and workplace relationships while restoring development and recognition. Finally, I would track high-performer retention, customer responsiveness and new-product development alongside financial performance. These are proposed interventions, so their effects need evaluation.李氏醫療用品公司完成了重要的財務轉型，因此我不會直接撤除績效管理。然而，個案也記載合格員工離職、合作減少、訓練停止、回饋不足，以及顧客與創新成果惡化。從馬斯洛的角度，我會加強安全感、歸屬感與成長機會。從目標設定理論出發，我會傳達共同目標、建立定期回饋，並平衡財務與策略指標。依 X／Y 理論，我會讓合適的員工有更多責任與參與，同時維持問責。依赫茲伯格理論，我會改善督導與職場關係，恢復員工發展及表揚。最後，我會同時追蹤高績效員工留任、顧客回應能力、新產品開發與財務績效。這些是提出的改善措施，效果仍需評估。

    8. Practice questions8. 練習題

    What is the strongest evidence that the problem is not simply low pay?哪些證據最能說明問題不只是薪資太低？

    Many of those leaving are the best-trained, best-qualified and well-paid employees.許多離職者是受訓最充分、能力最好且薪資高的員工。

    Why must a recommendation keep some of Apichart's changes?為什麼改善建議仍須保留 Apichart 的部分改革？

    He delivered real gains: higher sales and profit, accountability and accurate reporting. The old LMS was not obviously healthy either.他確實提高營收與利潤、問責程度及報表準確性；原本的 LMS 也不見得健康。

    Which goal-setting gap is in the handout, and which does the slide add?講義和投影片分別指出什麼目標設定缺口？

    The handout shows weak performance feedback. The slide adds that staff do not know the overall profit goal.講義呈現績效回饋不足；投影片另外指出員工不知道整體利潤目標。

    Does decentralized authority mean Apichart uses Theory Y?權限下放是否代表 Apichart 採用 Y 理論？

    No. Formal authority was decentralized, but the climate stays control-heavy and quota-driven.不是。形式上的權限雖然下放，職場氛圍仍偏重控制與業績配額。

    Why is calling the commission scheme variable ratio premature?為什麼現在就把佣金制度歸為變動比率時制還太早？

    The case does not give the exact rule linking rewards to output. The name "commission" is not enough.個案未說明獎酬與產出連結的確切規則；「佣金」這個名稱本身不夠。

    How would you test whether an intervention worked?如何檢驗改善措施是否有效？

    Set a baseline, compare similar units or periods, and rule out other causes such as market shifts or staff mix.先建立基準，對照相似單位或時期，並排除市場變化或員工組成等其他原因。
