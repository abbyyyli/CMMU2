    1. The case at a glance1. 個案概覽

    KP Industries buys distressed companies and installs new leaders to turn them around. Lee Medical Supply (LMS) distributes medical items, with sales of about $300 million and net earnings of about $12 million.KP Industries 收購經營困難的公司，派任新主管推動轉型。李氏醫療用品公司（LMS）經銷醫療用品，營收約 3 億美元，淨利約 1,200 萬美元。

    Task: bring sales and profit up to KP standards. Review planned after 18 months.任務：將營收與獲利提升至 KP 的標準；原訂 18 個月後檢討。

    Finances meet KP standards, but a consultant report shows serious people and strategic problems.財務已達 KP 標準，但顧問報告指出嚴重的人員與策略問題。

    Apichart must deliver a plan within two weeks to cure the "corporate cancer". First review after six months.Apichart 必須在兩週內提出計畫，醫治所謂的「企業癌症」；六個月後進行第一次檢討。

    2. Two sides of the story2. 故事的兩個視角

    Consultant and employees顧問與員工

    Written orders, intolerance of mistakes, unwillingness to discuss problems. Managers "only interested in numbers". Tense competition replaced a collegial, family-oriented atmosphere.書面命令、不容許犯錯、不願討論問題；主管「只在乎數字」。緊張的競爭取代了原先友善、如家庭般的氛圍。

    ApichartApichart 的觀點

    He inherited "a happy but unproductive workplace": 22-year average tenure, low technical skills, seniority-based promotion, straight salaries, tolerance of poor performance. Progress has costs; his job is results.他接手的是「快樂卻缺乏生產力的職場」：平均年資 22 年、技術能力不足、依年資升遷、固定薪資制，並容忍低績效。進步有代價；他的工作是交出成果。

    What Apichart changedApichart 做了哪些改變

    People人員Replaced managers he judged incompetent; encouraged mediocre staff to leave撤換他認為能力不足的主管，鼓勵表現平庸的員工離開。
    Structure組織結構Divisions, regions and districts; each unit a profit center; staffing and pay authority decentralized設立事業部、區域與地區單位；各單位成為利潤中心，並下放人事及薪酬權限。
    Control控制機制Performance review department, better accounting, resources allocated by performance設置績效考核部門、改善會計制度，並依績效分配資源。
    Goals目標Management by objectives (MBO), performance standards, required appraisals導入目標管理（MBO）、績效標準與強制考核。
    Rewards獎酬Profit-based bonuses, sales commissions, incentives tied to measurable results以利潤為基礎的獎金、銷售佣金，以及與可衡量成果連動的誘因。
    Culture文化Internal competition made formal policy將內部競爭制度化。

    The CEO's new definition of success執行長對成功的新定義

    Financial performance財務績效+Lower turnover among high performers降低高績效員工的流失率+More customer influence on operations提高顧客對營運決策的影響+Better new-product development改善新產品開發

    3. Evidence discipline3. 證據使用原則

    Strong answers separate what the case states from what we infer.好的答案會區分個案明確記載的事實與我們的推論。

    Stated in the case個案明確記載

    Turnover; leavers well paid; training stopped; weak feedback; no new products; rising complaints; bonuses, commissions and mandated competition exist.員工流失；離職者薪資高；訓練停止；回饋薄弱；沒有新產品；客訴增加；獎金、佣金與強制競爭確實存在。

    Reasonable inference合理推論

    Relationships and growth opportunities weakened; financial KPIs may be too narrow; the climate may discourage ideas.人際關係和成長機會可能變弱；財務指標可能過於狹窄；職場氛圍可能抑制創意。

    Cannot conclude無法下結論

    That low pay caused turnover; that bonuses alone caused competition; that staff fear speaking up (this is only the CEO's suspicion); the exact reward schedule.不能斷定低薪造成流失、單靠獎金造成競爭、員工害怕發言（這只是執行長的懷疑），也不知道獎酬的確切發放規則。

    11. Cold call practice11. 課堂問答練習

    How do you choose the main theory when the question leaves the choice open?題目讓你自選時，如何挑主理論？

    Choose the framework whose mechanism best explains the strongest facts. State the issue it explains, then trace evidence to the gap and action.選最能用機制解釋關鍵事實的框架。先說它解釋的問題，再將證據連到缺口與行動。

    How can Maslow explain high-paid employees leaving?馬斯洛如何解釋高薪員工離職？

    Pay addresses physiological needs. Competition weakens belonging, public insult undermines esteem and stopped development restricts self-actualization. Check safety concerns rather than assume them.薪資處理生理需求；競爭削弱歸屬、公開辱罵傷害尊重，發展中斷限制自我實現。安全感問題須進一步查明。

    How does Herzberg sharpen a needs-based answer?赫茲伯格如何讓需求分析更精準？

    Fix poor supervision and relationships to reduce dissatisfaction; restore development, recognition and responsibility to build motivation. These are different tasks.改善督導與關係以減少不滿；恢復發展、肯定與責任以建立動機。兩者是不同任務。

    What does X/Y add beyond “give everyone autonomy”?X／Y 分析如何超越「讓所有人自主」？

    Examine managerial assumptions and their effect on control. Keep useful structure for routine work and add bounded participation where initiative is needed.檢查主管假設如何影響控制。例行工作保留有用結構，需要主動性處則增加有邊界的參與。

    How do goals and reinforcement work together?目標與增強如何共同作用？

    Goals specify direction, time and feedback; rewards shape the behavior pursued. Align both with finance, customers, retention and innovation.目標說明方向、期限與回饋；獎酬塑造實際追求的行為。兩者都須對齊財務、顧客、留才與創新。

    What makes an EQ recommendation actionable?怎樣的 EQ 建議才可執行？

    Name the trigger, factor and replacement behavior: when a report is weak, pause, correct factual errors privately and agree follow-up. Practice across leadership levels.說明觸發點、因素與替代行為：報告不佳時，先暫停，私下修正具體錯誤並約定追蹤。各層級主管都要練習。

    How can EVLN and OCB help evaluate an intervention?EVLN 與 OCB 如何協助評估改善？

    Track constructive Voice, voluntary resignations and voluntary helping separately. Syamol’s criticism is Voice; her later departure is Exit. Neither alone measures every attitude.分別追蹤建設性建言、自願離職與自願協助。Syamol 的批評是 Voice，後來離職是 Exit；單一行為無法代表所有態度。

    Worked answers: two ways to analyze the same case完整示範：同一個案的兩種解法

    Main theory: Maslow主理論：馬斯洛

    Using Maslow, LMS’s main problem is that financial rewards coexist with unmet higher-level needs. Many capable leavers are well paid, so low pay is not the strongest explanation. Mandated competition weakens belonging, public humiliation undermines esteem, and stopped training and career planning restrict self-actualization. I would restore cross-unit customer projects, recognize useful contributions and reopen development paths. These actions provide connection, respect and growth. Apichart and other supervisors should give private, factual feedback so these changes are delivered respectfully. Keep financial targets, but add shared customer and development goals with regular progress reviews. Track staff feedback on belonging and growth, high-performer resignations and customer outcomes. This links the need diagnosis to job design, leadership behavior and goal management.以馬斯洛分析，LMS 的核心問題是財務獎勵與高層需求缺口並存。許多有能力的離職者薪資高，因此低薪不是最有力的解釋。強制競爭削弱歸屬，公開羞辱傷害尊重，停止培訓與生涯規劃則限制自我實現。我會恢復跨部門顧客專案、肯定有用貢獻並重啟發展途徑，提供連結、尊重與成長。Apichart 及其他主管應私下針對事實回饋，讓改善能以尊重的方式落實。保留財務目標，同時增加共同顧客與發展目標，定期追蹤進度。檢查員工的歸屬與成長感受、高績效者自願離職及顧客成果。這將需求診斷連到工作設計、領導行為與目標管理。

    Main theory: Theory X / Y主理論：X／Y 理論

    Using Theory X/Y, the issue is a control-heavy management approach applied too broadly. Apichart’s written orders, intolerance of mistakes and rejection of feedback discourage the initiative needed for customer service and innovation. Yet Chalermsak likes clear routines, so useful structure should remain. I would keep clear standards and give experienced staff bounded authority over customer problems, participation in goals and responsibility for results. The mechanism is greater self-direction and ownership while accountability remains. Apichart and other leaders must listen without retaliation, explain decision rights and correct mistakes privately. Agree specific goals and review dates; recognize cooperation as well as individual output. Check decision quality, customer resolution, constructive Voice and financial performance. This joins the main style diagnosis with EQ, job design and aligned goals.以 X／Y 理論分析，問題是偏重控制的管理方式被套用得過廣。Apichart 的書面命令、不容錯誤與拒絕回饋，抑制了顧客服務和創新所需的主動性。但 Chalermsak 喜歡清楚例行安排，因此有用的結構應保留。我會維持明確標準，讓有經驗者在界線內處理顧客問題、參與目標並對結果負責。其機制是增加自我引導與責任感，同時保留問責。Apichart 與其他主管須傾聽且不報復，說清楚決策權，私下修正錯誤。共同約定具體目標及檢討日期，肯定合作與個人產出。追蹤決策品質、顧客問題處理、建設性建言及財務績效。這把管理方式診斷連到 EQ、工作設計與一致目標。
