    Supply, Demand and Equilibrium供給、需求與均衡

    For any market event, ask what changes first, whether it hits buyers or sellers, which curve shifts and which way, then read the new equilibrium price and quantity from the new intersection.遇到任何市場事件，先問什麼最先改變、影響買方還是賣方、哪條曲線向哪個方向移動，再從新交點讀出新的均衡價格與數量。

    1. Demand1. 需求

    Demand is the relationship between price and the quantity consumers are willing to buy, other things equal.需求是其他條件不變時，價格與消費者願意購買數量之間的關係。

    Law of demand需求法則

    Price ↑ → quantity demanded ↓. At higher prices some buyers stop, some buy less, some switch to substitutes.價格 ↑ → 需求量 ↓。價格較高時，有些買方停止購買、有些減少購買、有些轉向替代品。

    Reading the curve如何閱讀曲線

    Horizontally: at this price, how much do buyers want? Vertically: the height is the marginal buyer's willingness to pay 願付價格.水平看：在這個價格下，買方想買多少？垂直看：曲線的高度代表邊際買方的願付價格。

    Change in quantity demanded需求量變動

    The good's own price changes → movement along the same curve. Quinoa gets cheaper, people buy more.商品本身價格改變 → 沿同一條曲線移動。藜麥變便宜，人們買得更多。

    Change in demand需求變動

    A non-price factor changes → the whole curve shifts. Right = more at every price; left = less.非價格因素改變 → 整條曲線移動。右移＝每個價格下都買更多；左移＝買更少。

    2. Supply2. 供給

    Supply is the relationship between price and the quantity producers are willing to sell, other things equal.供給是其他條件不變時，價格與生產者願意出售數量之間的關係。

    Law of supply供給法則

    Price ↑ → quantity supplied ↑. Producing more uses higher-cost sources, which only pay at higher prices.價格 ↑ → 供給量 ↑。擴大產量需要使用成本較高的生產來源，只有價格更高時才划算。

    Reading the curve如何閱讀曲線

    The height is the minimum acceptable price 最低願售價 for that unit.曲線高度代表出售該單位商品所能接受的最低價格。

    3. Equilibrium: the quinoa market3. 均衡：藜麥市場

    Equilibrium is where quantity demanded equals quantity supplied: no shortage, no surplus.均衡是需求量等於供給量的狀態：沒有短缺，也沒有過剩。

    Curves are straight lines fitted through the slide's points ($500 / 45k in 2001, $1,200 / 80k in 2010) so the shortage and surplus can be measured.曲線是配合投影片上的點所建立的直線模型：2001 年每噸 500 美元、4.5 萬噸；2010 年每噸 1,200 美元、8 萬噸。這樣便能量化短缺與過剩。

    Price價格Relationship關係Situation市場狀況Who competes誰與誰競爭Pressure價格壓力
    Below equilibrium低於均衡價格Qd > Qs需求量＞供給量Shortage 短缺短缺Buyers with buyers買方與其他買方競爭P ↑
    Above equilibrium高於均衡價格Qs > Qd供給量＞需求量Surplus 過剩過剩Sellers with sellers賣方與其他賣方競爭P ↓

    Consumer and producer surplus消費者剩餘與生產者剩餘

    Consumer surplus消費者剩餘

    Willingness to pay − price paid. Willing to pay ฿80, pay ฿50: surplus ฿30. Area under demand, above price.願付價格－實付價格。願付 80 泰銖、支付 50 泰銖，剩餘為 30 泰銖。圖上是需求曲線下方、價格線上方的面積。

    Producer surplus生產者剩餘

    Price − minimum acceptable price. Cost ฿30, sell at ฿50: surplus ฿20. Area above supply, below price.價格－最低可接受價格。成本 30 泰銖、售價 50 泰銖，剩餘為 20 泰銖。圖上是供給曲線上方、價格線下方的面積。

    At equilibrium every trade where the buyer values the good more than it costs to produce takes place, so total surplus is maximized.在均衡下，買方評價高於生產成本的交易都會發生，因此總剩餘最大。

    Shift移動方向Price價格Quantity數量

    Classic trap: a drought shifts supply left and raises the price. Buyers then buy less, but that is a fall in quantity demanded. Demand did not move.常見陷阱：乾旱使供給左移並推高價格，買方於是少買；這是需求量下降，需求曲線沒有移動。

    7. Seven-step method7. 七步分析法

    1 · Market1・市場

    Define it: the world market for quinoa.先界定：藜麥的全球市場。

    2 · Event2・事件

    What changed?什麼改變了？

    3 · Side3・受影響的一方

    Buyers (demand) or sellers (supply)?買方（需求）還是賣方（供給）？

    4 · Price or factor4・價格或其他因素

    Own price = movement; other factor = shift.自身價格改變＝沿曲線移動；其他因素改變＝曲線移動。

    5 · Direction5・方向

    Left or right?左移還是右移？

    6 · New E6・新均衡

    Read P2 and Q2.讀出 P2 與 Q2。

    7 · Explain7・解釋

    Event → factor → curve → direction → P → Q.事件 → 因素 → 曲線 → 方向 → 價格 → 數量。

    9. Do not mix these up9. 不要混淆

    Price fell, so demand increased.價格下降，所以需求增加。

    Quantity demanded increased: a movement.增加的是需求量：沿曲線移動。

    Price rose, so supply increased.價格上升，所以供給增加。

    Quantity supplied increased.增加的是供給量。

    Supply fell, so demand also fell.供給減少，所以需求也減少。

    The higher price cut quantity demanded.較高的價格使需求量減少。

    Income ↑ → demand ↑.所得 ↑ → 需求 ↑。

    Only for normal goods.只有正常財才是如此。

    Above equilibrium = shortage.高於均衡＝短缺。

    Above = surplus; below = shortage.高於均衡是過剩；低於均衡是短缺。

    Both curves shift, so P and Q are both known.兩條曲線都移動，所以價格與數量方向都能確定。

    One is ambiguous.其中一項結果不確定。

    10. Cold call practice10. 課堂口答練習

    Demand versus quantity demanded?需求與需求量有何不同？

    Demand is the whole curve; quantity demanded is the amount at one price. Own price changes move along the curve.需求是整條曲線；需求量是在某個價格下的數量。自身價格變動，會使位置沿同一條曲線移動。

    Why does demand slope downward?需求曲線為何向下傾斜？

    At higher prices some buyers stop, some buy less and some switch to substitutes.價格較高時，有些買方停止購買、有些少買、有些改買替代品。

    Why does supply slope upward?供給曲線為何向上傾斜？

    More output needs higher-cost sources, which need a higher price.增加產量需要成本較高的生產來源，必須有較高價格才值得生產。

    What happens below equilibrium, and who competes?低於均衡價格時會如何？誰與誰競爭？

    A shortage: buyers compete with other buyers and push the price up.出現短缺：買方與其他買方競爭，推高價格。

    Why can rare-earth deposits reduce quinoa supply?為何稀土礦藏可能減少藜麥供給？

    They raise the opportunity cost of land, labor and capital used for quinoa, so resources move to mining.稀土提高了用於藜麥的土地、勞動與資本之機會成本，因此資源轉向採礦。

    Drought cuts supply. Must demand shift too?乾旱減少供給，需求也一定會移動嗎？

    No. The higher price lowers quantity demanded along the same curve.不會。較高價格使需求量沿同一條需求曲線下降。

    Why is equilibrium efficient?均衡為何具有效率？

    All trades where value exceeds cost take place, so consumer plus producer surplus is maximized.所有價值高於成本的交易都會發生，因此消費者剩餘加生產者剩餘達到最大。
