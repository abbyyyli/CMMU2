    Marketing Fundamentals and Customer Value行銷基礎與顧客價值

    Marketing starts before the advertisement. Understand the customer and the market, create value that matters, deliver it through a coherent mix, then judge success by customer behavior and sustainable business results.行銷在廣告之前就已開始：了解顧客與市場，創造顧客重視的價值，透過一致的行銷組合交付價值，再以顧客行為與可持續的經營成果判斷成效。

    1. What marketing is1. 什麼是行銷？

    The professor's definition: understanding and satisfying consumer wants and needs.教授的定義：理解並滿足消費者的想要與需要。

    Advertising廣告

    One communication tool inside Promotion.促銷之中的一種溝通工具。

    Marketing行銷

    Customer + value + market + strategy + execution: product, price, distribution, service, branding, research, experience and demand management.顧客＋價值＋市場＋策略＋執行：包括產品、價格、通路、服務、品牌、研究、體驗與需求管理。

    The order matters. A firm first learns whose problem it can solve, decides what value to offer, and arranges price, access and delivery. Advertising then helps people understand that offer. An attractive ad cannot compensate for a product that is unavailable or solves the wrong problem.順序很重要：先了解能解決誰的問題，決定提供什麼價值，再安排價格、取得方式與交付。廣告接著幫助顧客理解提案。產品買不到，或解決的不是顧客問題，再吸引人的廣告也難以補救。

    Evidence, not common sense依據證據，而非只靠常識

    Store A sells more iced coffee than Store B. "It is hotter there" is plausible, but customer profile, traffic, competitors, price, opening hours or promotions could explain the same pattern.A 店的冰咖啡賣得比 B 店多。「那裡比較熱」是合理假設，但客群、來客量、競爭者、價格、營業時間或促銷，也可能解釋同樣的現象。

    Observation觀察→Possible explanation可能的解釋≠Proven explanation已獲證實的解釋

    Common sense can generate a hypothesis 假設. Evidence is needed to support a conclusion. Always ask: what other variables could explain the same pattern?常識可以產生假設，結論則需要證據支持。要持續追問：還有哪些變數能解釋同樣的現象？

    Store A sells more iced coffee than Store B. A manager credits hotter weather alone. What competing explanations must be checked before making that causal claim?A 店的冰咖啡銷量高於 B 店，主管把原因全部歸給天氣較熱。要做出這項因果判斷前，還必須檢查哪些其他可能的解釋？

    Customers are not perfectly rational顧客並非完全理性

    The professor questions the economic assumptions that customers always minimize money, time and effort. People also value experience, identity, status, habit, convenience and variety. The cheapest or fastest option does not automatically deliver the highest value.教授質疑消費者總是追求金錢、時間與力氣最小化的經濟學假設。人也重視體驗、認同、地位、習慣、便利與多樣性。最便宜或最快的選項，不一定提供最高價值。

    Customer value顧客價值

    Customer value ≈ perceived benefits ÷ perceived costs顧客價值 ≈ 感知利益 ÷ 感知成本

    Costs include money, time, effort, risk and inconvenience. Benefits can be functional, emotional, social, convenience or experience.成本包括金錢、時間、力氣、風險與不便；利益則可能來自功能、情緒、社交、便利或體驗。

    Read the comparison from the customer's perspective. A THB 70 coffee may involve more waiting and travel than a THB 100 coffee near the office. Someone in a hurry may prefer the second shop; someone with spare time may prefer the first. The benefit–cost ratio is a conceptual aid: it does not put all emotions, risk and time on one objectively correct scale. The calculator below isolates price and time so this trade-off is easier to see.站在顧客角度比較。70 泰銖的咖啡可能比辦公室附近 100 泰銖的咖啡多花等待與交通時間。趕時間的人可能選後者，時間充裕的人可能選前者。利益與成本的比值是概念工具，不能把情緒、風險與時間放到唯一客觀的量尺上。下方計算器先單獨比較價格與時間，讓取捨更容易看懂。

    3. From product thinking to customer thinking3. 從產品思維轉向顧客思維

    A product can be a good, service, person, place, organization, activity or idea. The key question is not "what are we selling?" but "what customer benefit is being satisfied?"產品可以是實體商品、服務、人、地點、組織、活動或理念。關鍵不只是「我們賣什麼」，而是「滿足了顧客的什麼利益？」

    Product view產品觀點

    "We sell drills." "We are in the DVD business."「我們賣鑽孔機。」「我們經營 DVD 生意。」

    Customer view顧客觀點

    The customer wants a hole, a finished DIY job. The DVD buyer wants entertainment, so streaming is a competitor.顧客想要的是一個洞，或完成一項 DIY 工作。DVD 買家需要娛樂，因此串流也是競爭者。

    Marketing myopia 行銷近視行銷近視症

    Define the business too narrowly過度狹隘地定義業務→Focus on the current product只專注於現有產品→Ignore substitutes忽略替代方案→Miss environmental change錯過環境變化

    Ask: what customer need are we actually serving, and what other solutions can satisfy it?要問：我們實際服務的是什麼需要？還有哪些方案也能滿足它？

    Difference is not meaningful differentiation有差異，不代表有意義的差異化

    A feature can be new, unusual or unique and still create almost no customer benefit. Test any claimed USP:一項功能可以很新奇、特殊或獨特，卻幾乎不帶來顧客利益。檢驗任何宣稱的獨特銷售主張（USP）：

    Who cares?誰在乎？

    2

    Why do they care?為什麼在乎？

    3

    Does it solve a real problem?它解決真實問題嗎？

    4

    Can competitors easily copy it?競爭者容易模仿嗎？

    5

    Would customers pay or switch because of it?顧客會因此付錢或轉換品牌嗎？

    Me-too marketing跟隨模仿式行銷

    Firms copy features, products and promotions. In the phone-camera example, customers rarely remember who introduced a feature first. Technical leadership does not guarantee preference.企業模仿功能、產品與促銷。在手機相機的例子中，顧客通常不記得誰先推出某項功能。技術領先不保證顧客偏好。

    Branding品牌經營

    When features are copied, brand meaning matters more: recognition, associations, expectations and trust. A brand is what customers associate with the offering, not a logo.功能被模仿後，品牌意義更重要：辨識度、聯想、期待與信任。品牌是顧客對產品或服務的聯想，而不只是標誌。

    A Unique Selling Proposition (USP) is a distinct selling claim. Its commercial value depends on a chain: customers notice the difference, understand its benefit, value it relative to alternatives, and choose accordingly. A copied feature may lose uniqueness quickly. Consistent experience and recognizable brand associations can preserve a reason to choose even when technical features converge.USP（獨特銷售主張）是與眾不同的銷售訴求。它要轉成商業價值，須經過：顧客注意到差異、理解利益、相對於替代品重視這項利益，並因此選擇。功能被模仿後可能很快失去獨特性；一致體驗與可辨識的品牌聯想，仍可能維持選擇理由。

    Pause & think暫停，想一想

    A phone maker adds an unusual camera feature that rivals do not offer. What must be true about the target customer before the feature counts as meaningful differentiation rather than mere difference?手機品牌增加競爭者沒有的特殊相機功能。從目標顧客角度看，還必須滿足哪些條件，這項功能才能算有意義的差異化，而不只是不同？

    4. Understanding the market4. 理解市場

    Attractive for whom?對誰有吸引力？

    The professor asks: how big is the Thai market, how attractive is it, and for whom? A market is only attractive for a specific offering, target and business model.教授問：泰國市場有多大、多有吸引力，又是對誰而言？市場吸引力必須相對於特定產品或服務、目標客群與商業模式來判斷。

    Mercedes賓士Tesco LotusTesco Lotus
    Target目標客群High-income buyers高收入買家Mass households大眾家庭
    Purchase frequency購買頻率Rare, high value低頻、高金額Weekly, low value每週、低金額
    What matters most最重要的因素Buying power, status購買力與地位Population size, access, price人口規模、可取得性與價格

    The Mercedes–Tesco Lotus comparison illustrates why the same population figure can lead to different decisions. A car business needs enough reachable buyers with the required purchasing power, even though purchases are infrequent. A mass retailer needs convenient access and repeated shopping trips. Market size becomes useful only after the category, customer, occasion and economics have been defined.Mercedes 與 Tesco Lotus 說明，同一個人口數字對不同企業意義不同。汽車業需要可觸及、具足夠購買力的買家，即使購買不頻繁；大眾零售商更依賴便利據點與重複購物。先界定品類、顧客、情境與經濟性，市場規模才有決策價值。

    Check population, age, income, purchasing power, needs, growth, competition and distribution access. A big population with weak buying power, poor fit or hard distribution can still be unattractive.檢查人口、年齡、所得、購買力、需要、成長、競爭與通路可及性。即使人口多，若購買力弱、契合度差或配送困難，仍可能沒有吸引力。

    Pause & think暫停，想一想

    Thailand has a large population, yet a premium brand may still find a target market unattractive. Which customer, purchasing-power, competitive and channel facts would matter more than population size alone?泰國人口眾多，高價品牌的目標市場卻仍可能缺乏吸引力。除了人口規模，還應看哪些顧客、購買力、競爭與通路證據？

    Seasonality and demographics季節性與人口結構

    Seasonality季節性 季節性

    Weather, holidays, time of day and routines move demand: coffee shops, 7-Eleven mornings, gasoline, taxis near malls. Ask when, where, which customer and why, then adjust inventory, staff, price and hours.天氣、節日、時段與日常習慣都會改變需求，例如咖啡店、早晨的 7-Eleven、汽油與商場旁的計程車。先問何時、何地、哪種顧客及原因，再調整庫存、人力、價格與營業時間。

    Demographics人口結構

    Age is relatively predictable. An aging Thailand brings threats (shrinking labor force, fewer students) and opportunities (home care, telemedicine, telepharmacy, assisted living, smart homes).年齡結構相對可預測。泰國高齡化帶來威脅，例如勞動力縮減、學生減少；也帶來居家照護、遠距醫療、遠距藥事服務、輔助生活與智慧住宅等機會。

    Trend趨勢→Future pain point未來痛點→Unmet need未被滿足的需要→Possible opportunity可能的機會

    External environment外部環境

    Monitor customers, competitors, the economy, technology, culture, demographics, the natural environment and globalization. A strategy can fail because the environment changed, even if the company did not.觀察顧客、競爭者、經濟、科技、文化、人口結構、自然環境與全球化。即使公司沒有改變，策略也可能因環境改變而失效。

    How environmental change reaches the 4Ps環境變化如何影響 4Ps

    Change變化Mechanism作用機制Possible response可能回應
    Changing customer values顧客價值改變A once-relevant benefit may matter less as needs, routines and social expectations change.需要、作息與社會期待改變時，原本相關的利益可能不再重要。Research whether the value proposition still fits the target.研究價值主張是否仍符合目標顧客。
    Recession景氣衰退Desire can remain while buying power falls; some wants no longer become demand.想要可能仍在，但購買力下降，部分想要無法轉成有效需求。Reconsider pack sizes, value tiers and payment/access friction.重新檢視包裝規格、價值層級與付款／取得阻礙。
    E-commerce電子商務Search and comparison become easier; delivery and returns become part of the offer.搜尋比較更容易，配送與退貨成為提案的一部分。Coordinate product information, total price, fulfillment and communication.協調產品資訊、總價格、履約與溝通。
    Globalization全球化Customers gain alternatives beyond familiar local competitors.顧客的替代選項超出熟悉的本地競爭者。Recheck the benefit and competitive set, including substitutes.重新檢驗利益與競爭範圍，包含替代品。

    Demand management and GET + KEEP需求管理與獲客＋留客

    Demand management需求管理 需求管理

    Too much demand means queues, lower service quality and unhappy customers; too little means idle staff and assets. The goal is appropriate demand at the right time for the capacity. A gym can shift peak users to off-peak hours.需求太多會造成排隊、服務品質下降與顧客不滿；需求太少則使人力與資產閒置。目標是在適當時間，讓需求符合產能。健身房可以把尖峰使用者引導到離峰時段。

    GET + KEEP獲客＋留客

    Demand comes from new and repeat customers. Starting customer base + acquired customers − customers lost = ending customer base, using consistent definitions and periods. 10,000 new customers with 9,500 lost gives a net addition of 500. Session 2 develops acquisition, retention and relationship economics.需求來自新客與重複購買。定義與期間一致時，期初顧客＋新增顧客－流失顧客＝期末顧客。新增 10,000、流失 9,500，淨增為 500。Session 2 會展開獲客、留存與關係經濟性。

    A café has empty seats on weekdays but long queues at weekend peak times. Why might maximizing total demand be the wrong objective? Suggest a more useful demand-management goal.咖啡店平日有空位，週末尖峰卻大排長龍。為什麼「把總需求最大化」可能是錯的目標？請提出更合適的需求管理目標。

    Selling Concept銷售觀念

    Product first → sell harder.產品先行 → 更努力推銷。

    Marketing Concept行銷觀念

    Customer first → design the offering to fit.顧客先行 → 設計契合需要的產品或服務。

    Why the starting assumption changes the decision為什麼出發假設會改變決策

    Production thinking is useful when affordability and availability are the main customer problems. Product thinking becomes risky when managers equate more features with better value. Selling starts with existing output and tries to persuade buyers; marketing starts with the target's need and designs an offer around it. Societal marketing checks long-term welfare. Strategic marketing also checks whether logistics, costs and competitive conditions let the firm deliver that value profitably.當顧客主要問題是買不起或買不到，生產觀念有用。若管理者把功能多直接當成價值高，產品觀念就有風險。銷售觀念從既有產品出發、說服人購買；行銷觀念從目標顧客需要出發設計提案。社會行銷檢查長期福祉；策略行銷還要檢查物流、成本與競爭條件，能否讓公司有利潤地交付價值。

    Diagnose the reason for an action. Cutting delivery cost could express a production focus on efficiency, or support a customer-led promise of convenient service. The visible action alone does not identify the philosophy; the decision's starting logic does.判斷行動背後的理由。降低配送成本可能是生產效率導向，也可能支援顧客導向的便利服務承諾。只看行動外表不足以判斷觀念，要看決策的出發邏輯。

    7. The strategic marketing system7. 策略行銷系統

    15Cs5Cs 情境分析

    Understand: company, customers, competitors, collaborators, context.理解：公司、顧客、競爭者、合作夥伴與總體情境。

    2STPSTP

    Choose: segmentation, targeting, positioning. Whom to serve, why choose us.選擇：市場區隔、目標市場與定位。服務誰？為什麼選我們？

    34Ps4Ps 行銷組合

    Execute: deliver and communicate the value.執行：交付並溝通價值。

    Positioning comes before promotion. Promotion communicates a defined value to a chosen target; it should not decide the target afterward.定位先於促銷。促銷是把已定義的價值傳達給已選定的目標客群，不應做完促銷才決定客群。

    Is marketing anti-sustainability?行銷是否違背永續？

    Marketing drives consumption, yet society may need less waste, reuse and healthier behavior. Marketing can also promote better behavior (reusable bags, energy saving, recycling) and create value through durability, repair, reuse and access rather than more units.行銷推動消費，但社會可能需要減少浪費、重複使用與更健康的行為。行銷也能推廣環保袋、節能、回收等行為，並透過耐用、維修、重複使用與使用權創造價值，而非只追求多賣幾件。

    Pause & think暫停，想一想

    A marketing campaign raises sales but encourages unnecessary waste. What additional business and social evidence is needed before calling the campaign successful or sustainable?一項行銷活動提高銷售，卻也鼓勵不必要的浪費。在稱它成功或永續之前，還需要哪些經營與社會面的證據？

    Customer value and societal value can diverge. A convenient disposable product may satisfy buyers today while increasing waste. A repair service may sell fewer replacement units while earning service revenue and building trust. Evaluate customer benefit, business viability and resource consequences together; reduced physical consumption alone does not establish commercial failure.顧客價值與社會價值可能不同。方便的一次性產品可能滿足當下顧客，卻增加廢棄物；維修服務可能減少替換品銷量，同時帶來服務收入與信任。要一起檢查顧客利益、經營可行性與資源後果，不能只因實體消費減少就判定商業失敗。

    Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) concerns a company's broader social and environmental responsibilities. Societal marketing applies a related welfare check to marketing decisions: an offer should be assessed through customer satisfaction, company viability and long-term effects on society. A charitable message alone does not show that the product, sourcing or daily operations are responsible.企業社會責任（CSR）關注公司較廣泛的社會與環境責任。社會行銷把相關福祉檢查帶入行銷決策，從顧客滿意、公司可行性及長期社會影響評估提案。單靠公益訊息，不能證明產品、採購或日常營運負責任。

    9. Do not mix these up9. 別混淆這些概念

    Marketing = advertising.行銷＝廣告。

    Promotion is one of four Ps; marketing starts earlier.促銷只是 4Ps 之一；行銷更早就開始。

    Want = demand.想要＝需求。

    Demand needs buying power.需求需要購買力支持。

    Satisfied = loyal.滿意＝忠誠。

    Satisfied customers still switch.滿意的顧客仍會轉換品牌。

    Lowest price = highest value.最低價格＝最高價值。

    Value counts time, effort and risk too.價值也計入時間、力氣與風險。

    Different = differentiated.有差異＝有差異化。

    The target must care about the difference.目標顧客必須在乎這項差異。

    Large population = attractive market.人口多＝市場有吸引力。

    Attractive for whom, with what fit?對誰有吸引力？契合點是什麼？

    Low sales = awareness problem.低銷售＝知名度問題。

    Diagnose the funnel stage first.先診斷漏斗卡在哪個階段。

    More demand is always better.需求越多越好。

    Excess demand can hurt service quality.過多需求可能損害服務品質。

    Customer orientation ignores profit.顧客導向不考慮利潤。

    Strategic marketing includes margins.策略行銷也包括利潤率。

    Going online is a strategy.上網就是策略。

    A channel is an execution choice.通路是執行上的選擇。

    10. Cold call practice10. 課堂即答練習

    Why do one million ad views not prove marketing success?為什麼一百萬次廣告觀看不能證明行銷成功？

    Views show exposure, not trial, repeat purchase, value or profit.觀看代表接觸，不代表試用、重購、價值或利潤。

    A hungry person wants omakase but cannot afford it. Need, want, demand?一個飢餓的人想吃無菜單料理，卻負擔不起：如何區分需要、想要與需求？

    Hunger is the need, omakase is the want, and without buying power there is no demand.飢餓是需要，無菜單料理是想要；缺乏購買力，就沒有對它的需求。

    Why can a pricier coffee shop give more value?為什麼較貴的咖啡店可能提供更高價值？

    A shorter wait, closer location or less effort can outweigh the higher price for a busy customer.對忙碌顧客而言，較短等待、較近地點或更省力，可能足以抵銷較高價格。

    What does the drill example teach?鑽孔機的例子教我們什麼？

    Customers buy the result, a hole or a finished job, not the product for its own sake.顧客買的是洞或完成工作等成果，而非為產品本身而買。

    What is the risk of defining a business by its current technology?用現有科技定義業務有什麼風險？

    Marketing myopia: new solutions can satisfy the same need and take the market.行銷近視症：新方案可能滿足同樣需要，並搶走市場。

    Awareness and interest are high but trial is low. What first?知名度與興趣高，但首次購買低，先做什麼？

    Check price, availability, perceived risk and purchase friction before buying more awareness.先檢查價格、供貨、感知風險與購買阻力，再考慮增加知名度。

    How do the Selling, Marketing, Societal and Strategic Concepts differ?銷售、行銷、社會行銷與策略行銷觀念有何不同？

    Selling pushes existing products; Marketing starts with target needs; Societal adds long-run welfare; Strategic also requires efficiency and sustainable profit.銷售推動現有產品；行銷從目標需要出發；社會行銷加入長期福祉；策略行銷也要求效率與可持續利潤。

    What does 5Cs → STP → 4Ps mean?5Cs → STP → 4Ps 代表什麼？

    Understand the situation, choose the target and positioning, then execute the mix.先理解情境，再選擇目標市場與定位，最後執行行銷組合。

    Why is lower consumption not necessarily a failure?為什麼消費減少不一定代表失敗？

    A firm can create value through durability, repair or reuse while building trust and long-run viability.企業可以透過耐用、維修或重複使用創造價值，同時建立信任與長期經營能力。
