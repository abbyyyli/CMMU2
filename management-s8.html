    1. What culture is and what it does1. 文化是什麼、有什麼作用

    “Culture is the way we do things around here, or what keeps the herd moving in roughly the right direction.” (Deal and Kennedy, 1982)「文化就是我們這裡的做事方式，也就是讓一群人大致朝正確方向前進的力量。」（Deal and Kennedy，1982）

    Put simply, culture is what people really do, not what the rulebook says. It keeps everyone moving in roughly the same direction without a manager pushing each person.簡單說，文化是大家實際怎麼做，而不是規章怎麼寫。它讓每個人大致朝同一個方向走，不需要主管一個一個推。

    The deck also mentions sustainability culture: the shared beliefs and habits about a company's impact on the environment. The slide says culture matters most during change (Assoratgoon and Kantabutra, 2023).投影片也提到永續文化：大家對公司環境影響的共同信念和習慣。投影片說，文化在改變的時候最重要（Assoratgoon and Kantabutra，2023）。

    Slide p.6 calls culture a way of controlling behavior without formal rules. Staff pass the unwritten rules to each other. Public and private organizations both have a culture.第 6 頁說，文化是一種不靠正式規定來控制行為的方式。員工會把不成文的規則傳給彼此。公部門和私部門都有文化。

    Informal control: what people do because “that is how we work here.”非正式控制：大家這樣做，是因為「我們這裡就是這樣工作」。

    2. How culture forms2. 文化如何形成

    • From the top: the founders' beliefs shape what senior managers aim for and how they act.由上而下：創辦人的信念，影響高階主管的目標和做法。
    • From the bottom: who the company hires, and what new staff learn from coworkers.由下而上：公司找誰進來，以及新人從同事身上學到什麼。
    • Both ways: once a culture exists, it also shapes how managers and staff behave.雙向：文化一旦形成，也會影響主管和員工的行為。
    • Results: satisfaction, commitment and performance.結果：滿意度、承諾和績效。

    中文邏輯文化不是主管宣布就成立：上面要有價值與行動，下面要「選對人」和「同事帶新人」，兩邊都做到才穩。

    3. Six elements: the toolkit3. 六要素：建立文化的工具

    Slide p.8 lists six elements under the title “This is how to create a corporate culture.” So they are tools you can use, not just things to notice.第 8 頁把六要素列在「這就是建立企業文化的方法」這個標題下。所以它們是可以拿來用的工具，不只是觀察的對象。

    The easiest way to tell them apart is to ask what form each one takes: a principle, a person, an object, an activity or a story.最容易分辨的方法，是問每個要素是什麼形式：一句原則、一個人、一個東西、一個活動，還是一段故事。

    1. VisionIt appears in the company vision願景：寫在公司願景裡
    2. LeadersLeaders act on it領導者：領導者照著做

    Heroes: values in a person英雄：價值的化身

    Heroes show what success looks like in real life. They are role models. They stand for what the company believes, and they set the standard others aim for (p.12).英雄讓人看到成功在現實中長什麼樣子。他們是榜樣，代表公司相信的東西，也設定大家努力的標準（第 12 頁）。

    Mahidol University: Prince Mahidol瑪希敦大學：Mahidol 親王

    Stands for service to society, knowledge built on research, and serving everyone, not only the elite (Kantabutra and Saratun, 2013).代表服務社會、以研究為基礎的知識，以及服務所有人，而不只是菁英（Kantabutra and Saratun，2013）。

    Microsoft: Bill GatesMicrosoft：Bill Gates

    Stands for hard work, persistence and competition: do what it takes to get the job done and win.代表努力、堅持和競爭：為了完成工作、贏得勝利，什麼都願意做。

    The two heroes stand for very different things. Mahidol stands for service; Gates stands for winning. Each one makes his organization's values easy to picture.兩位英雄代表的東西很不一樣：Mahidol 代表服務，Gates 代表勝利。重點是，他們都讓自己組織的價值很容易想像。

    Shared events: rites, rituals and ceremonies共同活動：通過儀式、例行儀式和典禮

    Shared events carry the culture and show people “the way we do things around here” (p.16). Three questions tell the types apart:共同活動承載文化，讓大家看到「我們這裡的做事方式」（第 16 頁）。用三個問題就能分辨類型：

    Ask問If yes, it is a是的話，就是Who goes through it誰會經歷Example例子
    Does someone change status?有人換了身分嗎？Rite of passage通過儀式（rite of passage）Everyone who reaches that point, however well they perform每個走到這一步的人，不管表現好壞Induction, promotion, retirement入職、升遷、退休
    Is it a routine everyone repeats, with no star?是大家固定重複、沒有主角的做法嗎？Ritual例行儀式（ritual）Everyone, again and again所有人，一再重複The weekly meeting format, yearly reviews, retreats每週會議的固定流程、年度考核、靜修營
    Is the main point to honor people in public?主要目的是公開表揚人嗎？Ceremony典禮Only the people chosen for it只有被選中的人Team of the year awards年度最佳團隊頒獎

    Short version: everyone goes through a rite, everyone repeats a ritual, and only standout people are honored at a ceremony.簡單記：通過儀式人人都會經歷，例行儀式大家固定做，典禮只表揚表現突出的人。

    Promotion shows the difference. The day someone becomes a manager is a rite. The quarterly managers' review meeting is a ritual. The “manager of the year” award is a ceremony.用升遷來看差別：升上主管那天是 rite；主管每季的檢討會議是 ritual；「年度最佳主管」頒獎是 ceremony。

    A rite of passage does three things for a newcomer (p.17): it turns an outsider into an insider, gives a sense of belonging, and shows “how we do things around here.” A tradition or symbol is different again: it is an object, such as a company song, that lasts and keeps the culture stable.通過儀式對新人有三個作用（第 17 頁）：讓外人變成自己人、給人歸屬感、讓人知道「我們這裡怎麼做事」。傳統和象徵又不一樣：它是一個東西，例如公司歌，會長期存在，讓文化保持穩定。

    The slide lists retirement as a rite. A farewell party that thanks the person in public also works as a ceremony.投影片把退休列為 rite。如果另外辦一場公開感謝的歡送會，那也算是典禮。

    Stories故事

    Stories, gossip and the grapevine all carry messages about what the company values (p.18). The slides give two examples. One is a quote from Prince Mahidol: “True success is not in learning but in its application for the benefit of mankind” (p.19). The other is Saha Group's story about its founder, shown with its values: social responsibility, advancement, harmony, passion for excellence and truthfulness (p.20).故事、八卦和小道消息，都在傳達公司重視什麼（第 18 頁）。投影片舉了兩個例子。一個是 Mahidol 親王的名言：「真正的成功不在於學習，而在於將所學應用於造福人類」（第 19 頁）。另一個是 Saha 集團創辦人的故事，搭配它的價值：社會責任、進步、和諧、追求卓越和誠實（第 20 頁）。

    A value on its own is abstract. A story shows the value in action. People pass stories around anyway, so managers should pay attention to which stories are spreading.價值本身很抽象，故事讓人看到價值實際上怎麼做。大家本來就會傳故事，所以主管該注意的是：現在傳的是哪些故事。

    Pairs people mix up容易搞混的組合

    Pair組合How to tell them apart怎麼分辨
    Values / stories價值／故事A value is the rule, such as “customer first.” A story is an event that proves it, such as the founder driving all night to deliver an urgent order.價值是規則，例如「顧客優先」。故事是證明這條規則的一件事，例如創辦人連夜開車把急件送到。
    Heroes / stories英雄／故事The person is the hero. What people say about what that person did is the story. The same founder can be both.人是英雄；大家講這個人做過的事，就是故事。同一位創辦人可以兩個都是。
    Symbols / rituals象徵／例行儀式The company song is a symbol. Singing it at the start of every meeting is a ritual.公司歌是象徵；每次開會前都唱公司歌，是例行儀式。
    Values / vision價值／願景A vision says where the company is going. Values say how people should act on the way.願景說公司要去哪裡；價值說路上大家該怎麼做。
    Ceremonies / rewards典禮／獎勵A reward is money or a prize for one person. A ceremony is the public event where everyone sees it given.獎勵是給某個人的錢或獎品；典禮是大家一起看著頒發的公開活動。

    判斷順序先問形式：一句原則、一個人、一個東西、一個活動，還是一段經過。是活動的話，再問有沒有人換身分、是不是固定重複、是不是為了表揚。

    4. Culture, performance and change4. 文化、績效與變革

    Kotter and Heskett (1992), p.21Kotter 與 Heskett（1992），第 21 頁What it means for a manager對主管的意義
    Culture has a big effect on long-term financial results.文化對長期財務表現有很大影響。Culture affects results, so it is not just a “soft” issue.文化會影響成果，所以不只是「軟性」議題。
    The effect is even bigger when things change fast.在變化快的時候，影響更大。During a turnaround, culture can decide whether the change works.轉型期間，文化可能決定改變會不會成功。
    Cultures that hold back growth are common and form easily.阻礙成長的文化很常見，也很容易形成。If no one shapes the culture, it tends to go in the wrong direction.沒有人去塑造文化，它往往會往錯的方向走。
    Culture is hard to change, but it can be reshaped.文化很難改，但可以重新塑造。Change is possible, but it is slow and has to be planned.改變是可能的，但很慢，而且要有計畫。

    In a weak culture, people are not lazy. They are pushing in different directions. Vision, values and strategy give everyone the same direction. Leadership then gets everyone moving that way.弱文化不代表大家偷懶，而是大家往不同方向用力。願景、價值和策略給大家同一個方向，領導再讓所有人朝那個方向走。

    Culture changes more easily when (p.23)文化比較容易改變的情況（第 23 頁）

    A crisis threatens the company's survival. A new leader takes over with backing from the top, such as the board. The company is small and young. Or the culture is weak.危機威脅到公司生存；新領導人上任，並得到上層（例如董事會）支持；公司小而年輕；或原本的文化比較弱。

    Even then即使如此

    Changing a culture takes years. Strong cultures are harder to change than weak ones. That helps when the culture is good and hurts when it needs to change.改變文化要好幾年。強文化比弱文化更難改。文化好的時候這是優點，需要改變時就成了缺點。

    Learning objective 3 mentions culture “health and strength,” but the slides do not define strength. A common way to read it: strength is how many people share the culture; health is whether the culture helps the company reach its goals. A culture can be strong and still unhealthy.學習目標 3 提到文化的「健康度和強度」，但投影片沒有定義強度。常見的理解是：強度是多少人共享這個文化，健康度是這個文化有沒有幫公司達成目標。文化可以很強，卻不健康。

    5. Building an ethical culture5. 建立倫理文化

    Ethics: “standards of conduct that indicate how one should behave based on moral duties and virtues arising from principles about right and wrong.” (Josephson, 1990)倫理：「根據源自是非原則的道德義務與美德，指出應如何行為的行為標準。」（Josephson，1990）

    In plain words, ethics is a standard for how people should act, based on what is right and wrong. Slide p.27 shows the six elements again, with an arrow labeled “Ethical value” pointing to Values. So you build an ethical culture with the same six tools. You just put ethics into the core values.白話說，倫理就是依照是非對錯，判斷人應該怎麼做的標準。第 27 頁再次列出六要素，並有一個標著「倫理價值」的箭頭指向「價值」。所以建立倫理文化用的是同樣六個工具，只是把倫理放進核心價值。

    What managers can do (p.15)主管可以做什麼（第 15 頁）Element it uses運用的要素Example例子
    Make ethics a core value把倫理列為核心價值Values價值List integrity as a value and describe what it looks like at work把誠信列為價值，並說明它在工作中長什麼樣子
    Be a visible role model成為看得見的榜樣Heroes英雄A leader turns down a profitable shortcut that is unfair, in front of the team領導者當著團隊的面，拒絕一個有利可圖但不公平的捷徑

    Read the pyramid from the bottom. A company has to make money first, or it cannot do anything else for long. Above that, it must obey the law. Being ethical goes further than the law: something can be legal and still wrong. Giving back to the community is the top layer, and it is voluntary.金字塔要從下往上讀。公司必須先賺錢，否則什麼都做不久。往上是遵守法律。倫理比法律要求更多：合法的事也可能是錯的。最上層是回饋社區，屬於自願。

    The pyramid comes from slide p.29, which cites the World Business Council for Sustainable Development. Most textbooks call it Carroll's pyramid of corporate social responsibility.金字塔取自投影片第 29 頁，標示的出處是 World Business Council for Sustainable Development。多數教科書稱它為 Carroll 的企業社會責任金字塔。

    Integrity in action誠信要怎麼做

    Integrity is the value most likely to stay on paper. So it needs the most concrete behaviors. For a medical supplier, it means:誠信是最容易只停在紙上的價值，所以最需要寫成具體行為。對醫療用品商來說，它代表：

    • Tell customers the truth about stock, delivery dates and product limits.對顧客如實說明庫存、交期和產品限制。
    • Never sell expired, damaged or wrongly stored products.絕不賣過期、損壞或保存不當的產品。
    • No gifts or favors to win hospital orders.不用送禮或好處換醫院訂單。
    • Report mistakes early, including your own.及早回報錯誤，包括自己的。
    • Speak up when something feels wrong.覺得不對勁就說出來。

    8. Do not mix these up8. 易混淆概念

    Concepts概念First前者Second後者
    Culture / structure文化／結構Culture is the unwritten way people work.文化是大家不成文的做事方式。Structure is the formal chart of who reports to whom.結構是誰向誰匯報的正式組織圖。
    Corporate / national culture企業文化／國家文化Corporate culture belongs to one company.企業文化屬於一家公司。National culture (Session 6) is an average for a whole country.國家文化（第 6 堂）是整個國家的平均。
    Vision / values願景／價值A vision says where the company is going.願景說公司要去哪裡。Values say how people should act on the way.價值說路上大家該怎麼做。
    Rite / ritual通過儀式／例行儀式A rite marks a change in one person's status.通過儀式標記某個人身分的改變。A ritual is a routine everyone repeats.例行儀式是大家固定重複的做法。
    Ritual / ceremony例行儀式／典禮A ritual is part of normal work.例行儀式是日常工作的一部分。A ceremony is a special event to honor people.典禮是表揚人的特別活動。
    Strong / healthy強／健康A strong culture is shared by most people.強文化是大多數人共享的文化。A healthy culture helps the company reach its goals. A culture can be strong but unhealthy.健康的文化能幫公司達成目標。文化可以很強卻不健康。
    Legal / ethical合法／合乎倫理Legal means the law allows it.合法是法律允許。Ethical means it is right and fair, even where the law says nothing.合乎倫理是對而且公平，即使法律沒規定。
    Reward / ceremony獎勵／典禮A reward goes to one person.獎勵給某一個人。A ceremony shows it to everyone and spreads the value.典禮讓所有人看到，並把價值傳出去。
    Symbol / event象徵／活動A symbol is an object that lasts, like the Patient Chair.象徵是會留下來的東西，例如病人的椅子。An event is something people do, like the induction.活動是大家去做的事，例如入職活動。
    Case fact / our proposal個案事實／我們的提案The case gives LMS a vision but no values.個案中 LMS 有願景，沒有價值。The five values and every event, symbol and story are our proposal.五個價值和所有活動、象徵、故事都是我們的提案。

    9. Cold call practice9. 課堂問答練習

    What is corporate culture?什麼是企業文化？

    “The way we do things around here” (Deal and Kennedy, 1982). It is the shared values and habits that guide people, even when no one is supervising them.「我們這裡的做事方式」（Deal and Kennedy，1982）。也就是即使沒人監督，也會引導大家的共同價值和習慣。

    Name the five functions of culture.說出文化的五項功能。

    It gives the company an identity, gives work meaning, sets standards, helps people make sense of events, and guides behavior without supervision.它讓公司有識別、讓工作有意義、設定標準、幫大家理解發生的事，並在沒有監督時引導行為。

    How does culture form?文化如何形成？

    From the top, through the founders' beliefs and what senior managers do. From the bottom, through who is hired and what they learn from coworkers. Culture then affects satisfaction, commitment and performance.由上而下，來自創辦人的信念和高階主管的做法；由下而上，來自找誰進來、新人跟同事學到什麼。文化再影響滿意度、承諾和績效。

    What are the six elements?六要素是什麼？

    Values, heroes, traditions and symbols, rites and rituals, ceremonies, and stories.價值、英雄、傳統與象徵、儀式與例行儀式、典禮，以及故事。

    Why are values the center?為什麼價值是中心？

    Values plus everyday behavior make a healthy culture, and a healthy culture makes an effective organization. Leaders have to act on values, use them, defend them and reward them.價值加上日常行為，形成健康的文化；健康的文化，形成有效能的組織。領導者要照著做、用它、捍衛它並獎勵它。

    What does a rite of passage do?通過儀式有什麼作用？

    It turns a newcomer into an insider, gives them a sense of belonging and shows them how things are done.讓新人變成自己人，給他們歸屬感，並讓他們知道事情怎麼做。

    How does culture affect performance?文化如何影響績效？

    It has a big effect on long-term results, especially when things change fast. Bad cultures form easily. Culture is hard to change, but it can be reshaped.它對長期成果影響很大，在變化快的時候更明顯。壞文化很容易形成。文化很難改，但可以重新塑造。

    When is culture easier to change?什麼時候文化比較容易改？

    After a crisis, when a new leader has backing from the top, in small young companies, or when the culture is weak. It still takes years.發生危機、新領導人得到上層支持、公司小而年輕，或文化原本就弱的時候。還是要好幾年。

    How do you build an ethical culture?如何建立倫理文化？

    Make ethics a core value. Then model it, communicate it, train people, reward and punish openly, and protect people who speak up.把倫理列為核心價值。然後以身作則、說清楚期待、訓練員工、公開獎懲，並保護發言的人。

    What are the four corporate responsibilities?企業的四種責任是什麼？

    Economic (the base), legal, ethical and discretionary.經濟（基礎）、法律、倫理和自發性責任。
