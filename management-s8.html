    1. What culture is and what it does1. 文化是什麼、有什麼作用

    “Culture is the way we do things around here, or what keeps the herd moving in roughly the right direction.” (Deal and Kennedy, 1982)「文化就是我們這裡的做事方式，也就是讓一群人大致朝正確方向前進的力量。」（Deal and Kennedy，1982）

    The definition has two halves. Culture is how people actually behave, and it steers a group without a fence. “Roughly” matters: culture guides direction; it does not dictate every step. The deck opens with a related idea: sustainability culture, the beliefs, norms and practices tied to an organization's environmental impact, matters most during transitions, when practices are changing (Assoratgoon and Kantabutra, 2023).這個定義有兩半：文化是人實際的行為方式，也像沒有圍欄的引導，讓群體朝同一方向走。「大致」很重要：文化指引方向，不規定每一步。投影片開頭也提到相關概念：永續文化，是與組織環境影響有關的信念、規範與做法，在轉型、做法改變時最重要（Assoratgoon and Kantabutra，2023）。

    2. How culture forms2. 文化如何形成

    • From the top: founders' philosophy and values shape senior management's mission and goals, and management's strategy and actions.由上而下：創辦人的理念與價值，影響高階主管的使命與目標，以及管理階層的策略與行動。
    • From the bottom: who is hired (selection) and how newcomers learn from peers (socialization).由下而上：選誰進來（甄選），以及新人如何向同事學習（社會化）。
    • Two-way: once culture forms, it shapes what managers and staff do next.雙向：文化形成後，也會影響主管與員工接下來的行為。
    • Outcomes: satisfaction, commitment and performance.結果：滿意度、承諾與績效。

    中文邏輯文化不是主管宣布就成立：上面要有價值與行動，下面要「選對人」和「同事帶新人」，兩邊都做到才穩。

    Stories故事

    Stories, the grapevine, rumor and gossip all carry key messages (p.18). The slides show a Prince Mahidol quote, “True success is not in learning but in its application for the benefit of mankind” (p.19), and Saha Group's founder story alongside values such as social responsibility, advancement, harmony, passion for excellence and truthfulness (p.20).故事、小道消息、謠言與八卦都承載關鍵訊息（第 18 頁）。投影片展示 Mahidol 親王的名言：「真正的成功不在於學習，而在於將所學應用於造福人類」（第 19 頁），以及 Saha 集團的創辦人故事，搭配社會責任、進步、和諧、追求卓越、誠實等價值（第 20 頁）。

    A value is abstract; a story shows it in action with a person, a problem and a result. The grapevine spreads stories whether managers like it or not, so the practical question is which stories are spreading.價值很抽象；故事用一個人、一個問題和一個結果，讓價值動起來。不論主管喜不喜歡，小道消息都會傳播故事，所以實際的問題是：正在流傳的是哪些故事。

    4. Culture, performance and change4. 文化、績效與變革

    Kotter and Heskett (1992), p.21Kotter 與 Heskett（1992），第 21 頁What it means for a manager對主管的意義
    Culture can significantly affect long-term economic performance.文化會明顯影響長期經濟績效。Culture is a performance lever, not a soft extra.文化是績效槓桿，不是可有可無的軟性項目。
    It matters even more in times of rapid change.在快速變動時，影響更大。Turnarounds and transitions are when culture decides success.轉型與過渡期，正是文化決定成敗的時候。
    Cultures that impede financial growth are common and develop easily.阻礙財務成長的文化很常見，也很容易形成。An unshaped culture tends to drift the wrong way.沒有刻意形塑的文化，容易往錯的方向漂。
    Cultures are hard to change but can be reshaped.文化很難改變，但可以重新形塑。Change is possible, deliberate and slow.改變是可能的，需要刻意而且緩慢。

    A weak culture is not people being lazy; it is people working in different directions. Vision, values and strategy supply a direction; leadership gets everyone aligned with it.弱文化不是大家偷懶，而是大家往不同方向用力。願景、價值與策略提供方向；領導讓所有人對齊方向。

    Culture is more likely to change when (p.23)文化較容易改變的情況（第 23 頁）

    A dramatic crisis threatens survival; leadership turnover is backed by the top, such as the board; the company is small and young; or the culture is weak.重大危機威脅生存；領導人更替並獲得上層（例如董事會）支持；公司規模小且年輕；或原本文化較弱。

    Even then即使如此

    Cultural change takes years. Strong cultures change less easily than weak ones, so strength cuts both ways.文化改變需要好幾年。強文化比弱文化更難改變，所以「強」是兩面刃。

    Learning objective 3 asks about culture “health and strength.” The slides label weak, better and healthy cultures but do not define strength separately. A common reading: strength is how widely the culture is shared; health is whether it supports the organization's goals.學習目標 3 要分析文化的「健康度與強度」。投影片標示弱、較好與健康的文化，但沒有另外定義強度。常見理解：強度是文化被共享的廣度，健康度是它是否支持組織目標。

    8. Do not mix these up8. 易混淆概念

    Concepts概念First前者Second後者
    Culture / structure文化／結構Culture is informal control: how we do things.文化是非正式控制：我們怎麼做事。Structure is formal control: who reports to whom.結構是正式控制：誰向誰匯報。
    Corporate / national culture企業文化／國家文化Corporate culture belongs to one organization.企業文化屬於一個組織。National culture (Session 6) is a country-level average.國家文化（第 6 堂）是國家層級的平均。
    Vision / values願景／價值A vision says where the organization is going.願景說組織要往哪裡去。Values say how people should behave on the way.價值說路上大家該怎麼做。
    Rite / ritual通過儀式／例行儀式A rite marks one person's key moment.通過儀式標記某個人的關鍵時刻。A ritual is a routine way of working.例行儀式是固定的工作方式。
    Ritual / ceremony例行儀式／典禮A ritual repeats as part of work.例行儀式是工作中重複的做法。A ceremony honors people at a major event.典禮是在重大活動中表揚人。
    Strong / healthy強／健康Strength is how widely a culture is shared.強度是文化被共享的廣度。Health is whether it supports the goals; a strong culture can be unhealthy.健康是文化是否支持目標；強文化也可能不健康。
    Legal / ethical合法／合乎倫理Legal is what the law requires.合法是法律要求的事。Ethical is what is right, just and fair, beyond the law.合乎倫理是法律之外，對、公正、公平的事。
    Reward / ceremony獎勵／典禮A reward is paid to a person.獎勵是給某個人的。A ceremony celebrates it in front of others and carries the value.典禮是在眾人面前慶祝，並承載價值。

    9. Cold call practice9. 課堂問答練習

    What is corporate culture?什麼是企業文化？

    “The way we do things around here” (Deal and Kennedy, 1982): shared values and norms that guide behavior, even without supervision.「我們這裡的做事方式」（Deal and Kennedy，1982）：即使沒有監督，也能引導行為的共同價值與規範。

    Name the five functions of culture.說出文化的五項功能。

    Identity; meaning and commitment; performance expectations and standards; sense-making; shaping behavior without direct control.識別；意義與承諾；績效期待與標準；理解事件；在沒有直接控制時形塑行為。

    How does culture form?文化如何形成？

    From founders' values, senior management's mission and actions, and from selection and socialization of staff. Culture then shapes satisfaction, commitment and performance.來自創辦人的價值、高階主管的使命與行動，以及員工的甄選與社會化。文化再影響滿意度、承諾與績效。

    What are the six elements?六要素是什麼？

    Values, heroes and heroines, traditions and symbols, rites and rituals, ceremonies, and stories.價值、英雄、傳統與象徵、儀式與例行儀式、典禮、故事。

    Why are values the core?為什麼價值是核心？

    Core values with norms of behavior create a healthy culture, which creates an effective organization. Leaders must model, use, defend and reward them.核心價值加上行為規範形成健康文化，進而形成有效能的組織。領導者必須示範、使用、捍衛並獎勵價值。

    What does a rite of passage do?通過儀式有什麼作用？

    It turns outsiders into insiders, creates belonging and teaches newcomers how things are done.讓外人變成自己人，建立歸屬感，並教新人怎麼做事。

    How does culture affect performance?文化如何影響績效？

    It strongly affects long-term performance, even more in rapid change. Harmful cultures form easily; culture is hard to change but can be reshaped.文化強烈影響長期績效，在快速變動時更明顯。有害的文化容易形成；文化難改，但能重新形塑。

    When is culture more likely to change?什麼時候文化較容易改變？

    After a crisis, with leadership turnover backed by the top, in small young firms, or when the culture is weak. It still takes years.發生危機、領導人更替且獲上層支持、公司小而年輕，或文化較弱時。仍需要好幾年。

    How do you build an ethical culture?如何建立倫理文化？

    Make ethics a core value, model it, communicate it, train it, visibly reward and punish, and provide protective mechanisms.把倫理列為核心價值，親自示範、傳達、訓練，公開獎懲，並提供保護機制。

    What are the four corporate responsibilities?企業的四種責任是什麼？

    Economic (the foundation), legal, ethical and discretionary.經濟（基礎）、法律、倫理與自發性責任。

    Main framework: six elements of corporate culture主框架：企業文化六要素

    Using the six elements, LMS has improved its systems but not yet its culture. Values: it has a vision but no named values, so I would run a values workshop and make leaders model, use and reward three to five values. Heroes: Apichart is the only visible hero and is retiring, so recognize employee role models and develop a successor with Krisada's support. Traditions and symbols: none are described; a short values pledge at events can provide one. Rites and rituals: add a Welcome to LMS induction and turn the quarterly feedback into a Customer and Ideas Day. Ceremonies: rewards exist but are not celebrated, so hold an annual Values Day. Stories: word of mouth already brought high-flyers back; collect and retell stories that carry the values. Add hiring and appraisal on values, and integrity as a core value. Because culture change takes years, review values understanding, retention, customer results and new products annually.用六要素來看，LMS 改善了制度，但還沒改變文化。價值：有願景但沒有具名價值，所以我會舉辦價值工作坊，讓領導者示範、使用並獎勵三到五個價值。英雄：Apichart 是唯一明顯的英雄且即將退休，所以要表揚員工榜樣，並在 Krisada 支持下培養接班人。傳統與象徵：沒有描述；可在活動中加入簡短的價值宣言。儀式與例行儀式：新增「Welcome to LMS」入職，並把每季回饋變成顧客與構想日。典禮：有獎勵但沒有慶祝，所以舉辦年度價值日。故事：口碑已讓 high-flyer 回來；蒐集並重述承載價值的故事。另外依價值招募與考核，並把誠信列為核心價值。文化改變需要好幾年，所以每年檢視價值理解、留任、顧客成果與新產品。
